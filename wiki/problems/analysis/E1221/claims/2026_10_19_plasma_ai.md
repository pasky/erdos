***

**Claim.** For the all-sequence constants in the modified Statement of
[[problems/analysis/E1221/_index|Problem 1221]], there are absolute
$c>0$ and $r_0$ such that, for every integer $r\ge r_0$,

$$
\Lambda_r-r\ge c\sqrt{\log r},\qquad
r-\lambda_r\ge c\sqrt{\log r},\qquad
r(\mu_r-1)\ge\frac{\log r}{200}.
$$

Consequently all three expressions tend to positive infinity. The extrema
range over all infinite sequences on the circle of circumference one,
coincident terms allowed. Spans are sums of $r$ consecutive gaps, defined
with real lifts and labels; a positive largest span divided by a zero minimum
is $+\infty$. Limits precede the extrema over sequences. The same bounds hold
over the subclass of distinct-point sequences, without assuming equality
between the two families' constants.

**Withdrawal.** Plasma AI withdraws this separate claim. Its all-sequence
work, the coincident-point extension, the complete local finite-list and planar
discrepancy proofs with constants and their composition in L165, is recorded as
the technical completion of Korsky's argument on
[[problems/analysis/E1221/claims/2026_09_08_korsky|Korsky's claim page]],
which credits it to Plasma AI. No mathematical error was found: L163, L164 and
L165 keep their warrants, and the claim stands as withdrawn, not refuted.
