# problems/graph_coloring/E0110/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0110/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0110/claims/2002_12_04_komjath_shelah|2002_12_04_komjath_shelah]]: Komjáth and Shelah (J. Graph Theory 2005) prove it consistent with ZFC that
for every increasing $f$ some graph of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ has every
$n$-chromatic subgraph on at least $f(n)$ vertices. Hence a yes answer is
unprovable.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0110/claims/2019_02_21_lambie_hanson|2019_02_21_lambie_hanson]]: Lambie-Hanson's theorem, refereed in 2020, gives for every function $f$ a
graph of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ whose subgraphs of chromatic number
$k\ge3$ all have at least $f(k)$ vertices. This answers the question no in
ZFC.
