# problems/polynomials/E0116/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|1961_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke proves that a monic polynomial of degree $n$ with zeros in the
closed unit disk has modulus at most one on a disk of radius $1/(2en^2)$, so
on a set of area at least a constant times $n^{-4}$. The result is refereed
and credited by erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/polynomials/E0116/claims/2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: Krishnapur, Lundberg and Ramachandran prove that the least area of the set
where a monic polynomial of degree $n$ with zeros in the closed unit disk has
modulus below one lies between $c/\log n$ and $C/\log\log n$. This 2025
preprint is credited by erdosproblems.com.
