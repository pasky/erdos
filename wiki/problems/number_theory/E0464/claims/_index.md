# problems/number_theory/E0464/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0464/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0464/claims/1979_12_01_pollington|1979_12_01_pollington]]: Pollington's Theorem: for every sequence $(t_k)$ with consecutive ratios at
least $\alpha>1$ there are $\beta>0$ and uncountably many $\xi$ such that
every fractional part of $\xi t_k$ lies in $[\beta,1-\beta]$. An irrational
such $\xi$ answers Problem 464.

[[problems/number_theory/E0464/claims/1980_09_01_de_mathan|1980_09_01_de_mathan]]: De Mathan's Corollary 1: for every sequence $(q_n)$ with consecutive ratios at
least $\lambda>1$, the $x$ in an interval for which $(q_nx)$ is not everywhere
dense modulo 1 form a set of Hausdorff dimension 1. An irrational such $x$
answers Problem 464.

[[problems/number_theory/E0464/claims/2001_04_01_katznelson|2001_04_01_katznelson]]: Katznelson's Theorem 1.2 and Claim 2: for a lacunary sequence, the multipliers
$\alpha$ that keep every $\lambda\alpha$, with $\lambda$ in the sequence, away
from the integers form a set of Hausdorff dimension 1. An irrational such
$\alpha$ answers Problem 464.
