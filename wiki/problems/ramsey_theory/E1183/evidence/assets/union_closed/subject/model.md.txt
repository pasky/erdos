# Union-closed-family model

For n>=1, [n]={1,...,n}. For an integer k>=2 a k-coloring is any
map chi:2^[n]->[k]. A family F of subsets is monochromatic when chi
is constant on F, and union-closed when A union B belongs to F for
all A,B in F. There is no intersection condition and the empty set
need not belong to the family. Define

F_k(n)=min_chi max{|F|: F is a monochromatic union-closed family},
F(n)=F_2(n).

These finite extrema exist. A singleton family gives F_k(n)>=1.
A two-coloring can be regarded as a k-coloring with unused colors,
so F_k(n)<=F(n) for k>=2.

The two target growth questions, labeled eq:conj in the supplied source,
are F(n)>=n^{omega(n)} for some omega(n)->infinity, and a strict bound
F(n)<(1+epsilon(n))^n for every n>=1 with epsilon(n)->0. The precise
explicit conclusions under examination are Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 and
all four union-family clauses of Corollary 1.3 in statements.tex.txt.
The symbol f for a sublattice minimax quantity is outside this subject.
