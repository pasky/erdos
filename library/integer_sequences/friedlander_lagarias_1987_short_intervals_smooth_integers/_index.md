## Scope

The introduction separates two questions for smooth integers in short intervals:
whether every sufficiently late interval contains one, and whether it contains a
positive proportion. Its functions $f(\alpha)$ and $f^*(\alpha)$ retain a
quantifier over every smoothness exponent $\alpha_1>\alpha$. Theorem 4 gives
an explicit product construction for the existence question. A statement about
an infimum of admissible exponents must not be read as a bound at its endpoint
without checking the quantifiers.
