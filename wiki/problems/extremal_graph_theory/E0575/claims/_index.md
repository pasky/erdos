[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0575/claims/2022_07_25_wigderson|2022_07_25_wigderson]]: Wigderson's unpublished elementary note observes that the two-edge star and
the two-edge matching, both bipartite, have joint extremal number 1, while
each alone has linear extremal number. No outside acceptance is documented.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0575/claims/2026_08_01_openai|2026_08_01_openai]]: OpenAI gives a finite family $F$ of connected bipartite graphs, each
containing a cycle, with $\mathrm{ex}(n,F)=O(n^{4/3-1/48})$ and
$\mathrm{ex}(n,G)=\Omega(n^{4/3})$ for each $G\in F$. erdosproblems.com
accepted this disproof on 31 August 2026, and it has no refereed version.
