# problems/factorials_binomials/E0401/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0401/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0401/claims/2026_01_11_barreto_price|2026_01_11_barreto_price]]: Kevin Barreto and Liam Price, with GPT-5.2 Pro, prove that for every $r$
infinitely many $n$ admit $a_1,a_2$ with $a_1+a_2>n+\omega(r)\log n$ and
$a_1!\,a_2!\mid n!\,P_r^{\,n}$, where $\omega(r)\to\infty$ and $P_r$ is the
product of the first $r$ primes. The Lean proof is by Aristotle.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0401/claims/2026_01_11_sothanaphan|2026_01_11_sothanaphan]]: The valuation argument behind the Lean proof of Problem 728, generalized to a
density-one theorem, yields the examples Problem 401 asks for. Sothanaphan's
arXiv write-up states this in its appendix, with a sketched proof.
