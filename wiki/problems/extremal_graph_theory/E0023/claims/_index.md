# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/claims/2021_03_25_balogh_clemen_lidicky|2021_03_25_balogh_clemen_lidicky]]: Theorem 2(b),(c) of Balogh, Clemen and Lidický: for every sufficiently large
$n$, a triangle-free graph on $5n$ vertices with at least
$0.3197\binom{5n}{2}$ or at most $0.2486\binom{5n}{2}$ edges can be made
bipartite by deleting at most $n^2$ edges. The proof is a sketch in an
extended abstract, not refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/claims/2026_06_26_ferudun|2026_06_26_ferudun]]: Ferudun's arXiv preprint of June 2026 asserts that for every $n$ from 1 to 40,
the most edges a triangle-free graph on $5n$ vertices needs deleted to become
bipartite is exactly $n^2$. Its certificates are not reviewed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/claims/2026_10_19_plasma_ai|2026_10_19_plasma_ai]]: Every triangle-free graph on 5n vertices whose edge density 2e/(5n)^2 is at
most 0.2486 or at least 0.3197, boundaries included, can be made bipartite
by deleting at most n^2 edges, for every n, by a blow-up transfer of
Balogh, Clemen and Lidický's large-order theorem; author-recorded at tier 0,
catalog acceptance pending.
