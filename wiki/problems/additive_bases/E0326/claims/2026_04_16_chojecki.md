***

**Claim.** Przemek Chojecki's post of 16 April 2026 in the discussion thread
of [[problems/additive_bases/E0326/_index|Problem 326]] links a short note
with a counterexample that the post credits to GPT-5.4. The site's wording
then asked Erdős and Graham's "another way of stating the problem": does
every basis of order $2$ have a subset which is also a basis and for which
$\lim a_k/k^2$ does not exist? The note answers no. Its basis is the set of
natural numbers whose ternary digits are all $0$ or $1$, a basis of order
$2$ since the sum of two such numbers can have any ternary digit, and every
sub-basis $\{b_k\}$ of it has $b_k/k^2\to0$, so the limit exists for every
sub-basis.

**Submission note.** Posted to the site's forum by Przemek Chojecki on 16
April 2026:

> Looks like this is false. A counterexample given by GPT-5.4 is
> $$
> A = \Bigl\{ \sum_{j\ge 0} \varepsilon_j 3^j : \varepsilon_j\in\{0,1\},
> \ \text{for all but finitely many j} \Bigr\}
> $$
> Thus $A$ is the set of natural numbers whose ternary digits are all 0 or 1
> (alternatively exclude 0 depending on the convention). Here's a short note
> with a proof that this is indeed a counterexample

**Why it is rejected.** It answers a variant, not the Statement. The note
answers the site's earlier wording, in which a limit $0$ of $a_k/k^2$ counts
as a limit. The site's curator replied the same day that the limit was meant
to be a constant $c\ne0$, since otherwise there are easy counterexamples such
as this one, and rewrote the problem as Erdős and Graham's minimal-basis
question, the Statement of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0326/_index|Problem 326]]: is there a minimal basis
of order $2$ with $a_k/k^2\to c$ for some $c\ne0$? A basis whose sub-bases
all have $b_k/k^2\to0$ has no such sub-basis and says nothing about minimal
bases with a nonzero limit, so its mathematics answers the variant and does
not settle this problem; the value `disproved` is its answer to that variant.

**Standing.** Rejected, as answering a variant. The note is unrefereed, the
thread records no review of it, and this corpus has not checked it.
