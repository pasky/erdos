# problems/unit_fractions/E0047/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0047/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0047/claims/2021_12_07_bloom|2021_12_07_bloom]]: Bloom's theorem that a subset of the first $N$ integers with reciprocal sum at
least a constant times $\frac{\log N\log\log\log N}{\log\log N}$ has a subset
with reciprocal sum $1$. For large $N$ this threshold is below $\delta\log N$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0047/claims/2024_04_10_liu_sawhney|2024_04_10_liu_sawhney]]: Liu and Sawhney's theorem that a subset of the first $N$ integers with
reciprocal sum at least $(\log N)^{4/5+\varepsilon}$ has a subset with
reciprocal sum $1$. For large $N$ this threshold is far below $\delta\log N$.
