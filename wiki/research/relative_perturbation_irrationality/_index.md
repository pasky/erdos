# Relative perturbations and the growth of irrationality sequences

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/relative_perturbation_irrationality/increasing_blocks|increasing_blocks]]: A recursive block choice makes every relative perturbation irrational while forcing the root liminf to be one.

***

The [[research/relative_perturbation_irrationality/increasing_blocks|complete argument]]
reconstructs the increasing-block idea recorded on the
[[problems/irrationality/E0263/claims/2026_05_09_price|Price claim page]]
with a complete denominator estimate. The reconstruction is author-recorded
and gives no new acceptance to the uninspected manuscript or formal file.

The two demands on the blocks are compatible: their lengths greatly
exceed the logarithm of their starting points, yet their reciprocal mass
can be made arbitrarily small relative to all earlier denominators.
The first gives root liminf one, and the second makes a rational tail,
after multiplication by its denominator, a positive integer below one.

This resolves the growth implication at author scope. The separate
question in [[problems/irrationality/E0263/_index|Problem 263]] about
$a_n=2^{2^n}$ remains untouched: the construction chooses different
blocks and does not specialize to that fixed sequence. No finite test
or generic statement excluding only countably many bases identifies
the behavior of the particular base $2$.
