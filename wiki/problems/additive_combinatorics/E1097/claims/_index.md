# problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/claims/1999_06_14_katz_tao|1999_06_14_katz_tao]]: Katz and Tao's sums-differences bound $N^{2-1/6}$ gives, through an elementary
embedding, at most $n^{11/6}$ common differences of three-term progressions in
a set of $n$ integers.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/claims/2014_04_14_lemm|2014_04_14_lemm]]: Lemm's counterexample to the sums-differences statement with exponent above
$1.77898$ gives, through an elementary embedding, sets with more than
$n^{1.77898}$ common differences. So $O(n^{3/2})$ does not always suffice.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/claims/2026_05_28_firsching|2026_05_28_firsching]]: Moritz Firsching claims a self-contained Lean 4 proof, in his fork of
formal-conjectures, that no constant $C$ bounds the number of common
differences by $Cn^{3/2}$. The claim answers the second question no.
