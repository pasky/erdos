# Banks, Ford, Luca, Pappalardi and Shparlinski: values of the Euler function

[[arithmetic_functions/_index|..]]

[[arithmetic_functions/banks_et_al_2005_values_euler_function_various_sequences/theorem_4_1|theorem_4_1]]: An upper bound of x/(log x)^2 times a subpower of log x for primes p
admitting phi(n)=p-1 with n other than p or 2p.

***

William D. Banks, Kevin Ford, Florian Luca, Francesco Pappalardi and Igor E.
Shparlinski, *Values of the Euler Function in Various Sequences*,
Monatshefte für Mathematik **146** (2005), 1--19,
[DOI:10.1007/s00605-005-0302-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00605-005-0302-7).
The publisher records online publication on 6 April 2005.

The edition read is the 25-page
[author manuscript](https://www.ford126.web.illinois.edu/wwwpapers/philam.pdf).
Its pagination differs from the journal edition, which has not been compared.
The paper studies totient and Carmichael values and totients in polynomial
sequences. Its
[[arithmetic_functions/banks_et_al_2005_values_euler_function_various_sequences/theorem_4_1|Theorem 4.1]]
bounds the exceptional shifted-prime values relevant here.

**Read status.** Claims checked: the exceptional-prime definition on p. 3,
the constant on pp. 13--14 and Theorem 4.1 on p. 14 were read from page
images. The proof on pp. 14--15 was read for its largest-prime split and
totient-value sum, without checking its sieve and counting inputs. No
independent proof verification is recorded.

**Terms.** The author download and
[publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00605-005-0302-7)
were inspected on 2026-10-15; no open redistribution license was found.
No file of this source is held. No full transcription is provided under these
terms.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/arithmetic_functions/E0409/_index|Problem 409]]:
the result restricts the primes that can have a nontrivial basin under
$n\mapsto\varphi(n)+1$. It does not settle the exceptional basins.
