# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/claims/1992_07_01_chung|1992_07_01_chung]]: Chung's 1992 partition of the edges of the $n$-cube into four subgraphs
without a six-cycle gives a class with at least a quarter of the edges and no
$C_6$, so the statement fails for every $\epsilon\le1/4$. The paper is
refereed and credited by the site.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/claims/1993_03_01_brouwer_dejter_thomassen|1993_03_01_brouwer_dejter_thomassen]]: Brouwer, Dejter and Thomassen give an explicit four-coloring of the $n$-cube's
edges with no monochromatic four- or six-cycle, so a color class with at least
a quarter of the edges avoids $C_6$ and the statement fails for every
$\epsilon\le1/4$. The paper is refereed and credited by the site.
