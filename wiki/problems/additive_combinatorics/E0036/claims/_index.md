# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2022_01_14_white|2022_01_14_white]]: Theorem 1 of White's paper (Acta Arith. 2023) shows that the minimum overlap
constant is at least 0.379005, the erdosproblems.com record lower bound. The
paper is refereed, and the library card records two inconsistencies in its
printed formulas.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_01_22_yuksekgonul_et_al|2026_01_22_yuksekgonul_et_al]]: The TTT-Discover system of Yuksekgonul and ten coauthors (arXiv 2026) found a
600-piece step function that bounds the minimum overlap constant above by
0.380876, the erdosproblems.com record. The certificate is not in the PDF, so
the bound is claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_06_30_kim_pilanci|2026_06_30_kim_pilanci]]: Kim and Pilanci (arXiv 2026) give a semidefinite strengthening of White's
convex relaxation, found with AI agents and human review, and report the lower
bound 0.37912. It is a preprint without outside review, so it is claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_07_12_russell|2026_07_12_russell]]: Russell's Zenodo paper (2026) certifies, by exact rational evaluation of
explicit step functions, that the minimum overlap constant is below
0.38085906. It is self-published without outside review, so it is claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_07_20_price|2026_07_20_price]]: Liam Price submitted to the erdosproblems.com proof-claims tab on 2026-07-20 a
partial proof claim, credited to GPT Pro, that the minimum overlap constant is
at least 0.38055470. erdosproblems.com has not examined it, so it is claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_09_19_drynshock|2026_09_19_drynshock]]: The user Drynshock submitted to the erdosproblems.com proof-claims tab on
2026-09-19 a partial proof claim, credited to GPT 6 Pro, that the minimum
overlap constant exceeds 0.3805634. erdosproblems.com has not examined it, so
it is claimed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_10_09_wetch|2026_10_09_wetch]]: Justin Wetch submitted to erdosproblems.ai on 2026-10-09 a partial proof claim
of computer-assisted bounds $0.380627425<c<0.38085858$ for the minimum overlap
constant. The site has not reviewed it and no outside review is known, so it
is claimed.
