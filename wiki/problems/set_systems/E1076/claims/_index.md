[[problems/set_systems/E1076/claims/2018_02_12_glock_kuhn_lo_osthus|2018_02_12_glock_kuhn_lo_osthus]]: For every fixed $k$, Glock, Kühn, Lo and Osthus build $n$-vertex partial
Steiner triple systems with $(1/6-o(1))n^2$ triples and no $j$ vertices
spanning $j-2$ triples for $4\le j\le k$. With linearity, this refereed
Combinatorica result, credited by the curator, settles the corrected
Statement.

Statement, and it is refereed and credited by the site's curator.
