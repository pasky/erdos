**Claim.** C. Axler, *Some results on a conjecture of Hardy and Littlewood*,
arXiv:1909.12625 (first version 27 September 2019; the second, of 30
September 2019, is the copy read),
[[../library/primes/axler_2019_some_results_conjecture_hardy_littlewood/theorem_1_3|Theorem 1.3]]
(p. 2): with $c_0=0.70881678090424862707121$,

$$
\pi(m+n)\le\pi(m)+\pi(n)
$$

for all integers $m\ge n\ge2$ with $n\ge c_0m/\log^2m$. The proof (pp. 5--6)
applies Proposition 5.1, which turns explicit two-term bounds for $\pi$ into
a range of the inequality, together with the computer check of
[[../library/primes/axler_2019_some_results_conjecture_hardy_littlewood/proposition_2_4|Proposition 2.4]]
(p. 2), which gives the inequality whenever $m+n\le39\,708\,229\,123$.
[[../library/primes/axler_2019_some_results_conjecture_hardy_littlewood/theorem_1_1|Theorem 1.1]]
(p. 2) proves the cone $m/1950\le n\le m$, and
[[../library/primes/axler_2019_some_results_conjecture_hardy_littlewood/theorem_1_5|Theorem 1.5]]
(p. 2) the range $n\ge\sqrt m\log m\log(m\log^8m)/(4\pi)$ under the Riemann
hypothesis; neither widens the unconditional range for large pairs. The
paper is compiled at
[[../library/primes/axler_2019_some_results_conjecture_hardy_littlewood/_index|its card]],
whose statements were read clause by clause and whose proofs were read but
not checked.

**Covers.** Every integer pair whose smaller entry $n\ge2$ and larger entry
$m$ satisfy $n\ge c_0m/\log^2m$. For large pairs this contains the ranges of
[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2002_01_01_dusart|Dusart's claim]] and
[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2022_06_03_alkan|Alkan's claim]]. Not covered:
the pairs with $n<c_0m/\log^2m$, which include pairs with both entries
arbitrarily large, so the problem as posed is not settled.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Standing.** The paper is on arXiv only: its record carries no journal
reference, and no published version is known. So the claim is `claimed`
with no evidence listed. The page is named by the first version's posting
date.
