[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0580/claims/2011_02_04_zhao|2011_02_04_zhao]]: Zhao's main theorem (Electronic Journal of Combinatorics) shows that for every
$n\ge n_0$, with $n_0$ not stated, a graph on $n$ vertices in which half the
vertices have degree at least $n/2$ contains every tree with at most $n/2$
edges. The paper is refereed.

computer-assisted verification that the site's vertex formulation holds for
every $n\le19$. The claim is unreviewed, was reclassified as partial by a
moderator, and says nothing about $n\ge20$.
