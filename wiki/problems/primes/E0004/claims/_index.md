# problems/primes/E0004/claims

[[problems/primes/E0004/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/1938_10_01_rankin|1938_10_01_rankin]]: Rankin's 1938 lower bound for the largest prime gap below $X$, with the
constant $1/3$, answers the question yes for every $C<1/3$. The refereed paper
appeared in the Journal of the London Mathematical Society.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2014_08_20_ford_green_konyagin_tao|2014_08_20_ford_green_konyagin_tao]]: Ford, Green, Konyagin and Tao prove that the largest prime gap below $X$
exceeds Rankin's 1938 scale by a factor tending to infinity, so the question's
bound holds for every constant. The refereed paper appeared in the Annals and
is credited by the site's curator.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2014_08_21_maynard|2014_08_21_maynard]]: Maynard proves that the ratio of the prime gap to Rankin's scale has infinite
limit superior, so the question's bound holds for every constant. The refereed
paper appeared in the Annals and is credited by the site's curator beside the
independent 2014 proof.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2014_12_16_ford_green_konyagin_maynard_tao|2014_12_16_ford_green_konyagin_maynard_tao]]: Ford, Green, Konyagin, Maynard and Tao prove that the largest prime gap below
$X$ is $\gg\log X\log\log X\log\log\log\log X/\log\log\log X$, a factor
$\log\log\log X$ beyond the question's bound. The refereed paper appeared in
JAMS and is credited by the curator.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev|2026_08_26_alexeev]]: Two Lean modules in Boris Alexeev's lean-proofs repository prove the
question's statement for every $C>0$ and the 2018 five-author bound, and they
name no informal author. It is a claim, and this corpus has not built the
modules.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2026_08_26_dottedcalculator|2026_08_26_dottedcalculator]]: A manuscript written by an AI system and posted by the forum user
DottedCalculator proves prime gaps of order
$\log n\log\log n/\log\log\log\log n$, beyond the question's bound. The result
is credited by the curator.

[[problems/primes/E0004/claims/2026_09_03_openai|2026_09_03_openai]]: A short OpenAI note attributing its proof to GPT 6 Astra claims that the
largest prime gap below $X$ exceeds a constant times
$\log X(\log\log X)^2\log\log\log\log X/(\log\log\log X)^2$. The claim is
pending.
