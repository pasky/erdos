# problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/1990_09_01_erdos_pach|1990_09_01_erdos_pach]]: Erdős and Pach prove that among $n$ pairwise disjoint translates of one
compact convex planar set, at most $O(n^{4/3})$ pairs are at distance
exactly 1.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/2006_03_31_valtr|2006_03_31_valtr]]: Valtr's Oberwolfach abstract of 2005 announces $h(n)=\Theta(n^{4/3})$ for
disjoint translates of a compact convex set, and the same with centrally
symmetric translates. The construction was outlined only in the talk.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0956/claims/2026_04_27_chojecki|2026_04_27_chojecki]]: Chojecki's note claims that a small parabolic-cap body gives $n$ disjoint
translates with at least $c_0\,n^{4/3}$ unit distances. With the bound of
Erdős and Pach this gives $h(n)=\Theta(n^{4/3})$, so any $c<1/3$ answers the
question.
