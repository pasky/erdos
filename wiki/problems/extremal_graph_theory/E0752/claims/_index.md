# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims/1999_04_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1999_04_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp prove the case $s=2$ of Problem 752: a
graph of minimum degree $k$ and girth at least five has $\Omega(k^2)$ distinct
cycle lengths. The paper is in Discrete Math. 200 (1999) and is known through
later citations.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/claims/2007_07_14_sudakov_verstraete|2007_07_14_sudakov_verstraete]]: Sudakov and Verstraëte (Combinatorica 28 (2008)) prove that a graph of average
degree $d$ and girth $g$ has $\Omega(d^{\lfloor(g-1)/2\rfloor})$ consecutive
even cycle lengths. This gives the $\gg k^s$ distinct cycle lengths that
Problem 752 asks for.
