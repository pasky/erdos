[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0814/claims/2017_05_28_sauermann|2017_05_28_sauermann]]: Sauermann's Theorem 1.3 (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2019) shows that for
$k\ge3$, a graph one edge above the threshold has a subgraph of minimum degree
at least $k$ on at most $(1-1/(10^4k^3))n$ vertices. The result is refereed
and credited by erdosproblems.com.
