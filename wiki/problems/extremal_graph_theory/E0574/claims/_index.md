# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/claims/1994_03_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar|1994_03_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar]]: The site's first disproof, by Lazebnik, Ustimenko and Woldar, gives bipartite
$C_{2k}$-free graphs with constant $2/3^{4/3}$ at $k=3$ and $4/5^{6/5}$ at
$k=5$, above the proposed $2^{-1-1/k}$. It was refereed in J. Combin. Theory
Ser. B and is credited by the site.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/claims/2005_06_10_furedi_naor_verstraete|2005_06_10_furedi_naor_verstraete]]: Füredi, Naor and Verstraëte give an alternative disproof at $k=3$: $C_6$-free
bipartite graphs with parts of sizes $m$ and $2m$ and with $2m^{4/3}+O(m)$
edges, with constant at least $2/3^{4/3}>2^{-4/3}$ along a sequence of orders.
It was refereed in Adv. Math. and is credited by the site.
