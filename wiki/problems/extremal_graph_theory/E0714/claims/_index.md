# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1954_01_01_kovari_sos_turan|1954_01_01_kovari_sos_turan]]: In a refereed paper in Colloq. Math. 3 (1954), Kővári, Sós and Turán prove
that the least number of ones in an $n\times n$ 0-1 matrix that forces a
$2\times2$ minor of ones is asymptotic to $n^{3/2}$. This gives
$\mathrm{ex}(n;K_{2,2})\gg n^{3/2}$, the case $r=2$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1966_02_01_erdos_renyi_sos|1966_02_01_erdos_renyi_sos]]: In a refereed paper in Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. 1 (1966), Erdős, Rényi and
Sós prove that the largest number of edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no
four-cycle is asymptotic to $n^{3/2}/2$. So
$\mathrm{ex}(n;K_{2,2})\gg n^{3/2}$, the case $r=2$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/claims/1966_08_01_brown|1966_08_01_brown]]: Brown's refereed paper in Canad. Math. Bull. 9 (1966) builds sphere graphs
over $GF(p)$ with no $K_{3,3}$ and about $n^{5/3}/2$ edges, and its Section 3
gives the $C_4$ asymptotic. So $\mathrm{ex}(n;K_{r,r})\gg n^{2-1/r}$ holds for
$r=3$ and $r=2$.
