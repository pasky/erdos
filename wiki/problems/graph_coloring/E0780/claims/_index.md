# problems/graph_coloring/E0780/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0780/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0780/claims/1978_11_01_lovasz|1978_11_01_lovasz]]: Lovász proves topologically that a $t$-coloring of the $r$-subsets of an
$n$-set with $n\ge2r+t-1$ has two disjoint sets of one color. This gives the
chromatic number of the Kneser graph and is the case $k=2$ of the problem.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0780/claims/1986_11_01_alon_frankl_lovasz|1986_11_01_alon_frankl_lovasz]]: Alon, Frankl and Lovász prove topologically, for every $k$, that a
$t$-coloring of the $r$-subsets of an $n$-set with $n\ge kr+(t-1)(k-1)$ has
$k$ pairwise disjoint sets of one color. This gives the chromatic number of
the Kneser hypergraph.
