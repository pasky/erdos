# problems/set_systems/E0499/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1962_08_01_marcus_minc|1962_08_01_marcus_minc]]: Marcus and Minc (1962) prove that every doubly stochastic $n\times n$ matrix
has a permutation along which the product of the entries is at least $n^{-n}$.
The site's curator credits the refereed paper, which appeared in Proc. Amer.
Math. Soc.

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1980_01_01_egorychev|1980_01_01_egorychev]]: Egorychev (1980, published 1981) proves van der Waerden's conjecture that
every doubly stochastic $n\times n$ matrix has permanent at least $n!/n^n$,
which yields a diagonal with product at least $n^{-n}$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1980_01_01_gyires|1980_01_01_gyires]]: The site credits Gyires (1980) with a proof of van der Waerden's conjecture,
but the paper derives it from its own unproved conjectures and proves it only
for symmetric positive semidefinite matrices. The credit is therefore
rejected.

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/claims/1981_06_01_falikman|1981_06_01_falikman]]: Falikman (1981) proves van der Waerden's conjecture that every doubly
stochastic $n\times n$ matrix has permanent at least $n!/n^n$. This yields a
diagonal whose product is at least $n^{-n}$.
