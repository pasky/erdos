[[problems/divisors/E1217/claims/2026_04_16_tang_li|2026_04_16_tang_li]]: A manuscript that Tang and Li posted on 16 April 2026, with a proof produced
by GPT 5.4 Pro, proves the problem's inequality under the density hypothesis.
It also remarks that a positive growth rate of the weighted sum suffices.

[[problems/divisors/E1217/claims/2026_05_01_alexeev_barreto_li_lichtman_price_shah_tang_tao|2026_05_01_alexeev_barreto_li_lichtman_price_shah_tang_tao]]: In their preprint of May 2026, Alexeev and seven coauthors prove that if
$\sum_{a\in A,\,a\le x}1/(a\log a)$ has a positive upper growth rate relative
to $\log\log x$, then $A$ contains an infinite divisibility chain whose count
has at least that upper growth rate.
