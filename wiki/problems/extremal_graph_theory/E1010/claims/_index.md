# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/claims/1962_03_01_erdos|1962_03_01_erdos]]: Erdős (Illinois J. Math. 1962) proves that a graph on $n$ vertices with
$\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+t$ edges has at least $t\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ triangles
whenever $t$ is below a constant times $n$. The paper is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/claims/1976_01_01_lovasz_simonovits|1976_01_01_lovasz_simonovits]]: Lovász and Simonovits prove that a graph on $n$ vertices with
$\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+k$ edges, where $k<n/2$, has at least
$k\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ triangles. The proof is in the 1976 paper and in the
1983 chapter.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/claims/1981_01_01_nikiforov_khadzhiivanov|1981_01_01_nikiforov_khadzhiivanov]]: The site credits a 1981 note by Nikiforov and Khadzhiivanov in the Comptes
rendus of the Bulgarian Academy with an independent proof of the
Erdős–Rademacher conjecture. The note is not held, and no record of it was
found.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev|2026_08_26_alexeev]]: A Lean development in Boris Alexeev's repository, first committed on 26 August
2026, proves for every $n$ that a graph with $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+t$ edges,
where $t<\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, has at least $t\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ triangles. It
was read as text only.
