# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1009/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1009/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1009/claims/1988_01_01_gyori|1988_01_01_gyori]]: Győri (Colloq. Math. Soc. János Bolyai 52, 1988) proves that a graph on $n$
vertices with $t_2(n)+k$ edges, where $k=o(n^2)$, has at least $k-O(k^2/n^2)$
edge-disjoint triangles. This gives $f(c)$ for every $c$, and the claim is
accepted on curator credit.
