# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0717/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0717/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0717/claims/2011_07_11_fox_lee_sudakov|2011_07_11_fox_lee_sudakov]]: Fox, Lee and Sudakov prove that every graph $G$ on $n$ vertices has
$\chi(G)\le Cn^{1/2}\sigma(G)/\log n$, where $C$ is an absolute constant and
$\sigma(G)$ is the order of the largest clique subdivision in $G$. This
answers Problem 717 affirmatively.
