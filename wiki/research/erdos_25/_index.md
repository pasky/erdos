## Where things stand

**Unresolved.** No general proof or
counterexample was obtained for distinct singleton moduli with activation
$n\ge q$. For finite-prefix
survivors $A^{(k)}$, put $\delta=\lim_k d(A^{(k)})$. The central
missing assertion remains
$$
 \lim_{k\to\infty}\overline{\operatorname{ld}}(A^{(k)}\setminus A)=0.
$$
The [basic reductions](basic_reductions.md) prove that this gives
logarithmic density $\delta$, and that an excess over the periodic
candidate yields an oscillating subfamily. Natural density, logarithmic
density, and their upper and lower versions are kept distinct throughout.

**Latest established results.**

- [Full-survivor conditional marginals](full_survivor_trace_obstruction.md)
  cannot be transferred to arithmetic signed traces merely using bounded
  weights and square-tail control. Explicit singleton systems have
  $\delta>1/4$, actual natural density $\delta$, and pointwise
  $\alpha_p(n)\le1/p$, yet their canonical infinite-survivor weights
  give nonzero fixed-prime traces and uniformly positive high-prime-block
  traces. This refutes that particular infinite-marginal transfer, not
  a transfer retaining finite activated weights or the density statement.
- [Bounded-height smaller-part centers](bounded_height_prime_centers.md)
  give full arithmetic capped-square tails, closing the middle-prime
  range for this subclass. On $(N,2N]$, the centers may vary with
  the prime and number up to $\exp(\kappa L_N)$,
  $L_N=\log N\log\log\log N/\log\log N$, $\kappa<1/4$,
  with polynomial height (or the stated larger height).
  All largest-prime exponents and arbitrary new-prime digits are
  allowed. The unrestricted square estimate and the finite-activated
  conditioned transfer remain unproved.
- [Exact capped prime fractions](capped_prime_fractions.md) have uniformly
  vanishing harmonic square tails outside
  $\exp(c\log n\,\log\log\log n/\log\log n)<p\le n^{1/3}$,
  for every fixed $0<c<1/2$, after a fixed prime cutoff tends to
  infinity. An optimized Rankin argument enlarges the earlier
  small-prime range and removes the dyadic constant loss. It handles
  all prime-power exponents and retains original activation.
  The remaining middle prime range is untreated;
  even its square bound would still need a conditioned arithmetic
  digit transfer. In the exponent-one core, random digit assignments
  show that a uniform square bound is equivalent up to a constant to
  one for the capped smaller-part incidence potential.
- [Very smooth smaller parts](smooth_largest_prime_reduction.md) with
  $m>p^{c\log\log p/\log\log\log p}$, $c>1$, form a
  reciprocal-summable exception. The threshold is sharp away from
  $c=1$; this reduces both the density and capped-square targets,
  but does not settle their remaining core. The same argument excludes
  bounded-degree polynomial CRT templates with zero constant term
  and sufficiently many conductor primes from violating square-tail
  control. Large varying coefficients and translations are not covered.
- [Complete central-rank power bands](central_rank_bands.md) force Haar
  coverage and delayed natural density one, even with arbitrary
  coherent centers varying between ranks. Thus the complete-band
  rank-coding construction cannot produce a counterexample. Sparse
  selections are not covered by this theorem.
- [Prime-power preparations](prepared_blocks_entropy.md#a-prime-power-preparation-can-be-reduced-to-small-full-footprint)
  admit a period-uniform reduction from incremental to full footprint,
  and a square-root bound for bounded-quotient hits. This excludes the
  proposed endpoint amplification through overlapping binary selectors,
  not arbitrary multi-prime preparations.
- [Coherent maximal matching](coherent_maximal_matching.md) proves
  $H_{E_J}(X)/H_X\ll\tau\log\log(3X)$ for selected coherent
  first-kill cells of Haar mass $\tau$. Hence original activation
  has logarithmic density if
  $\sum_{q>Y}e_q=o(1/\log\log Y)$, in particular if
  $\sum_q e_q\log\log(3q)<\infty$. This is not a natural-density
  conclusion or a theorem for incompatible residues.
- [Harmonic affine completion](harmonic_affine_completion.md) converges
  to Haar in total variation, uniformly even when a preserved period
  varies with the cutoff. It gives an averaged conditioned return
  inequality. The return weights cannot simply be removed: a primitive
  zero-residue system can delete every integer at least $Q$ while
  its averaged return is at most $1/(Q-1)$.
- [Weighted divisibility moments](weighted_divisibility_moments.md)
  exclude arbitrary-overlap refinements of the rank-$2\lambda$
  parents when $H_X\le\exp((1+2\log2-\varepsilon)\lambda)$,
  including every fixed polynomial-in-$z$ epoch. The
  [residual-rank theorem](parent_refinement_overlap.md#arbitrary-overlap-with-bounded-residual-rank)
  excludes them at all cutoffs if $\Omega(q/d)=o(\lambda)$ and the
  incremental footprint is exponentially small. The original
  bounded-overlap theorem also remains valid, with an improved error.
- [Lower-rank parents](overlapping_parent_preparations.md#lower-rank-parents-another-proved-preparation)
  have positive protected Haar and harmonic mass and exponentially small
  conditional survivor fractions. They do not supply an appended
  congruence construction: controlling its incremental union over an
  entire logarithmic epoch is still missing.

For arbitrary residues under original activation, the strongest general
positive criterion retained here is
[bounded exponent-one prime rank](squarefull_kernel_truncation.md),
including all bounded-$\omega(q)$ singleton families. The
[quasipolynomial activation theorem](quasipolynomial_equidistribution.md)
and [protected-set distortion bound](protected_set_distortion.md) have
only their stated stronger-delay and finite-sieve scopes. Earlier
positive cases and checked obstructions are listed below.

**Unresolved approaches; provisional only.** Numerical maximality did
not remove the coherent $\log\log X$ loss; a proposed
[coherent energy comparison](coherent_maximal_matching.md#final-status-of-the-maximality-approach)
is only a conditional sufficient estimate. The
[exact replacement exclusions](coherent_maximal_matching.md#exact-replacement-exclusions)
add quotient moduli beyond the quotient interval's length; no simultaneous
switching estimate was found. No adaptive completion kernel
or uniform arithmetic first-kill entropy bound was proved. The
[spectral route](spectral_sieve.md) still lacks high-conductor trace
control. [Mixed-root coding](overlapping_parent_preparations.md#status-of-mixed-root-and-coding-attempts)
did not control incomparable cross-scale collisions or produce the
required small incremental union. Finally,
[private canonical endpoint nullity](unresolved_estimates.md#final-status-of-the-private-endpoint-attempt)
and its proposed uniform finite bound remain unproved. These gaps are
not resolved by the preparation lemmas, the completion theorem, or
purely profinite private witnesses. No unrestricted counterexample
mechanism was ruled out by the last unsuccessful attempts.

### Established positive results

- Summable and pairwise-coprime systems have natural density. Zero
  residues and [finite rational patterns](structured_residues.md) have
  logarithmic density. [Prime-rank criteria](bounded_prime_rank.md)
  cover bounded $\Omega$, coherent bounded $\omega$, and the stated
  bounded pairwise-gcd rank cases.
- The [one-unbounded-prime-power theorem](one_unbounded_prime_power.md)
  permits arbitrary residues with
  $\Omega(q)-\max_p v_p(q)\le r$ for a fixed $r$, even with multiple
  residues. Combining it with a reciprocal-summable exception proves
  natural density for singleton systems with $\omega(q)\le2$.
  The stronger [squarefull-kernel truncation](squarefull_kernel_truncation.md)
  proves natural density whenever the number of prime factors
  occurring to exponent exactly one is bounded. This includes
  **all bounded-$\omega$ singleton systems without coherence**, even with
  a uniformly bounded number of residues per original modulus.
- [Coherent](coherent_power_cutoff.md) and
  [locally finitely colored](local_colored_power_cutoff.md) systems have
  natural density under every fixed power delay. For original activation,
  the coherent reciprocal-shell condition gives natural density, while
  [the weaker weighted criterion](coherent_logarithmic_criterion.md)
  $\sum_{q\le X,\ q\in P}(\log q)/q=O(\log X)$ gives logarithmic density.
  The new [Haar-tail criterion](coherent_maximal_matching.md#a-haar-tail-criterion-for-original-activation)
  gives another sufficient condition: $\sum_{q>Y}e_q=o(1/\log\log Y)$,
  in particular $\sum_qe_q\log\log(3q)<\infty$.
- [Bounded-quotient first kills](profinite_residue_endpoints.md) have
  nonatomic limiting residue measures, and are logarithmically null when
  normalized residues have countable profinite closure.
  [Brjuno slopes](irrational_slope_endpoints.md), including finite
  irrationality exponent, give natural nullity. Neither statement
  controls unbounded quotients.
- [Curved profiles](curved_residue_profiles.md) give natural endpoint
  nullity for $\lfloor\theta q+q^\alpha\rfloor$, every
  $\theta\in[0,1)$ and $0<\alpha<1$, including bounded integer
  perturbations. [Sublogarithmically many ordered coherent pieces](ordered_coherent_runs.md)
  give logarithmic endpoint nullity; this includes nondecreasing
  canonical residues $a_q=o(\log q)$.
- [Coprime entropy](coprime_entropy.md) is uniformly bounded. The
  [prepared-block bounds](prepared_blocks_entropy.md#removing-coprimality-by-exact-gcd-strata)
  now control a small integer center beyond an arbitrary preparatory
  period, including shared prime factors. They give both entropy control
  and $H_T(X)/H_X\ll\sqrt\tau$, uniformly in that period. The same note proves
  logarithmic density for countably many blocks with disjoint prime
  supports and centers $0\le\alpha_j<\min D_j$, without rank bounds.
  For [pairwise-coprime preparations](prime_additive_excess.md#a-related-improvement-of-the-preparatory-period-restriction),
  appended coprime small-center moduli need exceed only the largest
  old modulus, not the full preparatory period.
- [Bounded prime overlap between small-center blocks](bounded_prime_overlap_blocks.md)
  now suffices as well: if each prime occurs in at most $R$ blocks,
  logarithmic density exists. The finite harmonic bound is
  $H_B(X)/H_X\le R\log(1/(1-\beta))$. In particular, coherent residues
  satisfying $a_q<C P^-(q)$ have logarithmic density. For coherent
  systems, unbounded overlap is also allowed at any prime set with
  convergent reciprocal sum. Unbounded overlap on a divergent prime
  set is not controlled.
- [Primewise branch separation](branch_separated_centers.md) allows
  arbitrarily many integer-center blocks at a prime when their
  first-digit branches have bounded collision multiplicity. Signed
  centers are allowed. It proves logarithmic density under
  $\min(a_q,q-a_q)<C P^-(q)$ without coherence, with a uniform finite
  harmonic bound.
- [Repeated largest prime factors](prime_filtration.md#removing-repeated-largest-prime-factors-from-the-tail)
  occur in a reciprocal-summable set of moduli. For the remaining
  general tail problem one may restrict to $q=pm$, $P^+(m)<p$,
  retaining the omitted family's fixed initial classes in approximations.
- [Coherent annulus and prime-shadow bounds](coherent_annuli_and_shadows.md)
  and [rational-phase conditional estimates](rational_phase_overlap.md)
  are proved; their required simultaneous arithmetic transfers remain open.
- [Weighted prime-residue windows](weighted_prime_residue_windows.md)
  give a one-block harmonic bound $\min(1,C/\mathcal H)$, allowing
  growing local residue supports, and verify the exponent
  $0.086071\ldots$ for prime-coherent polynomial-length intervals.
  No summation over arbitrary windows or conditioned tail follows.
- [Weighted blocking-prime groups](weighted_blocking_groups.md) give a
  uniform coherent trace bound and control the spanning-tree obstruction
  to positive divisor majorants. They do not control arbitrary coherent
  small-footprint families.

### Checked obstructions and limits

- A nonempty singleton first-kill cell satisfies the sharp-order bound
  [$qe_q\ge\exp(-C\sqrt q)$](rank_and_moment_obstructions.md#size-of-a-nonempty-first-kill-cell).
  This pointwise bound does not give the required summed entropy estimate.
- [Globally private canonical families](unresolved_estimates.md#globally-private-canonical-endpoints-can-have-quadratic-small-cells)
  can still have a final cell $e_q=1/(q(q+1))$. Privacy therefore
  does not supply a polylogarithmic pointwise lower bound for $qe_q$;
  this does not refute the proposed summed entropy or endpoint targets.
- [Energy counterexamples](energy_counterexample.md) refute linear
  energy charging, uniform $O(\log\log X)$ errors, and subexponential
  rank-error constants. Their excess is unbounded but sublogarithmic,
  and their normalized entropy tends to zero. They are not infinite
  counterexamples. A new [rank-filtered refinement](energy_counterexample.md#rank-filtered-residue-channels)
  gives excess at least $(\log X)^\gamma$ for every
  $\gamma<0.100469471\ldots$, with energy still tending to
  $2\log2$. Its excess remains $o(\sqrt{\log X})$, so the
  square-root energy target survives.
- [Prime-only amplification](prime_additive_excess.md) gives unbounded
  finite additive harmonic excess at rank one. Thus **all** finite
  rank-only additive constants are impossible, not just subexponential
  ones. This remains a finite obstruction, not logarithmic-density
  failure.
- [Disjoint prepared copies](prepared_blocks_entropy.md#disjoint-copies-cannot-amplify-these-blocks)
  cannot amplify those small-center blocks into a fixed harmonic anomaly
  with vanishing incremental footprint. The new
  [separate-phase obstruction](coherent_annuli_and_shadows.md#separate-phase-maxima-cannot-be-summed)
  also shows that even optimal individual coherent first-kill bounds cannot
  be summed: their normalized separate maxima can diverge while the
  footprint tends to zero. Simultaneous control of one center is essential.
- [Overlapping parent preparations](overlapping_parent_preparations.md)
  retain positive Haar and prescribed long-interval harmonic survivor
  mass, while their selected parent cylinders have exponentially small
  conditional survivor fractions. Product truncation now permits all
  preparatory moduli to be at most $z$, parent moduli below $z^2$,
  and harmonic sampling already on $[z^2,X]$. Thus the disjoint-parent
  obstruction is not universal. Distinct appended congruences with
  sufficiently long protected progression traces remain missing.
  [Weighted moment bounds](weighted_divisibility_moments.md) exclude
  every fixed polynomial-in-$z$ epoch even with arbitrary overlap;
  [small residual rank](parent_refinement_overlap.md#arbitrary-overlap-with-bounded-residual-rank)
  is excluded at all cutoffs as well.
- [Primitive prime blocks](prime_block_obstructions.md) refute both the
  unsieved atom-kernel bound and a tautification route to the weighted
  reciprocal criterion.
- [Overlapping first-kill potentials](overlapping_entropy_obstruction.md)
  are not uniformly integrable, even for zero residues; their
  $L\log L$ norms can diverge. The disjoint first-kill entropy target
  is not refuted by this construction.
- [Positive divisor-cover majorants](divisor_cover_obstruction.md)
  have cost at least one for some primitive families whose Haar
  footprints tend to zero. The spanning-tree construction rules out
  that entire positive-majorant strategy, not the actual harmonic bound.
- [Single-Bohr-set protection](bohr_protected_preparations.md) cannot
  produce a near-cover preparation with a positive proportion of
  survivors on $[N,N^K]$ and moduli at most $N$, for fixed $K>1$.
  The bound is uniform even when the rotation slope depends on $N$;
  arbitrary protected sets remain uncontrolled.
- [Finite-return obstructions](recurrence_obstruction.md) and the new
  [diagonal profinite example](prime_block_obstructions.md#even-diagonal-profinite-fibers-need-not-return-in-haar-measure)
  rule out return arguments using only the stated limiting marginals.
  [Private canonical witnesses](unresolved_estimates.md#private-canonical-witnesses-can-occur-in-a-full-finite-cover)
  can occur in a finite full cover. None gives an infinite private family.
  [Affine rough shadows](unresolved_estimates.md#affine-rough-shadows-can-overlap-with-arbitrarily-large-multiplicity)
  of canonical private witnesses can have arbitrarily large multiplicity,
  ruling out bounded-multiplicity disjointification of that attempt.
- The stronger [rough-shadow energy obstruction](rough_shadow_energy.md)
  gives globally private canonical endpoints with bounded total shadow
  measure but unbounded second moment. A uniform quadratic shadow
  charge therefore fails too. The [fixed flat profile](curved_residue_profiles.md#a-fixed-flat-profile-can-have-large-natural-endpoint-spikes)
  $a_q=\lfloor\log\log q\rfloor$ can have positive upper natural
  endpoint density and a delayed union without natural density, while
  both relevant logarithmic densities still exist.
- [Sharp finite comparisons](finite_sharp_counterexamples.md),
  [face capacities](product_capacity_obstruction.md), and
  [higher moments](rank_and_moment_obstructions.md) fail as stated there.
  [Wang refinements](compression_and_blocks.md) do not transfer its
  multi-residue counterexample. [Spectral](spectral_sieve.md) and
  [prime-stage](prime_filtration.md) identities still lack the required
  arithmetic trace estimate, even after the checked power-delay moment transfer.

### Open targets; provisional directions only

The constant disjoint first-kill entropy bound and a uniform additive
$o(\log X)$ comparison remain unproved and unrefuted.
[Averaging that entropy in harmonic time](rank_and_moment_obstructions.md#averaging-in-harmonic-time-does-not-weaken-the-entropy-target)
is equivalent up to constants to its pointwise bound, not a weaker
available route. A weaker
[square-root energy bound](spectral_sieve.md#a-weaker-energy-target-unresolved)
would also suffice; this continuation neither proved nor refuted it.
The [activated projection identity](spectral_sieve.md#isolating-the-negative-energy-term-in-the-activated-trace)
isolates a negative energy term. The new cumulative innovation budget
also controls the sum of all quotient-sieve conductor energies, but
does not control high-conductor point evaluation and does not itself
use singleton distinctness. Rank-only bounded additive errors are refuted, even
at rank one, and are not proof targets.
For coherent systems, the uniform finite harmonic small-footprint bound
would suffice by the proved period-independent conditioning reduction.
In particular, the proposed absolute-constant bound
$H_B(X)\le C\beta H_X$ remains unproved and unrefuted in that setting;
the checked [coprime bound](coprime_entropy.md#a-simpler-unconditioned-finite-bound)
has $C=e/(e-1)$. The new common-phase transport controls matching
multiples within a bounded factor of a sampled integer; large-divisor-gap
points remain uncontrolled. No size-sensitive fractional allocation for
private endpoints was obtained.

The [maximal-matching bound](coherent_maximal_matching.md) supplies a
simultaneous coherent estimate with a $\log\log X$ loss. The
positive rough-quotient kernel used in its proof has that loss even
for one fixed generator; removing it requires additional arithmetic
information about the numerical maximality. The
[total-variation completion](harmonic_affine_completion.md) instead
leaves a return-probability weight. Neither result is a general
conditioned logarithmic-tail bound.

[Private endpoint nullity](unresolved_estimates.md#private-witness-reduction-for-endpoints)
and its [dyadic equivalent](dyadic_splitting.md) remain open. The new
rational-phase conditional bound needs a quantitative endpoint transfer;
the prime-shadow route needs a simultaneous common-phase estimate, not
separate predecessor-sieve bounds. These are provisional directions, not
solutions of the remaining difficulty. No general conditioned logarithmic
tail estimate has been obtained. By the
[prefix-independence identity](basic_reductions.md#the-conditioned-excess-does-not-depend-on-the-prefix-proved),
the conditioned tail exceeds its Haar mass by the same amount after every
prefix, so such an estimate may lose any factor whose product with the
remaining mass tends to zero along infinitely many prefixes. A bounded factor
would suffice. The universal divisor-count bound $2^k$ for a squarefree
prefix cannot be absorbed in this way, even for some summable prime families
with positive limiting survivor density. This does not mean those prime
families have actual quotient-modulus multiplicity $2^k$.

The latest continuation tried arithmetic private-endpoint recurrence,
affine amplification, and the missing intermediate-prime incidence
estimate. It obtained no positive-density private family or general
tail bound. The substantive new results are the bounded-height
square-tail theorem and the explicit full-survivor transfer obstruction
above; neither resolves Problem 25.
