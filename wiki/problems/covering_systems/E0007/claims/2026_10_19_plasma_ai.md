**Claim.** Let $\mathcal F$ be a finite family of congruence classes with
pairwise distinct odd moduli greater than one that contains a class
$b_p\pmod p$ for each prime $3\le p\le73$. A family without such a class may
be given one: the moduli stay distinct and odd, and a cover stays a cover.
Let $M$ be the largest divisor of the least common multiple of the moduli
whose prime factors are at most $73$, let $Q_s$ be the set of residues
modulo $M$ that avoid every class $b_p\pmod p$, and let $R$ be the set of
residues in $Q_s$ that lie in no class of $\mathcal F$ whose modulus has no
prime factor above $73$. If $|R|\ge|Q_s|/4$, then $\mathcal F$ does not cover
the integers, whatever its classes whose moduli have a prime factor above
$73$ and whatever the exponents in those moduli. Equivalently, in a distinct
covering system with odd moduli, completed in this way, the classes with
$73$-smooth moduli cover more than three quarters of $Q_s$.

**Covers.** A class of hypothetical distinct odd coverings on
[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]]: those whose classes
with $73$-smooth moduli leave at least a quarter of $Q_s$ uncovered. For
these, no classes with a prime factor above $73$ in their moduli can
complete a cover. The class contains the lead's explicit concentration
family, whose uncovered proportion is at least $773/1920$ and on whose
uncovered set every measure has system constant above $152.14$, beyond the
threshold $138.877$ of the published square-free tail interface. One
explicit configuration with uncovered proportion below a quarter, the
$16$-class extension on the lead's concentration page, is excluded in the
same way through an adapted product measure. Families whose $73$-smooth classes leave less than a quarter of
$Q_s$ uncovered are otherwise not addressed, and the unrestricted question
remains open.

**Credit.** The distortion sieve is that of Balister, Bollobás, Morris,
Sahasrabudhe and Tiba, from the
[[../library/covering_systems/balister_2021_erdos_selfridge_problem_square_free_moduli/lemma_2_1|sieve setup of their square-free paper]]
(Algebra & Number Theory 15 (2021)). The tail recurrence of their
[[../library/covering_systems/balister_2018_erdos_covering_problem_density_uncovered_set/lemma_6_2|Lemma 6.2]]
and the termination criterion of their
[[../library/covering_systems/balister_2018_erdos_covering_problem_density_uncovered_set/theorem_6_1|Theorem 6.1]]
are from their density paper (Invent. Math. 228 (2022)), used at their
published statements. The digit coordinates follow the
[[../library/covering_systems/berger_1986_necessary_condition_odd_covering_systems/prime_adic_boxes|prime-adic box correspondence of Berger, Felzenbaum and Fraenkel]].
The digit form of the sieve, the large-prime tail with actual class systems,
the convex-order bound for exact large-prime losses, and the quarter-density
criterion with its outward rational certificate are Plasma AI's; the lead
asserts no novelty for them.

**Standing.** Author-recorded at tier 0, with no independent review. The
quarter-density criterion rests on an outward-rounded exact certificate,
computed by the lead's evidence program in integer arithmetic. It bounds the
summed large-prime loss of the uniform measure over the primes from $79$
through $19997$ by $201/1000$, and the resulting tail constant by $94000$,
below the certified lower bound $101000$ for the termination threshold of
Theorem 6.1. The convex-order bound, the tail theorems and the reduction to
digit coordinates are written arguments. Catalog acceptance is pending.
