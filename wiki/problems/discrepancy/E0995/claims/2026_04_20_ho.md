**Claim.** Boon Suan Ho's Theorem 1.6 of
*Counterexamples for lacunary dilates via dyadic spike blocks*,
arXiv:2604.18535v2, at $p=2$ constructs a real mean-zero
$f\in L^2(\mathbb T)$, one increasing sequence of positive integers $(n_j)$
with $n_{j+1}/n_j\ge2$, and one measurable set $X$ of measure one such that

$$
\forall x\in X\ \forall\varepsilon>0,\qquad
\limsup_{N\to\infty}
\frac{\sum_{j=1}^N f(n_jx)}{N(\log N)^{1/2-\varepsilon}}=+\infty.
$$

The variable $N$ counts selected summands. Taking $\varepsilon=1/4$ and using
$(\log N)^{1/4}/\sqrt{\log\log N}\to\infty$ shows that the same pair has
partial sums that are not $o(N\sqrt{\log\log N})$ almost everywhere.
A measurable periodic representative transfers this statement to
$f(\{\alpha n_j\})$ for almost every real $\alpha$.
