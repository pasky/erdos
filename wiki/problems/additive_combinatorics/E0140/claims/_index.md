# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/claims/2023_02_10_kelley_meka|2023_02_10_kelley_meka]]: Theorem 1.1 of Kelley and Meka (FOCS 2023) bounds the size of a
progression-free subset of the first $N$ integers by $N/(\log N)^C$ for every
fixed $C$. It is accepted on the site's credit and on Bloom and Sisask's
refereed exposition.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: OpenAI's Theorem 1.1 of September 2026 bounds $r_k(N)$ by
$C_kN\exp(-c_k(\log N)^{\varepsilon_k})$ for $k\ge3$, which at $k=3$ is below
$N$ over any power of $\log N$. Its Lean formalization proves a weaker bound
that a library lemma turns into the problem's statement.
