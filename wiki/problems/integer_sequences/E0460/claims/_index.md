# problems/integer_sequences/E0460/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0460/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0460/claims/2026_01_13_chojecki|2026_01_13_chojecki]]: Two ulam.ai notes of 13 and 14 January 2026 by Przemyslaw Chojecki claimed
that the truncated greedy reciprocal sum diverges in both formulations. After
the curator noted the gap, the author withdrew the claim as a reduction.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0460/claims/2026_01_14_chojecki|2026_01_14_chojecki]]: Przemyslaw Chojecki's note claims that in both formulations the truncated
greedy sum is at least the rough sum $f(n)$, whose mean over $n$ up to $N$ is
at least a constant times $\log\log N$. It concludes that the truncated sum is
unbounded and leaves the limit open.
