# problems/set_theory/E0594/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/claims/1966_03_01_erdos_hajnal|1966_03_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Erdős and Hajnal (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 17, 1966) state, without
proof, that a graph of chromatic number above $\omega_2$ contains all
sufficiently long odd circuits. This is a special case of the question of
Problem 594.

[[problems/set_theory/E0594/claims/1974_01_01_erdos_hajnal_shelah|1974_01_01_erdos_hajnal_shelah]]: Erdős, Hajnal and Shelah prove, as Theorem 3 of their 1974 paper, that every
graph of chromatic number greater than $\aleph_0$ contains odd cycles of every
sufficiently large length. This is the statement of Problem 594.
