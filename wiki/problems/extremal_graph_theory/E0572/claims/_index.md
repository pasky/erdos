# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/claims/1966_01_01_benson|1966_01_01_benson]]: Benson's regular bipartite graphs of girth eight and twelve (Canad. J. Math.
1966) give $\mathrm{ex}(n;C_6)\gg n^{4/3}$ and
$\mathrm{ex}(n;C_{10})\gg n^{6/5}$. These are the instances $k=3$ and $k=5$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/claims/1995_01_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar|1995_01_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar]]: The $q$-regular bipartite graphs $D(3,q)$ of Lazebnik, Ustimenko and Woldar,
of girth at least eight (Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 1995), give
$\mathrm{ex}(n;C_6)\gg n^{4/3}$, the instance $k=3$. For $k\ge4$ their general
bound is of smaller order.
