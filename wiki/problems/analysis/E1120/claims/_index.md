# problems/analysis/E1120/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1120/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1120/claims/2026_06_17_pendyala|2026_06_17_pendyala]]: An arXiv preprint of Pendyala claims $c\sqrt{\log n}\le S(n)\le\pi n$ for the
worst-case escape length $S(n)$, so that $S(n)$ is unbounded. It is unrefereed
and unreviewed.

[[problems/analysis/E1120/claims/2026_06_17_pendyala_radial|2026_06_17_pendyala_radial]]: An SSRN preprint of Pendyala claims that every admissible polynomial of degree
at most three has a radius in the origin's component, so $S(n)=1$ for $n\le3$.
It also claims that a degree-six polynomial blocks every radius, so $S(6)>1$.
