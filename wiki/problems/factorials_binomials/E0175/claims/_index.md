# problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1985_02_01_sarkozy|1985_02_01_sarkozy]]: Sárközy proves in the Journal of Number Theory (1985) that $\binom{2n}{n}$ is
not squarefree for every sufficiently large $n$. The proof uses exponential
sums and gives no explicit threshold.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1995_01_01_velammal|1995_01_01_velammal]]: Velammal proves in the Hardy-Ramanujan Journal (1995) that $\binom{2n}{n}$ is
never squarefree for $n>4$, using Vaughan's identity and exponent pairs. The
proof is independent of Granville and Ramaré's.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0175/claims/1996_06_01_granville_ramare|1996_06_01_granville_ramare]]: Granville and Ramaré prove in Mathematika (1996) that $\binom{2n}{n}$ is never
squarefree for $n>4$, using explicit bounds on exponential sums. The argument
has a third-party Lean formalization.
