# problems/number_theory/E0981/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0981/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0981/claims/1969_01_25_elliott|1969_01_25_elliott]]: Elliott proves Erdős's display (80) of 1965 for the two-sided eventual-time
threshold of the Legendre-symbol partial sums, for every $\epsilon\in(0,1]$.
The paper, in Indag. Math. 1969, states that simple changes give the one-sided
form.

[[problems/number_theory/E0981/claims/2025_12_27_tang_zhang|2025_12_27_tang_zhang]]: Answers a variant (the site's earlier first-passage threshold), not the
Statement (the eventual-time threshold), so it does not count toward the
problem's standing. Quanyu Tang and Hao Zhang's preprint proves
$\sum_{p\le x}f_\epsilon(p)\sim c_\epsilon x/\log x$ for
$f_\epsilon(p)=\min\{\ell:S_\ell(p)<\epsilon\ell\}$.
