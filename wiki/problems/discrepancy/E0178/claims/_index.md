# problems/discrepancy/E0178/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0178/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0178/claims/1981_09_01_beck|1981_09_01_beck]]: In 1981 Beck proved that a single $\pm1$ function keeps the initial partial
sums along each of the first $d$ of infinitely many integer sequences bounded
in terms of $d$. This answers Problem 178 yes, and the paper is refereed, in
Combinatorica.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0178/claims/2026_09_19_korsky|2026_09_19_korsky]]: Samuel Korsky's 2026 manuscript claims that the constant in Problem 178 can be
taken as a fixed power of $d$ with exponent $3/2+\sqrt2$, improving Beck's
exponent $4+\epsilon$. It is an erdosproblems.com proof claim with no review
known.
