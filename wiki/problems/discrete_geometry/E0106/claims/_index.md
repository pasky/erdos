# problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/claims/2026_07_29_silverstein|2026_07_29_silverstein]]: Conner Silverstein's explicit packing of seventeen squares in the unit square
has side lengths summing to more than $4$, so $f(17)>4$ and $f(k^2+1)=k$ fails
at $k=4$. It was found with Claude Opus 5 and checked in exact rational
arithmetic.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/claims/2026_07_29_singh|2026_07_29_singh]]: A different seventeen-square packing has side lengths summing to more than
$4$, so $f(17)>4$ and $f(k^2+1)=k$ fails at $k=4$. Its Lean proof in Alexeev's
repository, which names Raj Singh as informal author, was built and checked
here.
