# problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/claims/1987_12_01_slade|1987_12_01_slade]]: Slade's refereed 1987 theorem shows that in every sufficiently large dimension
the uniform $n$-step self-avoiding walk has mean-square displacement
asymptotic to $Dn$. So $d_k(n)\ll n^{1/2}$ for all large $k$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/claims/1991_10_01_hara_slade|1991_10_01_hara_slade]]: Hara and Slade's theorem gives, for every $k\ge5$, mean-square displacement
$Dn(1+O(n^{-\varepsilon}))$ for the uniform $n$-step self-avoiding walk on
$\mathbb{Z}^k$, so $d_k(n)\ll n^{1/2}$. It was announced in 1991 and proved in
two 1992 papers.
