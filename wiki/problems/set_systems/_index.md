# Set Systems, Designs and Hypergraphs

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0020/_index|E0020/]]: Asks whether the number of
$n$-element sets needed to force a $k$-sunflower is at most exponential in $n$,
that is, below $c_k^n$ for a constant $c_k$ depending on $k$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0021/_index|E0021/]]: Asks whether the smallest
intersecting family of $n$-element sets in which every set of size at most $n-1$
misses some member has size linear in $n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0083/_index|E0083/]]: Bounds the size of a family of
$2n$-element subsets of a $4n$-element set in which every two members share at
least two elements.

[[problems/set_systems/E0207/_index|E0207/]]: Asks whether, for every $g\ge2$,
large Steiner triple systems exist in which any $j$ edges span at least $j+3$
vertices for $2\le j\le g$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0231/_index|E0231/]]: Asks whether every string of
length $2^k$ over $k$ letters (erdosproblems.com prints $2^k-1$, Erdős's
misprint) contains two adjacent blocks that are permutations of each other.
Keränen's word on four letters disproves it.

[[problems/set_systems/E0447/_index|E0447/]]: Asks how large a family of subsets
of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ can be when no member is the union of two other distinct
members.

[[problems/set_systems/E0497/_index|E0497/]]: Asks for the number of antichains
of subsets of an $n$-element set, that is, families in which no member contains
another.

[[problems/set_systems/E0499/_index|E0499/]]: Asks whether every $n\times n$
doubly stochastic matrix has a permutation along which the product of the
entries is at least $n^{-n}$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0624/_index|E0624/]]: Asks to prove that $H(n)-\log_2
n\to\infty$, where $H(n)$ is the least size such that some map from the subsets
of an $n$-element set $X$ to $X$ sends the subsets of every set that large onto
$X$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0643/_index|E0643/]]: Asks to estimate how many edges
force a $t$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices to have four edges $A,B,C,D$ with
$A\cup B=C\cup D$ and $A\cap B=C\cap D=\emptyset$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0644/_index|E0644/]]: Bounds the size of a set needed to
meet every member of a family of $k$-element sets in which any $r$ members are
all met by one pair. The question is whether this size is asymptotically a
constant times $k$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0664/_index|E0664/]]: Concerns families of subsets of
$\{1,\ldots,n\}$, each of size above $c\sqrt n$ for a constant $c$, in which any
two members share at most one element. It asks whether some set meets every
member in a nonempty, bounded number of points.

[[problems/set_systems/E0665/_index|E0665/]]: Asks whether, for some constant
$C$ and all large $n$, there is a pairwise balanced design on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$
(a family of sets in which every pair of distinct elements lies in exactly one
set) whose sets all have more than $n^{1/2}-C$ elements.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/_index|E0701/]]: Asks whether a family of sets
closed under taking subsets always has an element contained in as many members
as its largest intersecting subfamily has sets.

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/_index|E0702/]]: Asks whether, for $k\ge4$ and $n$
large in terms of $k$, more $k$-subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ than those through a
fixed pair force two that meet in one point. Frankl proved it, but the
erdosproblems.com wording, with no range on $n$, fails.

[[problems/set_systems/E0703/_index|E0703/]]: Asks for the largest family of
subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ in which no two members meet in exactly $r$
elements, and whether for each $\epsilon>0$ it is below $(2-\delta)^n$ for some
$\delta>0$ when $\epsilon n<r<(1/2-\epsilon)n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0716/_index|E0716/]]: Asks whether the largest 3-uniform
hypergraph on $n$ vertices with no three edges spanning six vertices has
$o(n^2)$ edges, that is, fewer than any fixed fraction of $n^2$ for large $n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0719/_index|E0719/]]: Asks whether every $r$-uniform
hypergraph on $n$ vertices is a union of at most $\mathrm{ex}_r(n;K_{r+1}^r)$
edges and $(r+1)$-cliques, no two sharing an edge. It is the Erdős–Sauer
conjecture, known for $r=2$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/_index|E0722/]]: Asks whether a Steiner system on
$n$ points with blocks of size $k$, covering every $r$-set exactly once, exists
for all large $n$ that satisfy the divisibility conditions.

[[problems/set_systems/E0723/_index|E0723/]]: Asks whether the order of every
finite projective plane must be a prime power.

[[problems/set_systems/E0724/_index|E0724/]]: Asks whether the largest number of
mutually orthogonal Latin squares of order $n$ is $\gg n^{1/2}$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0725/_index|E0725/]]: Asks for an asymptotic formula for
the number of $k\times n$ Latin rectangles.

[[problems/set_systems/E0732/_index|E0732/]]: Asks whether, for some $c>0$, at
least $\exp(cn^{1/2}\log n)$ non-decreasing sequences of block sizes arise from
designs on $n$ points in which every pair of points lies in exactly one block.

[[problems/set_systems/E0734/_index|E0734/]]: Asks, for all large $n$, for a
non-trivial pairwise balanced design on $n$ points in which each block size is
used only $O(n^{1/2})$ times.

[[problems/set_systems/E0747/_index|E0747/]]: Asks how many edges a random
$3$-uniform hypergraph on $3n$ vertices needs so that it almost surely has $n$
vertex-disjoint edges.

[[problems/set_systems/E0775/_index|E0775/]]: Asks whether a $3$-uniform
hypergraph on $n$ vertices can have at least $n-O(1)$ different sizes of maximal
complete subgraphs.

[[problems/set_systems/E0776/_index|E0776/]]: Asks how large $n$ must be, as a
function of $r$, for some antichain of subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ that uses
each occurring size at least $r$ times to have $n-3$ distinct sizes; three
claims are pending, none accepted.

[[problems/set_systems/E0834/_index|E0834/]]: Asks whether there is a
$3$-critical $3$-uniform hypergraph in which every vertex has degree at least
$7$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0835/_index|E0835/]]: Asks whether, for some $k>2$, the
$k$-element subsets of $\{1,\ldots,2k\}$ can be colored with $k+1$ colors so
that the $k$-subsets of every $(k+1)$-set show all $k+1$ colors.

[[problems/set_systems/E0837/_index|E0837/]]: Asks for the set $A_3$ of $\alpha$
such that $3$-uniform hypergraphs of limiting density above $\alpha$ must
contain growing subgraphs of density above some fixed $\beta>\alpha$, while
limiting density at least $\alpha$ need not.

[[problems/set_systems/E0857/_index|E0857/]]: Asks for an estimate of the least
$m$ such that any $m$ subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ contain a sunflower of size
$k$, meaning $k$ of the sets with equal pairwise intersections.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/_index|E0901/]]: Asks for an estimate of the least
number of edges in an $n$-uniform hypergraph that cannot be colored with two
colors but can with three.

[[problems/set_systems/E0903/_index|E0903/]]: Asks whether a block design on
$n=p^2+p+1$ points, with $p$ a prime power and every pair of points in exactly
one block, must have at least $n+p$ blocks if it has more than $n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|E1020/]]: Asks whether, for $r\ge3$ and
$n\ge rk$, the most edges in an $r$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices with no
$k$ pairwise disjoint edges is the larger of the clique count and the star
count.

[[problems/set_systems/E1022/_index|E1022/]]: Asks whether a family of sets of
size at least $t$, with few members inside any given set relative to its size,
can always be two-colored with no monochromatic member.

[[problems/set_systems/E1023/_index|E1023/]]: Asks whether the largest family of
subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ in which no member is a union of others has size
$\sim c\,2^n/n^{1/2}$ for some constant $c>0$.

[[problems/set_systems/E1024/_index|E1024/]]: Asks to estimate how large an
independent set every $3$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices must have when any
two edges share at most one vertex.

[[problems/set_systems/E1025/_index|E1025/]]: Asks to estimate how large an
independent set must exist for every map $f$ sending each pair $x,y$ from
$\{1,\ldots,n\}$ to a third element, where $X$ is independent if $f(x,y)\notin
X$ for all $x,y\in X$.

[[problems/set_systems/E1026/_index|E1026/]]: Asks how large, relative to
$n^{-1/2}\sum x_i$, the largest sum of a monotonic subsequence of a sequence
$x_1,\ldots,x_n$ of distinct real numbers must be.

[[problems/set_systems/E1027/_index|E1027/]]: Asks whether, for a family
$\mathcal{F}$ of at most $c2^n$ sets of size $n$ with union $X$ and $n$ large,
there are $\gg_c 2^{\lvert X\rvert}$ sets $B\subset X$ that meet every member of
$\mathcal{F}$ but contain none.

[[problems/set_systems/E1075/_index|E1075/]]: Asks whether each $r\ge3$ has some
$c_r>r^{-r}$ such that, for every $\epsilon>0$ and large $n$, every $r$-uniform
hypergraph on $n$ vertices with at least $(1+\epsilon)(n/r)^r$ edges has
subgraphs on $m\to\infty$ vertices with at least $c_rm^r$ edges.

[[problems/set_systems/E1076/_index|E1076/]]: Asks whether, for $k\ge5$, the
most edges of a $3$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices with no $j$ vertices
spanning $j-2$ edges for any $4\le j\le k$ is asymptotic to $n^2/6$. The version
as printed, with only the single family $j=k$, is false.

[[problems/set_systems/E1159/_index|E1159/]]: Asks whether there is a constant
$C>1$ such that every finite projective plane has a set of points meeting each
line in at least $1$ and at most $C$ points.

[[problems/set_systems/E1178/_index|E1178/]]: Asks to prove that
$d_r(e)=(r-2)e+3$ for all $r,e\ge3$, where $d_r(e)$ is the least $d$ such that
forbidding all $r$-uniform hypergraphs with $d$ vertices and $e$ edges forces
$o(n^2)$ edges.
