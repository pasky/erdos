# problems/polynomials/E0230/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/1980_09_01_kahane|1980_09_01_kahane]]: Kahane's 1980 theorem gives, for every $n$, a polynomial of degree $n$ with
complex coefficients of modulus one whose modulus is $(1+o(1))\sqrt n$
uniformly on the unit circle, so the answer is no.

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/2009_06_30_bombieri_bourgain|2009_06_30_bombieri_bourgain]]: Theorem 4 of Bombieri and Bourgain gives unimodular polynomials whose modulus
on the unit circle is $\sqrt n$ up to an error of order
$n^{7/18+\varepsilon}$. Theorem 7 gives the same by an effective construction.

[[problems/polynomials/E0230/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 23 September 2026 states that
for every $\eta>0$ and every large $N$, some signs $\pm1$ give a polynomial of
maximum modulus at most $(1+\eta)\sqrt N$.
