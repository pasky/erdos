# problems/divisors/E1099/claims

[[problems/divisors/E1099/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E1099/claims/1984_10_01_vose|1984_10_01_vose]]: Vose constructs an infinite sequence of integers $N$ along which
$h_\alpha(N)=\sum_i(d_{i+1}/d_i-1)^\alpha$, summed over the consecutive
divisors of $N$, stays bounded for every $\alpha>1$. So the limit inferior of
$h_\alpha(n)$ is bounded.

[[problems/divisors/E1099/claims/1987_01_01_tenenbaum|1987_01_01_tenenbaum]]: Tenenbaum's Théorème 1 bounds $h_\alpha(N)=\sum_i(d_{i+1}/d_i-1)^\alpha$,
summed over the consecutive divisors of $N$, uniformly along the factorials,
the least common multiples and the primorials. This settles Erdős's question
again.
