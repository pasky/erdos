# problems/additive_bases/E0863/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0863/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0863/claims/2026_04_22_ho|2026_04_22_ho]]: Boon Suan Ho proves that for every $r\ge2$, a subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with
at most $r$ representations of each nonzero difference has at most
$(\sqrt r+o(1))\sqrt N$ elements. This is fewer than the
Cilleruelo-Ruzsa-Trujillo sum construction, so the constants differ.
