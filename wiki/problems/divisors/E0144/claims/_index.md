# problems/divisors/E0144/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0144/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0144/claims/1964_01_01_erdos|1964_01_01_erdos]]: In 1964 Erdős announced a proof that almost all integers $n$ have two divisors
with ratio below $1$ plus a negative power of $\log n$, which contains the
problem. The claim was withdrawn in his 1979 paper with Hall.

[[problems/divisors/E0144/claims/1984_02_01_maier_tenenbaum|1984_02_01_maier_tenenbaum]]: Maier and Tenenbaum prove that for almost all $n$ the closest pair of divisors
of $n$ has logarithmic ratio below a negative power of $\log n$. So the
integers with divisors $d_1<d_2<2d_1$ have density one.
