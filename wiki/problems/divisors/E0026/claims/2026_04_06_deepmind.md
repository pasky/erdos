**Claim.** The answer to [[problems/divisors/E0026/_index|Problem 26]] is no,
by a set with divergent reciprocal sum. The claimant is Google DeepMind
(AlphaProof Nexus). The proof is a Lean 4 development by the DeepMind prover
agent in a fork of formal-conjectures, posted to the erdosproblems.com thread by
GTsoukalas on 2026-04-06 with an informal write-up and merged into
formal-conjectures the same day as the solution of
`erdos_26.variants.tenenbaum`, whose docstring there says that the DeepMind
prover agent found the formal disproof. The refereed paper of Tsoukalas et al.
(Science, 2026) identifies that agent as AlphaProof Nexus, proves the result in
its Appendix B.4, recorded on the library page
[[../library/additive_combinatorics/tsoukalas_2026_advancing_mathematics_research_ai_driven_formal_proof_search/erdos_26_variant|Erdős #26, More General Variant]],
and calls the thread its original communication of it; `authors` lists the
paper's twenty-one authors in their published order.

**The construction.** Fix an increasing sequence of primes $q_0<q_1<\cdots$
with $q_0\geq 29$. The set is built in blocks. Block $m$ consists of the
numbers $R_m+jP_m$ for $1\leq j\leq L_m$, where $J_m$ is the number of terms
before the block, $P_m$ is divisible by $q_k$ for every $k\leq 10J_m$, $R_m$
is chosen by the Chinese remainder theorem so that $q_k$ divides $R_m+k$ for
the same $k$, and $L_m$ is chosen so that the block's reciprocal sum lies
between $1/10$ and $2/10$. The reciprocal sum of the whole set therefore
diverges, and for a shift $k$ every term $a$ of a block with $10J_m\geq k$
has $q_k\mid a+k$, so from some block on every shifted term is a multiple of
the prime $q_k$.

**What the file proves.** `elementary_thick_sequence_exists` gives a strictly
increasing $A:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ with divergent reciprocal sum such
that for every $k$ the set of multiples of $A+k$ has lower density at most
$1/2$; `counterexample_exists` derives from it that no shift $A+k$ is weakly
Behrend with $\varepsilon=1/4$; and `erdos_26.variants.tenenbaum` concludes
that Tenenbaum's variant, that for every $\varepsilon>0$ some shift makes the
multiples of $A+k$ reach lower density at least $1-\varepsilon$, is false.
The thread's write-up and the erdosproblems.com commentary state the stronger
bound that the multiples of every $A+k$ have upper density below $0.34$, which
the file does not state. One further step, which the file does not state either,
settles the problem: a set of multiples of density one has lower density one,
so no $A+k$ is Behrend, and the answer to the question is no for this thick
set. The same set refutes the formal-conjectures statement `erdos_26`, which
restricts the question to sequences with divergent reciprocal sum and which
the formalization of Ruzsa's construction on
[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1995_01_01_ruzsa|Ruzsa's claim page]] does
not prove.

**What the paper proves.** The paper's Appendix B.4 (pp. 38--40 of arXiv v2)
builds the same blocks and states the theorem as upper density below
$3/4=1-\epsilon$, with $\epsilon=1/4$, for every $k\ge1$; its union bound gives
about $0.334$. That bound settles the problem directly, since multiples of
upper density below $3/4$ do not have density one. The Lean file that the paper
links, in DeepMind's results repository at its commit of 2026-05-19, is not the
fork's: it builds its own sequence `seqA` and shows, for every $k$, that the
multiples of `seqA + k` have lower density below $1/2$, so that no shift is
weakly Behrend with $\varepsilon=1/2$. Prose and Lean thus state different
bounds, and each refutes Tenenbaum's variant.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper's publication in Science 394 (2026),
234--239, on 8 October 2026, is the `refereed` evidence; the Science text and
its supplement were not read here, and the account follows arXiv v2. This
corpus has not built or audited the Lean files, so no `formalized` evidence is
listed. The erdosproblems.com curator credits DeepMind for the negative answer
to Tenenbaum's variant only. The erdosproblems.com label rested on the earlier
claims of
[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1951_01_01_davenport_erdos|Davenport and Erdős]]
and Ruzsa, so nothing is `reviewed`.
