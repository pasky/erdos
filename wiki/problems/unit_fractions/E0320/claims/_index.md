# problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims/1975_01_01_bleicher_erdos|1975_01_01_bleicher_erdos]]: Bleicher and Erdős's Corollary 3:
$\log S(N)\ge\frac{N\log2}{\log N}\prod_{j=3}^{k}\log_jN$ when $\log_kN\ge k$.
This falls below the order of magnitude by an unbounded factor.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims/1976_12_01_bleicher_erdos|1976_12_01_bleicher_erdos]]: Bleicher and Erdős's Theorem 3:
$\log S(N)\le\frac{N\log_rN}{\log N}\prod_{j=3}^{r}\log_jN$ when
$\log_{2r}N\ge1$. This exceeds the order of magnitude by an unbounded factor.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims/2025_09_12_bettin_grenie_molteni_sanna|2025_09_12_bettin_grenie_molteni_sanna]]: Bettin, Grenié, Molteni and Sanna's Theorem 1:
$\log S(N)\ge2\log2\,(1-\frac{3/2}{\log_kN})\frac{N}{\log N}\prod_{j=3}^{k}\log_jN$
for every $k\ge4$ with $\log_kN\ge3/2$. This is the lower half of the order of
magnitude.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims/2026_07_15_young_zhu_luo|2026_07_15_young_zhu_luo]]: Young, Zhu and Luo's AI-assisted proof that $\log S(N)$ has the order of
$\frac{N}{\log N}\prod_{j=3}^{k}\log_jN$, with $\log_kN$ the last iterated
logarithm exceeding $1$, matching the refereed lower bound. The site accepts
it.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/claims/2026_07_22_kominers_neu|2026_07_22_kominers_neu]]: Kominers and Neu's AI-assisted claim of an asymptotic formula for $\log S(N)$
with a non-constant periodic phase factor and a second-order term. It comes
with a Lean 4 development resting on isolated assumptions and is not accepted.
