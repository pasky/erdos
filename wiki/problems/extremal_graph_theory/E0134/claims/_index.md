# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/claims/1998_01_01_erdos_gyarfas_ruszinko|1998_01_01_erdos_gyarfas_ruszinko]]: Erdős, Gyárfás and Ruszinkó show that a triangle-free graph of maximum degree
$o(n^{1/4}/\log n)$ reaches diameter two with $o(n^2)$ added edges. This
answers the problem yes for every $\epsilon>1/4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/claims/2024_07_01_alon|2024_07_01_alon]]: Alon's Theorem 3.2 shows that a triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices of maximum
degree at most $c(n)\sqrt n$ reaches diameter two after adding at most
$2.5c(n)n^2$ edges. With $c(n)$ a negative power of $n$, the problem's answer
is yes.
