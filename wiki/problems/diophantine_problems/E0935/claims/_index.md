# problems/diophantine_problems/E0935/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0935/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0935/claims/2026_01_29_feng|2026_01_29_feng]]: Feng and coauthors report Aletheia's construction: Pell solutions give $n$
with $n$ and $n+1$ powerful and $n+2$ divisible by $p^2$ for each prime
$p\equiv 5\pmod 8$. They claim that the powerful part of
$n(n+1)\cdots(n+\ell)$ divided by $n^2$ is unbounded for $\ell\geq 2$.
