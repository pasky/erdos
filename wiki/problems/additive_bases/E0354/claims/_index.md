# problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/1989_03_01_hegyvari|1989_03_01_hegyvari]]: In a refereed 1989 paper, Hegyvári proves that the doubling floor sequences
$\lfloor2^n\alpha\rfloor$ and $\lfloor2^n\beta\rfloor$ together form a
complete sequence when one coefficient is a dyadic rational and the other is
not. This answers the first question yes in that case.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_05_kitamura|2026_09_05_kitamura]]: Kitamura's unreviewed Lean proof of September 2026 shows that at the base
$\sqrt\varphi$, the square root of the golden ratio, one floor sequence is
already complete, proving the catalog's existential form of the second
question of Problem 354. The claim is disputed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_11_jenw1n|2026_09_11_jenw1n]]: A Lean 4 proof credited to the solver handle JenW1N shows that the doubling
floors of two reals with irrational ratio form a complete sequence, answering
the first question. The bounty site Conjectures.io certified the proof and
paid the bounty in September 2026.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_13_yu_chen|2026_09_13_yu_chen]]: Yu and Chen's unrefereed manuscript of September 2026, with its own Lean
formalization, claims that the nonzero values of $\lfloor2^n\alpha\rfloor$ and
$\lfloor2^n\beta\rfloor$ for two reals with irrational ratio stay complete
after any finite deletion. It has no outside review; it was reviewed
internally and graded sound, and its acceptance is pending.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/claims/2026_09_20_geneson|2026_09_20_geneson]]: Geneson's unrefereed preprint of September 2026 gives a Salem base between
$6/5$ and $13/10$ and two coefficients with irrational ratio whose interleaved
floors are all even, so the sequence is not complete. This answers the
every-base reading of the second question no. It was reviewed internally and
graded sound, and its acceptance is pending.
