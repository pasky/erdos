***

**Claim.** Every sufficiently large integer is the sum of at most three
2-powerful (squarefull) numbers, integers $n$ such that $p\mid n$ implies
$p^2\mid n$. This is the case $r=2$ of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1107/_index|Problem 1107]]. The result is D.
R. Heath-Brown, *Ternary quadratic forms and sums of three square-full
numbers*, Séminaire de Théorie des Nombres, Paris 1986–87, Progress in
Mathematics 75, Birkhäuser Boston (1988), 137–163. Only its publication year is
known, so the page is dated to the start of 1988.

**Covers.** The case $r=2$ only: every large integer is a sum of at most three
2-powerful numbers. Nothing is proved for $r\geq3$.

**Acceptance.** The statement of Problem 941 is exactly this case. The
erdosproblems.com curator, Thomas Bloom, marked Problem 941 proved and credits
this paper, as the
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0941/claims/1988_01_01_heath_brown|Problem 941 claim page]]
records. That credit is the `reviewed` evidence. Bloom's catalog labeled Problem
1107 OPEN, and its remark on Problem 1107 is not counted on its own. The volume
is an edited seminar proceedings rather than a journal, so no `refereed`
evidence is listed. The formal-conjectures statement file tags the case as a
solved variant, but a statement file is not a formalization.

**Depends on.** No other wiki page; the claim rests on the cited paper.
