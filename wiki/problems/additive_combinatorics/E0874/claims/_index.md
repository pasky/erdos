# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0874/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0874/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0874/claims/1995_02_01_deshouillers_freiman|1995_02_01_deshouillers_freiman]]: Theorem 1 of Deshouillers and Freiman's 1995 Israel Journal paper: an
admissible subset of the first $N$ integers has at most $2N^{1/2}+CN^{5/12}$
elements, so, with Straus's block, $k(N)\sim2N^{1/2}$. The paper is refereed
but is not the erdosproblems.com key reference.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0874/claims/1999_01_01_deshouillers_freiman|1999_01_01_deshouillers_freiman]]: Deshouillers and Freiman's theorem that for all large $N$ an admissible subset
of the first $N$ integers has at most $2\sqrt{N+1/4}-1$ elements, which
Straus's top block attains, so $k(N)\sim2\sqrt N$. The paper is refereed
(Astérisque) and erdosproblems.com credits it.
