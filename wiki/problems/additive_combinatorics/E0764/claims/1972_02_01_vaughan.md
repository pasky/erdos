***

**Claim.** The answer to
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0764/_index|Problem 764]] is no: for no
$A\subseteq\mathbb N$ and no constant $c>0$ is
$\sum_{n\le N}1_A*1_A*1_A(n)=cN+O(1)$. The claimed result is the theorem
of R. C. Vaughan, *On the addition of sequences of integers*, in the form
the erdosproblems.com commentary records: for $c>0$ the three-fold
representation count through $N$ cannot equal $cN$ with an error
$o(N^{1/4}(\log N)^{-1/2})$, the three-summand case of a general theorem
on $h$-fold convolutions that allows other main terms; it extends the
Erdős–Fuchs theorem for two summands, the subject of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0763/_index|Problem 763]]. The paper is
not held; the statement follows the erdosproblems.com commentary and the
formal-conjectures docstring.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki.

**Acceptance.** Refereed publication: J. Number Theory 4 (1972), no. 1, 1--16,
doi:10.1016/0022-314X(72)90008-X. The Crossref record dates the issue to
February 1972, and this page's name fills that to the first of the month.

Reviewed: the erdosproblems.com curator, Thomas Bloom, labeled the problem
disproved. The problem page's commentary credits the answer, in its strong form,
to Vaughan (empty proof-claim tab and no thread posts).

A Lean 4 development declares itself a formalization of the negative answer. It
is `src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos764.lean` of Boris Alexeev's lean-proofs
repository, first added 2026-08-17, with 3,873 lines at the pinned commit of
2026-09-15. Its header names Vaughan as informal author and Codex and GPT-5.6
Sol as formal authors, and it cites this paper. The header describes the proof
as the bounded-error specialization of Vaughan's argument, by the differentiated
generating-function identity, Fourier orthogonality on a circle and a geometric
kernel. Its `not_erdos_764` proves that for no `A : Set ℕ` and `c > 0` is the
summatory ordered three-fold convolution through `N` equal to `c * N` up to
`O(1)`. This is the bounded-error case only. The file closes with `#print axioms
Erdos764.not_erdos_764`, without the printed output. The formal-conjectures
statement for the problem (commit of 2026-09-20) is tagged solved and names line
3760 of the file, the theorem, as its formal proof. It also states Vaughan's
error term as a variant without proof. The corpus has not built the development,
so the page lists no `formalized` evidence.
