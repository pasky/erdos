# Bilyk: Roth's orthogonal function method in discrepancy theory

[[discrepancy/_index|..]]

[[discrepancy/bilyk_2011_roth_orthogonal_function_method/equation_1_14|equation_1_14]]: The counting discrepancy of every planar N-point set has L1 norm at
least an absolute constant times the square root of log N.

***

Dmitriy Bilyk, *On Roth's orthogonal function method in discrepancy theory*,
Uniform Distribution Theory **6** (2011), no. 1, 143--184.
The edition read is the 42-page
[author-hosted journal PDF](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~dbilyk/papers/bilyk-survey.pdf),
listed in the [journal issue](https://pcwww.liv.ac.uk/~karpenk/JournalUDT/vol06/no1/vol6nr1.htm).

**Read status.** Claims checked: definition (1.4), p. 145, and Halász's
bound (1.14), p. 148. The proof outline (4.15)--(4.17), p. 169, was read
from the page image; its earlier Haar estimates and higher-order remainder
bound have not been checked. This is Bilyk's exposition of Halász's theorem,
not an examination of the original 1981 paper.

The extracted
[[discrepancy/bilyk_2011_roth_orthogonal_function_method/equation_1_14|planar L1 bound]]
supplies a precise source interface for
[[../wiki/problems/analysis/E1221/_index|Problem 1221]].
