# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/claims/2004_09_01_verstraete|2004_09_01_verstraete]]: Verstraëte proves that the number of cycle sets on $\{1,\dots,n\}$, the sets
of cycle lengths of graphs on $n$ vertices, is $o(2^{n-n^c})$ for an absolute
$c>0$. So $f(n)=o(2^n)$, the problem's first assertion.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/claims/2025_01_17_nenadov|2025_01_17_nenadov]]: Nenadov shows that graphs on $n$ vertices realize at most $2^{n-n^{1/2-o(1)}}$
distinct cycle sets, which sharpens Verstraëte's bound on the problem's first
assertion. The paper appeared in Combinatorial Theory (2026).
