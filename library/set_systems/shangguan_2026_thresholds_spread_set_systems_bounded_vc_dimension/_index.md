# Thresholds and spread in set systems of bounded VC-dimension

[[set_systems/_index|..]]

***

Shangguan, Chong, *Thresholds and spread in set systems of bounded VC-dimension*, arXiv:2609.30263v1 (24 September 2026).

The abstract reports an absolute-constant comparison between the threshold and expectation threshold with a factor log(d+1) for VC dimension at most d. A spread-measure estimate gives a sunflower consequence: an n-uniform family of VC dimension at most d with more than (C r log(d+1))^n members contains an r-sunflower. The restriction on VC dimension is part of the statement; this does not establish the unrestricted sunflower conjecture.
