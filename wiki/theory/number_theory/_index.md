average of the cofactor's reciprocal least-preimage ratio.

[[theory/number_theory/L104_shifted_prime_totient_fibers/_index|L104_shifted_prime_totient_fibers/]]: The exceptional-prime theorem of Banks, Ford, Luca, Pappalardi and
Shparlinski implies that all but x/(log x)^(2-delta) primes up to x
have basin {p,2p} under phi(n)+1. Their union has integer density zero.

[[theory/number_theory/L105_totient_inverse_chain_cofactors/_index|L105_totient_inverse_chain_cofactors/]]: An infinite increasing odd inverse chain for phi(n)+1 cannot have an
eventually periodic largest-prime cofactor sequence, or have that
cofactor eventually at most 9. A repeated block has an explicit 2-adic
length bound; nonperiodic sequences remain uncontrolled.

[[theory/number_theory/L154_local_lucas_valuation/_index|L154_local_lucas_valuation/]]: When the first quotient digit is at least a polynomial's local multiplicity, full Lucas domination at one prime still permits a column with exactly the initial valuation gain.

[[theory/number_theory/L198_three_layer_prime_blocks/_index|L198_three_layer_prime_blocks/]]: Primes indexed by integer cells and placed by Chinese remaindering give a block of floor(alpha P) integers, 3<=alpha<4, whose multiples are exactly three layers counted by three projections of the cells.

[[theory/number_theory/L199_separated_grid_third_layer/_index|L199_separated_grid_third_layer/]]: Extending a rectangular prime-grid block with separated rows past three times its largest prime adds at least half as many new locations as primes, so this construction costs at least linearly.

[[theory/number_theory/L200_long_block_sums_differences_exponent/_index|L200_long_block_sums_differences_exponent/]]: For 3<=alpha<4 the least number of distinct prime multiples in a block of floor(alpha P) integers grows like u^(1/beta+o(1)), where beta is the best ratio log|S|/log of the largest of three projections of an integer cell set.

[[theory/number_theory/L201_type_class_long_block_bound/_index|L201_type_class_long_block_bound/]]: Three-cell type classes give an explicit exponent theta=log(27/4)/log 27 for long prime-multiple blocks, with a small logarithmic factor under Green--Tao realization and an elementary alternative using the prime number theorem.

[[theory/number_theory/L202_prime_realization_three_projections/_index|L202_prime_realization_three_projections/]]: Every finite integer cell pattern with distinct differences has a dilated prime realization with exactly its three projection counts, giving the arithmetic Kakeya comparison for all length ratios from three up to four.

[[theory/number_theory/L203_rational_entropy_prime_block_bound/_index|L203_rational_entropy_prime_block_bound/]]: A rational measure on Lemm's seven-point sums–differences configuration gives an absolute bound C u^0.56213 for the minimum distinct-multiple count, uniformly over all length ratios from three up to four.

[[theory/number_theory/L204_cartesian_prime_grid_obstruction/_index|L204_cartesian_prime_grid_obstruction/]]: Unique row-plus-column sums force a large third projection; even sliding a long block while retaining its row and column locations costs at least a constant times the number of grid primes to the two-thirds power.

[[theory/number_theory/L212_bounded_ratio_dissociated_subset/_index|L212_bounded_ratio_dissociated_subset/]]: A positive real set of ratio at most 2 has fewer than 3 times 2^d elements, and ratio at most 4/3 gives the sharp positive bound |P| < 2^d. A quantitative bound also uses the least possible nonzero coefficient sum of a relation.

[[theory/number_theory/L213_triple_log_dissociated_subset/_index|L213_triple_log_dissociated_subset/]]: Every set of n >= 5 reals has a dissociated subset of size at least log_2(n-1) - log_2(ceil(log_2 log_2 n) + 2 + sqrt 2) - log_2 6, so f(n) >= log_2 n - log_2 log_2 log_2 n - O(1).

[[theory/number_theory/L22_near_logarithmic_dissociated_subset/_index|L22_near_logarithmic_dissociated_subset/]]: Every set of n real numbers contains an additively dissociated subset of

[[theory/number_theory/L270_recentered_pivot_obstruction/_index|L270_recentered_pivot_obstruction/]]: For every R > 1, a positive odd set of ratio below R and size 2^(k-1)+1 can have no recentered graded certificate with k companions.
