# Lemmas

[[research/erdos_774/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/axis_line_flat_preimages|axis_line_flat_preimages]]: Every bounded integer point vector in the line image of a box with at most three directions has an equally bounded integer line preimage; no actual-color support is controlled, and four-direction samples are not a theorem.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/binary_circuit_lifting|binary_circuit_lifting]]: Every binary dependence on at most eighteen distinct-prime-grid columns contains a ternary dependence; nineteen is sharp, and singleton-layer descent gives a prime-tail refinement.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/bounded_support|bounded_support]]: Proportional dissociation for bounded-support bounded-entry integer vectors forces a uniform rational-independent partition.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/canonical_circuit_cover_colors|canonical_circuit_cover_colors]]: For a primitive nonternary rational circuit, sufficiently large degree-2P canonical interval lifts are fully dissociated-two-colorable by sheet parity; mixed-circuit and arbitrary-cover amplification remain open.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/chain_circuit_box_complexity|chain_circuit_box_complexity]]: Explicit ternary rational circuits in prime grids need k disjoint product boxes yet admit one 30-color proper coloring; unbounded box complexity alone forces nothing.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/coefficient_two_factor_products|coefficient_two_factor_products]]: For d,e at least four, the product of two coefficient-two circuits C_d and C_e has two rationally independent color classes, so full-product flattening of two staircase factors forces no relation.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/cofactor_template_coloring|cofactor_template_coloring]]: A quadratic matching statistic gives a uniform seven-color avoidance of all translated rank-correct single-cofactor templates for bases with three universal rows; even moduli need only two colors. This is not a coloring of all ternary relations.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/colored_line_preimages|colored_line_preimages]]: Common real spans yield simultaneous flat same-color lifts in two directions, but an explicit rationally independent twelve-line class in three directions has a flat point-supported image and no nonzero flat lift on that support.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/cover_projection_and_templates|cover_projection_and_templates]]: Row duplication is chromatically redundant and inherits the stronger ordinary-cover extraction constant; fixed repeated-type partial templates cannot be fiber-focused with three colors.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/cyclic_cover_amplification|cyclic_cover_amplification]]: An explicit fourteen-column two-sheet cover needs three dissociated colors; cyclic interval bases admit density-sharp cover lower bounds, and the proposed rational-coloring lower bound holds for distinct nonzero binary matrices of rank at most three.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/decomposable_cycle_colors|decomposable_cycle_colors]]: Balanced totally unimodular Plucker matrices admit a controlled support count, proving simultaneous three-color avoidance of all simple-wedge ternary cycles when log n=o(k).

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/difference_tower_color_limits|difference_tower_color_limits]]: Small-loss towers on valuation-separated seeds are exactly two-colorable despite arbitrarily large rational coloring number; canonical height-halving witnesses contain whole coordinate cycles.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/finite_blocks|finite_blocks]]: A complete elementary proof of the sufficient finite-block criterion and finite determination of coloring.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/fixed_base_defect|fixed_base_defect]]: The largest dissociated subset of B tensor S_p eventually has size p times r(B) minus one fixed integer; the changing-base coloring gap remains.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/full_relation_rank|full_relation_rank]]: An explicit saturated signed-relation lattice disproves a uniform span-dimension gap for proportionately dissociated integer sets.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/galois_color_slices|galois_color_slices]]: Orbitwise sign coherence makes differences of equal-value root supports ternary after all collisions; actual-color slice counting needs no monochromatic whole support, but a generating-function ceiling excludes its coordinatewise counting test for linearly growing axis lines.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/galois_squaring|galois_squaring]]: Galois squaring transforms a coefficient-two root relation into an integral pair-product relation; character dynamics prove it is nonzero for more than four leaves, but coefficient collisions and actual colors remain uncontrolled.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/height_descent|height_descent]]: Many bounded-height relation directions can be counted, and two suitably arranged directions descend one height step; rank alone and a single monochromatic support do not force the needed color matching.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/joined_boundary_colors|joined_boundary_colors]]: When log n=o(k), one two-coloring avoids all joined simplex boundaries and all flat cycles with vertex excess at most floor(sqrt(k/(16 log(2n)))); other flat cycles remain uncontrolled.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/long_shift_extraction|long_shift_extraction]]: Full k-edge increasing-path relations on pair labels force a signed-cube subset, bounding n by O_delta(k log k) under uniform full extraction; an elementary binary rank theorem also constrains their realizations.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/matching_profile_limits|matching_profile_limits]]: Bounded reciprocal direction mass gives bounded rational coloring, while sixteen plane labels defeat full-profile homogenization along every later multiscale line; neither result colors all mixed trades.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_affine_classes|moment_affine_classes]]: An affine cube class is dissociated for degree-k moments exactly when its restricted moment columns are rationally independent; a missing Fourier coset gives an explicit ternary relation.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_flattening_limits|moment_flattening_limits]]: Critical full moment tensors lose extraction, low-degree height-two functions need not have ternary substitutes on their support, and recoverable pointwise ternarization incurs full original degree.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_integer_graph|moment_integer_graph]]: The exact graph-kernel and puncturing reductions remain valid; a separate weak stable-lattice theorem supplies the critical fixed-scale cancellation needed for extraction.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_lattice_stability|moment_lattice_stability]]: Exact coordinate compression proves full semistability of Boolean polynomial lattices; with the weak stable-lattice Gaussian theorem it gives critical-boundary extraction.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_middle_layer|moment_middle_layer]]: The exact middle-layer determinant grows at the critical boundary; hereditary extraction follows from the full moment-family theorem, not from slice stability.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_modular_depth|moment_modular_depth]]: Growing congruence depth exactly captures the moment cancellation target; the critical target itself is proved by a separate stable-lattice argument.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/moment_palette_height|moment_palette_height]]: Congruence-sensitive entropy forces a monochromatic relation of height floor(sigma_star^q) in every fixed q-coloring at the critical boundary; no coefficient-flattening theorem is supplied.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/periodic_mask_circuits|periodic_mask_circuits]]: Periodic and connected masks can be dissociated despite rational convolution kernels; binary-power voltages nevertheless rule out arbitrary palettes of bounded period under an explicit type-to-row inequality.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/polynomial_offset_trades|polynomial_offset_trades]]: A full-graph lifting lemma and an adaptive four-direction character identity defeat every common-linear-intercept binary palette with arbitrary direction offsets by stage two; arbitrary colorings remain open.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/prime_pivot_obstruction|prime_pivot_obstruction]]: An explicit growing saturated prime-grid family has a full height-two kernel basis, but its next simplex extension has only full fibers as minimal ternary supports.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/prime_staircase_obstruction|prime_staircase_obstruction]]: A staircase of linear-size prime-grid circuits has a connected integral height-two circuit basis of density tending to one, yet every signed sum has a mandatory coordinate and one-prime extensions have only full-fiber minimal ternary supports.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/prime_tensor_circuit|prime_tensor_circuit]]: An explicit twenty-column dissociated circuit in S3 tensor S5 tensor S7 has rank nineteen and a saturated column lattice, isolating the signed-coefficient obstruction.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/private_conductor_lifts|private_conductor_lifts]]: Private projections permit shared conductors and an exact relation-preserving lift, but that lift always has two rationally independent colors.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/random_orientations|random_orientations]]: An exact probability bound shows why a dense planted relation family needs correlated signs to admit distinct labels.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/rational_realization|rational_realization]]: Finite rational vector configurations and their signed support hypergraphs can be realized by distinct positive integers.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/root_height_escape|root_height_escape]]: Conductor-disjoint rational root circuits with doubly exponential pivot coefficients have dissociated full products and recurrent tails of exactly increasing minimum relation height.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/signed_cographic_limit|signed_cographic_limit]]: A genuine vector configuration whose full rational relation space is a signed graph cut space has rational rank at least one third on every subset and hence at most three dissociated colors.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/stable_lattice_free_energy|stable_lattice_free_energy]]: Regular M-ellipsoids and a fixed-parameter Voronoi induction give log theta_L(t) <= epsilon(t) rank(L), with epsilon(t) tending to zero uniformly over stable lattices.

[[research/erdos_774/lemmas/unit_arithmetic_descent|unit_arithmetic_descent]]: Equal-modulus configurations can have nearly full height-two relation rank without descent; odd roots obey stronger binary relation-code restrictions that still do not force a coloring contradiction.
