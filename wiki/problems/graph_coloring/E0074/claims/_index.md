# problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0074/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims/1982_12_01_rodl|1982_12_01_rodl]]: As credited by the site, Rödl (Combinatorica, 1982) builds for every fixed
$\epsilon>0$ a graph of infinite chromatic number whose $n$-vertex subgraphs
become bipartite after deleting at most $\epsilon n$ edges. This corpus does
not hold the paper.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0074/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: GPT-6 Astra's disproof, published in Tom Adamczewski's Lean repository, shows
that for some divergent $f$, every graph whose $n$-vertex subgraphs become
bipartite after $f(n)$ edge deletions is $3$-colorable. The Lean disproof was
built here.
