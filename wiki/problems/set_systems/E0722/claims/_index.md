# problems/set_systems/E0722/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1847_01_01_kirkman|1847_01_01_kirkman]]: Kirkman (1847, refereed) constructs, for every $n\equiv1$ or $3\pmod 6$, a set
of triples on $n$ symbols containing every pair exactly once. This is the case
$(r,k)=(2,3)$ of the problem, a partial answer.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1960_01_01_hanani|1960_01_01_hanani]]: Hanani (1960, refereed) proves that a Steiner quadruple system $S(3,4,n)$
exists if and only if $n\equiv2$ or $4\pmod 6$. This is the case $(r,k)=(3,4)$
of the problem, a partial answer.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1961_06_01_hanani|1961_06_01_hanani]]: Hanani (1961, refereed) proves that the divisibility conditions suffice for a
$2$-design with blocks of size $3$ or $4$ and any index, and with blocks of
size $5$ and index $1$. This settles the cases $(r,k)=(2,4)$ and $(2,5)$, a
partial answer.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/1975_01_01_wilson|1975_01_01_wilson]]: Wilson (1972-1975, refereed) proves that for every $k$ the divisibility
conditions suffice for a Steiner system $S(2,k,n)$ once $n$ is large. This is
the case $r=2$ of the problem for every $k$, a partial answer.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/2014_01_15_keevash|2014_01_15_keevash]]: Keevash (2014) proves that for fixed $k>r$ the divisibility conditions suffice
for a Steiner system $S(r,k,n)$ once $n$ is large, settling the existence
conjecture. The claim is accepted on the erdosproblems.com curator's credit.

[[problems/set_systems/E0722/claims/2016_11_21_glock_kuhn_lo_osthus|2016_11_21_glock_kuhn_lo_osthus]]: Glock, Kühn, Lo and Osthus give an independent proof, by iterative absorption,
that the divisibility conditions suffice for an $F$-decomposition of a large
complete $r$-uniform hypergraph for every $F$, hence for Steiner systems. The
work was refereed in 2023.
