[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0993/claims/2026_09_17_fang_lu_nevo_yao_zheng|2026_09_17_fang_lu_nevo_yao_zheng]]: Fang, Lu, Nevo, Yao and Zheng claim that the independence sequence of every
forest with at least $N_0$ vertices is unimodal, for an absolute $N_0$ that is
not specified. Their arXiv preprint, with Lean code, argues by a central limit
theorem and end monotonicity.

unimodal, and Li, Vallier and Zhang's second proof has a Lean formalization
registered on the Palomar registry. It is accepted on Plasma AI's build and
kernel replay of that formalization and on the sign-off of Jacob Parish at
Plasma AI.
