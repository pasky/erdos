# Larcher: On the star discrepancy of sequences in the unit interval

[[analysis/_index|..]]

***

Gerhard Larcher, *On the star discrepancy of sequences in the unit interval*,
[arXiv:1407.2094v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.2094v1), submitted 8 July 2014,
13 pages. The journal version has not been compared.

The main statement concerns a logarithmic lower bound for the
unnormalized star discrepancy of infinitely many prefixes. Section 3 of
the arXiv proof starts with an arbitrary finite list and estimates its
largest prefix discrepancy through a recursive range argument. That
finite-list quantifier was inspected. The auxiliary minimization proof,
rounding conventions and treatment of repeated points have not been
fully checked here; no numerical constant is certified by this reading.

The arXiv record, retrieved 2026-10-14, specifies
[arXiv's non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/),
with other rights reserved. No file of this source is held. No full
transcription is provided under these terms.

The distinct Halasz input and the whole consecutive-gap proof remain
