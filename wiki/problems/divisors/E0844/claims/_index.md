# problems/divisors/E0844/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0844/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0844/claims/2025_07_01_weisenberg|2025_07_01_weisenberg]]: Desmond Weisenberg shows that a maximal set contains every non-squarefree
number. The author then uses Chvátal's 1974 theorem to show that the even
squarefree numbers are the largest pairwise non-coprime set of squarefree
numbers, which answers the question yes.

[[problems/divisors/E0844/claims/2025_07_02_alexeev_mixon_sawin|2025_07_02_alexeev_mixon_sawin]]: Alexeev, Mixon and Sawin prove that the vertices of the squarefree graph split
into cliques that each hold one even vertex. So the even vertices form a
maximum independent set, and the Erdős–Sárközy guess is correct.
