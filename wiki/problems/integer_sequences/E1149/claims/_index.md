# problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/1955_01_01_lambek_moser|1955_01_01_lambek_moser]]: Lambek and Moser (Canad. J. Math. 1955, refereed) give a density criterion for
the $n$ coprime to $f(n)$. It yields density $6/\pi^2$ for
$f(n)=\lfloor n^{1/k}\rfloor$ with $k\ge2$ an integer.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/2002_09_01_delmer_deshouillers|2002_09_01_delmer_deshouillers]]: Delmer and Deshouillers (Period. Math. Hungar. 2002, refereed) show that for
every positive non-integer $c$, the integers $n$ coprime to
$\lfloor n^c\rfloor$ have density $6/\pi^2$.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1149/claims/2016_11_24_bergelson_richter|2016_11_24_bergelson_richter]]: Theorem 1 of Bergelson and Richter (arXiv 2016, Springer 2017) concerns
Hardy-field functions $f$ growing faster than $\log t\,\log_4 t$ and strictly
between consecutive powers of $t$. For such $f$, the $n$ with
$\gcd(n,\lfloor f(n)\rfloor)=1$ have density $6/\pi^2$.
