# problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/1993_01_01_erdos_tuza|1993_01_01_erdos_tuza]]: Erdős and Tuza (Ann. Discrete Math. 55 (1993)) bound $d(n,F)$ for the
triangle, the four-cycle and every forest, which puts these graphs in the
answer set. The work is a book chapter with no documented refereeing.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2022_09_28_axenovich_clemen|2022_09_28_axenovich_clemen]]: In a refereed paper (J. Graph Theory 106 (2024)), Axenovich and Clemen exclude
from the answer set the cliques $K_q$ with $q\ge10$ and $q\equiv2,3\pmod4$,
further graphs with an odd number of edges, and almost all clique sizes.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2023_03_26_clemen_wagner|2023_03_26_clemen_wagner]]: Theorem 1.2 of Clemen and Wagner (Electron. J. Combin. 30 (2023), refereed)
gives balanced six-colorings of $K_{13^k}$ with no rainbow $K_4$, so $K_4$ is
outside the answer set.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0811/claims/2026_09_15_kitamura|2026_09_15_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's forum claim of 15 September 2026 reports a Lean-verified
proof of the Axenovich--Clemen conjecture, which puts every clique on at least
four vertices outside the answer set. The claim is partial, unreviewed and
unread here.
