**Lemma 1.** *Assume that $x \geq 1$, $m \in \mathbb{N}$,*

$$
m \leq x^{1/2}, \qquad (2.1)
$$

*$h \in \mathbb{Z}$, and $f(n)$ is a non-negative additive arithmetic function such that*

$$
f(p^\alpha) = 0 \quad \text{for } p \mid m,\ \alpha \in \mathbb{N}. \qquad (2.2)
$$

*Let*

$$
K = \max\{f(p^\alpha) : p^\alpha \leq x\}, \qquad A = \sum_{p \leq x} \frac{f(p)}{p}. \qquad (2.3)
$$

*Then we have*

$$
\sum_{\substack{n \leq x\\ n \equiv h \pmod{m}}} (f(n)-A)^2 < c_3 \frac{x}{m}(KA+K^2)
$$

*(where $c_3$ is an absolute constant independent of $x$, $m$, $h$ and $f$).*
