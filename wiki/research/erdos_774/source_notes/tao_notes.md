# 254A, Notes 1: Elementary multiplicative number theory

[Library card and partial reading copy](../../../../library/number_theory/tao_2014_254a_notes_1_elementary_multiplicative_number_theory/_index.md).

***

Terence Tao, "254A, Notes 1: Elementary multiplicative number theory,"
*What's new* (blog), November 23, 2014. The source is a web page with no
PDF; the card holds a partial Markdown reading copy.

## What the research consumes

The card carries the digest of the notes: the Chebyshev and Mertens
estimates, the Euler-product comparison, and the divisor-function and
Dirichlet-series sections. The notes are background for the research, not
a consumed premise. Proposition 12 and Theorems 13 and 15 (the Chebyshev
bound and the first and second Mertens theorems) explain why a prime
chosen with harmonic weight has a nondegenerate scale-invariant
distribution, and Exercise 16(iii), posed without proof, states that
independent prime coordinates tensorize; the
[prime-tensor extraction proof](../routes/prime_tensor_extraction.md)
proves the specific finite prime-sum inequalities and numerical constants
it uses directly, in particular
$\sum_{p>P}Q(P,p^-)/(2p^2)<2^{-18}$ at $P=2^{40}$, rather than invoking
the qualitative $o(1)$ statements of Exercise 16 as a black box. No
theorem of the research depends on the notes.
