# problems/irrationality/E0069/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0069/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0069/claims/2024_09_23_pratt|2024_09_23_pratt]]: Pratt (2024, refereed 2025) proves that a uniform quantitative prime tuples
conjecture implies that $\sum_n\omega(n)/t^n$ is irrational for every integer
$t\ge2$. The problem is the case $t=2$.

[[problems/irrationality/E0069/claims/2025_12_01_tao_teravainen|2025_12_01_tao_teravainen]]: Tao and Teräväinen (arXiv, December 2025) prove unconditionally that
$\sum_n\omega(n)/2^n$, which equals $\sum_p1/(2^p-1)$, is irrational. This
answers the question yes.
