***

**Claim.** Michael Schroeder's *Seven Prime Divisors in Odd Distinct Covering Systems* asserts that the least common
multiple of the moduli of any finite covering system with distinct odd
moduli greater than one has at least 7 distinct prime divisors.

**Covers.** The negative answer to
[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]] for periods with at most
6 prime divisors, regardless of exponents. It leaves periods involving
more primes untreated and does not resolve the unrestricted question.

**Standing.** Claimed. The dated description on the author's research page
and the forum announcement were read on 2026-10-16. They report a Lean
formalization and supporting exact certificates. Neither the proof nor the
formalization has been checked here, and no independent review or journal
acceptance was located in those records. The date in this page's name is the
paper date stated on the author's page; the linked manuscript was not read.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki. No claim of an audited dependency chain
is made.

**Submission note.** The author announced the seven-, eight- and nine-prime
results together in the problem's discussion on 2026-09-15.
