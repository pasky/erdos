[[problems/graph_coloring/E0627/claims/2025_12_18_araujo_filipe_miyazaki|2025_12_18_araujo_filipe_miyazaki]]: Theorem 1.2 of Araujo, Filipe and Miyazaki (arXiv, 2025) states that if
$R(s,t)\le R(k,k)$ whenever $st\le k^2$, and $\log_2R(k,k)/k\to\ell$, then
$f(n)/(n/(\log_2n)^2)\to\ell^2$. The result is conditional on these two
unproved hypotheses.
