[[problems/integer_sequences/E0970/claims/1978_01_01_iwaniec|1978_01_01_iwaniec]]: The Corollary of Iwaniec's 1978 paper on Jacobsthal's problem,
$C(r)\ll r^2\log^2r$, gives the refereed upper bound $h(k)\ll(k\log k)^2$. On
this source, the order of $h(k)$ and the bound $h(k)\ll k^2$ stay open.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0970/claims/2014_12_16_ford_green_konyagin_maynard_tao|2014_12_16_ford_green_konyagin_maynard_tao]]: Display (1.2) of the 2018 long-gaps paper of Ford, Green, Konyagin, Maynard
and Tao, applied through (1.3) at $x=p_k$, gives the refereed lower bound
$h(k)\gg k(\log k)^2\log_3k/\log_2k$, stronger than the site's display. The
order of $h(k)$ stays open.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0970/claims/2026_09_25_openai|2026_09_25_openai]]: OpenAI's theorem $h(k)\ll k^2/(\log\log 3k)^2$ answers the displayed question
$h(k)\ll k^2$ yes. This partial claim is accepted on the Lean declaration
erdos_970_quadratic ($h(k)\le Ck^2$), built and audited here, and the order of
$h(k)$ stays open.
