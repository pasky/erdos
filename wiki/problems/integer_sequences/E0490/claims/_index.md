# problems/integer_sequences/E0490/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0490/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0490/claims/1972_05_02_szemeredi|1972_05_02_szemeredi]]: Szemerédi's theorem (J. Number Theory, 1976) shows that if two subsets of the
first $N$ integers have all cross products distinct, the product of their
sizes is below $cN^2/\log N$ for an absolute constant $c$. It is refereed and
credited by the site.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0490/claims/1974_12_01_erdos_szemeredi|1974_12_01_erdos_szemeredi]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős and Szemerédi (J. Austral. Math. Soc., 1976) gives a
simpler proof that if two subsets of $\{1,\ldots,x\}$ have all cross products
distinct, the product of their sizes is below $cx^2/\log x$. It is refereed
and linked from the site's thread.
