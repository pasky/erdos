# problems/divisors/E0448/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0448/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0448/claims/1981_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum|1981_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum]]: By the Erdős–Tenenbaum theorem, the integers with $\tau^+(n)\le\alpha\tau(n)$
have upper density at most $c(\varepsilon)\alpha^{1-\varepsilon}$, so the
conjectured density-one statement fails. Alexeev's Lean formalization is
linked.

[[problems/divisors/E0448/claims/1988_09_15_hall_tenenbaum|1988_09_15_hall_tenenbaum]]: The Hall–Tenenbaum theorem states that $\tau^+(n)/\tau(n)$ has a limiting
distribution function $\nu$ with $z/\sqrt{\log(2/z)}\ll\nu(z)\ll z\log(2/z)$.
So the integers with $\tau^+(n)<\epsilon\tau(n)$ do not have density one.
