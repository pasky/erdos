# Interfaces in arithmetic constructions

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/factorial_divisor_cutoff|factorial_divisor_cutoff]]: The least prime factor bounds the factorial indices for a fixed composite divisor of a factorial plus one, without giving a subpower count of such divisors.

[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/multiplicative_selection|multiplicative_selection]]: Sign reduction for interval products, an exact residue formulation for
each fixed input, and the one-way passage from initial-segment matchings to
arbitrary divisor sets.

[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/odd_greedy|odd_greedy]]: An elementary finite-prefix reduction equates universal termination for
ordinary odd greedy and for odd greedy with distinct denominators, without
asserting equal behavior on each input or proving termination.

[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/progression_transfers|progression_transfers]]: Root growth versus adjacent ratios, the failure of a direct integer
extension of a natural-number permutation argument, and the structural or
numerical hypotheses missing from two proposed finite reductions.

[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/sequence_upper_bound_scope|sequence_upper_bound_scope]]: White's pending upper bound supplies the formal little-o improvement
in Problem 1063. The stronger claimed LEAP formula has the same target
interface; its proof fidelity and the sharp growth remain separate.

***

These notes examine when a construction for one formulation reaches another.
The arguments distinguish a complete elementary reduction from an external
premise and from the unresolved target. They are author-recorded work by
Plasma AI; they supply no independent verification or change of problem
standing.

The [[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/odd_greedy|odd-greedy
reduction]] identifies the same universal termination question behind two
algorithms that can behave differently on a fixed input. The
[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/multiplicative_selection|divisibility
interfaces]] explain the sign convention, simultaneous choices, and the
unbounded parameter left by a finite residue reduction. The
[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/progression_transfers|progression
interfaces]] locate the missing uniform assertions in several tempting
transfers. The
[[research/arithmetic_construction_interfaces/sequence_upper_bound_scope|sequence
upper-bound specialization]] compares a claimed bound with the exact formal
question it would answer.
