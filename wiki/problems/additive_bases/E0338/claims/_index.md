# problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/1957_04_01_kelly|1957_04_01_kelly]]: Kelly's refereed 1957 paper proves that every basis of order $2$ in the
classical sense has a restricted order at most $4$. It also proves that an
asymptotic basis of order $2$ with counting function at least $Cx/\log\log x$
has a restricted order at most $3$.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2005_03_01_hennecart|2005_03_01_hennecart]]: Hennecart's refereed 2005 paper proves that every asymptotic basis of order
$2$ has a restricted order at most $4$, and that $4$ is attained. This settles
the order-$2$ case of the existence and boundedness questions.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2026_07_28_white|2026_07_28_white]]: An unreviewed July 2026 working report by Patrick White classifies when an
eventually periodic set has a restricted order. It shows this equals the order
when every finite removal keeps the order, and gives an order-$3$ basis of
restricted order $6$ that stays a basis after any finite removal.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0338/claims/2026_10_01_veljjanoski|2026_10_01_veljjanoski]]: A write-up of October 2026 by Daniel Veljjanoski states that a set of positive
lower density $\delta$ that stays a basis after removing any finite set has
restricted order at most $3\lceil8/\delta^2\rceil-2$. It is unreviewed.
