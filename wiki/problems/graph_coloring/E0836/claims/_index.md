# problems/graph_coloring/E0836/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0836/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0836/claims/1975_01_01_erdos_lovasz|1975_01_01_erdos_lovasz]]: Theorem 8 of Erdős and Lovász (1975) bounds the largest number of points of a
$3$-chromatic $r$-uniform clique below by $\frac12\binom{2r-2}{r-1}+2r-2$, so
the first question's $O(r^2)$ bound fails. It is a proceedings result, and its
status is claimed.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0836/claims/2026_04_27_price|2026_04_27_price]]: A note posted by Liam Price and produced with GPT-5.5 Pro claims that every
intersecting $r$-uniform hypergraph of chromatic number exactly $3$ has two
edges meeting in at least $\lfloor r/2\rfloor$ vertices, so the second
question has the answer yes. The note is unreviewed.
