# problems/integer_sequences/E0438/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0438/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0438/claims/2000_12_01_khalfalah_lodha_szemeredi|2000_12_01_khalfalah_lodha_szemeredi]]: Khalfalah, Lodha and Szemerédi prove that a subset of the first $N$ integers
with no square among its pairwise sums has at most $(11/32+o(1))N$ elements,
matching Massias's set. The result was a DIMACS report in 2000 and was
refereed in Discrete Math. 2002.
