# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0001/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0001/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0001/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: No constant $c>0$ makes $N>c\,2^n$ hold for every sum-distinct set of $n$
integers in $\{1,\ldots,N\}$. The disproof was found by GPT-6 Astra in Epoch
AI's benchmark, has a public Lean development from Tom Adamczewski, and is
accepted on Lean built here.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0001/claims/2026_09_15_alexeev|2026_09_15_alexeev]]: A second Lean 4 disproof, in Boris Alexeev's lean-proofs repository and
credited to GPT-6 Astra, gives for every $n\ge2$ a sum-distinct $n$-set in
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with $N<2^{n+1}/\log_2 n$. It also gives a cube-root saving,
and it is accepted.
