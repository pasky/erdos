# problems/analysis/E0996/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0996/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0996/claims/1936_01_01_raikov|1936_01_01_raikov]]: Raikov proves that for every integrable $f$ of period $1$ and every integer
$a\ge2$ the averages of $f(a^kx)$ converge to the integral of $f$ almost
everywhere. So for $n_k=a^k$ no Fourier-tail condition is needed.

[[problems/analysis/E0996/claims/2026_04_20_ho|2026_04_20_ho]]: Ho claims that for every $C>0$ there are a mean-zero function in every $L^p$
with $p<\infty$ and a lacunary sequence with Fourier tail
$O((\log\log\log N)^{-C})$ whose lacunary averages diverge almost everywhere,
so no absolute $C$ exists.

[[problems/analysis/E0996/claims/2026_09_26_yang|2026_09_26_yang]]: Yang claims to construct a $\{0,1\}$-valued function and a sequence with
$n_{j+1}\ge2n_j$ whose Fourier tail is at most $8/\sqrt{\log\log m}$ while its
lacunary averages have limit superior at least $3/4$ almost everywhere.
