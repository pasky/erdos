# problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/claims/1972_03_01_erdos|1972_03_01_erdos]]: In a refereed paper credited by erdosproblems.com, Erdős proves that every
integer between an absolute constant times $n^{3/2}$ and $\binom n2$, except
two values, is the number of lines determined by some $n$ points in the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/claims/1988_06_01_salamon_erdos|1988_06_01_salamon_erdos]]: Salamon and Erdős determine, for every sufficiently large $n$, exactly which
integers occur as the number of lines determined by $n$ points in the plane.
This answers the modified Statement of Problem 606 in full, and
erdosproblems.com credits the refereed paper as the solution.
