# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims/2005_03_21_verstraete|2005_03_21_verstraete]]: Verstraëte proves that some set of integers with $O(n^{0.99})$ elements up to
$n$ is unavoidable: every graph of average degree at least $10$ has a cycle
whose length lies in it. This answers Problem 72 affirmatively.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/claims/2020_10_29_liu_montgomery|2020_10_29_liu_montgomery]]: Liu and Montgomery prove that every increasing sequence of even integers of at
most stretched-exponential growth, such as the powers of two, is unavoidable
at large average degree. This gives an explicit density-zero set for
Problem 72.
