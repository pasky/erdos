# problems/group_theory/E1098/claims

[[problems/group_theory/E1098/_index|..]]

[[problems/group_theory/E1098/claims/1976_06_01_neumann|1976_06_01_neumann]]: Neumann (1976) proved that the non-commuting graph of a group $G$ has no
infinite complete subgraph exactly when the center has finite index $n$. He
also proved that for non-abelian $G$ every complete subgraph has at most $n-1$
vertices.
