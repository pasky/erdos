# problems/divisors/E0882/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0882/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0882/claims/1999_04_01_erdos_lev_rauzy_sandor_sarkozy|1999_04_01_erdos_lev_rauzy_sandor_sarkozy]]: Erdős, Lev, Rauzy, Sándor and Sárközy prove that the largest subset of
$\{1,\ldots,n\}$ whose nonzero subset sums never divide one another has
$\log n/\log2+O(\log\log n)$ elements. This refereed theorem fixes the leading
term the problem asks for and leaves the second open.

[[problems/divisors/E0882/claims/2026_07_27_korsky|2026_07_27_korsky]]: Korsky's manuscript, posted as a forum proof claim with GPT-5.6 Pro's help,
claims to narrow the largest size to two consecutive values,
$\lfloor\log_2(n+1)\rfloor$ or one more. It also claims that the lower value
is exact on an initial stretch of every dyadic block.
