# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims/1978_01_01_odlyzko_stanley|1978_01_01_odlyzko_stanley]]: Odlyzko and Stanley's 1978 Bell Laboratories memorandum describes the members
of $A(3^m)$ and $A(2\cdot3^m)$ by their ternary digits, with growth of order
$k^{\log_2 3}$. It states these results without proof and is unrefereed.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/claims/2014_08_08_rolnick|2014_08_08_rolnick]]: Rolnick's 2017 European J. Combin. paper proves that $A(3^k)$ and
$A(2\cdot3^k)$, for $k\ge1$, are independent Stanley sequences with the
ternary descriptions of Odlyzko and Stanley and growth of order
$n^{\log_2 3}$.
