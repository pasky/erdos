The [whole-claim examination](order_26_examination.md) and its
[distinct passing grade](order_26_grade.md), dated 2026-10-14, support the
**refutation-failed** verdict for every finite simple bipartite graph of
order at most 26 being four-choosable and for $n(4)\ge27$. Both disclosed
GPT-6 Astra and fresh, isolated contexts; neither the examiner nor the
grader authored the subject. The grade is the acceptance warrant recorded
by [L78](../../_index.md), not a new assertion by the filing integrator.

The grade rules explicitly on the disclosed preparation-concurrency
breach: it does not void mathematical independence on the supplied content
evidence, but full concurrency compliance is not certified. The grade also
retains two mathematical/runtime qualifications. Support on subsets
meeting both fixed lists is sufficient, not universally necessary; adding
unit mass at the empty set preserves a strict contradiction. The retained
side counts are integers, but value-equivalent floating-point counts can
pass the validator. Neither qualification refutes the theorem.

The [frozen subject manifest](../assets/review_subject/manifest.json)
identifies every original path and the extraction date. The original
statement, proof, source and inputs are kept under
`../assets/review_subject/`. The reports and runnable checks retain their
mathematical content; only wiki frontmatter and navigation are normalized
for filing. The canonical proof's closing support discussion states the
grade's sufficiency qualification, and the examined wording remains in
the frozen proof. No inspected theorem statement, certificate or checker
is replaced by that clarification.

The [original failure-exit keeper](check_failure_exits.py) checks actual
failure behavior in ordinary and optimized Python. The
[direct arithmetic check](check_grade_arithmetic.py) independently counts
palette subsets and has an explicit negative mutation. The
[grading replay adapter](replay_grade.py) uses the unchanged
[retained evidence harness](grade_harness.py); it is an adapted
infrastructure replay, not full initialization of the repository's tools
package. Complete commands and limits are in the reports. The paper
calculation and analytic reduction remain essential: successful finite
execution alone does not establish the universal graph statement.

The exact value of $n(4)$, a solution for general $k$, novelty, optimizer
certification and formal verification are outside this acceptance.
