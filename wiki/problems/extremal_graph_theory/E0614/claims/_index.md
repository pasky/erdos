[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0614/claims/2026_01_07_ma_tang|2026_01_07_ma_tang]]: An unrefereed note posted on the erdosproblems.com thread determines
$f(n,1)=\binom n2-\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$. For each fixed $k\ge2$ it writes
$f(n,k)$ as $\binom n2$ minus the Turán number of a finite family of graphs on
$k+2$ vertices, a number undetermined in general; for $k=2$ it is
$\mathrm{ex}(n,C_4)$, known exactly at $n=q^2+q+1$ for $q$ a power of $2$.
