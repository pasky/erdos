# Countable partition ordinals at three summands

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_592/height_three_attack|height_three_attack]]: The three-game step at omega to the omega cubed is constructed online,
for strategies that read numerical history, when all outputs are outside
or when they are inside with the first tree's top split at its first or
last pair of selected leaves. The coherent finite-height dichotomy and
Builder implication reduce the positive case to middle strategies,
where the construction is open.

[[research/erdos_592/height_three_evidence/_index|height_three_evidence/]]: Checks explicit selected union trees and their partition-cell endpoints,
the first-tree synchronization obstruction, fixed-shape schedules through
height three, and a separately qualified finite global-state adversary
model. No adaptive-game or ordinal conclusion is certified.

[[research/erdos_592/pattern_level_obstruction|pattern_level_obstruction]]: A subtree-containment reading of Schipperus's pattern levels makes the
height-three pattern contain cliques of every finite size, even with
distinct root lengths, but makes it impossible on good pairs. Under a
reading by selection shapes, a triangle-free pattern must be inside and
middle. On the unrestricted tree space a fixed-prefix embedding rules
out every coloring supported only on disjoint leaf sets; a negative
construction needs a separated host and unavoidability there.

[[research/erdos_592/successor_builder_theorem|successor_builder_theorem]]: Nash-Williams fusion gives the dichotomy and finite-cover refinement
in the coherent successor game. A direct construction supplies its
normalized Builder implication. This closes the finite-height source
transfer, without supplying the middle-strategy triangle construction.

[[research/erdos_592/successor_tree_interface|successor_tree_interface]]: Equal-block normalization for finite-height ordinal trees follows from
Erdős-Milner with a factor-two increase in root length. At height three,
a fixed strategy defeats one synchronization mechanism but admits another
schedule. A companion proof supplies the coherent finite-height source
interface; compatibility for every adaptive middle strategy remains open.

***

The first unresolved finite parameter in
[[problems/set_theory/E0592/_index|Problem 592]] is
$\omega^{\omega^3}\to(\omega^{\omega^3},3)^2$. The
[[research/leads/four_clique_versus_triangle_colors/_index|four-clique lead]]
explains why a positive answer would also settle the stated question of
Komjáth. The
[[research/erdos_592/successor_tree_interface|successor-tree interface]]
reconstructs the equal-block normalization and states the remaining
compatibility problem without introducing limit-ordinal machinery into this
finite-height case. The normalization is an author-recorded deduction from a
published theorem; the triangle construction is open for middle
strategies.

The [[research/erdos_592/height_three_attack|height-three attack]] is
the canonical sketch, and the
[[research/leads/three_summand_partition/_index|three-summand lead]]
points to it. It builds the three games online, for strategies that read
numerical history, when all outputs are outside or all are inside with
the first tree's top split at its first or last pair of selected leaves.
The [[research/erdos_592/successor_builder_theorem|source reconstruction]]
proves the dichotomy, finite-cover refinement and normalized Builder
implication for the coherent game at finite height. The positive case
is thereby reduced, at author standing, to middle strategies, where
only special constructions are known. The page also proves that one
mechanism cannot synchronize an explicit fixed strategy, and constructs a
different schedule for that same strategy. Its exact finite evidence
retains full-tree consistency within the stated bounded ranges.

The [[research/erdos_592/pattern_level_obstruction|pattern-level page]]
gives cliques of every finite size for a containment reading of the
negative pattern, which rules out that reading. A second reading by
selection shapes turns the printed patterns into fixed pairs of shapes,
places every triangle-free pattern in the middle class, and gives an
explicit triangle for Theorem 32's pattern. Its fixed-prefix embedding
shows that every coloring supported on disjoint leaf sets on unrestricted
$W_h$ has a homogeneous set of full type. Negative patterns therefore
need a separated host and an unavoidability theorem on every full-type
subset of that host. None of these arguments decides the partition
question or changes the published relations.
