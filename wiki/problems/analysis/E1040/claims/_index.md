# problems/analysis/E1040/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1040/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/1958_12_01_erdos_herzog_piranian|1958_12_01_erdos_herzog_piranian]]: In the problem's source, Erdős, Herzog and Piranian show that a line segment
or a closed disc of transfinite diameter at least one has minimal lemniscate
area zero. They derive the two cases from Chebyshev polynomials and from zeros
on a circle.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2024_02_03_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2024_02_03_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: Krishnapur, Lundberg and Ramachandran prove that a compact set of capacity
above one whose equilibrium measure obeys a power bound on discs has minimal
lemniscate area zero. This is Corollary 1.6 in the journal version of their
paper Inradius of random lemniscates.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran|2025_03_24_krishnapur_lundberg_ramachandran]]: A preprint of Krishnapur, Lundberg and Ramachandran claims that if $K$ has
capacity one and is the closure of a bounded open set with $C^2$ boundary,
then the minimal area of $\{|p|<1\}$ over monic polynomials $p$ with zeros in
$K$ has infimum zero over the degrees.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_01_29_feng|2026_01_29_feng]]: Feng and coauthors claim two countable compact sets of transfinite diameter
zero, one with minimal lemniscate area at least $\pi/4$ and one with minimal
area below any prescribed bound, so that the minimal area is not a function of
the transfinite diameter.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_04_03_ghosh_ramachandran|2026_04_03_ghosh_ramachandran]]: Ghosh and Ramachandran give compact sets of equal capacity in $(0,1)$ with
different minimal areas, so the minimal area is not determined by the
capacity. They also show that for compact $K$ of capacity $t>1$, the
degree-$n$ minimal area of $\{|p|<1\}$ decays like $t^{-2n}$.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_05_tzachristas|2026_09_05_tzachristas]]: Tzachristas claims that every compact set of capacity one admits monic
polynomials with zeros in it whose unit lemniscates have arbitrarily small
area, and hence that every closed infinite set of transfinite diameter at
least one has minimal area zero.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_gessel|2026_09_06_gessel]]: Gessel claims that for every closed infinite set of transfinite diameter at
least one, the areas of $\{|p|<1\}$ over monic polynomials $p$ with all roots
in the set have infimum zero. The Lean proof was checked by an outside
verifier but not built here.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_kitamura|2026_09_06_kitamura]]: Kitamura's independent Lean 4 development claims to prove that a closed
infinite set whose finite Fekete diameters have infimum at least one has
minimal lemniscate area zero. The bridge to the classical transfinite diameter
is not formalized.

[[problems/analysis/E1040/claims/2026_09_06_shlummi|2026_09_06_shlummi]]: The claim is that every closed infinite set of transfinite diameter at least
one admits monic polynomials with all roots in it whose unit lemniscates have
arbitrarily small area. The author ran the Lean proof, which is not built
here.
