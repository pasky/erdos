## Scope

Here Sidon means the harmonic-analysis property for a subset $\Lambda$ of the
dual of a compact abelian group: the sum of absolute coefficients is bounded by
a constant times the supremum norm of the associated character polynomial.
It is not merely the additive $B_2$ condition on pair sums.

The theorem in Section 2, printed pp. 138–139, includes the following equivalent
condition. There is $\delta>0$ such that every finitely supported scalar family
on $\Lambda$ has a quasi-independent subset retaining at least $\delta$ times
its total absolute weight. Quasi-independence forbids nonzero finite relations
with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$. The same theorem includes the unweighted
proportional-subset condition.
