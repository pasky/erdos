# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/1978_01_01_gyarfas_lehel|1978_01_01_gyarfas_lehel]]: Gyárfás and Lehel (1978) prove the tree packing conjecture when all but at
most two of the trees are stars, and when every tree is a star or a path. The
paper is in conference proceedings, so the claim is pending.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/1983_09_01_fishburn|1983_09_01_fishburn]]: Fishburn (J. Graph Theory 7 (1983)) verifies the Gyárfás–Lehel tree packing
conjecture for every $n\le9$. The claim is accepted on the refereed
publication, and the result is known from the paper's abstract and its
citations.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/1990_01_01_guichard_massman|1990_01_01_guichard_massman]]: Guichard and Massman (J. Combin. Math. Combin. Comput. 8 (1990)) verify by
computer that the tree packing conjecture holds for $n=10$ and $n=11$,
extending Fishburn's $n\le9$. The claim is accepted on the journal
publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/2016_06_13_joos_kim_kuhn_osthus|2016_06_13_joos_kim_kuhn_osthus]]: Joos, Kim, Kühn and Osthus prove the tree packing conjecture for all large $n$
whenever the trees beyond the first $\varepsilon n$ have bounded maximum
degree. The claim is accepted on the refereed publication in J. Eur. Math.
Soc. 21 (2019).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/2021_06_22_allen_bottcher_clemens_hladky_piguet_taraz|2021_06_22_allen_bottcher_clemens_hladky_piguet_taraz]]: An arXiv preprint of 2021 by Allen, Böttcher, Clemens, Hladký, Piguet and
Taraz proves the tree packing conjecture for all large $n$ when every tree has
maximum degree at most $cn/\log n$. It has no journal record, so the claim is
pending.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/claims/2024_10_17_chalise_clark_gnang|2024_10_17_chalise_clark_gnang]]: An arXiv preprint of October 2024 by Chalise, Clark and Gnang claimed the tree
packing conjecture for every $n$ by a polynomial method. The authors withdrew
it on 1 September 2026, citing an error in the proof of their Composition
Lemma.
