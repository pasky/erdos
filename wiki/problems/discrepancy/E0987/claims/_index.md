# problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1965_03_01_erdos|1965_03_01_erdos]]: Erdős's 1965 theorem says that every sequence has $A_k>c\log k$ for infinitely
many $k$, so $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$. It was the first answer to the first
question, is refereed in the Israel Journal of Mathematics, and is credited by
the curator.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1967_01_01_clunie|1967_01_01_clunie]]: Clunie's 1967 theorem says that every sequence has $A_k\gg k^{1/2}$ for
infinitely many $k$, so $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$. It answers the first question
and is refereed, in the Journal of the London Mathematical Society.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/1969_06_01_liu|1969_06_01_liu]]: Liu's 1969 theorem says that a sequence taking finitely many distinct values
has $\limsup_kA_k/k^{1-\delta}>1/5$ for every $\delta>0$. It answers the first
question for such sequences and is refereed, in Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/2025_08_30_tao|2025_08_30_tao]]: Tao's 2025 forum proof shows that $\limsup_k A_k=\infty$ for every sequence.
It reproves the answer to the first question, is credited by the site's
curator, and has a Lean 4 formalization in his analysis repository that was
not built here.

[[problems/discrepancy/E0987/claims/2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant|2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant]]: Alexeev, Putterman, Sawhney, Sellke and Valiant's 2026 theorem, due to an
internal OpenAI model, says that some sequence has
$A_k\ll\sqrt{k\log 2k}=o(k)$ for all $k$. It answers the second question and
is accepted by the site's curator.
