# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/claims/1971_12_01_choi|1971_12_01_choi]]: Choi (Proc. London Math. Soc. 1971) proves $f(n)\ll n^{3/4}$ for his interval
function, the first upper bound for Problem 788, and states it with his
conjecture $n^{1/2+o(1)}$. The paper is refereed and is known here through the
papers that cite it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/claims/2000_01_01_baltz_schoen_srivastav|2000_01_01_baltz_schoen_srivastav]]: Theorem 2 of Baltz, Schoen and Srivastav (Colloq. Math. 2000, refereed) proves
$f(n)=O(n^{2/3}(\log n)^{2/3})$, the best refereed upper bound for
Problem 788. The paper also proves the greedy lower bound $f(n)\ge\sqrt n$ on p. 172.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/claims/2026_07_19_wang|2026_07_19_wang]]: A 2026 manuscript by Shouqiao Wang, whose proof was found by an AI pipeline
using GPT-5.6 Sol, claims $c\sqrt{n\log n}\le f(n)\le n^{1/2+o(1)}$ and so
Choi's conjectured exponent $1/2$. It comes with a Lean development and is
unreviewed.
