# problems/polynomials/E0485/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0485/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0485/claims/1987_01_01_schinzel|1987_01_01_schinzel]]: Schinzel proves that the square of a polynomial with $k$ nonzero terms has
more than $\log\log k/\log2$ terms, so the minimum tends to infinity and the
answer is yes. The refereed paper appeared in Acta Arithmetica in 1987.

[[problems/polynomials/E0485/claims/2009_03_31_schinzel_zannier|2009_03_31_schinzel_zannier]]: Schinzel and Zannier prove that the square of a polynomial with $k$ nonzero
terms has at least $2+\log(k-1)/\log8$ terms, so the minimum grows at least
logarithmically. The refereed paper appeared in Rendiconti Lincei in 2009.
