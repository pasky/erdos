[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0898/claims/1935_01_01_mordell|1935_01_01_mordell]]: Mordell proved that for a point inside a triangle, the distances to the
vertices sum to at least twice the sum of the distances to the sides. The
proof, published in 1935 and, with Barrow, as the 1937 Monthly solution, is
refereed and credited by the curator.
