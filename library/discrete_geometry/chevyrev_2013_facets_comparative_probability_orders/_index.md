# Chevyrev, Searles and Slinko: facets of comparative probability orders

[[discrete_geometry/_index|..]]

***

I. Chevyrev, D. Searles and A. Slinko, *On the Number of Facets of Polytopes
Representing Comparative Probability Orders*, Order **30** (2013), no. 3,
749–761, [DOI 10.1007/s11083-012-9274-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11083-012-9274-0).
The [arXiv preprint, 1103.3938v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.3938v1), dates
from 21 March 2011. The journal version has not been compared.

Definitions 4–5, Theorem 3(a), and its construction were inspected in the
arXiv version. They provide strict orders of subsets induced by positive
weights, with at least $F_{k+1}$ flippable pairs on $k$ elements,
where $F_1=F_2=1$. Each flippable pair is disjoint and adjacent in the
same order. This is a source reading, not an independent proof audit.

The arXiv record, retrieved 2026-10-14, specifies
[arXiv's non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/),
with other rights reserved. No file of this source is held. No full
transcription is provided under these terms.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/divisors/E1100/_index|Problem 1100]]:
the finite strict order persists when scaled weights are approximated by
logarithms of distinct primes. Disjoint adjacent subsets then give
coprime consecutive divisors of the squarefree product. The problem page
records the complete author argument for this transfer and its
source-dependent bound $g(k)\ge F_{k+1}$; it does not determine $g(k)$.
