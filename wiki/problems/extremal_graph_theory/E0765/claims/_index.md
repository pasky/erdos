# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/claims/1966_01_01_erdos_renyi_sos|1966_01_01_erdos_renyi_sos]]: In a refereed paper in Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. 1 (1966), Erdős, Rényi and
Sós prove that the largest number of edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no
four-cycle is asymptotic to $n^{3/2}/2$, the formula Problem 765 asks for. A
Lean development is linked.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/claims/1966_08_01_brown|1966_08_01_brown]]: Brown proves, independently of Erdős, Rényi and Sós, that the largest number
of edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no four-cycle is asymptotic to
$n^{3/2}/2$. The result is Section 3 of a refereed paper in Canad. Math. Bull.
9 (1966).
