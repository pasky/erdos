# Rare distances in planar point sets

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_132/affine_deformations|affine_deformations]]: A nonsimilarity affine map preserving distance equalities forces
R at least ceil(n/4); the finite matrix test exposes the restriction.

[[research/erdos_132/algebraic_concentration|algebraic_concentration]]: A translated-hyperbola argument gives linear rarity from a positive
collinear fraction, completing the bounded-degree algebraic-curve criterion.

[[research/erdos_132/angular_interpolation|angular_interpolation]]: Complex barycentric weights retain angular information, but an exact
rich-shell example defeats fixed-sign holomorphic and Hermitian charges.

[[research/erdos_132/arithmetic_palettes|arithmetic_palettes]]: Uniform lattice counts on ellipses force bounded-rarity palettes to need
at least n^(1/10-o(1)) polynomial-length arithmetic progressions.

[[research/erdos_132/circle_incidence|circle_incidence]]: Translated cosine-curve incidences and additive covering give linear
rarity from any fixed positive fraction of concyclic points.

[[research/erdos_132/circular_configurations|circular_configurations]]: A cocircular hull with two nonvertices has a second rare distance;
near-circular and fixed-ring families have linear rarity growth.

[[research/erdos_132/constant_width|constant_width]]: Gives a geometric all-rarity criterion and its consequence for sets in
which every point is incident with a diameter pair.

[[research/erdos_132/constant_width_inverse|constant_width_inverse]]: Bounded excess over m/2 distances on a constant-width boundary forces
a positive circular fraction; this treats boundedly many diameter-inactive
points.

[[research/erdos_132/deletion_criticality|deletion_criticality]]: A single spanning bicyclic minimum graph can account for every deletion;
this persists with nonstar diameter and a sublinear distance palette.

[[research/erdos_132/diameter_activity|diameter_activity]]: Rich fixed-long-rank cores have linear cycle surplus under bounded
rarity; supporting disks and active-circle bounds constrain their supports.

[[research/erdos_132/distance_partition_algebra|distance_partition_algebra]]: Exact-spectrum symmetry tests and three-dimensional countermodels
expose the missing planar condition in uncentered rank-four arguments.

[[research/erdos_132/dyadic_distances|dyadic_distances]]: Dyadic hypotheses give logarithmic growth; a five-point equality pattern
obstructs invariant reduction, while a split F2 valuation has a rare
class at its lowest norm level.

[[research/erdos_132/eisenstein_motif|eisenstein_motif]]: Preserves the finite grid search; a direct five-candidate certificate
excludes one-point extensions of its eleven-point two-rare-distance motif.

[[research/erdos_132/evidence/_index|evidence/]]: Exact valuation obstructions, a rigid split-class example, and a bounded
Eisenstein-grid enumeration; none resolves either unrestricted question.

[[research/erdos_132/first_exceptional_distance|first_exceptional_distance]]: A complete lattice patch propagates up to the first nonlattice length;
in a one-rare sequence that length has unbounded rank from both ends.

[[research/erdos_132/four_point_collisions|four_point_collisions]]: A three-value Monge obstruction and reflection-pair analysis prove
four cross distances for two collapsing four-point analytic clusters.

[[research/erdos_132/full_similarity_orbits|full_similarity_orbits]]: All growing full word orbits of fixed planar contractive similarities
have R tending to infinity, with no separation or density assumption.

[[research/erdos_132/generic_cluster_replacement|generic_cluster_replacement]]: Replacing each point by an arbitrary fixed b-point cluster gains a rare
distance outside finitely many scale parameters, including all small scales.

[[research/erdos_132/independent/_index|independent/]]: Archived independent input; checked additions and limitations are
documented in the canonical topical notes, not certified wholesale here.

[[research/erdos_132/layers_and_obstructions|layers_and_obstructions]]: A balanced-circle 2-core argument gives the rank bound (2k-1)|B_k|;
the short-edge inverse also proves divergence for linear aspect ratios.

[[research/erdos_132/line_covers|line_covers]]: A complete distance-rank count gives linear growth for a fixed number
of covering lines, without arithmetic assumptions.

[[research/erdos_132/long_chord_rigidity|long_chord_rigidity]]: An odd convex polygon with uniform lengths in its two longest
cyclic separation classes is regular; richness has not forced this pattern.

[[research/erdos_132/long_rank_gaps|long_rank_gaps]]: Bounded rarity forces every rich fixed long rank to lie within O(Delta/n^2)
of its predecessor; fixed top-rank unions have odd girth Omega(n^(1/8)).

[[research/erdos_132/minimum_graph_obstruction|minimum_graph_obstruction]]: Strictly convex examples have n+1 minimum-distance edges but only two
triangular cycles, invalidating a proposed large-cycle reduction.

[[research/erdos_132/minkowski_sum_layers|minkowski_sum_layers]]: Support directions control full digit sums; a convex-sumset incidence
theorem also gives polynomial rarity for their dense selected subsets.

[[research/erdos_132/mixed_valuation_selector|mixed_valuation_selector]]: Two equilateral triangles joined by a bridge defeat maximal triadic
valuation followed by maximal length inside a dyadic norm shell.

[[research/erdos_132/monotone_chain_inverse|monotone_chain_inverse]]: Bounded rank errors force a large circular subset of a convex monotone
chain; half-sized chains therefore force rarity growth in the full set.

[[research/erdos_132/motion_incidence|motion_incidence]]: A rich-motion inverse criterion isolates the missing moment step;
ordinary incidences and cubic distance energy alone do not detect rarity.

[[research/erdos_132/moving_frames|moving_frames]]: Bounded rarity forces linearly many congruent rooted triangles and a
superlinear inverse-image union; quantitative short incidences give growing
lattice patches.

[[research/erdos_132/nearly_convex|nearly_convex]]: The first assertion holds with n minus one points strictly convex,
and at even order with n minus two, using the circular extension theorem.

[[research/erdos_132/nonlinear_cluster_limits|nonlinear_cluster_limits]]: Two collapsing analytic clusters of two or three points have at least
their size in cross distances, removing generic-scale exceptions for these
sizes.

[[research/erdos_132/one_rare_asymptotics|one_rare_asymptotics]]: A hypothetical unbounded R=1 family has a two-thirds outer-layer split,
an exact triangular-lattice spectrum at every fixed short scale,
and lattice minimum-distance components.

[[research/erdos_132/order_type_relaxations|order_type_relaxations]]: Affine sweeps and finite-alphabet Monge blocks omit metric rank;
isosceles counting gives a necessary but insufficient surplus for R equal to
one.

[[research/erdos_132/quantitative_circle|quantitative_circle]]: A circle with at least C q^$31/32$ points forces many rare distances;
rarity plus diameter inactivity is at least a power of n.

[[research/erdos_132/quantitative_rank_inverse|quantitative_rank_inverse]]: Two ordered anchors replace van der Waerden in the constant-width
inverse argument, giving an explicit polynomial dependence on the error.

[[research/erdos_132/quantitative_short_incidence|quantitative_short_incidence]]: Counting anchor multiplicities sharpens short incidences to n^(55/56)
up to logarithms and gives polynomial rarity growth for convex covers.

[[research/erdos_132/regular_cores|regular_cores]]: Gives regular-core bounds and an elementary small-difference proof of
the explicit circular bound R at least h minus n/2 minus 10n^(6/7).

[[research/erdos_132/remote_anchors|remote_anchors]]: The short-edge inverse extends to bounded-radius neighborhoods of convex
subsets; a remote grid-and-parabola model defeats geometry-only unpinning.

[[research/erdos_132/rich_degree_profiles|rich_degree_profiles]]: Under bounded rarity, a positive-sized common rich-degree profile forces
a positive circular fraction; no such profile is forced in general.

[[research/erdos_132/second_distance_extremality|second_distance_extremality]]: Analytic even-polygon deformations attain the 3n/2 second-distance
bound without five concyclic points, but their whole small neighborhood
has linearly many rare distances by exact palette interlacing.

[[research/erdos_132/short_distance_structure|short_distance_structure]]: Local spectra constrain convex matchings and zigzags; deleting a large
convex subset gives only a cutoff-dependent residual-rarity bound.

[[research/erdos_132/short_incident_inverse|short_incident_inverse]]: A bin-to-triangle argument proves rarity divergence for convex sets
and fixed convex covers; arbitrary sets retain an interior-support gap.

[[research/erdos_132/side_rank_attempt|side_rank_attempt]]: A possible side-rank bound is proved only for concyclic sets;
regular-polygon subsets disprove its proposed matching shortcut.

[[research/erdos_132/similarity_word_sets|similarity_word_sets]]: A self-similar measure gives zero mass to every convex boundary;
this excludes dense selected similarity-word sets as bounded-R families.

[[research/erdos_132/small_orders|small_orders]]: Classifications and the unique-diameter theorem prove R at least two
for orders 5 through 10 and 12 through 14; order 11 remains unexcluded.

[[research/erdos_132/source_notes/_index|source_notes/]]: Paper summaries and source comparisons used in the research on Problem 132.

[[research/erdos_132/spectral_gaps|spectral_gaps]]: Any occurring r with no distance in (r,2r) is rare, including when 2r
occurs; a seven-point family proves the factor two cannot be lowered.

[[research/erdos_132/split_class_deformations|split_class_deformations]]: The affine rarity bound survives when one quadratic form splits only a few
distance classes; rotation-rich classes defeat every such form.

[[research/erdos_132/star_diameter|star_diameter]]: A star diameter graph always gives R at least two; with at least
three diameter edges the second-largest distance itself is rare.

[[research/erdos_132/three_off_circle|three_off_circle]]: Explicit regular-polygon palette extensions and radius-palette moments
prove the first assertion when n minus three points are concyclic.

[[research/erdos_132/topological_filtrations|topological_filtrations]]: Triangular lattice triangles have arbitrarily many initial rich classes
while their Rips shadows, Rips fundamental groups, and disk-union topology have
already stabilized. This rules out specific topological accounting arguments.

[[research/erdos_132/translated_triangles|translated_triangles]]: The improved three-anchor bound limits translated triangle families to
n^(5/7) and forces n^(2/7) minimum-lattice orientations under R equal to one.

[[research/erdos_132/triangle_diameter|triangle_diameter]]: A diameter triangle forces two rare distances; a bounded-length odd
diameter cycle forces rarity divergence along an unbounded sequence.

[[research/erdos_132/two_cover_diameter|two_cover_diameter]]: Ten diameter edges suffice when two vertices cover the diameter graph;
the matching-two sharp case and even active equality cases are excluded.

[[research/erdos_132/two_off_circle|two_off_circle]]: A small-difference lemma and explicit regular-polygon palette tests
prove the first assertion whenever n minus two points are concyclic.

[[research/erdos_132/unique_diameter_obstruction|unique_diameter_obstruction]]: The sharp n+1 second-distance bound and convex equality classifications
prove the first assertion for every configuration with a unique diameter.

[[research/erdos_132/unrestricted_frontier|unrestricted_frontier]]: States the remaining unrestricted gap, proves a regular-core completion
lemma, and gives an exact obstruction to scalar Fourier positivity.

[[research/erdos_132/variational_reduction|variational_reduction]]: A minimal-distance-class counterexample would have a max-min realization
without collisions or class mergers. The remaining equilibrium-stress
obstruction is the absence of edgewise signs and possible abnormal multipliers.

[[research/erdos_132/weighted_circle_energy|weighted_circle_energy]]: Weighting chord coincidences improves the circular concentration threshold
to q to the seven-eighths power and the inactivity tradeoff to n to the
one-twenty-fourth.

[[research/erdos_132/weighted_diameter|weighted_diameter]]: The exact vertex-weight bound already holds for pseudoforests; every
linear forest is a planar diameter graph, strengthening the hereditary
obstruction.

## Where things stand

**Both unrestricted questions remain unresolved.** No verified $n\ge5$
configuration has $R\le1$, no unbounded bounded-$R$ family is known here,
and no unrestricted proof is established. See the
[frontier and precise gaps](unrestricted_frontier.md).

**Latest checked progress.**

- [Small orders](small_orders.md): the published six-distance classification
  proves $R\ge2$ at orders 13 and 14. The established finite range is
  **5 through 14 except 11**. The
  [rotational report](independent/c3_13_unreviewed.md) is unreviewed and is not
  needed for order 13.
- [Long-rank gaps](long_rank_gaps.md): under $R\le K$, a rich fixed rank
  satisfies $r_{k-1}-r_k=O_{K,k}(\Delta/n^2)$. For $R=1$, this gives
  $\Delta-r_k=O_k(\Delta/n^2)$ at every fixed rank. Every fixed top-rank
  union has odd girth $\Omega_{K,k}(n^{1/8})$; bipartite rich examples
  prevent replacing odd cycles by arbitrary cycles.
- [Immediate-shell surplus](spectral_gaps.md) forces many pairs in
  $(r,2r)$ from core excess, but not many distinct successor lengths.
  [Deletion-criticality examples](deletion_criticality.md) have a critical
  rich minimum graph, nonstar diameter, and even a sublinear palette;
  the other classes are not all rich.

**Retained bounds and scopes.** The first assertion holds with
[n minus three points concyclic](three_off_circle.md), for
[diameter triangles](triangle_diameter.md), [stars](star_diameter.md),
the stated [two-cover cases](two_cover_diameter.md), and the specified
[nearly convex cases](nearly_convex.md). The
[convex-cover estimate](quantitative_short_incidence.md) is
$R+1\gg n^{1/64}/[L^{7/8}(\log(2n))^{1/8}]$.
The [circle threshold](weighted_circle_energy.md) remains $q^{7/8}$;
[linear aspect ratios](layers_and_obstructions.md) force divergence.
Under bounded rarity, polynomial-length progression covers of the squared
palette require [at least $n^{1/10-o(1)}$ progressions](arithmetic_palettes.md).

**Remaining gap.** Rich short classes can lie deep inside, away from
large convex layers. For $R=1$, the
[local triangular-lattice reduction](one_rare_asymptotics.md) and growing
patches do not control their long cross-distances. The moving-frame union
bounds $n^{17/16}/\sqrt{\log n}\ll_K N\ll_K n^{12/7}$ remain compatible.
The new [field-alignment observation](translated_triangles.md) is local,
not a global multiplicity bound. [Invariant valuations](dyadic_distances.md)
provide another extremal rare class but no general iteration. Analytic
collision increments are proved only for sizes two, three, and four;
the [monic-quadratic model](nonlinear_cluster_limits.md) is a precise
unresolved subproblem, not a reduction of arbitrary configurations.
Stronger many-anchor expansion and joint palette control remain
provisional directions only. The
[split-class test](split_class_deformations.md) extends the affine
bound to configurations in which one quadratic form splits few
distance classes. With the author-recorded weighted-circle bound, it
forces every form to split polynomially many classes under bounded
rarity; rotation-rich lattice classes are split
by every form, so this replacement stops short of the rigid case that
a one-rare family must contain.

## Standing of the topical notes

The topical notes are author-recorded. Each records what the author
checked and the exact scope of its argument; the cited source theorems
were read in their full primary papers where the notes say so, and the
published six-distance classification's exhaustive computation was not
rerun. No independent verification record is retained in this folder,
and no note claims a higher standing.

The [third independent report](independent/layer_bound_and_circular_majority.md)
was compared with the indexed research. Its checked elementary
small-difference proof and explicit circular-majority bound are in
[regular cores](regular_cores.md); its disk-core proof is retained as a
weaker alternative in [layers and obstructions](layers_and_obstructions.md).
Finite models and superseded gaps are recorded in the relevant topical
notes and the [review summary](independent/_index.md). Missing companion
files and the unused Ramsey constant were not certified. The rotational
thirteen-point case analysis is retained as unreviewed input. These
additions are partial results, not rigorous answers to the two
unrestricted questions, and both remain unresolved.
