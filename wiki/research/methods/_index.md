# research/methods

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/methods/bounded_countable_coloring|bounded_countable_coloring]]: A domain bridge for the proposed negative model in Problem 598, with the forcing and positive-in-L imports kept explicit.

[[research/methods/central_binomial_product_windows|central_binomial_product_windows]]: A density comparison connects downward products and squared upward

congruence construction and in a density-one specialization.

[[research/methods/chromatic_threshold_interfaces|chromatic_threshold_interfaces]]: Exact path, cochromatic and inverse-extremum reductions, with counterexamples to dropping parity, local cardinality, or anti-concentration hypotheses.

[[research/methods/dense_graph_minimum_degree|dense_graph_minimum_degree]]: Keeps the constant and cardinality change in graph pruning, transferring

[[research/methods/partitioned_vector_energy|partitioned_vector_energy]]: Exact quantifiers, a weighted dual, and scoped obstruction tests for a finite vector-repair route to countable coloring.

[[research/methods/private_progression_discounts/_index|private_progression_discounts/]]: Extracts minimum private progressions from irredundant covers and derives
a discounted reciprocal budget through nonuniform set pairs and digit
averaging.

[[research/methods/shifted_prime_moments|shifted_prime_moments]]: Turns equidistribution of the prime factors of p - 1 into the first two
moments over primes of the additive function behind the totient iteration
count, the route by which its normal order was made unconditional.

[[research/methods/steiner_color_partitions|steiner_color_partitions]]: Exact design divisibility and complement closure for Problem 835, with the gap between one derived design and a coloring of the whole layer.

[[research/methods/subgroup_counting_interfaces|subgroup_counting_interfaces]]: Exact consequences and limitations of the Roney-Dougal–Tracey statements for subgroup counts and order statistics.
