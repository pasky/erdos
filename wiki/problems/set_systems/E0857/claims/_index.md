# problems/set_systems/E0857/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0857/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0857/claims/2016_06_30_naslund_sawin|2016_06_30_naslund_sawin]]: Naslund and Sawin (2017) use the polynomial method to show that a family of
subsets of $[n]$ with no three sets of pairwise equal intersections has at
most $3(n+1)\sum_{i\le n/3}\binom{n}{i}$ members. Hence
$m(n,3)\le(3/2^{2/3})^{(1+o(1))n}$.
