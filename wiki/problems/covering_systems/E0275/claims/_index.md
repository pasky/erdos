# problems/covering_systems/E0275/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0275/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0275/claims/1970_03_01_crittenden_vanden_eynden|1970_03_01_crittenden_vanden_eynden]]: Crittenden and Vanden Eynden proved in Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. (1970) that $r$
congruences covering $2^r$ consecutive integers cover every integer, a
conjecture of Erdős that Selfridge also proved. It is accepted as a refereed
paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0275/claims/2019_09_05_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2019_09_05_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: The four-page 2020 Acta Math. Hungar. paper of Balister, Bollobás, Morris,
Sahasrabudhe and Tiba gives a short proof that $r$ congruences covering $2^r$
consecutive integers cover every integer. It is accepted on the refereed
paper.
