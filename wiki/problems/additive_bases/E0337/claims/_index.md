# problems/additive_bases/E0337/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0337/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0337/claims/1984_01_01_turjanyi|1984_01_01_turjanyi]]: Turjányi's 1984 note builds, for every $k\ge4$, an additive basis $A$ of order
$k$ with counting function $o(N)$ such that, along a subsequence, the counting
function of $A+A$ stays within a constant factor of that of $A$. This gives a
negative answer.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0337/claims/1985_01_01_ruzsa_turjanyi|1985_01_01_ruzsa_turjanyi]]: Theorem 1 of Ruzsa and Turjányi (1985) gives, for every $h\ge3$, a basis of
order $h$ with counting function $o(N)$ whose $(h-1)$-fold sumset stays within
a constant factor of it along a subsequence. The case $h=3$ answers the
question no, and it is formalized in Lean.
