# problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/claims/1987_01_01_danzer|1987_01_01_danzer]]: At the 1985 Siófok meeting, Danzer found a finite family of pairwise disjoint
unit segments in the unit square to which no further unit segment can be
added, and Erdős reported it. This answers the first question yes.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/claims/2026_01_25_alexeev|2026_01_25_alexeev]]: A countably infinite family of pairwise disjoint unit segments in the unit
square, to which no further unit segment can be added, answers the second
question yes. Credited to Boris Alexeev, it was posted in the site's comments
and proved in Lean.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/claims/2026_02_13_alexeev|2026_02_13_alexeev]]: A Lean proof, formalized by Aristotle and posted by Boris Alexeev, shows that
five unit segments and the four sides form a finite maximal disjoint family in
the unit square. This is Erdős's second 1987 example, and the proof was built
and audited here.
