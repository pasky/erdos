***

**Claim.** Let $S_4$ be the set of natural numbers expressible as sums of at
most four $4$-powerful numbers: every prime divisor $p$ of a summand divides
it to at least the fourth power. There are a positive integer $C$ and an
integer $N_0$ such that, for every $N\ge N_0$,

$$
N\le C\,\#\{n<N:n\notin S_4\}.
$$

Consequently $\underline d(\mathbb N\setminus S_4)>0$, and infinitely many
integers are not sums of at most four $4$-powerful numbers. Allowing zero
summands does not change the conclusion, since they can be removed.

**Covers.** The first question of
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0940/_index|Problem 940]] at $r=4$ only.
It does not settle whether the representable integers have density zero,
at this or any other exponent, or the infinitude question for $r\ge5$.

**Argument and formalization.** The
[[research/erdos_940/quartic_complement|retained prose argument]] gives an
author-recorded proof of a stronger small-relative-density statement on
suitable progressions and deduces the complement bound. The separate Lean
project [plasma-ai/erdos-940](https://github.com/plasma-ai/erdos-940) supplies
`Erdos940.quartic_complement_density` and
`Erdos940.quartic_large_integers`; its exact version, statement conventions,
authorship and checking status are recorded on the
[[../library/diophantine_problems/plasma_ai_2026_powerful_sum_complements/_index|formalization source card]].
The Lean development is maintained in that separate repository.

**Attribution.** The quartic result is Plasma AI's. Jacob Parish is the
formalization's author and maintainer named by the source. The cubic
($r=3$) result formalized in the same project is Beyer de Ryke's and belongs
to
[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0940/claims/2026_07_26_beyer_de_ryke|his separate claim]].
The quartic argument follows the cubic plan with new reciprocity,
coefficient-uniform Euler-product, and prime-selection ingredients. The
formalization's metadata records that GPT-6 Sol, in Codex, produced the Lean
proofs of both cases under human direction, and that Claude Opus 5.5, in Claude
Code, ported them to Lean 4.35.0-rc4.
