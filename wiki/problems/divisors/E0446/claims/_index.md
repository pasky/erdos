# problems/divisors/E0446/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0446/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0446/claims/2004_01_18_ford|2004_01_18_ford]]: Ford determines the order of the density of integers with a divisor in
$(n,2n)$, which is $1/\bigl((\log n)^{\alpha}(\log\log n)^{3/2}\bigr)$. He
also proves that the integers with exactly one such divisor keep a positive
proportion of that density.
