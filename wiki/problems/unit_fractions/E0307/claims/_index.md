# problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims/2012_03_25_kovic|2012_03_25_kovic]]: Kovič (J. Integer Seq. 2012) proves necessary conditions on a two-cycle of the
arithmetic derivative, which a solution of Problem 307 gives. The two members
are not both semiprimes, both are at least $10^4$, and an odd smaller member
has at least nine prime factors.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/claims/2026_06_13_bonfioli|2026_06_13_bonfioli]]: An unrefereed, AI-assisted manuscript by Vico Bonfioli claims that two sets of
primes whose reciprocal sums multiply to one have at least 60 primes in all
and products above $3.5\times10^{57}$. It was first posted on 13 June 2026,
and the 60-prime bound was added on 2 July 2026.
