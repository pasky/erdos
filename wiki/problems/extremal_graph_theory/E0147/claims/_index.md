# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/claims/2020_06_01_janzer|2020_06_01_janzer]]: In Israel J. Math. 253 (2023), Janzer bounds the Turán number of the
$r$-blow-up of a long even cycle, a bipartite graph of minimum degree $2r$,
below the problem's exponent. erdosproblems.com credits this with the disproof
for even minimum degree at least $4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/claims/2021_09_13_janzer|2021_09_13_janzer]]: Janzer's Theorem 1.4 gives, for every $\eta>0$, a 3-regular bipartite graph
with extremal number $O(n^{4/3+\eta})$, below the problem's exponent at
minimum degree three. The refereed version is in IMRN 2023.
