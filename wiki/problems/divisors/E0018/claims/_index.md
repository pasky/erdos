# problems/divisors/E0018/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0018/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0018/claims/2026_07_24_price|2026_07_24_price]]: Liam Price posted a proof claim, produced with GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, that
infinitely many practical $n$ have $h(n)\ll(\log\log n)^2$, answering the
first question. The write-up is an unaccepted Overleaf draft.

[[problems/divisors/E0018/claims/2026_09_16_jenw1n|2026_09_16_jenw1n]]: A kernel-checked Lean proof, certified by the bounty site Conjectures.io,
shows that for every $\varepsilon>0$ one has $h(n!)<n^{\varepsilon}$ for all
large $n$. This answers the second of the problem's three questions.

[[problems/divisors/E0018/claims/2026_09_16_van_doorn|2026_09_16_van_doorn]]: Wouter van Doorn and GPT-6 Astra Pro claim, by an elementary extension
construction with an author-side Lean formalization, that infinitely many
practical $n$ have $h(n)\le c_0(\log\log n)^2$ with $c_0=14/\log2$. This
answer to the first question is not accepted.
