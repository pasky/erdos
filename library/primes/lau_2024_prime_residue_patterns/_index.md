## Scope

The main theorem concerns a squarefree modulus and a family of residue patterns
constructed from an ordered list of reduced residue classes. Its index maps are
nondecreasing and have a specified bound on repetitions. It proves that the
resulting family is attained infinitely often by consecutive primes. The
conclusion does not assert that every prescribed member is attained.

The introduction also gives quantitative lower bounds on the number of
attainable patterns. Their parameter restrictions must be retained: for example,
the simplified large-range corollary assumes
$\varphi(q)>8e^2m\log m$ and sufficiently large $m$.
