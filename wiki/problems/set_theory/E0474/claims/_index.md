# problems/set_theory/E0474/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/1965_03_01_erdos_hajnal_rado|1965_03_01_erdos_hajnal_rado]]: Theorem 17 of Erdős, Hajnal and Rado (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar., 1965)
gives, under CH, the three-coloring of pairs of reals that Problem 474 asks
for. Gödel showed CH consistent with ZFC, so ZFC does not refute that the
coloring exists.

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/1988_10_01_shelah|1988_10_01_shelah]]: Shelah (Israel J. Math., 1988) proved, from a large cardinal, that it is
consistent that every three-coloring of pairs of reals has an uncountable set
missing a color. So the coloring Erdős asked for is not provable in ZFC.

[[problems/set_theory/E0474/claims/2026_01_06_shelah|2026_01_06_shelah]]: Shelah (arXiv, 2026) claims the consistency of positive square-bracket
relations below a small continuum, with no large cardinal. It is read in the
problem's thread as settling Problem 474 without large cardinal strength.
