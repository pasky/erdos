# problems/polynomials/E0975/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0975/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0975/claims/1963_01_01_hooley|1963_01_01_hooley]]: Hooley's Theorem 2 of 1963 shows that for a nonzero integer $a$ with $-a$ not
a square, $\sum_{n\le x}d(n^2+a)=A_2(a)x\log x+A_3(a)x+O(x^{8/9}\log^3x)$. It
gives the asymptotic of Problem 975 for the quadratics $x^2+a$ and is
refereed.

[[problems/polynomials/E0975/claims/1995_05_01_mckee|1995_05_01_mckee]]: McKee's 1995 theorem shows that for $x^2+bx+c$ with discriminant $b^2-4c<0$,
$\sum_{n\le x}d(n^2+bn+c)=\lambda x\log x+O(x)$, with $\lambda$ given by a
Hurwitz class number. It gives Problem 975 for these quadratics and is
refereed.

[[problems/polynomials/E0975/claims/1997_01_30_mckee|1997_01_30_mckee]]: McKee's 1997 note, in a proceedings volume, states that for $an^2+bn+c$ with
$b^2-4ac<0$ and $\gcd(a,b^2-4ac)=1$,
$\sum_{n\le x}d(an^2+bn+c)=Cx\log x+O(x)$. This is Problem 975 for these
quadratics.

[[problems/polynomials/E0975/claims/1999_01_01_mckee|1999_01_01_mckee]]: McKee's 1999 theorem shows that for $x^2+bx+c$ with $b^2-4c$ positive and not
a square, $\sum_{n\le x}d(n^2+bn+c)=\lambda x\log x+O(x)$, with $\lambda$
given by a weighted class number. It gives Problem 975 for these quadratics
and is refereed.
