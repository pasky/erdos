# problems/ramsey_theory/E0800/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0800/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0800/claims/1994_07_01_alon|1994_07_01_alon]]: In a refereed paper in J. Graph Theory 18 (1994), Alon proves that a graph on
$n$ vertices with no two adjacent vertices of degree at least three has
two-color Ramsey number at most $12n$. This answers Problem 800 yes, and the
site credits it.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0800/claims/1997_06_01_li_rousseau_soltes|1997_06_01_li_rousseau_soltes]]: In a refereed 1997 paper, Li, Rousseau and Šoltés prove that a graph on $n$
vertices whose vertices of degree at least three are independent has two-color
Ramsey number at most $6n$. This answers Problem 800 yes, and only the paper's
abstract has been read here.
