# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/claims/2025_03_25_chen_ma|2025_03_25_chen_ma]]: Chen and Ma (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2026) prove that for $n\ge600$,
$K_{n,n+1}$ is the only graph with $2n+1$ vertices and at least $n^2+n$ edges
in which no two vertices of equal degree are joined by a path of three edges.
This is a refereed partial result.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/claims/2025_05_01_liu_zeng|2025_05_01_liu_zeng]]: A preprint of Liu and Zeng states that for every $n\ge2$, $K_{n,n+1}$ is the
only graph with $2n+1$ vertices and at least $n^2+n$ edges in which no two
vertices of equal degree are joined by a path of three edges. The preprint is
unrefereed.
