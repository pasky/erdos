# Bugeaud and Laurent: values of Hecke–Mahler series

[[irrationality/_index|..]]

***

Y. Bugeaud and M. Laurent, *Transcendence and continued fraction expansion of
values of Hecke-Mahler series*,
[arXiv:2203.12901v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12901v1), submitted 24 March
2022, 29 pages.

Theorem 1.1 and the defining series were inspected in the arXiv version.
For irrational $0<\theta<1$, $0\le\rho<1$, and nonzero algebraic
$\alpha,\beta$ with $|\beta|<1$ and
$|\beta\alpha^\theta|<1$, it gives transcendence of the
Hecke–Mahler value $F_{\theta,\rho}(\beta,\alpha)$.
The source proof has not been audited here.

The arXiv record, retrieved 2026-10-14, specifies
[arXiv's non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/),
with other rights reserved. No file of this source is held. No full
transcription is provided under these terms.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/irrationality/E0269/_index|Problem 269]]:
for distinct primes $p<q$, the choices
$\theta=\log p/\log q$, $\rho=0$, $\beta=1/p$ and $\alpha=1/q$
meet these hypotheses. The
[[../wiki/research/smooth_lcm_series/_index|two-prime running-lcm deduction]]
expresses each factor as a rational affine function of the Hecke–Mahler
value and the full sum as a nonconstant rational quadratic in that value.
It retains the earlier Fan attribution and has author-recorded standing;
no conclusion for three or more primes follows.
