# problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_01_17_chojecki|2026_01_17_chojecki]]: Chojecki's claimed proof of 17 January 2026 used a dyadic deletion scheme with
the algorithmic local lemma. It was rewritten on 19 January after commenters
found errors that the author acknowledged, and was replaced by their different
proof of July 2026.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_06_28_sharma|2026_06_28_sharma]]: Sharma's greedy-scan proof was first posted to GitHub on 28 June 2026 and
claimed on the site on 26 July, with a Lean file that takes three literature
results as axioms. It was made with Claude, reviewed by nobody and not built
here.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_07_10_kielhorn|2026_07_10_kielhorn]]: Kielhorn's Zenodo preprint of 10 July 2026 claims a density-one set with
distinct consecutive products, built by a sparse deletion scheme on prime
gaps. The curator linked it from the thread, and it has not been read in
detail or reviewed by anyone.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_07_13_chojecki|2026_07_13_chojecki]]: Chojecki's gap-greedy construction over consecutive primes gives a set of
density one whose consecutive-block products are all distinct, so the answer
is yes. The AI-assisted proof of 13 July 2026 was accepted by the site after
Pratt's digest.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_07_13_pauwels|2026_07_13_pauwels]]: Pauwels's proof attempt, dated 10 July 2026 and linked from the thread by the
site's curator on 13 July, claims the density-one set by a construction that
Chojecki found similar to their own. It was made with GPT-5.6 Sol and reviewed
by nobody.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0421/claims/2026_07_21_sneiderman|2026_07_21_sneiderman]]: Sneiderman's note of 21 July 2026 reproves Chojecki's density-one
construction, with short rejected gaps bounded by $X^{43/50+o(1)}$. It was
made with GPT 5.6 Sol Ultra, posted as a site proof claim and followed by
Pratt's digest.
