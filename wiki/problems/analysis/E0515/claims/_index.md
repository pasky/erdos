# problems/analysis/E0515/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0515/claims/1977_01_01_zhang|1977_01_01_zhang]]: Zhang proves that every non-polynomial entire function of finite order has a
path to infinity on which every negative power of its modulus is integrable.
The paper is refereed in Sci. Sinica, and erdosproblems.com credits it for
that case.

[[problems/analysis/E0515/claims/1984_12_01_lewis_rossi_weitsman|1984_12_01_lewis_rossi_weitsman]]: Lewis, Rossi and Weitsman prove that every nonpolynomial entire function has a
locally rectifiable path to infinity along which every negative power of its
modulus is integrable. The paper is refereed in Ark. Mat. and credited by
erdosproblems.com.
