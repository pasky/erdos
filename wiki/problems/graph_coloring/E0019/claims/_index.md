# problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims/1981_06_01_hindman|1981_06_01_hindman]]: Hindman (Canad. J. Math. 1981) shows, with a computer check, that an
edge-disjoint union of $n$ copies of $K_n$ has chromatic number $n$ for every
$n\le10$. erdosproblems.com gives the range as $n<10$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims/2007_01_01_romero_sanchez_arroyo|2007_01_01_romero_sanchez_arroyo]]: Romero and Sánchez-Arroyo prove the conjecture for intersecting linear
hypergraphs whose edges can be numbered so that the labels at each vertex
form at most two runs of consecutive integers (Ars Combin. 2007).

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims/2016_05_11_araujo_pardo_vazquez_avila|2016_05_11_araujo_pardo_vazquez_avila]]: Araujo-Pardo and Vázquez-Ávila (Ars Combin. 2016) prove the conjecture for the
arithmetic decompositions of $K_n$ with different central vertices, an
infinite class of $n$-quasiclusters.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims/2020_10_12_alesandroni|2020_10_12_alesandroni]]: Alesandroni (Discrete Math. 2021) proves that a linear $n$-uniform hypergraph
with $n$ edges has chromatic number $n$ when, for each $k$ with
$2\le k<\sqrt n$, at most $k^2$ of its vertices have degree $k$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0019/claims/2021_01_12_kang_kelly_kuhn_methuku_osthus|2021_01_12_kang_kelly_kuhn_methuku_osthus]]: Kang, Kelly, Kühn, Methuku and Osthus (Ann. of Math. 2023) prove that for
every sufficiently large $n$, an edge-disjoint union of $n$ copies of $K_n$
has chromatic number $n$. The threshold is not computed, and erdosproblems.com
reads the remaining finite range of $n$ as decidable.
