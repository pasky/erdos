# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/claims/1994_09_01_erdos_hajnal_simonovits_sos_szemeredi|1994_09_01_erdos_hajnal_simonovits_sos_szemeredi]]: The upper bound $\delta_3(5)\le1/12$ of Erdős, Hajnal, Simonovits, Sós and
Szemerédi proves the statement for every $\delta>1/12$. It was refereed in
Combin. Probab. Comput. 3 (1994).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/claims/2011_09_20_balogh_lenz|2011_09_20_balogh_lenz]]: Theorem 3 of Balogh and Lenz gives $\theta_3(K_5)\ge1/64$, so $K_5$-free
graphs with sublinear triangle-free sets can have quadratically many edges. It
was refereed in Israel J. Math. 194 (2013) and is credited by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/claims/2021_03_18_liu_reiher_sharifzadeh_staden|2021_03_18_liu_reiher_sharifzadeh_staden]]: Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 of Liu, Reiher, Sharifzadeh and Staden give
$K_5$-free graphs with sublinear triangle-free sets and $(1/12-o(1))n^2$
edges, so $\delta_3(5)=1/12$. It was refereed in J. Eur. Math. Soc. 28 (2025).
