# problems/divisors/E1054/claims

[[problems/divisors/E1054/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E1054/claims/2026_05_11_kovac|2026_05_11_kovac]]: Kovač's note claims a bound of $\exp(-e^{(1/\delta)^c})$, uniform in $X$, on
the proportion of $N\le X$ with $f(N)\le\delta N$, so that $f(N)=o(N)$ fails,
even along every set of density one.

[[problems/divisors/E1054/claims/2026_06_22_principia_math|2026_06_22_principia_math]]: Principia Math's write-up, posted with a Lean formalization, claims that for
every $A\ge1$ the represented $N$ with $f(N)>AN$ have positive lower density,
so that $\limsup f(N)/N=\infty$.

[[problems/divisors/E1054/claims/2026_10_03_chae_fraiture_hou_kovac_kudeba_shakov_vidal|2026_10_03_chae_fraiture_hou_kovac_kudeba_shakov_vidal]]: A seven-author manuscript claims that $f(N)$ is defined for every $N\ne2,5$,
that $f(N)=o(N)$ fails in a strong sense, even for almost all $N$, and that
$\limsup f(N)/N=\infty$. It comes with Lean proofs of 33 of its 37 results.

[[problems/divisors/E1054/claims/2026_10_05_xu|2026_10_05_xu]]: Linmiao Xu's Lean development, registered in the Palomar registry, claims to
prove that $f(n)$ is not $o(n)$, is not $o(n)$ on any set of density one, and
has $\limsup f(n)/n=\infty$ along every set of density one.
