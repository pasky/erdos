***

**Claim.** The optimal constant $c$ of
[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/_index|Problem 36]], the minimum overlap
constant, which the claim calls $\mu$, satisfies

$$
0.380627425<c<0.3808585738345707361812673.
$$

The claim was submitted to erdosproblems.ai on 2026-10-09 by Justin Wetch as a
partial result, with its proof packet and verification code in the release
linked above. Both bounds are computer-assisted and use White's continuous
formulation: for $0\le h\le1$ on $[0,2]$ with $\int h=1$ and $g=1-h$, both
extended by zero, put $p(t)=\int h(x)g(x+t)\,dx$; then
$c=\inf_h\lVert p\rVert_\infty$. For the lower bound, kernel inequalities
$\int Kp\ge L(m)$, where $m=\int tp(t)\,dt\in[-1,1]$, give
$\lVert p\rVert_\infty\ge L(m)/D$ for certified $D\ge\int K_+$; a case split of
$m$ into central, transition and outer intervals, with a separate argument near
$m=0$, covers $[-1,1]$, and the certificates were replayed with Arb and with
separately implemented rational and dyadic arithmetic. For the upper bound, an
explicit rational step density on 14,337 cells has exact overlap peak
$Q=10919977028984812147789295189227209/28672000000000000000000000000000000$,
checked at all 86,017 shift knots by two exact methods, and a rounding and
balancing argument carries the density back to partitions of
$\{1,\ldots,2N\}$ with an $o(N)$ error. The method is taken from the claim and
the release's README. The lower bound lies above the refereed $0.379005$ of
White ([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2022_01_14_white|claim page]])
and above the claimed $0.37912$ of Kim and Pilanci
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_06_30_kim_pilanci|claim page]]),
$0.38055470$ of Price
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_07_20_price|claim page]])
and $0.3805634$ of Drynshock
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_09_19_drynshock|claim page]]).
The upper bound lies below the claimed $0.380876$ of TTT-Discover, the
erdosproblems.com record
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_01_22_yuksekgonul_et_al|claim page]]),
and $0.38085906$ of Russell
([[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/claims/2026_07_12_russell|claim page]]).

> Let $M_N$ be the minimum, over partitions $A\sqcup B=\{1,\ldots,2N\}$ with $|A|=|B|=N$, of $\max_k|\{(a,b)\in A\times B:a-b=k\}|$, and let $\mu=\lim_{N\to\infty}M_N/N$.
>
> Use the equivalent continuous formulation: $0\le h\le1$ on $[0,2]$, $\int h=1$, and $g=1-h$ on that interval, with both functions extended by zero. Put $p(t)=\int h(x)g(x+t)\,dx$. Then $\mu=\inf_h\|p\|_\infty$.
>
> Lower bound. Fourier and phase-averaged Gram inequalities give universal kernel inequalities $\int Kp\ge L(m)$, where $m=\int tp(t)\,dt\in[-1,1]$. Writing $K_+(t)=\max\{K(t),0\}$, certified upper bounds $D\ge\int K_+$ with $D>0$ give $\|p\|_\infty\ge L(m)/D$ on the covered cases, where $L(m)>0$. The transition cases strengthen this return using the same physical odd profile $d(t)=p(t)-p(-t)$: its boundary value, reflection identity, 1-Lipschitz bound and moment identity yield rational flow certificates with every residual and covariance error charged explicitly.
>
> The central case covers $|m|\le0.00004$ by a separate exact radius deduction from executions at $|m|\le0.00007$. Central, transition and outer intervals cover all of $[-1,1]$. Their finite collection of strict margins gives a uniform bound $\mu>0.380627425$.
>
> The lower checkpoint passed a complete fresh-copy replay with Arb and separately implemented rational/dyadic arithmetic, exact coverage auditing, and three control suites. Its 115 frozen input files remained unchanged. Numerical optimizer output is used only to propose certificates.
>
> Upper bound. An admissible rational step density on 14,337 nonuniform cells has exact overlap peak
> $$
> Q=\frac{10919977028984812147789295189227209}{28672000000000000000000000000000000}.
> $$
> Two exact arithmetic methods verified its mass, box constraints and all 86,017 shift knots. The overlap is affine between endpoint-difference knots, so these checks cover every real shift. For the return to discrete partitions, hold this finite step density fixed, independently round its sampled values, and balance the two parts. Bounded-differences estimates control all shifts simultaneously, and the rounding and balancing errors are $o(N)$. Dividing by $N$ and taking the limit gives
> $$
> \mu\le Q<0.3808585738345707361812673.
> $$
>
