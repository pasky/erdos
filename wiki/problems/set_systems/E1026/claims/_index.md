# problems/set_systems/E1026/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E1026/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E1026/claims/2016_08_14_tidor_wang_yang|2016_08_14_tidor_wang_yang]]: Tidor, Wang and Yang (2016) prove that distinct reals $x_i$ have a monotone
subsequence with sum at least $(\sum_i\max(x_i,0)^2)^{1/2}$, which gives the
lower bound $c\ge1$. The site's curator credits this as the first proof.

[[problems/set_systems/E1026/claims/2025_12_07_alexeev|2025_12_07_alexeev]]: In December 2025 Boris Alexeev posted a Lean proof, generated by Aristotle, of
the $k^2$ statement and then of the exact least ratio $c(n)$ for every $n$, so
$c=1$. The curator credits it, and the corpus has not built the proof.
