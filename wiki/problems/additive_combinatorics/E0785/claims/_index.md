# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/1994_09_01_sarkozy_szemeredi|1994_09_01_sarkozy_szemeredi]]: Sárközy and Szemerédi (Acta Math. Hungar. 1994) prove that infinite additive
complements with $A(x)B(x)\sim x$ have $A(x)B(x)-x\to\infty$, and that this
excess is not even $o(A(x))$. The claim is accepted, refereed and credited by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/2010_02_05_fang_chen|2010_02_05_fang_chen]]: Fang and Chen (Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 2010) prove that infinite $A,B$ with
$A+B$ covering all large integers and $\limsup A(x)B(x)/x<5/4$ have
$A(x)B(x)-x\to\infty$. The claim is accepted, refereed and credited by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/2014_08_01_fang_chen|2014_08_01_fang_chen]]: Fang and Chen (J. Number Theory 2014) prove that infinite $A,B$ with $A+B$
covering all large integers and $\limsup A(x)B(x)/x<3-\sqrt3$ have
$A(x)B(x)-x\to\infty$. The claim is accepted, refereed and credited by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/2015_01_01_chen_fang|2015_01_01_chen_fang]]: Theorem 0.2 of Chen and Fang (Acta Arith. 2015) shows that infinite additive
complements with $\limsup A(x)B(x)/x\le1$ have
$A(x)B(x)-x\ge\min(A(x),B(x))^M$ for every $M>1$. The claim is accepted,
refereed and credited by erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/2015_10_03_ruzsa|2015_10_03_ruzsa]]: Ruzsa (Q. J. Math. 2017) proves $A(x)B(x)-x>(1-o(1))\,a^*(x)/A(x)$ for exact
additive complements, which reproves the problem. The claim is accepted,
refereed and credited by erdosproblems.com, and van Doorn's 2026 Lean proof is
linked, not built.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/claims/2026_08_05_van_doorn_liu_tang|2026_08_05_van_doorn_liu_tang]]: A proof claim of 2026-08-05 by van Doorn, Liu and Tang asserts Chen's
conjecture that infinite additive complements with $\limsup A(x)B(x)/x<3/2$
have $A(x)B(x)-x\to\infty$. The note is by GPT-5.6 Sol and the Lean proof by
Aristotle, and no review is recorded.
