# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0491/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0491/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0491/claims/1945_02_24_erdos|1945_02_24_erdos]]: Erdős proves that an additive function that is nondecreasing, or whose
consecutive differences tend to zero, is exactly a constant multiple of
$\log n$. The refereed paper appeared in the Annals of Mathematics.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0491/claims/1970_01_01_wirsing|1970_01_01_wirsing]]: Wirsing proves that an additive function whose consecutive differences are
bounded equals a constant multiple of $\log n$ up to a bounded error,
answering the question yes. The paper appeared in Symposia Mathematica and is
recorded as the proof.
