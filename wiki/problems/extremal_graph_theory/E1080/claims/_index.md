# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/claims/1992_01_01_de_caen_szekely|1992_01_01_de_caen_szekely]]: De Caen and Székely prove
$n^{10/9}\gg f(n,\lfloor n^{2/3}\rfloor)\gg n^{58/57+o(1)}$ for $C_4$- and
$C_6$-free bipartite graphs, so the answer to Problem 1080 is no. The
erdosproblems.com curator credits it, the chapter is not held here, and a Lean
file is linked.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/claims/1994_05_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar|1994_05_01_lazebnik_ustimenko_woldar]]: Lazebnik, Ustimenko and Woldar construct $C_4$- and $C_6$-free bipartite
graphs with parts of about $n^{2/3}$ and $n$ vertices and $n^{16/15+o(1)}$
edges, which alone answers Problem 1080 in the negative. It is a refereed
paper in JCTB 61 (1994), not held here.
