# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims/1975_01_01_stewart|1975_01_01_stewart]]: Stewart's 1975 Acta Arithmetica paper proves that $P(a^n-b^n)/n\to\infty$ as
$n$ runs through the integers with at most $\kappa\log\log n$ distinct prime
factors, for each fixed $\kappa$ with $0<\kappa<1/\log2$. The paper is
refereed.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0977/claims/2010_08_06_stewart|2010_08_06_stewart]]: Stewart's 2013 Acta Mathematica theorem bounds the greatest prime factor of
$a^n-b^n$ below by $n$ times a factor that tends to infinity, so
$P(2^n-1)/n\to\infty$. The paper is refereed, and the site accepts the result.
