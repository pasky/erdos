# problems/additive_bases/E0530/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0530/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0530/claims/1975_01_01_komlos_sulyok_szemeredi|1975_01_01_komlos_sulyok_szemeredi]]: In a refereed result credited by erdosproblems.com, Komlós, Sulyok and
Szemerédi (1975) show that every set of $N$ integers has a Sidon subset of
size at least $cN^{1/2}$. This is the lower half of the order question, and
with the trivial upper bound it fixes the order of $\ell(N)$ at $N^{1/2}$.
