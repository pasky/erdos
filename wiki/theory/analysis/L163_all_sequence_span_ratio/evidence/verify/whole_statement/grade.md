## Scope, restatement and reading depth

The target is the following statement. There is an absolute $r_0$ such that
for every integer $r\ge r_0$ and every infinite sequence $X$ on
$\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, allowing repeated terms,

$$
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{M_n^{(r)}}{m_n^{(r)}}
\ge 1+\frac{\log r}{200r}.
$$

For $n\ge r$, sort the first $n$ labels cyclically, breaking position ties
by arrival index, and use lifts $y_{i+n}=y_i+1$. The minimum and maximum are
over all $n$ spans $y_{i+r}-y_i$. These are clockwise lifted spans, not
shortest circle distances. Counts retain multiplicity; arcs are half-open
$(x,x+\ell]$ with $\ell<1$. Logarithms are natural unless a base is
written. The spans sum to $r$, so $M_n^{(r)}>0$, and the prescribed ratio
when $m_n^{(r)}=0$ is $+\infty$.

Here $\mu_r$ is the infimum over all such sequences of their extended-real
limsup ratios. The assigned lower bound and divergence for $r(\mu_r-1)$
follow with the same absolute $r_0$. The report preserves every one of these
quantifiers and conventions. It does not claim the assertion for every small
$r$, a uniform starting time over all sequences, an attained infimum, an
optimal coefficient, or an exact value of $\mu_r$.

**First vulnerable derivation.** The report's label-map proof was checked
against both injection directions, including cyclic wraparound and coincident
endpoints. With $q=E/(kr)<1$, the unnormalized bounds have exactly the
factors $(D+3kA)(1+q)$ and $[D(1-q)-kA(3-q)]_+$. Retaining these forms
prevents a hidden threshold as $D$ tends to zero. In the scale calculation,
$\theta=\sqrt{A/r}$, $\varepsilon=\theta\log(r/A)$ and a finite number
of positive time factors give $U_t(\sqrt{Ar})\le1+60\varepsilon$ and
$V_t(\sqrt{Ar})\ge1-60\varepsilon$. The descent gives the stated
$3A+400A/\log(r/A)$ bound. The constants and the terminal inequalities
were checked, not accepted from their labels.

**Second vulnerable derivation.** For the extracted list, the exact prefix
error is $(1-u)f(u)-ug(u)$, with both errors bounded by $B$. The total
absolute coefficient is one. In FP, the differences of late and early extrema
have decreasing affine pieces, slopes of magnitude less than $4m$, and jumps

$$
\int |f|(-f')=\sum_v\int_{f(v)}^{f(v)+h(v)}|s|\,ds
\ge\tfrac14\sum_v h(v)^2\ge m/2.
$$

The same holds for the minimum difference. Thus each contributes at least
$1/8$ in integral, yielding the $+1/8$ range recurrence and then the full
$\lfloor\log_4L\rfloor/16$ lower bound. The initial right jump at zero,
active-index changes, repeated entries and the non-power-of-four case are
explicitly handled. The lower bound is proved internally, without assuming a
literature theorem at a stronger constant.
