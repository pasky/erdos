# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/claims/2001_07_01_haxell|2001_07_01_haxell]]: Haxell (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2001) proves the independent-transversal bound
that settles the 1975 conjecture of Bollobás, Erdős and Szemerédi. The paper
is refereed, and the erdosproblems.com curator names it as the proof.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/claims/2006_01_01_haxell_szabo|2006_01_01_haxell_szabo]]: Haxell and Szabó (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2006) determine the sharp
minimum-degree threshold for a $K_r$ in an $r$-partite graph, which lies below
$(r-\frac32)n$ for every $r$ and $n$. The paper is refereed and named by
erdosproblems.com.
