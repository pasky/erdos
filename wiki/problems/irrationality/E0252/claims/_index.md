# problems/irrationality/E0252/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1952_06_01_erdos|1952_06_01_erdos]]: Erdős's Monthly Problem 4493 of 1952, solved in print by J. B. Kelly in 1953,
states that $\sum_n\sigma(n)/n!$ is irrational. This is the case $k=1$.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1953_01_01_erdos_kac|1953_01_01_erdos_kac]]: Erdős and Kac's Monthly Problem 4518 of 1953, solved in print by R. Breusch in
1954, states that $\sum_n\sigma_2(n)/n!$ is irrational, the case $k=2$. Every
later paper on the series cites this case as settled there.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/1971_03_01_erdos_straus|1971_03_01_erdos_straus]]: Erdős and Straus's general theorems on series over the products
$a_1\cdots a_n$ (1971, Theorem 2.26; 1974, Theorem 3.7) give, with $a_n=n$,
that $\sum_n\sigma(n)/n!$ is irrational. This is the case $k=1$.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2006_12_01_schlage_puchta|2006_12_01_schlage_puchta]]: Schlage-Puchta's 2006 paper, refereed in Ramanujan J., proves by a sieve count
and exponential-sum estimates that $\sum_n\sigma_3(n)/n!$ is irrational, the
case $k=3$. The proof is independent of Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu's.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2006_12_01_schlage_puchta_conditional|2006_12_01_schlage_puchta_conditional]]: Schlage-Puchta's 2006 paper proves that Schinzel's Hypothesis H implies that
$\sum_n\sigma_k(n)/n!$ is irrational for every $k$. The hypothesis is
unproven, so the page derives nothing for the problem's standing.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu|2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu]]: Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu's 2007 paper, refereed in Integers, proves by a
Chen-type sieve theorem that $\sum_n\sigma_3(n)/n!$ is irrational, the case
$k=3$. The proof is independent of Schlage-Puchta's.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu_conditional|2007_07_03_friedlander_luca_stoiciu_conditional]]: Friedlander, Luca and Stoiciu's 2007 paper proves that the prime $k$-tuples
conjecture in Dickson's form implies that $\sum_n\sigma_k(n)/n!$ is irrational
for every $k$. The hypothesis is unproven.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2022_09_22_pratt|2022_09_22_pratt]]: Pratt's paper, posted in 2022 and published in Acta Arithmetica in 2023,
proves by sieve methods and exponential sums that $\sum_n\sigma_4(n)/n!$ is
irrational, the case $k=4$.

[[problems/irrationality/E0252/claims/2026_09_08_tokengrinder|2026_09_08_tokengrinder]]: A kernel-checked Lean 4 development, published anonymously in September 2026
under the name Tokengrinder, proves that $\sum_n\sigma_k(n)/n!$ is irrational
for every natural $k$. It was rebuilt and its statement audited here.
