# problems/set_theory/E1171/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/1971_09_01_erdos_hajnal|1971_09_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Erdős and Hajnal (Period. Math. Hungar., 1971) proved in ZFC that
$\omega_1^2\to(\omega_1\omega,3)^2$, the case $k=1$ of Problem 1171.

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/1987_01_01_baumgartner_hajnal|1987_01_01_baumgartner_hajnal]]: Baumgartner and Hajnal (Contemp. Math. 65, 1987) prove that
$(\kappa^+)^2\to(\kappa^+\kappa,3,3)^2$ for regular $\kappa$ with
$\kappa^{<\kappa}=\kappa$. For $\kappa=\omega$ this is the ZFC case $k=2$ of
Problem 1171, and that case contains the case $k=1$.

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/1989_01_01_baumgartner|1989_01_01_baumgartner]]: Baumgartner (Lecture Notes in Math. 1401, 1989) proved that Martin's axiom for
$\aleph_1$ dense sets makes $\omega_1\omega$ a partition ordinal. The catalog
relation follows for every finite $k$, so it holds in a model of ZFC.

[[problems/set_theory/E1171/claims/2026_09_05_gao|2026_09_05_gao]]: Gao's unrefereed, AI-assisted Zenodo deposit derives the exact relation for
every finite $k$ from Martin's axiom for $\aleph_1$ dense sets, by a
color-reduction lemma. It was filed on the site as a partial claim, then
withdrawn by its author.
