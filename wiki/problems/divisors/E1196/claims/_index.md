[[problems/divisors/E1196/claims/2026_04_13_price|2026_04_13_price]]: Price submitted a proof, found by GPT-5.4 Pro, that
$\sum_{a\in A}1/(a\log a)\le1+O(1/\log x)$ for every primitive set $A$ above
$x$. Alexeev and seven coauthors wrote it up, and Math Inc. formalized it.

[[problems/divisors/E1196/claims/2026_04_16_sothanaphan|2026_04_16_sothanaphan]]: Three notes by Sothanaphan, written with GPT-5.4 Thinking, sharpen the bound
for primitive sets above $x$ to $1+\gamma/\log x+O(1/\log^2x)$ and recast the
argument as a pure resummation. The paper's Remark 4.1 credits them, and the
claim is pending.
