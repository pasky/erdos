# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/claims/1987_12_01_fan|1987_12_01_fan]]: Fan (Discrete Math. 67 (1987)) proves that a diameter 2-critical graph on $n$
vertices has at most $[n^2/4]$ edges for $n\le24$ and for $n=26$. This is the
inequality only, and the claim is accepted on the refereed publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/claims/1988_03_01_furedi|1988_03_01_furedi]]: Füredi (J. Graph Theory 1992) proves that a graph of diameter $2$ in which
deleting any edge raises the diameter has at most $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges
once $n$ exceeds a tower-type threshold. The claim is accepted on the refereed
publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/claims/2026_08_05_jstar|2026_08_05_jstar]]: A proof claim on the site's proof-claim tab asserts the bound
$\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ for every $n$ by induction on a deficiency count
defined for all graphs, with a Lean file. By its submitter's account it is
AI-generated, and it is unreviewed.
