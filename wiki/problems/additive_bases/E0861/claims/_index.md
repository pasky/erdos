# problems/additive_bases/E0861/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0861/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0861/claims/2012_04_30_saxton_thomason|2012_04_30_saxton_thomason]]: Saxton and Thomason show that $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has between
$2^{(1.16+o(1))\sqrt N}$ and $2^{(55+o(1))\sqrt N}$ Sidon subsets. Since
$f(N)\sim\sqrt N$, the lower bound answers the first question yes and the
second no.
