# problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/claims/1960_01_01_erdos_szekeres|1960_01_01_erdos_szekeres]]: Erdős and Szekeres prove that every configuration of $2^n$ points in the
plane, $n\ge3$, contains an angle greater than $\pi(1-1/n)$, so with
Szekeres's construction $\alpha_{2^n}=\pi(1-1/n)$. The paper also gives the
values of $\alpha_N$ for $N\le8$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/claims/1993_01_01_sendov|1993_01_01_sendov]]: Sendov determines $\alpha_N$ for every $N\ge4$: it is $\pi(1-1/n)$ when
$2^{n-1}+2^{n-3}<N\le2^n$ and $\pi(1-2/(2n-1))$ when
$2^{n-1}<N\le2^{n-1}+2^{n-3}$. This refutes the Erdős–Szekeres guess.
