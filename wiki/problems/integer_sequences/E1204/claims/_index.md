# problems/integer_sequences/E1204/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1204/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1204/claims/2020_10_02_granville|2020_10_02_granville]]: Granville (Acta Arith. 2022, refereed) proves that infinitely many Siegel
zeros would give admissible sets of $\sim2y/\log y$ elements in $[0,y]$ for
arbitrarily large $y$. In that case $A(k)\sim k\log k$ would fail. The result
is conditional.
