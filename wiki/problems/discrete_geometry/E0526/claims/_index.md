# problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/claims/1959_01_05_kahane|1959_01_05_kahane]]: In a refereed note, Kahane proves a sufficient condition and a necessary
condition for random arcs of nonincreasing lengths below one to cover the
circle almost surely. These give the lengths $(1+c)/n$ that erdosproblems.com
credits to him.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/claims/1972_09_01_shepp|1972_09_01_shepp]]: Shepp proves a necessary and sufficient condition for independent uniform
random arcs of nonincreasing lengths to cover the whole circle with
probability one. The result is refereed, and erdosproblems.com credits it as
the solution.
