# Smith-form covolumes of Walsh degree-band lattices

[[research/erdos_774/evidence/_index|..]]

***

## Purpose

Before the
[compression proof of semistability](../../lemmas/moment_lattice_stability.md)
was obtained, a finite diagnostic tested a necessary spectral-subspace
condition: that no degree band of Walsh characters spans a lattice of smaller
normalized covolume than the full band. The compression theorem now proves the
relevant inequality for all sublattices; this diagnostic is retained as an exact
finite record and supports no infinite claim.

## The 53 degree-union matrices

The finite range is $(d,\ell)=(4,2),(6,3),(6,4)$ and every nonempty
subset $J$ of the degrees $0,\ldots,\ell$: exactly $7+15+31=53$
matrices. For each, the rows are all Walsh characters whose degree belongs
to $J$, in ascending mask order, and the columns are all $2^d$ points in
ascending mask order; the entry is $(-1)^{\mathrm{popcount}(s\wedge x)}$.
The product of the nonzero Smith invariants is the index of the row
lattice in the saturated spectral lattice. The lattice generated by the
Walsh rows has covolume $2^{d\cdot\mathrm{rank}/2}$, so subtracting the
binary valuation of the index gives the exact saturated logarithmic
covolume. All invariants encountered are powers of two.

| $d,\ell$ | Cases | Full normalized $\log_2$ covolume | Smaller tested value |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 4,2 | 7 | 6/11 | None |
| 6,3 | 15 | 5/7 | None |
| 6,4 | 31 | 5/19 | None |

Run, from the repository root,

```sh
uv run --no-sync python wiki/research/erdos_774/evidence/moment_band_determinants/main.py
```

The [checker](main.py) recomputes every Smith form with an exact integer
reduction in the standard library and the shared root `tools` checker; it needs
no symbolic algebra package and finishes in a few seconds. Its named checks are
that every invariant is a power of two, that the retained
[record of degree sets, Smith multiplicities and covolumes](assets/moment_band_determinants.json)
is reproduced case by case, that the three full-band values in the table hold,
and that no degree union has smaller normalized covolume than its full band. A
failed clause exits nonzero. The record is the output of the original run,
retained as the exact input the checker compares against.

## Nine even-parity moment matrices

An earlier hand-run diagnostic, whose script is not retained, fixed the
range $3\le d\le7$ and $1\le k<d/2$ in advance, exactly nine pairs. For
each pair the rows were the masks $s$ with $\mathrm{bit\_count}(s)\le k$
in ascending order, including $s=0$; the columns were the masks $x$ with
even $\mathrm{bit\_count}(x)$, also ascending; and the entry was $1$
precisely when $(x\wedge s)=s$. These are the squarefree monomial rows
restricted to even-parity columns, **not** Walsh rows. The absolute values
of the first (number of rows) diagonal entries of the Smith form were
counted. Here $a^b$ means that the value $a$ occurs $b$ times.

| $d$ | $k$ | Rows | Smith diagonal multiplicities |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | 4 | $1^3, 2^1$ |
| 4 | 1 | 5 | $1^4, 2^1$ |
| 5 | 1 | 6 | $1^5, 2^1$ |
| 5 | 2 | 16 | $1^{11}, 2^4, 8^1$ |
| 6 | 1 | 7 | $1^6, 2^1$ |
| 6 | 2 | 22 | $1^{16}, 2^5, 8^1$ |
| 7 | 1 | 8 | $1^7, 2^1$ |
| 7 | 2 | 29 | $1^{22}, 2^6, 8^1$ |
| 7 | 3 | 64 | $1^{42}, 2^{14}, 4^1, 8^6, 16^1$ |

These nine observations prove no general Smith-form rule, uniform
extraction, covering-radius estimate, or chromatic growth. No theorem in
the main argument depends on either table.
