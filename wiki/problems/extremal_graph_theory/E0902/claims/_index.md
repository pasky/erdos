# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/claims/1963_10_01_erdos|1963_10_01_erdos]]: Erdős (Math. Gaz. 1963, refereed) shows that tournaments with Schütte's
property $S_k$ exist and finds $f(1)=3$ and $f(2)=7$. He proves
$f(k)\ge2^{k+1}-1$, and $f(k)\le2^kk^2\log(2+\varepsilon)$ for large $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/claims/1965_10_01_szekeres_szekeres|1965_10_01_szekeres_szekeres]]: E. and G. Szekeres (Math. Gaz. 1965, refereed) prove that the least order
$f(k)$ of a tournament with Schütte's property $S_k$ satisfies
$f(k)\ge(k+2)2^{k-1}-1$, and that $f(3)=19$. This is the best known lower
bound for general $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/claims/2004_03_01_reid_mcrae_hedetniemi_hedetniemi|2004_03_01_reid_mcrae_hedetniemi_hedetniemi]]: Reid, McRae, Hedetniemi and Hedetniemi (Australas. J. Combin. 2004, refereed)
show that a tournament with domination number at least $5$ has at least $48$
vertices, so $f(4)\ge48$. They also prove $f(3)=19$.
