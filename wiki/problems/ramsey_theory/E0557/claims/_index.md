# problems/ramsey_theory/E0557/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0557/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0557/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: The sharp tree-free edge bound proved for Problem 548 (found by GPT-6 Astra,
published in Adamczewski's repository), applied to each color class, gives
$R_k(T)\le k(n-2)+2$ for every tree on $n\ge2$ vertices. It is accepted on a
third party's Lean derivation, built and audited here.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0557/claims/2026_09_04_reed_stein|2026_09_04_reed_stein]]: Corollary 4 of Reed and Stein (arXiv September 2026) gives, for each $k$, an
$n_0$ with $R_k(T)<k(n-2)+3$ for every tree on $n\ge n_0$ vertices. So the
Statement holds when its constant may depend on $k$, but the uniform reading
is not covered.
