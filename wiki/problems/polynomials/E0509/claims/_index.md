# problems/polynomials/E0509/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0509/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0509/claims/1959_01_01_pommerenke|1959_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke's 1959 Theorem 3 shows that if $f$ is monic and
$\{\lvert f\rvert<1\}$ is connected, then one disc of radius $2$ covers
$\{\lvert f\rvert\le1\}$. This answers the question yes for those polynomials
and is refereed.

[[problems/polynomials/E0509/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|1961_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke's 1961 paper shows that if $f$ is monic and
$\{\lvert f\rvert\le1\}$ is connected, then this set lies in the closed disc
of radius $2$ about the centroid of the zeros. This answers the question yes
for those polynomials and is refereed.

[[problems/polynomials/E0509/claims/2026_07_19_hong|2026_07_19_hong]]: Hong's 2026 claim, made with ChatGPT, asserts that for monic $f$ of degree at
most four the set $\{\lvert f\rvert\le1\}$ can be covered by disks whose radii
sum to at most $2$. It is a partial claim, pending review.
