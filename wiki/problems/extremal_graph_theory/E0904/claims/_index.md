# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/1978_01_01_edwards|1978_01_01_edwards]]: Edwards (Bolyai 18, 1978, and Bull. London Math. Soc. 1977) proves the
conjecture for $2\le r\le8$ with $n\ge r^2$, and for every $r$ when
$m>(r-1)n^2/2r$. The results are given as Bollobás and Nikiforov state them.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/1992_09_01_faudree|1992_09_01_faudree]]: Faudree (J. Graph Theory 1992) proves the Bollobás–Erdős conjecture for every
$r\ge2$ when $n>r^2(r-1)/4$. The paper is unread, and the range is as Bollobás
and Nikiforov and the site's commentary state it.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/claims/2004_10_08_bollobas_nikiforov|2004_10_08_bollobas_nikiforov]]: Theorem 2 of Bollobás and Nikiforov (Electron. J. Combin. 2005) proves the
Bollobás–Erdős conjecture for $n\ge r$: there is an $r$-clique of degree sum
at least $2rm/n$. The claim is accepted on the refereed publication and the
site's credit.
