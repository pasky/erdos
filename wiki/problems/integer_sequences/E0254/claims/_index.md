# problems/integer_sequences/E0254/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0254/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0254/claims/1959_09_03_cassels|1959_09_03_cassels]]: Cassels's 1960 Theorem I shows that a set $A$ with
$(A(2n)-A(n))/\log\log n\to\infty$ and $\sum_{a\in A}\|a\theta\|^2=\infty$ for
every $\theta\in(0,1)$ represents every large integer as a sum of distinct
elements. The result is refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0254/claims/2026_07_15_fan|2026_07_15_fan]]: Fan's 2026 preprint proves that divergent distance sums at every non-integer
angle, together with five elements in every large dyadic interval, give strong
completeness. It is accepted on the basis of a Lean formalization, built here,
of its version with six elements per interval.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0254/claims/2026_07_15_snyder|2026_07_15_snyder]]: Snyder's claimed Lean 4 proof of the erdosproblems.com statement was produced
with GPT 5.6 and posted on 15 July 2026 with a write-up and a Lean project. It
is registered in formal-conjectures as the formal proof.
