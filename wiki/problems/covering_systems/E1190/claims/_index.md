# problems/covering_systems/E1190/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E1190/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E1190/claims/2013_01_01_de_la_breteche_ford_vandehey|2013_01_01_de_la_breteche_ford_vandehey]]: The 2013 counting bounds of de la Bretèche, Ford and Vandehey for disjoint
progressions with distinct moduli give
$L(m)^{-1+o(1)}\le\epsilon_m\le L(m)^{-\sqrt3/2+o(1)}$, so $\epsilon_m\to0$.
The paper is refereed, and Ho's sharp estimate supersedes these bounds.

[[problems/covering_systems/E1190/claims/2026_04_23_ho|2026_04_23_ho]]: Boon Suan Ho's 2026 manuscript, written with GPT-5.4 Pro, proves that the
supremum of reciprocal sums over disjoint classes with distinct moduli above
$m$ is $\exp\bigl(-(1+o(1))\sqrt{\log m\log\log m}\bigr)$. It is accepted on
the site's credit.

[[problems/covering_systems/E1190/claims/2026_04_28_zribi|2026_04_28_zribi]]: Malek Zribi's five-page note of 28 April 2026, written with GPT-5.5, derives
the sharp estimate for the reciprocal-sum supremum from the sharp asymptotic
for Problem 202, which it assumes as a hypothesis. The result is claimed and
conditional.

[[problems/covering_systems/E1190/claims/2026_04_30_chojecki|2026_04_30_chojecki]]: Przemek Chojecki posted on ULAM's site an unsigned eight-page draft, dated 30
April 2026 and credited to GPT-5.5 Pro. It claims to derive the sharp upper
bound of Problem 202 by a spread-core route and to transfer it to
Problem 1190.
