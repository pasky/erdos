# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0205/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0205/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0205/claims/2026_01_11_barreto_leeham|2026_01_11_barreto_leeham]]: A disproof posted to the site's discussion thread on 2026-01-11, found with
ChatGPT and formalized with Aristotle, gives infinitely many $n$ for which no
$m=n-2^k$ has $\Omega(m)<\log\log m$. The site's curator credits it, and it is
not refereed.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0205/claims/2026_01_11_tao_alexeev|2026_01_11_tao_alexeev]]: For infinitely many $n$, every $n-2^k$ with $2^k\le n$ has at least a constant
times $\sqrt{\log n/\log\log n}$ prime factors. This is the quantified
disproof that the site credits to Tao and Alexeev, and community Lean files
prove it.
