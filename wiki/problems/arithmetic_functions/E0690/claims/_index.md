# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0690/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0690/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0690/claims/2025_01_17_cambie|2025_01_17_cambie]]: Cambie's refereed result, behind the erdosproblems.com label, shows that the
density of integers whose $k$th smallest prime factor is $p$ is unimodal in
$p$ for $k=1,2,3$ and is not unimodal for each $k$ from $4$ to $20$. So it is
not unimodal for every fixed $k$.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0690/claims/2026_05_08_wang_crapis|2026_05_08_wang_crapis]]: Wang and Crapis claim that the density of integers whose $k$th smallest prime
factor is $p$ fails to be unimodal for every $k\ge4$, completing Cambie's
classification. The claim is a preprint with a forum endorsement and no
refereed publication.
