**Claim.** For a monic polynomial $p\in\mathbb C[z]$ let $\ell(p)$ be the
length of the curve $\{z\in\mathbb C:\lvert p(z)\rvert=1\}$. Every monic
cubic $p\in\mathbb C[z]$ satisfies

$$
\ell(p)\le\ell(z^3-1)=\frac{2\pi\,\Gamma(1/3)}{\Gamma(2/3)^2},
$$

with equality exactly when $p(z)=(z-z_0)^3-w$ for some $z_0\in\mathbb C$
and some $w$ with $\lvert w\rvert=1$. The core is the strict family
inequality $\ell(z^3+3a^2z-c)<\ell(z^3-1)$, with $c=\sqrt{1-4a^6}$, for
every real $a$ with $0<a\le2^{-1/3}$.

**Covers.** The degree-3 case of the question of
[[problems/polynomials/E0114/_index|Problem 114]], answered yes: $z^3-1$
maximizes the length among monic cubics and, up to rotation and
translation, is the only maximizer. No other degree is settled, and the
question in general degree remains open.

**Credit.** The passage from the family inequality to every cubic uses two
results of Eremenko and Hayman's refereed 1999 paper,
[[../library/polynomials/eremenko_1999_length_lemniscates/lemma_4|Lemma 4]],
the existence of a maximizer in each degree, and
[[../library/polynomials/eremenko_1999_length_lemniscates/lemma_5|Lemma 5]],
that some maximizer has all its critical points on its lemniscate; the
equality case also uses the deformation constructed in their proof of
Lemma 5. Tao's
[[../library/polynomials/tao_2025_maximal_length_erdos_herzog_piranian_lemniscate/proposition_1_2|Proposition 1.2]]
restates these results. The family inequality, its written reduction to
explicit integrals, the ball-arithmetic bounds over the whole parameter
range and the composition are Plasma AI's.
