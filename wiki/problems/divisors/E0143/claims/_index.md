# problems/divisors/E0143/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0143/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0143/claims/2025_02_13_koukoulopoulos_lamzouri_lichtman|2025_02_13_koukoulopoulos_lamzouri_lichtman]]: By a theorem of Koukoulopoulos, Lamzouri and Lichtman, a set $A$ of reals
above $1$ whose elements keep distance at least $1$ from every integer
multiple of the others has $\sum_{x\in A,\,x<n}1/x=o(\log n)$. This is the
second of the problem's two displayed assertions.

[[problems/divisors/E0143/claims/2026_10_02_apicella|2026_10_02_apicella]]: Apicella claims a countable set of rationals above $2$, each at distance at
least $1$ from every integer multiple of the others, for which
$\sum 1/(a\log a)$ diverges. It was posted as a forum proof claim with a Lean
project and prepared with GPT 6 Astra.
