# problems/integer_sequences/E0784/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0784/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0784/claims/1982_04_01_ruzsa|1982_04_01_ruzsa]]: Ruzsa determines the logarithmic order of the least number of integers up to
$x$ left unsifted by a set of bounded reciprocal sum, which answers the
question yes for $C\le1$ and no for every $C>1$. The paper appeared in J.
Number Theory (1982) and the site credits it.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0784/claims/2023_10_19_weingartner|2023_10_19_weingartner]]: Weingartner proves that the least unsifted count for reciprocal budget $C$ has
exact order $x^{e^{1-C}}/\log x$, uniformly for $C$ between $1$ and any fixed
bound, sharpening Ruzsa. The paper appeared in Research in Number Theory
(2025) and the site credits it.
