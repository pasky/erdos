# problems/additive_combinatorics/E1112/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1112/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1112/claims/1997_10_01_bollobas_hegyvari_jin|1997_10_01_bollobas_hegyvari_jin]]: Theorem 3 of Bollobás, Hegyvári and Jin (Discrete Math. 1997, refereed) shows
that for every increasing sequence of ratios $r_i$ some $B$ with
$b_{i+1}\ge r_ib_i$ meets $A+A+A$ for every $A$ with gaps in $[2,3]$. So
$r_3(2,3)$ does not exist.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1112/claims/2026_07_06_land|2026_07_06_land]]: Johan Land's dichotomy says that for $k\ge3$ and $1\le d_1<d_2$, a ratio for
Problem 1112 exists exactly when $d_2\ge k+1$. It is accepted on the Lean port
built here, which proves the ratio $192d_2$ and does not build the later bound
$d_2+2$.
