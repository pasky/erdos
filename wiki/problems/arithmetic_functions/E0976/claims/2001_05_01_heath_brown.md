***

**Claim.** D. R. Heath-Brown, *The largest prime factor of $X^3+2$*, Proc.
London Math. Soc. 82 (2001), 554--596, proves that for a positive proportion
of the integers $n\in(X,2X]$ the largest prime factor of $n^3+2$ exceeds
$X^{1+\varpi}$ with $\varpi=10^{-303}$, as Irving's 2015 paper on the same
polynomial states the result. The paper's abstract gives the weaker form
that the largest prime factor of $n^3+2$ is infinitely often at least
$n^{1+\delta}$. For $f(t)=t^3+2$ the positive-proportion form gives
$F_f(n)\geq(n/2)^{1+\varpi}$ for all large $n$, in the notation of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0976/_index|Problem 976]].

**Covers.** The first question for $f(t)=t^3+2$. It does not give
$F_f(n)\gg n^3$.
