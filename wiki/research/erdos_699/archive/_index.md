# Earlier P699 investigations

[[research/erdos_699/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_699/archive/other_approaches/_index|other_approaches/]]: Earlier algebraic and approximation arguments, with the deductions and obstructions retained for future work.

[[research/erdos_699/archive/previous_bounds/_index|previous_bounds/]]: Earlier complete cutoffs, intermediate effective bounds, and superseded descriptions of the remaining cases.

***

Start with the [current research guide](../_index.md) to understand what
is proved now. This archive preserves the route to those results and the
arguments that explain why several alternatives did not finish the problem.

- [Earlier bounds and status reports](previous_bounds/_index.md) records
  previous cutoffs, intermediate stopping bounds, and earlier summaries.
  Their lists of remaining cases are historical.
- [Other approaches and obstructions](other_approaches/_index.md) retains
  the elliptic transformations, surface calculations, approximation
  experiments, and intermediate arguments that are not needed on the
  current reading route. Some contain valid useful lemmas even though
  the proposed approach did not settle its target.

All scripts and records, including older finite computations, are kept
together in the [computation folder](../computations/_index.md). Current
proofs sometimes use an earlier computation as an input; its mathematical
note remains linked from the proof that needs it.
