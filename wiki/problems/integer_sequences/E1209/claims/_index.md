# problems/integer_sequences/E1209/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1209/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1209/claims/2015_06_27_gurev_korsky|2015_06_27_gurev_korsky]]: Problem 4 of ELMO 2015, proposed by Gurev with an official solution by Korsky,
states that for every integer $a>1$ some $2^{2^n}+a$ is composite. This
pending claim answers question (iii.a) no for every shift at least $2$.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1209/claims/2026_04_08_bloom|2026_04_08_bloom]]: The erdosproblems.com curator's commentary constructs primes $a_k$ with
$q_k\mid a_k+k$, growing as fast as desired, so that $0$ is the only integer
shift making every term prime. This answers question (i) no, and question (ii)
no for nonnegative shifts.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1209/claims/2026_04_15_barschkis|2026_04_15_barschkis]]: Barschkis's forum note of 15 April 2026, with a Lean file, answers no to the
two general questions and to the always-prime question. The erdosproblems.com
commentary contains the construction and credits the order argument.
