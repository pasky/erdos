# problems/graph_coloring/E0751/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0751/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0751/claims/1998_01_01_bondy_vince|1998_01_01_bondy_vince]]: Bondy and Vince prove that every graph other than $K_1$ and $K_2$ with at most
two vertices of degree below three has two cycles whose lengths differ by one
or two. So a $4$-chromatic graph, whatever its girth, has consecutive cycle
lengths at most two apart.
