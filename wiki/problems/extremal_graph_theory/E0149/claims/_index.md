# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/claims/1992_10_01_andersen|1992_10_01_andersen]]: Andersen's Theorem 1 (Discrete Math. 1992) gives every graph of maximum degree
at most three a strong edge-coloring with at most ten colors. So the statement
holds for maximum degree at most three, a refereed partial result.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/claims/1993_06_01_horak_he_trotter|1993_06_01_horak_he_trotter]]: The Theorem of Horák, He and Trotter (J. Graph Theory 1993) states that a
graph of maximum degree at most three has strong chromatic index at most ten,
which is best possible. So the statement holds for maximum degree at most
three, and the result is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/claims/2000_10_01_mahdian|2000_10_01_mahdian]]: Mahdian proves that a $C_4$-free graph of large maximum degree $\Delta$ has
strong chromatic index at most $(2+o(1))\Delta^2/\log\Delta$. The result is in
his Toronto M.Sc. thesis and in Random Structures Algorithms 17 (2000), and is
refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/claims/2002_01_01_vu|2002_01_01_vu]]: Vu's list-coloring theorem for locally sparse graphs (Combin. Probab. Comput.
2002) extends Mahdian's bound: graphs without a fixed bipartite $H$ have
strong chromatic index $O_H(\Delta^2/\log\Delta)$. This is a claimed partial
result.
