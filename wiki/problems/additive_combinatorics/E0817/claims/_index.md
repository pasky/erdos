# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims/1992_01_01_erdos_sarkozy|1992_01_01_erdos_sarkozy]]: Theorem 4 of Erdős and Sárközy (Discrete Math. 1992) bounds the threshold
$K(N)$ for a three-term progression among subset sums, which gives
$g_3(n)\gg3^n/n$ in the problem's notation. The paper is refereed and the site
credits it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims/2026_06_23_korsky|2026_06_23_korsky]]: Samuel Korsky's preprint of 2026-06-23 proves
$g_3(n)\ge(\sqrt3/(2\sqrt\pi)+o(1))3^n/\sqrt n$ by the ternary
characterization of progression-free subset sums, with exponential lower and
upper bounds for every fixed $k\ge4$. The author announced it in the site's
thread.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/claims/2026_09_05_costa|2026_09_05_costa]]: Simone Costa's preprint of 2026-09-05, developed with ChatGPT (OpenAI, GPT-5.6
Sol), proves that $\liminf g_3(n)/3^n=0$, so the displayed question of Problem
817 has a negative answer. It is on the site's tab, so its status is claimed.
