# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/claims/1998_04_01_erdos_gyarfas_ruszinko|1998_04_01_erdos_gyarfas_ruszinko]]: Theorem 2.3 of Erdős, Gyárfás and Ruszinkó (Combinatorica 1998) shows that a
triangle-free graph of fixed maximum degree $d$ needs at most $c(d)\,n\log n$
added edges to reach diameter two. The result is refereed and accepted as a
partial claim.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/claims/2024_07_01_alon|2024_07_01_alon]]: Alon proves that a triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices with maximum degree
$o(\sqrt n)$ can be completed, still triangle-free, to diameter at most two by
adding $o(n^2)$ edges, so the answer is yes.
