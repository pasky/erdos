# problems/analysis/E0511/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0511/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0511/claims/1961_01_01_pommerenke|1961_01_01_pommerenke]]: Pommerenke's refereed paper constructs, for every $l<4$ and every $k$, a monic
polynomial whose sublevel set has at least $k$ components of diameter at least
$l$. So the number of components above a fixed diameter is not bounded.

[[problems/analysis/E0511/claims/2025_09_15_huang|2025_09_15_huang]]: Linhang Huang constructs, for every $c<4$ and every $N$, a monic polynomial
whose sublevel set has at least $N$ components of diameter at least $c$. This
is an independent rediscovery of Pommerenke's theorem, credited by
erdosproblems.com.
