# problems/graph_coloring/E0759/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0759/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0759/claims/1986_01_01_gimbel|1986_01_01_gimbel]]: Gimbel shows that the largest cochromatic number of a graph embeddable on the
orientable surface of genus $n$ lies between constant multiples of
$n^{1/2}/\log n$ and $n^{1/2}$. The lower bound has the right order.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0759/claims/1997_11_01_gimbel_thomassen|1997_11_01_gimbel_thomassen]]: Gimbel and Thomassen show that the largest cochromatic number of a graph
embeddable on the orientable surface of genus $n$ is of order
$n^{1/2}/\log n$. It was refereed in Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. and is credited
by the site's curator.
