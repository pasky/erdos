# problems/distance_problems/E0982/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0982/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0982/claims/1952_02_01_moser|1952_02_01_moser]]: Moser's 1952 argument in the Amer. Math. Monthly gives every convex $n$-gon a
vertex with at least $\lceil n/3\rceil$ distinct distances to the others. This
bound reaches $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ for $n=3,4,5$ and $7$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0982/claims/1994_01_01_erdos_fishburn|1994_01_01_erdos_fishburn]]: Erdős and Fishburn (DCG 1994) prove that every convex $n$-gon with $n\ge4$ has
a vertex followed by $\lfloor n/3\rfloor+1$ successively farther vertices, so
that vertex has that many distinct distances. This bound reaches
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ for $n=4$ to $7$ and $n=9$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0982/claims/2006_09_29_dumitrescu|2006_09_29_dumitrescu]]: Dumitrescu proves that every convex $n$-gon has a vertex with at least
$\lceil(13n-6)/36\rceil$ distinct distances to the other vertices (Discrete
Comput. Geom., 2006). The bound equals $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ for $n=3,4,5,7$
and $9$.
