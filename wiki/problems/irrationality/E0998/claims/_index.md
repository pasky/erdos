# problems/irrationality/E0998/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0998/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0998/claims/1966_01_01_kesten|1966_01_01_kesten]]: Kesten's 1966 Theorem 4 shows that an interval has bounded discrepancy along
the multiples of an irrational $\alpha$ exactly when its length is a
fractional multiple of $\alpha$. This proves the modified Statement.

[[problems/irrationality/E0998/claims/2026_08_17_alexeev|2026_08_17_alexeev]]: Answers the site's wording (both endpoints on the orbit of $\alpha$), not the
modified Statement (the length on the orbit), so it does not count toward the
problem's standing. A Lean theorem in Alexeev's repository refutes that
wording with $\alpha=\sqrt2/10$ and the interval of length $\alpha$ starting
at $1/4$.
