# problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims/1965_12_01_erdos_renyi|1965_12_01_erdos_renyi]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős and Rényi (J. Analyse Math. 1965, refereed) equidistributes
the subset sums of about $2\log_2N$ random elements of an abelian group of
order $N$, so $g_\epsilon(N)\le(2+o(1))\log_2N+O_\epsilon(1)$. The bound was
superseded in 1976.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/claims/1976_01_01_erdos_hall|1976_01_01_erdos_hall]]: The Theorem of Erdős and Hall (Houston J. Math. 1976, refereed)
equidistributes the subset sums of almost all choices of $(1+o(1))\log_2N$
elements of an abelian group of order $N$, so $g_\epsilon(N)=(1+o(1))\log_2N$.
The site credits this result.
