***

**Claim.** With $n_k$ as in
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/_index|Problem 1063]], the README of the
repository `pw/erdos1063-upper-bound` states as its Theorem that there is an
absolute constant $C$ with

$$
n_k\le\exp\Bigl(C\,\frac{k\log\log k}{\log k}\Bigr)
$$

for all sufficiently large $k$, so that $n_k=\exp(o(k))=o(k\,[1,\ldots,k-1])$.
The proof is an explicit construction of $n$ through $p$-adic valuation and
carry conditions, with one simultaneous Dirichlet pigeonhole step. The README
is signed by Patrick White and Claude (MathDyad). It says that GPT-5.6 Sol
produced the construction through Codex, that Claude re-verified it with a
checker written from scratch over 17 values of $k$ from 4 to 300, that no
human mathematician has reviewed it, and that it was not posted to the
erdosproblems.com forum. The README says that it does not claim to solve the
problem. The formal-conjectures catalog's docstring for
`erdos_1063.variants.subexponential_upper_bound` cites the repository as an
earlier proposed bound.

**Covers.** The upper bound above. It is weaker than the bound claimed on
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_07_27_cipollini|Cipollini's page]]
and the one proved in the Lean file on
[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_10_01_leap|the LEAP page]]. Not
covered: any lower bound, the order of $\log n_k$, and the estimate Erdős and
Selfridge asked for.

**Standing.** Claimed partial result, with the sign-off of Jacob Parish at Plasma AI pending; the review below is coverage and lists no evidence. Plasma AI's
[independent whole-proof examination](evidence/verify/white_upper_bound/report.md)
has verdict **refutation-failed** for the eventual quantitative bound,
$\log n_k=o(k)$ and $n_k=o(k\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,k-1))$.
A distinct [independent report grade](evidence/verify/white_upper_bound/grading.md),
dated 2026-10-15 UTC, gives **PASS** separately for all three clauses.
The [frozen subject provenance](evidence/assets/white_review_subject/manifest.json)
identifies the immutable source, exact ranges and retained catalog extracts.
Credit for the construction and bound remains with Patrick White and the
collaborators credited by the source; the examination and grading are
Plasma AI's.

The examination preserves exactly one failed divisor, $n\ge2k$, existence
of the minimum, a common positive multiplier, every prime class and absolute
eventual constants. The accepted interfaces are Legendre valuations,
Chebyshev bounds and the elementary higher-prime-power estimate; the lcm
comparison separately uses $\psi(t)\sim t$. The external proofs and editions
of the classical estimates were not independently examined. No numerical
$C$ or threshold, optimal rate, source-checker run, Lean proof or other construction is covered. The source is identified by its immutable
URL; a full public copy or independent remote authentication is not claimed.
The report retains its derivations and exact arithmetic diagnostic.
