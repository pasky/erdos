# problems/number_theory/E0465/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0465/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0465/claims/1976_01_01_sarkozy|1976_01_01_sarkozy]]: Sárközy's Theorem of 1976:
$N(X,\delta)<\frac{4\cdot10^4}{\delta^3}\cdot\frac{X}{\log\log X}$ for large
$X$, so $N(X,\delta)=o(X)$, which answers the first question of Problem 465.
It was refereed and published in Studia Sci. Math. Hungar.

[[problems/number_theory/E0465/claims/2001_04_01_konyagin|2001_04_01_konyagin]]: Konyagin's Theorem of 2001: for every $\delta$ there is $C(\delta)$ with
$N(X,\delta)<C(\delta)\sqrt X$ for all $X\ge1$, which answers both questions
of Problem 465. It was refereed and published in Mat. Zametki, and the site
credits it.
