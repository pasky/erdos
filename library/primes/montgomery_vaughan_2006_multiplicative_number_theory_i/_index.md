# Multiplicative Number Theory I. Classical Theory

[[primes/_index|..]]

[[primes/montgomery_vaughan_2006_multiplicative_number_theory_i/theorem_3_9|theorem_3_9]]: Theorem 3.9 and its historical note give the error-free upper bound
2y/(phi(q) log(y/q)) for primes in a reduced residue class in an interval
of length y>=2q; only that domain and the statement are extracted.

***

Hugh L. Montgomery and Robert C. Vaughan, *Multiplicative Number Theory I.
Classical Theory*, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics **97**,
Cambridge University Press, 2006, ISBN 9780521849036,
[book DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511618314).

The selected artifact is the publisher's 32-page Chapter 3, *Principles
and first examples of sieve methods*, printed pp. 76–107, served on
Vaughan's author page. Its physical page 1 is printed p. 76; printed
pp. 90 and 102 are physical pages 15 and 27. The chapter carries the
Cambridge Books Online copyright notice of 2010. The
[author-hosted frontmatter](https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/MNTI/01.0_pp_i_vi_Frontmatter.pdf)
identifies the print publication as 2006; the electronic publication date
is distinct from that print year. The whole book is not the selected artifact.

Provenance: the
[author-hosted chapter](https://personal.science.psu.edu/rcv4/personal/Publications/MNTI/07.0_pp_76_107_Principles_and_first_examples_of_sieve_methods.pdf),
chapter DOI [10.1017/CBO9780511618314.005](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511618314.005).

The consumed result is
[[primes/montgomery_vaughan_2006_multiplicative_number_theory_i/theorem_3_9|Theorem 3.9 with the Section 3.5 notes on Section 3.3]]:
for a reduced residue class modulo $q$, the number of primes in an interval
of length $y\ge2q$ is at most $2y/(\varphi(q)\log(y/q))$. The theorem on
p. 90 first gives an error factor; the notes on p. 102 explicitly state
that the error can be omitted, crediting Montgomery and Vaughan's 1973
paper. This is a primary-author textbook statement of that sharpening,
not an examination of the 1973 proof.

**Read status.** Claims checked for Theorem 3.9 and the cited sharpening,
including the coprimality condition and the domain $x\ge0$, $y\ge2q$;
both printed pages were inspected. No source proof is verified here, and
no claim about the additional range $q<y<2q$ is extracted from these pages.

**Bears on.**
[[../wiki/problems/integer_sequences/E0012/_index|Problem 12(iii)]]:
the inequality supplies an upper bound for prime reciprocal sums in reduced
classes, relevant to the normalization route. It does not supply that
route's missing multiscale harmonic-mass estimate.
