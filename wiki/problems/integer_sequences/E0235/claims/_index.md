[[problems/integer_sequences/E0235/claims/1963_12_05_hooley|1963_12_05_hooley]]: Hooley's 1965 theorem states that when $n/\phi(n)\to\infty$, as it does for
the primorials, the gaps between consecutive integers coprime to $n$,
normalized by $n/\phi(n)$, have the limiting distribution $1-e^{-c}$. It is
refereed, and the site gives it as the solution.
