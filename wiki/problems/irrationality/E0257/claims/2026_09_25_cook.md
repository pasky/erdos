***

**Claim.** For a finite nonempty set $P$ of primes, put
$h_P(a)=\prod_{p\in P}p^{v_p(a)}$. Cook claims that, for every integer
$b\ge2$ and infinite set $A$ of positive integers,

$$
\sum_{a\in A}\frac{h_P(a)}{a(b^{h_P(a)}-1)}<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{a\in A}\frac1{b^a-1}\notin\mathbb Q.
$$

The selected declaration `divisibilityWeightedClaim` also states that if a
host $H$ satisfies this condition at base two for some such $P$, every
infinite subset $A\subseteq H$ gives an irrational sum at every integer
base $b\ge2$. The registration includes four further declarations about
positive displacements tending to zero and divisor-cover extensions, with
their own summability and majorization hypotheses.

**Covers.** At $b=2$ this proves instances of
[[problems/irrationality/E0257/_index|Problem 257]] with the displayed
weighted hypothesis. It contains the reciprocal-summable supports of
[[problems/irrationality/E0257/claims/2026_09_11_cook|Cook's earlier note]]:
for $h\ge1$ and $b\ge2$, $h/(b^h-1)\le1$, so the weighted sum is bounded
by $\sum_{a\in A}1/a$. The registration explicitly excludes arbitrary
infinite supports from its conclusion; failure of its criterion asserts
nothing about rationality. It does not decide whether $1/2$ or $1/21$ is a
reciprocal Mersenne subseries sum.
