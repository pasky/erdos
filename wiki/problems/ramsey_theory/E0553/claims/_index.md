# problems/ramsey_theory/E0553/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0553/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0553/claims/2005_03_01_alon_rodl|2005_03_01_alon_rodl]]: Theorem 3.2 of Alon and Rödl (Combinatorica 2005) gives the Ramsey number of
$k$ triangles against $K_m$ up to polylogarithmic factors. Its cases $k=2$ and
$k=1$ make $R(3,3,n)/R(3,n)$ grow like $n$ divided by a power of $\log n$.
