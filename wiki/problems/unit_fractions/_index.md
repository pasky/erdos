# Unit Fractions

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0045/_index|E0045/]]: Asks whether, for each $k\ge2$,
some integer $n_k$ is such that every $k$-coloring of its divisors strictly
between $1$ and $n_k$ has a monochromatic subset whose reciprocals sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0046/_index|E0046/]]: Asks whether every finite
coloring of the integers has a monochromatic set of distinct integers $n_i\ge2$
with $\sum 1/n_i=1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0047/_index|E0047/]]: Asks whether, for fixed
$\delta>0$ and large $N$, every $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with $\sum_{a\in
A}1/a>\delta\log N$ has a subset whose reciprocals sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0148/_index|E0148/]]: Asks for good estimates of
$F(k)$, the number of ways to write $1$ as a sum of reciprocals of $k$ distinct
positive integers.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0206/_index|E0206/]]: Asks whether, for almost every
$x>0$, the best underapproximations of $x$ by sums of $n$ distinct unit
fractions are eventually built greedily.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0242/_index|E0242/]]: Asks whether $4/n=1/x+1/y+1/z$
has a solution in distinct positive integers $x<y<z$ for every $n>2$; proofs are
claimed but none is accepted.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0282/_index|E0282/]]: Asks whether the greedy
algorithm for unit fractions, which always takes the least allowed denominator,
terminates for every rational in $(0,1)$ with odd denominator when only odd
denominators are allowed.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0283/_index|E0283/]]: Asks whether, for every integer
polynomial $p$ with positive leading coefficient and no $d\ge2$ dividing every
$p(n)$, every large $m$ equals $p(n_1)+\cdots+p(n_k)$ for some integers $1\le
n_1<\cdots<n_k$ with $\sum 1/n_i=1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0284/_index|E0284/]]: Asks whether $f(k)$, the
largest possible smallest denominator among $k$ distinct unit fractions summing
to $1$, satisfies $f(k)=(1+o(1))\frac{k}{e-1}$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0285/_index|E0285/]]: Asks whether $f(k)$, the least
possible largest denominator among $k$ distinct unit fractions summing to $1$,
satisfies $f(k)=(1+o(1))\frac{e}{e-1}k$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0286/_index|E0286/]]: Asks whether $k$ distinct unit
fractions summing to $1$ can always be chosen with all denominators in an
interval of width $(e-1+o(1))k$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0287/_index|E0287/]]: Asks whether every
representation $1=1/n_1+\cdots+1/n_k$ with $1<n_1<\cdots<n_k$ has two
consecutive denominators differing by at least $3$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0288/_index|E0288/]]: Asks whether only finitely many
pairs of integer intervals $I_1,I_2$ satisfy $\sum_{n\in
I_1}\frac{1}{n}+\sum_{n\in I_2}\frac{1}{n}\in\mathbb{N}$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0289/_index|E0289/]]: Asks whether, for every large
$k$, one can write $1$ as the sum of $1/n$ over $k$ distinct integer intervals,
none overlapping or adjacent and each of length at least $2$; proofs are claimed
but none is accepted.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0290/_index|E0290/]]: Asks whether, for each $a\ge1$,
some $b>a$ makes the reduced denominator of $\sum_{a\le n\le b}1/n$ drop when
$1/(b+1)$ is added, and how such $b(a)$ grows with $a$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0291/_index|E0291/]]: Asks whether $(a_n,L_n)=1$ and
$(a_n,L_n)>1$ each hold for infinitely many $n$, where
$L_n=\mathrm{lcm}(1,\ldots,n)$ and $\sum_{k\le n}1/k=a_n/L_n$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0292/_index|E0292/]]: Asks whether the integers that
occur as the largest denominator in some representation of $1$ by distinct unit
fractions have density $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0293/_index|E0293/]]: Asks for the growth of $v(k)$,
the least integer greater than $1$ that is not a denominator in any
representation of $1$ as a sum of $k$ distinct unit fractions.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0294/_index|E0294/]]: Asks for estimates of $t(N)$,
the least $t$ such that $1$ is not a sum of distinct unit fractions with
smallest denominator $t$ and all denominators at most $N$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0295/_index|E0295/]]: Asks whether
$k(N)-(e-1)N\to\infty$, where $k(N)$ is the least number of distinct unit
fractions, all with denominators at least $N$, that sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0296/_index|E0296/]]: Asks for estimates of $k(N)$,
the largest number of disjoint subsets of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ whose reciprocals
each sum to $1$, and whether $k(N)=o(\log N)$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0297/_index|E0297/]]: Asks how many subsets of
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ have reciprocals summing to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0298/_index|E0298/]]: Asks whether every set of
positive integers of positive density contains a finite subset whose reciprocals
sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0299/_index|E0299/]]: Asks whether some infinite
increasing sequence of integers with bounded gaps has no finite subset whose
reciprocals sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/_index|E0300/]]: Asks for estimates of $A(N)$,
the size of the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no subset whose
reciprocals sum to $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0301/_index|E0301/]]: Asks for estimates of $f(N)$,
the size of the largest $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ in which no $1/a$ is a sum of
reciprocals of other distinct members, and whether $f(N)=(\frac{1}{2}+o(1))N$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0302/_index|E0302/]]: Asks for estimates of $f(N)$,
the size of the largest $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no distinct $a,b,c\in A$
satisfying $1/a=1/b+1/c$, and whether $f(N)=(\frac{1}{2}+o(1))N$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0303/_index|E0303/]]: Asks whether every finite
coloring of the integers has distinct $a,b,c$ of the same color with
$1/a=1/b+1/c$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0304/_index|E0304/]]: Asks to estimate $N(b)$, the
fewest distinct unit fractions needed to write any fraction with denominator
$b$, and whether $N(b)\ll\log\log b$. Answered yes by Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI
release (2026), accepted on Lean evidence.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/_index|E0305/]]: Asks to estimate $D(b)$, the
smallest possible largest denominator needed to write any fraction with
denominator $b$ as a sum of distinct unit fractions, and whether $D(b)\ll b(\log
b)^{1+o(1)}$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/_index|E0306/]]: Asks whether every positive
rational with squarefree denominator is a sum of distinct unit fractions whose
denominators are each a product of two distinct primes.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0307/_index|E0307/]]: Asks whether there are two
finite sets of primes whose sums of reciprocals have product $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0308/_index|E0308/]]: Asks for the least integer that
is not a sum of distinct unit fractions with denominators at most $N$, and
whether the representable integers form an initial segment. A corrected version
asks for large $N$ whether that integer is $\lfloor\sum_{n\le N}1/n\rfloor$ or
one more.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0309/_index|E0309/]]: Asks how many integers are sums
of distinct unit fractions with denominators at most $N$, and whether there are
only $o(\log N)$ of them.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0310/_index|E0310/]]: Asks whether every
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with $|A|\ge\alpha N$ has a subset whose reciprocals
sum to some $a/b$ with $a\le b=O_\alpha(1)$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0311/_index|E0311/]]: Asks whether the least non-zero
distance $\delta(N)$ from $1$ to a sum $\sum_{n\in A}1/n$ with
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ equals $e^{-(c+o(1))N}$ for some constant
$c\in(0,1)$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0312/_index|E0312/]]: Asks whether, for some $c>0$,
every large enough finite multiset of positive integers with reciprocal sum
above $K$ has a subset whose reciprocal sum lies in $(1-e^{-cK},1]$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0313/_index|E0313/]]: Asks whether infinitely many
sums of reciprocals of distinct primes equal $1-1/m$ for an integer $m\ge2$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0314/_index|E0314/]]: Asks how small the excess
$\epsilon(n)$ over $1$ can be when $1/n+1/(n+1)+\cdots$ is summed until it
reaches $1$, and whether $\liminf n^2\epsilon(n)=0$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0315/_index|E0315/]]: Asks whether every increasing
sequence $a_1<a_2<\cdots$ with $\sum 1/a_k=1$, other than Sylvester's, has
$\liminf a_n^{1/2^n}<c_0=1.264085\cdots$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0316/_index|E0316/]]: Asks whether every finite set
of integers above $1$ with reciprocal sum less than $2$ can be split into two
parts, each with reciprocal sum below $1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0317/_index|E0317/]]: Asks whether, for some $c>0$
and every $n$, signs $\delta_k\in\{-1,0,1\}$ can be chosen with $0<|\sum_{k\le
n}\delta_k/k|<c/2^n$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0318/_index|E0318/]]: Asks whether every non-constant
$f:A\to\{-1,1\}$ on an infinite arithmetic progression $A$ has a finite
non-empty $S\subseteq A$ with $\sum_{n\in S}f(n)/n=0$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0319/_index|E0319/]]: Asks for the size of the
largest $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with signs $\delta_n=\pm1$ such that
$\sum_{n\in A}\delta_n/n=0$ but no proper non-empty subset of $A$ gives zero.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0320/_index|E0320/]]: Asks to estimate the number
$S(N)$ of distinct sums $\sum_{n\in A}1/n$ over $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0321/_index|E0321/]]: Asks for the size of the
largest $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ whose subsets all have distinct sums of
reciprocals.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0327/_index|E0327/]]: Asks how large
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ can be if $a+b\nmid ab$ for all distinct $a,b\in A$,
or if instead $a+b\nmid 2ab$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0355/_index|E0355/]]: Asks whether some sequence of
positive integers with $a_{n+1}/a_n\ge\lambda$ for a fixed $\lambda>1$ has
finite sums of reciprocals covering every rational in some open interval.
