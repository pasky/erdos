# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/claims/2013_08_21_dudek_mubayi|2013_08_21_dudek_mubayi]]: Dudek and Mubayi prove $f(n)\ge c\sqrt{n\log n/\log\log n}$ for Problem 620 by
combining Shearer's independence bound with the neighborhood of a vertex, in a
refereed paper in J. Graph Theory 76 (2014).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/claims/2024_01_04_mubayi_verstraete|2024_01_04_mubayi_verstraete]]: Theorem 1 of Mubayi and Verstraete gives $f_s(n)=O(\sqrt n\log n)$ for each
fixed $s\ge3$, which bounds $f(n)$ of Problem 620 from above. The paper is
refereed and appeared in Bull. Lond. Math. Soc. 57 (2025).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/claims/2026_07_17_morris_sahasrabudhe_verstraete|2026_07_17_morris_sahasrabudhe_verstraete]]: Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Verstraëte's arXiv preprint of 17 July 2026 claims
that the Erdős–Rogers function $f_{s,s+1}(n)$ has order $\sqrt{n\log n}$ for
every $s\ge2$, which at $s=3$ would determine the order of $f(n)$ up to
constants. It is unrefereed, so it remains a claim.
