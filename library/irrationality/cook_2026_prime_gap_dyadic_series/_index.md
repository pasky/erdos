# Prime-gap dyadic series: perturbations, exact criteria and certificates

[[irrationality/_index|..]]

***

Cook, Will, *Prime-Gap Dyadic Series: Perturbations, Exact Criteria and Certificates*, September 2026, 47-page author-hosted edition.

The abstract reports sparse perturbations of nonnegative integer coefficients that preserve specified congruence and asymptotic data while permitting rational or irrational dyadic sums. Applied to prime-gap comparison sequences, the construction does not ensure that cumulative positions are consecutive primes. The paper also gives exact tail criteria and finite denominator exclusions; it explicitly leaves irrationality of the actual prime series unresolved.
