# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/claims/2022_07_25_wigderson|2022_07_25_wigderson]]: Answers the erdosproblems.com wording (every finite family), not the modified
Statement (every family other than a star with a matching), so it does not
count toward the problem's standing. Wigderson's note records the folklore
example: the two-edge star and the two-edge matching have joint extremal
number 1, while each alone has a linear extremal number.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/claims/2026_04_28_kj_c|2026_04_28_kj_c]]: A forum post claims that a family whose members all have linear extremal
number has bounded extremal number exactly when it contains a star and a
matching, and linear extremal number otherwise. The post is unreviewed, and
its Lean development has not been built.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/claims/2026_08_01_openai|2026_08_01_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI's 2026 report gives a finite family of
connected bipartite graphs with cycles whose joint extremal number is a power
of $n$ below every member's. The erdosproblems.com curator credits the
disproof.
