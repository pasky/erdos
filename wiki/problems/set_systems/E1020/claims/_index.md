# problems/set_systems/E1020/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1961_01_01_erdos_ko_rado|1961_01_01_erdos_ko_rado]]: Erdős, Ko and Rado (1961) prove in a refereed paper that for $n\ge2r$ an
intersecting family of $r$-subsets of an $n$-set has at most
$\binom{n-1}{r-1}$ members. This is the case $k=2$ of the matching conjecture,
for every uniformity.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1965_01_01_erdos|1965_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős (1965) proves, in a refereed paper, the conjectured value of $f(n;r,k)$
for $n>c_rk$, the range where the covering term dominates. The constant $c_r$
depends only on $r$ and is not specified.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1968_09_01_kleitman|1968_09_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman (1968), in a refereed paper, bounds the size of a family of subsets
of an $n$-set with no $k$ pairwise disjoint members, sharply when $k$ divides
$n$ or $n+1$. Its $r$-uniform case at $n=rk$ gives the matching conjecture for
that $n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/1976_01_01_bollobas_daykin_erdos|1976_01_01_bollobas_daykin_erdos]]: Bollobás, Daykin and Erdős (1976) prove that for $n>2r^3(k-1)$ an $r$-uniform
hypergraph with no $k$ pairwise disjoint edges, and with more edges than the
covering family minus a correction term, lies inside that family. The paper is
refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov|2011_07_27_huang_loh_sudakov]]: Huang, Loh and Sudakov (2012) prove the matching conjecture for $n>3r^2k$,
using shifting and an asymptotic form of a rainbow-matching strengthening. The
paper is refereed and appeared in Combin. Probab. Comput.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_02_frankl_rodl_rucinski|2012_02_02_frankl_rodl_rucinski]]: Frankl, Rödl and Ruciński (2012) prove the matching conjecture for $3$-uniform
hypergraphs whenever $n\ge4k$, four times the forbidden matching size. The
paper is refereed and appeared in Combin. Probab. Comput.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_02_19_luczak_mieczkowska|2012_02_19_luczak_mieczkowska]]: Łuczak and Mieczkowska (2014) prove the matching conjecture for $3$-uniform
hypergraphs on $n$ vertices, for every matching number, once $n$ is large. The
cover and the clique are the only extremal hypergraphs, and the paper is
refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_05_30_frankl|2012_05_30_frankl]]: Frankl (2017) proves the matching conjecture for $3$-uniform hypergraphs for
every $n$ and every matching number, which completes the case $r=3$. The paper
is refereed and appeared in Discrete Applied Mathematics.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska|2012_06_13_frankl_luczak_mieczkowska]]: Frankl, Łuczak and Mieczkowska (2012) prove that once $n>2r^2(k-1)/\log r$, an
$r$-uniform hypergraph whose largest matching has $k-1$ edges has at most as
many edges as the covering family. The cover alone is extremal, and the paper
is refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2013_07_01_frankl|2013_07_01_frankl]]: Frankl's refereed 2013 paper in J. Combin. Theory Ser. A proves the matching
conjecture whenever $n\ge(2s+1)r-s$, where $s=k-1$ is the matching number.
This is the first range linear in both parameters.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2017_10_01_frankl|2017_10_01_frankl]]: Frankl (2017) proves the matching conjecture when $k-1>r$ and
$rk\le n<k(r+1/(2r^{2r+1}))$, the almost-perfect range where the clique term
is the maximum. The paper is refereed and appeared in Israel J. Math.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii|2018_06_22_frankl_kupavskii]]: Frankl and Kupavskii (2022) use concentration inequalities to prove the
matching conjecture for large matching number $s=k-1$ whenever
$n\ge\tfrac53sr-\tfrac23s$. The paper is refereed and appeared in J. Combin.
Theory Ser. B.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2022_06_03_kolupaev_kupavskii|2022_06_03_kolupaev_kupavskii]]: Kolupaev and Kupavskii (2023) prove the matching conjecture for $r\ge5$ and
$k-1>101r^3$ whenever $rk\le n<k(r+1/(100r))$, which widens Frankl's window
above $n=rk$. The paper is refereed and appeared in Discrete Mathematics.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_02_01_mishra|2026_02_01_mishra]]: Mishra's 2026 preprint claimed a complete proof of the Erdős matching
conjecture. A reader found an error in its Lemma 4 on the erdosproblems.com
thread, and the author withdrew the preprint on 1 June 2026.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_02_22_frankl_lu_ma_wu|2026_02_22_frankl_lu_ma_wu]]: Frankl, Lu, Ma and Wu (2026) claim a proof of the matching conjecture for
$4$-uniform hypergraphs when $n\ge5(k-1)$, five times the matching number, and
$n$ is large, using a stability theorem. The claim is in an arXiv preprint and
is not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_05_25_hou_hu_liu|2026_05_25_hou_hu_liu]]: Hou, Hu and Liu (2026) claim a proof of the matching conjecture for
$4$-uniform hypergraphs for every matching number $s\ge6004$ and $n\ge4s+4$.
The proof uses a finite-board reduction checked in exact arithmetic and is not
refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_08_19_cao_liu_zhang|2026_08_19_cao_liu_zhang]]: Cao, Liu and Zhang (2026) claim a proof of the matching conjecture for every
fixed uniformity $r$ once the matching number $k-1$ is large and
$n\ge(r+1)(k-1)$, the conjecture's crossover. The claim is in an arXiv
preprint and is not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E1020/claims/2026_09_13_babanskyy|2026_09_13_babanskyy]]: Babanskyy's 2026 manuscript claims a proof of the Erdős matching conjecture
for $4$-uniform hypergraphs and every matching size, by the finite-board
method with rational certificates. The work is AI-assisted, unreviewed and not
refereed.
