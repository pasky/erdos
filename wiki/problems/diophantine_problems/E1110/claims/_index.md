# problems/diophantine_problems/E1110/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1110/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1110/claims/2026_08_05_apiros3|2026_08_05_apiros3]]: The forum user Apiros3 claims, with a Lean development, that each of the three
pairs Yu and Chen left open has an infinite pairwise-coprime sequence of
non-representable numbers prime to $pq$. Together with the Yu-Chen range, this
answers the second question.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1110/claims/2026_08_24_ding_li_liu_zhang|2026_08_24_ding_li_liu_zhang]]: Ding, Li, Liu and Zhang claim that for the pair $(5,2)$ the integers that are
sums of numbers $2^a5^b$, no summand dividing another, have positive lower
density. The proof uses an injection from a class of admissible words.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1110/claims/2026_09_29_becart|2026_09_29_becart]]: Bécart claims, with computer assistance, that the integers representable by
antichains of numbers $2^a7^b$ have positive lower density, and a same-day
draft claims the same for $4^a3^b$. The proofs use C-checked certificates and
a Lean reduction.
