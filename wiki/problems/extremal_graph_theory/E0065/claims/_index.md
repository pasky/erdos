# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/claims/1984_12_01_gyarfas_komlos_szemeredi|1984_12_01_gyarfas_komlos_szemeredi]]: Gyárfás, Komlós and Szemerédi prove that the reciprocals of the distinct cycle
lengths of a graph of minimum degree $d$ sum to at least $c\log d$, which
answers the first question. The claim is accepted on the refereed J. Graph
Theory paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/claims/2020_10_29_liu_montgomery|2020_10_29_liu_montgomery]]: Liu and Montgomery's Corollary 1.2 shows that in every graph of average degree
$d$, the reciprocals of the distinct cycle lengths sum to at least
$(\tfrac12-o(1))\log d$. The constant is sharp for the first question, and the
claim is accepted on the refereed JAMS paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/claims/2026_09_22_milojevic_montgomery_pokrovskiy_sudakov|2026_09_22_milojevic_montgomery_pokrovskiy_sudakov]]: A 2026 arXiv preprint by Milojević, Montgomery, Pokrovskiy and Sudakov proves
that for large $k$ the graph $K_{k,n-k}$ uniquely minimizes the harmonic sum
of cycle lengths among $n$-vertex graphs with at least $k(n-k)$ edges.
