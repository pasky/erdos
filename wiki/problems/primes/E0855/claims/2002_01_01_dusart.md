***

**Claim.** P. Dusart, *Sur la conjecture $\pi(x+y)\le\pi(x)+\pi(y)$*, Acta
Arith. 102 (2002), no. 4, 295--308, Théorème 1 (p. 296): for all real $x,y$
with

$$
2\le x\le y\le\frac75x\log x\log\log x,
$$

$\pi(x+y)\le\pi(x)+\pi(y)$. The proof splits at the ratio $y/x=109$.
Proposition 3 (p. 304) proves the inequality for real $x,y\ge3$ with
$1/109\le y/x\le109$, through the prime-index inequality
$p_k+p_l\le p_{k+l-1}$ of Proposition 2 (p. 301), whose small indices are
checked by computer (Lemma 4, pp. 302--304). Proposition 1 (p. 299) covers
$109\le y/x\le\frac75\log x\log\log x$ for $x\ge0.4\cdot10^{11}$, from
explicit two-term bounds on $\pi$. Proposition 4 (p. 305) deduces that the
pairs $x,y\le X$ failing the inequality make up at most a proportion
$5/(7\log X\log\log X)$ of the square. The paper is compiled at
[[../library/primes/dusart_2002_sur_la_conjecture_pi_x_y_pi_x_pi_y/_index|its card]],
which holds the published article.

**Covers.** Every pair whose smaller entry $u\ge2$ and larger entry $v$
satisfy $v\le\frac75u\log u\log\log u$. Not covered: the pairs with
$v>\frac75u\log u\log\log u$, which include pairs with both entries
arbitrarily large, so the problem as posed is not settled.

**Acceptance.** Refereed: Acta Arithmetica 102 (2002), a refereed journal
(received 7 May 1999, revised 18 April 2001, as the article prints). The
journal volume gives the year 2002 and no month or day, so the page's date
