# problems/distance_problems/E0652/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0652/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0652/claims/2019_12_04_mathialagan|2019_12_04_mathialagan]]: Mathialagan proves that among $k\le n^{1/3}$ points, some point determines
$\Omega(\sqrt{kn})$ distinct distances to $n$ other points. So
$\alpha_k=\Omega(\sqrt k)$ and the answer is yes; the paper is refereed and
credited by the erdosproblems.com curator.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0652/claims/2026_01_29_feng|2026_01_29_feng]]: The Aletheia agent (Gemini Deep Think) gives a claimed proof that
$\alpha_k=\Omega(k^{1/4})$, hence the answer yes, using a Pach–Sharir
incidence count over the distance circles of the $k$ poorest points.
