# problems/integer_sequences/E0539/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0539/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0539/claims/1999_04_01_granville_roesler|1999_04_01_granville_roesler]]: Theorem 2 of Granville and Roesler's refereed 1999 Monthly paper shows that
$m^{1/2}\le h(m)$ and that $h(m)$ is at most about $(3/2)(2m)^{2/3}$. The
lower bound, by a pairing argument, is credited to Erdős and Szemerédi, and
the upper bound uses the Freiman–Lev sets.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0539/claims/2026_06_10_schmitt_gehrunger_dekoninck_berczi_kreitner_price_holmes|2026_06_10_schmitt_gehrunger_dekoninck_berczi_kreitner_price_holmes]]: Theorem A.1 of the July 2026 ProofCouncil preprint by Schmitt and six
coauthors gives $h(n)\le n^{1/2}\exp(C\sqrt{\log n})$, so the exponent of
$h(n)$ is $1/2$. It appears in the site's commentary under the site's label
OPEN and is not refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0539/claims/2026_09_05_kitamura|2026_09_05_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's public Lean 4 development of September 2026 states, for the
formal-conjectures threshold, that $h(n)/\sqrt n\to\infty$. This corpus has
not built or audited it, and no paper states the result.
