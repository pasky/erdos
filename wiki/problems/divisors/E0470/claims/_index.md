# problems/divisors/E0470/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0470/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0470/claims/2014_09_16_melfi|2014_09_16_melfi]]: Melfi (J. Number Theory 2015) proves that $2^kpq$ is primitive weird for
suitable primes $p$ and $q$ near $2^{k+2}$. The refereed result is
conditional: a prime-gap bound $p_{n+1}-p_n<0.1\sqrt{p_n}$ gives infinitely
many primitive weird numbers.
