# problems/irrationality/E0259/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E0259/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E0259/claims/1999_02_01_chen_ruzsa|1999_02_01_chen_ruzsa]]: Chen and Ruzsa's 1999 paper proves Erdős's stronger conjecture that every
infinite subseries of $\sum n/2^n$ over squarefree $n$ is irrational, which
answers the question yes. The paper is refereed, and the site's curator
credits it with this result.

[[problems/irrationality/E0259/claims/2026_09_28_ringer|2026_09_28_ringer]]: Ringer's manuscript of 2026-09-27, written with two AI systems, claims that
$\sum_{n\text{ squarefree}}n/B^n$ is normal to base $B$ for every $B\ge2$,
which at $B=2$ implies the irrationality asked for. The claim is unreviewed.
