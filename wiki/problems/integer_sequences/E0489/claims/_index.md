# problems/integer_sequences/E0489/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0489/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0489/claims/1951_05_04_erdos|1951_05_04_erdos]]: Erdős's refereed 1951 theorem (Publicationes Mathematicae Debrecen) shows that
the mean squared gap between consecutive squarefree numbers tends to a finite
limit. This is the prime-squares case, which the site credits.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0489/claims/2026_04_20_chojecki|2026_04_20_chojecki]]: A note of 20 April 2026 by Przemyslaw Chojecki, produced with GPT-5.4 Pro,
claims that the normalized sum of squared gaps always has a limit in
$[0,+\infty]$, equal to $1+2\sum q_A(h)$, and that the limit is finite for a
structured class.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0489/claims/2026_07_15_snyder|2026_07_15_snyder]]: Colin Snyder's July 2026 claim, produced with GPT 5.6 (custom harness) and
backed by a Lean 4 proof bundle, says that for every divisor set of size
$o(\sqrt x)$ the mean squared gap of the sieved integers converges. Nobody has
accepted it.
