# problems/set_systems/E0703/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0703/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0703/claims/1984_03_01_frankl_furedi|1984_03_01_frankl_furedi]]: Frankl and Füredi (1984) determine $T(n,r)$ exactly for every fixed $r$ and
all $n$ large in terms of $r$, and show that the extremal family is unique.
This is a partial answer to the problem's request for estimates.

[[problems/set_systems/E0703/claims/1987_03_01_frankl_rodl|1987_03_01_frankl_rodl]]: Frankl and Rödl (1987, Theorem 1.1) prove that a family of subsets of an
$n$-set with no two members meeting in exactly $r$ points, where
$\epsilon n<r<(1/2-\epsilon)n$, has fewer than $(2-\delta)^n$ members.
