# problems/irrationality/E1050/claims

[[problems/irrationality/E1050/_index|..]]

[[problems/irrationality/E1050/claims/1991_03_01_borwein|1991_03_01_borwein]]: Borwein proves that $\sum_{n\ge1}1/(q^n+r)$ is irrational for every integer
$q>1$ and every nonzero rational $r\ne-q^m$. At $q=2$ and $r=-3$ this gives
the irrationality asked about.
