# problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims/1965_01_01_erdos|1965_01_01_erdos]]: In 1965 Erdős stated that the lcm-triple conjecture is proved, resting on his
reduction to union-free families and on Sárközy and Szemerédi's unpublished
bound that such families have $o(2^n)$ members. Kleitman's theorem superseded
it.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0487/claims/1971_01_01_kleitman|1971_01_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman's 1971 theorem says that a union-free family of subsets of an $n$-set
has at most $(1+o(1))\binom{n}{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ members. By Erdős's
reduction it settles the lcm-triple question, and erdosproblems.com credits
it.
