# problems/ramsey_theory/E0439/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0439/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0439/claims/1989_01_01_erdos_sarkozy_sos|1989_01_01_erdos_sarkozy_sos]]: Theorem 3 of Erdős, Sárközy and Sós's 1989 chapter states that for any
partition of the positive integers into at most three classes, infinitely many
squares are sums of two distinct integers of one class. It is the partial
result before Khalfalah and Szemerédi.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0439/claims/2006_01_03_khalfalah_szemeredi|2006_01_03_khalfalah_szemeredi]]: Khalfalah and Szemerédi's refereed 2006 theorem states that if $f$ is a
non-constant integer polynomial with an even value, every finite coloring of
the integers has two distinct integers of one color whose sum is a value of
$f$.
