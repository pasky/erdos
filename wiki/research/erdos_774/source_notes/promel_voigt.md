# Prömel–Voigt: canonical partition theorems for parameter sets

***

[Held copy and library card](../../../../library/ramsey_theory/promel_voigt_1983_canonical_partition_theorems_parameter_sets/_index.md).

H. J. Prömel and B. Voigt, "Canonical partition theorems for parameter
sets," *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 35 (1983), no. 3,
309--327.

This paper proves a canonical (unrestricted) version of the Graham–Rothschild
partition theorem for $k$-parameter sets, rather than a merely monochromatic
one.  After passing to a structured subspace, a coloring with arbitrarily many
colors has one of a controlled set of canonical forms; the Erdős–Rado
canonization theorem follows as a corollary (abstract, p. 309).  It is
potentially useful for normalizing the intersection pattern of additive
circuits inside a product-box construction.

The theorem does not automatically create a high-chromatic relation
hypergraph.  Any use for E0774 must specify the parameter-set encoding, the
canonical form obtained, and why that form either preserves proportional
dissociated subsets or forces a monochromatic signed relation.  Treat it as a
structural extraction tool, not a black-box counterexample generator.
