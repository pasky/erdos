# problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/1960_01_01_erdos|1960_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős proved that for $d\ge4$ and $p=\lfloor d/2\rfloor$ the maximum number of
unit distances among $n$ points of $\mathbb{R}^d$ is
$\bigl(\frac{p-1}{2p}+o(1)\bigr)n^2$. Lenz's construction gives the lower
bound and the Erdős–Stone theorem the upper bound.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/1967_01_01_erdos|1967_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős proved that for even $d=2p\ge4$ and large $n$, the maximum number of
unit distances among $n$ points of $\mathbb{R}^d$ equals $t_p(n)+n$ when $2d$
divides $n$, where $t_p(n)$ is the Turán number. Otherwise it lies within $p$
of that value.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/1990_09_01_erdos_pach|1990_09_01_erdos_pach]]: Erdős and Pach proved that for odd $d\ge5$ and $p=\lfloor d/2\rfloor$, the
maximum number of unit distances among $n$ points of $\mathbb{R}^d$ is
$\frac{p-1}{2p}n^2$ plus a second-order term of exact order $n^{4/3}$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/1997_01_01_brass|1997_01_01_brass]]: Brass's result, with a number-theoretic step completed by van Wamelen, states
that for $n\ge5$ the maximum number of unit distances among $n$ points of
$\mathbb{R}^4$ is $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+n$ when $8$ or $10$ divides $n$, and
one less otherwise.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/2007_07_02_swanepoel|2007_07_02_swanepoel]]: Swanepoel proved that for $d\ge4$ and $n$ large in terms of $d$, the $n$-point
sets in $\mathbb{R}^d$ with the most unit distances are Lenz configurations.
This gives the exact value of $f_d(n)$ for every even $d\ge6$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1085/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 23 September 2026 proves that
$n$ points in the plane determine at most $Cn^{\beta}$ unit distances, for
absolute constants $C$ and $\beta<4/3$. It is accepted on built Lean as a
partial claim, for the plane only.
