# problems/analysis/E0498/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0498/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1945_12_01_erdos|1945_12_01_erdos]]: Erdős proves that for $n$ real numbers of absolute value at least one, at most
$\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ of the signed sums lie in any open interval of
length two. This is Problem 498 for real coefficients, and the paper is
refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1965_08_01_kleitman|1965_08_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman's refereed paper proves that at most $\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$
of the signed sums of $n$ complex numbers of modulus above one lie in a closed
unit disc. By finite scaling, the same holds for moduli at least one and an
open unit disc.

[[problems/analysis/E0498/claims/1970_08_01_kleitman|1970_08_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman's refereed paper resolves Erdős's 1945 Hilbert-space conjecture: for
$n$ vectors of norm at least one, at most $\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ of
the signed sums lie in any open ball of radius one.
