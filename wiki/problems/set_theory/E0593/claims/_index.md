# problems/set_theory/E0593/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0593/claims/2026_06_23_li|2026_06_23_li]]: Li claims that a finite triple system occurs in every triple system of
uncountable chromatic number exactly when, after its isolated vertices are
removed, it is linear, every edge-node of its Levi graph lies on a bridge, and
every Berge cycle is even.

[[problems/set_theory/E0593/claims/2026_07_15_petkov|2026_07_15_petkov]]: Petkov claims, by an alternative proof, the same characterization as Li's
preprint of the finite triple systems forced by uncountable chromatic number.
The proof has its own Lean development, which reuses Li's framework for the
converse.
