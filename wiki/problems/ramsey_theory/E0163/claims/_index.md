# problems/ramsey_theory/E0163/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0163/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0163/claims/2015_05_18_lee|2015_05_18_lee]]: Lee's theorem (2015 preprint, Ann. of Math. 2017) says that every
$d$-degenerate graph $H$ has Ramsey number at most $c(d)|V(H)|$, with $c(d)$
doubly exponential in $d$. This settles the Burr–Erdős conjecture, and the
result is refereed and credited by the curator.
