## What must be established

The decisive question is not whether a suspicious file existed, nor whether a
grader called itself independent. For each suspected source, the reasoning
must establish the actual content received and then test its possible role in
the report. Three distinctions prevent otherwise plausible false rulings:

1. Delivering bytes and copying them for custody need not display their
   contents. A file read becomes an informational exposure through its returned
   content or demonstrated effects, not automatically through its basename.
2. The original report's conclusion and its examiner's delivery messages are
   appropriate subjects for its grader. Unrelated prior-review status inside
   an operating record remains excluded. “Earlier verdict” is insufficient
   without identifying whose verdict and the commission authorizing it.
3. A recorded process output can exceed the text returned to the grader. The
   actual displayed prefix and suffix govern receipt. A later recovery read
   can extend that receipt, but must be shown rather than presumed.

These distinctions are applied independently below. The rulings also assume
the supplied final records are the historical records represented by the
commission. Their chunk identities were checked. That authenticates the
delivered package internally; it is not independent testimony about events
outside its custody boundary.

## Cubic: attacks on the usable-grade conclusion

**Attack: the historical gate was included, so its status claims reached the
grader.** The route does not support this inference. The identity helper
returns byte-comparison results. The later size command returns counts. The
preservation helper copies the manifest's allowed files and returns totals.
None prints the historical gate body. Reading the helpers matters here:
merely observing their names would not establish what they returned. The
actual displayed outputs agree with their restricted output behavior.

**Attack: a displayed success phrase proves exposure to a positive verdict.**
The actual hit is a quoted print string within permitted runtime source. Its
surrounding function and the requesting source read identify it as code. A
string that can be printed when a function succeeds is not a record that the
function ran, much less an independent mathematical acceptance. The returned
text contains no accompanying gate claim-status block.

**Attack: a launch or history retry recovered omitted rows.** All final
call/return pairs were inspected for read routes, including historical-record
decoding, truncation recovery and resumed work. The recoveries concern
authorized reviewer history and operating material. No continuation displays
the gate's hidden body. The startup capture alone could not have established
this: it ends near the start of the task. Its relation to the complete final
record was checked rather than assumed.

**Attack: the grader inherited the reviewed examiner's favorable judgment or
exposure self-waiver.** The original report was the required object of grading,
and the examiner explicitly left the import incident for its distinct grader.
Its outgoing diagnostic message preserves the four failing endpoint
assertions. The grade's relevant work separately tests the existential versus
universal maximizer inference, source deformation, and rectangle warrant;
it retains failed status and zero successful calibration weight. The allowed
report and keeper supply those precise questions. Their presence is not
evidence of an excluded sibling verdict or commissioner direction.

The reviewed report already contains both the adverse translated-cubic source
witness and the explanation that failed endpoint equality tests do not prove
the required enclosure inequalities. The grade's favorable conclusion is
therefore not used as the premise of this eligibility ruling. What matters is
the actual permitted provenance and the absence of a displayed excluded gate
body in the full route. No specific missing mathematical ingredient in the
grade is explained only by that undisplayed file.

**Result.** The proposed disqualifying route fails at actual receipt. No other
excluded content affecting the cubic grade was identified. This justifies
usability on the focused question, without asserting that a file-presence
audit or empty subscriptions establish universal cleanliness.

## Cancellation: attacks on immateriality

Rejected. The [exact excerpt](exhibits/unrelated_claim_excerpt.txt) includes
prior proof-review assertions and some mathematical content. In particular,
the two E0967 assertions claim verified proofs and an independently reviewed
chain. They are excluded information even though the file was delivered for
an operating-history purpose. The exposure requires a distinct materiality
decision.

**Attack: a truncated return leaves the exposure unknowable.** The final record
identifies the actual visible prefix and suffix. The prefix is byte-for-byte
the beginning of the supplied launch file. It includes 38 complete dependency
rows and a cut during the next source-path label; the literal truncation
marker then precedes a different, authorized history tail. Thus the omitted
middle is a known display exclusion, not a missing source original. The raw
process output does not erase the recorded truncation. Later displays repeat
the disclosure, not the omitted claim-status block.

**Attack: the record concealed a favorable P verdict under the claim of no P
verdict.** The later portion does display the reviewed examination's own
favorable P verdict. Its source, role and commissioned inclusion are explicit.
The gate fragment contains no P verdict. The original self-description is
therefore narrowed in the assessment instead of used as an absolute negative.
The authorized P verdict remains evidence to grade, not an outside instruction
to agree with it.

authority.** This is the strongest remaining exposure hypothesis. Other problem

**Attack: finishing the mathematical reading before exposure is itself a

retained failed attack and explicit limits. The same mechanisms persist.
There is no observed gate-derived relaxation or change of attack.

**Attack: the exposure changed the final disposition, so immateriality is
impossible.** It did change the acceptance procedure: the grader disclosed it

immateriality exception. The relevant distinction is between that protective
response and an influence on the substantive refutation work. The observed
record supports the former and supplies no identified instance of the latter.

An adverse administrative outcome is not independently sufficient proof of
rigor. Here it is combined with preserved substantive attacks, retained source
limits and the absence of an imported premise. The ruling is therefore
immateriality on the supplied observable evidence, not a claim to know an
unrecorded mental state.

## Continuation and unavailable evidence

Both retry probes are acknowledgment-only. Each repeated commission has the
same substantive core. Each visible compaction replacement message matches a
previous message in that record. Effective configuration comparisons identify
only retry-control changes, with unchanged model settings and no new
mathematical instruction. This explains their actual relevance rather than
assuming configuration identity alone guarantees independence.

Encrypted reasoning and compacted summaries remain opaque. Uninspected
mailboxes have no inferred contents. The assessment does not use those gaps
as fictitious evidence of either contamination or cleanliness. A concrete
new message or display showing substantive steering would be new evidence
outside the present ruling and would require reconsideration. None is a named
missing input needed to classify the demonstrated gate exposure.

The independent parsers retain meaningful failure witnesses: deleting the
consequential displayed return breaks call/return pairing; changing the first
byte of its displayed source prefix breaks the comparison with the exact
delivered original. These are checks of administrative evidence, not
mathematical experiments. No original command or program was rerun.

## Disposition

The cubic grade is usable because the suspected content was not demonstrated
as displayed and the inspected route supplies no other excluded influence.
The cancellation grade is usable because its actual excluded content has been
classified and found immaterial to its substantive findings, with the
administrative hold acknowledged. The reasons are separate; neither decision
is borrowed from the other.

No replacement is required. The original grades' mathematical limits and the
filing authority's other acceptance obligations remain intact. Exact private
custody reconstruction accompanies the report, while this keeper and the
visible mathematical excerpt can accompany the adopted records without
publishing private operational identities.
