# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1157/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1157/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1157/claims/2026_09_29_chao_huang_liu|2026_09_29_chao_huang_liu]]: An arXiv preprint of Chao, Huang and Liu claims the limit of
$\mathrm{ex}_r(n,\mathcal F)/n^t$ whenever $k=(r-t)s+t$ with $r>t\ge2$, in
closed form depending on the parity of $s$, except for $3$-graphs with even
$s\ge4$, where it shows that the limit exceeds $1/6$. This answers the problem
to first order on that family only.
