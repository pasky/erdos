# problems/integer_sequences/E0748/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0748/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0748/claims/1990_03_01_calkin|1990_03_01_calkin]]: Calkin proves in Bull. London Math. Soc. 22 (1990) that the number of sum-free
subsets of the first $n$ integers is $2^{n/2+o(n)}$, the displayed exponent
form of Problem 748. The result is refereed and is not credited by the
erdosproblems.com curator.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0748/claims/1991_06_01_alon|1991_06_01_alon]]: Alon proves in Israel J. Math. 73 (1991), independently of Calkin, that the
number of sum-free subsets of the first $n$ integers is $2^{n/2+o(n)}$, the
displayed exponent form of Problem 748. The result is refereed and not
credited by erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0748/claims/2003_01_01_sapozhenko|2003_01_01_sapozhenko]]: Sapozhenko proves, independently of Green, that the number of sum-free subsets
of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ is of order $2^{n/2}$, with the same two-valued
asymptotic. The result appeared as a Doklady note in 2003 and in full in
Discrete Math. in 2008.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0748/claims/2003_04_04_green|2003_04_04_green]]: Green proves that the number of sum-free subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ is
asymptotically $c(n)2^{n/2}$, with $c(n)$ depending only on the parity of $n$,
which gives the asked exponent. The paper is refereed (Bull. LMS 2004) and
erdosproblems.com credits it.
