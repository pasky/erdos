# problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/1992_05_01_brown|1992_05_01_brown]]: Brown's refereed 1992 paper in Discrete Math. gives an example of a vertex
$5$-critical graph with no critical edge. This is the case $k=5$, $r=1$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2002_12_01_jensen|2002_12_01_jensen]]: Jensen's refereed 2002 paper in Discrete Math. gives, for every $k\ge5$,
vertex-critical $k$-chromatic graphs in which no set of fewer than $m$ edges
at one vertex is critical. This gives the case $r=1$ for every $k\ge5$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2002_12_01_lattanzio|2002_12_01_lattanzio]]: Lattanzio's refereed 2002 paper in Discrete Math. builds circulant families of
vertex $k$-critical graphs with no critical edge whenever $k-1$ is not prime.
This gives the case $r=1$ for those $k$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2023_10_19_martinsson_steiner|2023_10_19_martinsson_steiner]]: In a refereed paper in Combin. Probab. Comput., Martinsson and Steiner prove
that for every $r$ there is $k_0$ such that for every $k\ge k_0$ some
vertex-critical $k$-chromatic graph has no critical set of at most $r$ edges.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2025_08_12_skottova_steiner|2025_08_12_skottova_steiner]]: Skottova and Steiner's 2025 preprint proves $f_k(n)=\Omega(n^{1/3})$ for every
fixed $k\ge5$, so suitable graphs exist for all $k\ge5$ and all $r$. The
preprint has no journal version and is unreviewed.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_09_chan|2026_09_09_chan]]: Alex Chan's claim, public from 9 September 2026, is an explicit $4$-chromatic
graph on $60$ vertices with every vertex critical and no critical edge, the
$k=4$ case of Dirac's conjecture. This corpus has not built its Lean
certificates.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_10_kitamura|2026_09_10_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura gives an AI-assisted Lean 4 proof, dated 10 September 2026,
that a $4$-chromatic graph on $48$ vertices has every vertex critical and no
critical edge. This is the case $k=4$, $r=1$ of Dirac's conjecture, and the
claim is pending.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0944/claims/2026_09_11_kruer_kohlmeyer|2026_09_11_kruer_kohlmeyer]]: Kruer and Kohlmeyer's Lean 4 proof, certified by Conjectures.io in September
2026, shows that for every $k\ge4$ and $r\ge1$ some $k$-chromatic graph has
every vertex critical and no critical set of at most $r$ edges. The case $k=4$
with $r\ge2$ is new.
