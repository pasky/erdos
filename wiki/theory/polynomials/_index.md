# theory/polynomials

[[theory/_index|..]]

[[theory/polynomials/L144_cubic_lemniscate_length/_index|L144_cubic_lemniscate_length/]]: A uniform ball-arithmetic family inequality and Eremenko-Hayman's
deformation prove the cubic lemniscate bound and its equality case at
tier 1 on an independently graded whole-claim examination.

[[theory/polynomials/L176_quartic_constant_term_comparison/_index|L176_quartic_constant_term_comparison/]]: An explicit family of centered monic quartics with three distinct unit
critical values tends to z^4+1 while its lemniscate is longer than that of
the unicritical quartic with the same constant term. A positive scaled
limiting difference is proved by a geometric estimate on a harmonic curve.

[[theory/polynomials/L177_quartic_maximizer_critical_values/_index|L177_quartic_maximizer_critical_values/]]: Every monic quartic that locally maximizes lemniscate length in coefficient
space has all its critical values on the unit circle. The equality case
in the holomorphic mean-value inequality strengthens the local deformation
used in Eremenko and Hayman's Lemma 5.

[[theory/polynomials/L178_quartic_length_variation/_index|L178_quartic_length_variation/]]: A local motion of three distinct unit critical values gives exact first
and second variations of quartic lemniscate length in terms of arclength
moments of two explicit polynomials. A further formula expresses convexity
in the angular gaps as a pointwise moment inequality.

[[theory/polynomials/L179_quartic_harmonic_deficit/_index|L179_quartic_harmonic_deficit/]]: A centered monic quartic with a nonzero quadratic or linear coefficient
has renormalized harmonic-level length at most minus its coefficient scale
divided by 2500. Two elementary real-root tests give
the explicit uniform constant in a projection argument.

[[theory/polynomials/L180_quartic_small_curvature_deficit/_index|L180_quartic_small_curvature_deficit/]]: Explicit root-window and far-field estimates transfer a quantitative
harmonic-line deficit to every sufficiently flat circle, uniformly over
centered quartic shapes whose largest critical-point modulus is one.
The curvature cutoff is explicit and very small.

[[theory/polynomials/L181_quartic_collision_pencil_deficits/_index|L181_quartic_collision_pencil_deficits/]]: Certified ball arithmetic bounds the rotated length-deficit integral
strictly below -1 on every circle and line through the critical values of
either normalized quartic collision stratum. The strict bound is uniform
up to the flattening limit.

[[theory/polynomials/L182_quartic_critical_value_mixtures/_index|L182_quartic_critical_value_mixtures/]]: Arbitrarily near a unicritical quartic, a quartic with three distinct unit
critical values admits no pointwise upper bound by a convex combination
of unicritical inverse densities centered at those values. Two opposite
tails of an explicit collision limit give the obstruction.

[[theory/polynomials/L183_quartic_variance_coefficient_bound/_index|L183_quartic_variance_coefficient_bound/]]: Five Laurent modes and an orthogonal projection give an explicit lower
bound for the arclength variance of the quartic length-variation
polynomial. The bound is strictly below the needed value at the
unicritical extremizer, so it does not settle the full sign condition.

[[theory/polynomials/L184_quartic_petal_length_obstruction/_index|L184_quartic_petal_length_obstruction/]]: The component containing zero for z^4+4*3^(-3/4)z has all three critical
points on its boundary. Their equilateral triangle gives a perimeter
bound greater than one quarter of the length of |z^4-1|=1.

[[theory/polynomials/L186_quartic_critical_value_parameters/_index|L186_quartic_critical_value_parameters/]]: Explicit compact coordinates cover centered monic quartics with unit critical values, including point and value collisions; the unicritical length comparison is also proved.

***

Each claim states its hypotheses, conclusion and evidence standing. The cubic
lemniscate claim combines a written reduction with a ball-arithmetic
computation whose coverage of the parameter range is checked by its evidence
program. The quartic claims treat degree four: coordinates, comparisons,
variation formulas, deficits and obstructions for the length of quartic
lemniscates.
