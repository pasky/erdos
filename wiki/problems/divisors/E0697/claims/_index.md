# problems/divisors/E0697/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0697/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0697/claims/1992_11_01_hall|1992_11_01_hall]]: Hall proves that the density of integers with a divisor $d$ such that
$1<d<\exp(m^\alpha)$ and $d\equiv1\pmod m$ tends to $0$ for $\alpha<1/\log2$
and to $1$ for $\alpha>1/\log2$. So the threshold Erdős conjectured exists.
