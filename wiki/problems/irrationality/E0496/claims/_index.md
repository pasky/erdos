[[problems/irrationality/E0496/claims/1989_01_01_margulis|1989_01_01_margulis]]: Margulis's theorem on indefinite irrational ternary forms gives, for every
irrational $\alpha>0$, positive integers $x,y,z$ with $|x^2+y^2-z^2\alpha|$
arbitrarily small. The site's curator records the problem as proved.

[[problems/irrationality/E0496/claims/2026_08_21_alexeev|2026_08_21_alexeev]]: A Lean theorem in Alexeev's repository, by Codex and GPT-5.6 Sol, refutes the
site's wording (every irrational $\alpha$) at $\alpha=-\sqrt2$ and
$\epsilon=1$. It does not answer the corrected Statement ($\alpha>0$), so it
does not count toward the problem's standing.
