[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0193/claims/2026_08_28_kalviainen|2026_08_28_kalviainen]]: Kalviainen's earlier construction takes points of a discrete Hilbert curve,
one per block of sixteen indices, and lifts each to its index as height. The
construction is claimed to give an infinite bounded-step walk with no three
collinear points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0193/claims/2026_09_01_cambie_kalviainen|2026_09_01_cambie_kalviainen]]: Cambie and Kalviainen build a Gaussian-integer walk: an infinite sequence in
$\mathbb{Z}^3$ with at most sixteen distinct steps and no three collinear
points. This answers the question negatively.
