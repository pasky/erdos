# problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/claims/2026_03_19_bucic_chen_ma|2026_03_19_bucic_chen_ma]]: Theorem 1.2 of the 2026 preprint of Bucić, Chen and Ma shows that, one edge
past the Turán number, the maximal anti-Ramsey function of $C_{2k+1}$ is
asymptotically $n^2/8$ for every $k\ge4$. It is unrefereed and formalized in
the $k\ge4$ branch of L17.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/claims/2026_09_24_plasma_ai|2026_09_24_plasma_ai]]: The project published its kernel-checked Lean proof on GitHub on 24 September
2026 and posted it to erdosproblems.com as Plasma AI on 27 September 2026. It
shows that for every $k\ge3$ the maximal anti-Ramsey function of $C_{2k+1}$,
one edge past the Turán number, is asymptotically $n^2/8$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/claims/2026_09_27_shahab|2026_09_27_shahab]]: In a proof claim of 27 September 2026, Asad Shahab proves that, one edge past
the Turán number, the maximal anti-Ramsey function of the seven-cycle is
asymptotically $n^2/8$. It comes with a Lean development for every $k\ge3$,
built and audited here.
