# problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2025_01_17_chan|2025_01_17_chan]]: Chan proves that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
$x^3-1=p^3y^2$, $x^3$, $x^3+1=q^3z^2$ with $p,q$ prime. This is a partial no,
refereed in Integers (2025).

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2025_07_07_she|2025_07_07_she]]: She proves that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
$x^3-1=p^2a^3$, $x^3$, $x^3+1=q^2b^3$ with $p,q$ prime. This is a partial no,
refereed in Integers (2025).

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2026_06_12_sayim|2026_06_12_sayim]]: Sayim claims that no triple of consecutive powerful numbers centered at a cube
has outer members of the mixed shapes $p^2a^3$ and $q^3z^2$, the two cases
Chan and She left open. The result is partial and leaves the question open.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0364/claims/2026_08_24_ma|2026_08_24_ma]]: Ma claims that no three consecutive powerful numbers have the form
$x^n-1=q_1^3y^2$, $x^n$, $x^n+1=q_2^3z^2$ with $q_1,q_2$ prime and $n\ge5$
built from primes $p\equiv5\pmod8$. This is a partial no, and it is
unrefereed.
