# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/1968_01_01_lovasz|1968_01_01_lovasz]]: Lovász proves that a graph with at most one vertex of even degree decomposes
into at most $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths, as later papers quote the result. The
source is a proceedings paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/1996_01_01_pyber|1996_01_01_pyber]]: Pyber proves, in a refereed paper in J. Combin. Theory Ser. B, that a graph in
which every cycle contains a vertex of odd degree decomposes into at most
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2004_11_11_fan|2004_11_11_fan]]: In a refereed paper in J. Combin. Theory Ser. B, Fan proves that a graph
decomposes into $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths when its even-degree vertices
induce an $\alpha$-graph. The $\alpha$-graphs include the forests and the
graphs whose blocks are triangle-free of maximum degree at most $3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2016_09_20_bonamy_perrett|2016_09_20_bonamy_perrett]]: Bonamy and Perrett prove Gallai's conjecture for connected graphs of maximum
degree at most $5$, in a refereed paper in Discrete Mathematics.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2021_08_24_blanche_bonamy_bonichon|2021_08_24_blanche_bonamy_bonichon]]: Blanché, Bonamy and Bonichon claim that every connected planar graph other
than $K_3$ and $K_5$ minus an edge decomposes into $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths.
The claim rests on an extended abstract and a 95-page preprint, with no
journal version.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2022_11_14_anto_basavaraju|2022_11_14_anto_basavaraju]]: Anto and Basavaraju prove that every connected $2$-degenerate graph other than
the triangle decomposes into at most $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ paths, in a refereed
paper in DMTCS.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2025_08_18_chu_fan_zhou|2025_08_18_chu_fan_zhou]]: Chu, Fan and Zhou prove, in a refereed paper in Discrete Mathematics, that a
graph whose even-degree vertices induce $K_m$ with $m\le15$ decomposes into at
most $\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1$ paths. For odd $n$ this is Gallai's bound.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2026_08_31_sallerk|2026_08_31_sallerk]]: A forum comment of August 2026 by the account sallerk reports an exhaustive
computer check that every connected graph on at most eleven vertices splits
into at most $\lceil n/2\rceil$ edge-disjoint paths. The check was reproduced
in the thread and is unreviewed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/claims/2026_09_17_herong|2026_09_17_herong]]: A forum comment of September 2026 by the account herong reports an independent
decider and exhaustive sweeps showing that every connected graph on at most
eleven vertices meets Gallai's bound. The comment is unreviewed.
