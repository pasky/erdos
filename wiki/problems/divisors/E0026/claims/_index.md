# problems/divisors/E0026/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0026/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1951_01_01_davenport_erdos|1951_01_01_davenport_erdos]]: Davenport and Erdős showed, in a result the site credits, that the multiples
of a sequence with convergent reciprocal sum have a density, the limit over
its finite stages. That limit is below one, so every shift of such a set
fails.

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/1995_01_01_ruzsa|1995_01_01_ruzsa]]: Ruzsa's explicit infinite sequence is built by the Chinese remainder theorem,
and each of its shifted copies misses the divisors of a whole arithmetic
progression. Erdős reported it in 1995, and others formalized it in Lean.

[[problems/divisors/E0026/claims/2026_04_06_deepmind|2026_04_06_deepmind]]: A Lean proof by the DeepMind prover agent gives an increasing sequence with
divergent reciprocal sum, none of whose shifts has multiples of lower density
above $1/2$. It refutes Tenenbaum's variant and the question.
