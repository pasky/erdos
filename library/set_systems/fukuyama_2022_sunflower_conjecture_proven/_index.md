# The Sunflower Conjecture Proven

[[set_systems/_index|..]]

***

Junichiro Fukuyama, *The Sunflower Conjecture Proven*,
[arXiv:2212.13609v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13609v3), revised
22 June 2026.

The manuscript claims an exponential upper bound in the uniformity for each
fixed number of petals. Its Section 2 uses a spread reduction, counts of
intersection sequences, and a core-selection argument. This is an unrefereed
proof claim; the claim alone supplies no accepted resolution.

The arXiv record read on 15 October 2026 lists its non-exclusive distribution
license; other rights are reserved. No file of this source is held.

**Read status.** The abstract and bibliographic record were read. The
parameter setup and sequence definitions were read in the PDF text. Lemma 2.1,
equation (2.3) and the Case 3 argument on printed page 4, and equation (2.4)
on printed page 5, were checked against page images. No complete proof review
or independent acceptance is asserted.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/set_systems/E0020/_index|Problem 20]]:
the paper claims the full exponential sunflower bound and its sequence
selection is a subject of proof examination.
