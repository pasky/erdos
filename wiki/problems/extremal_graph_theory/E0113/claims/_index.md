[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/claims/2021_09_13_janzer|2021_09_13_janzer]]: Janzer constructs, for every $\eta>0$, a finite 3-regular bipartite graph $H$
with Turán number $O(n^{4/3+\eta})$. Such an $H$ is not 2-degenerate, so the
Erdős–Simonovits equivalence of Problem 113 is false.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/claims/2026_08_01_openai|2026_08_01_openai]]: Theorem 1.2 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI's 2026 report gives a connected bipartite
2-degenerate graph $H$ with $\mathrm{ex}(n,H)\ge c\,n^{3/2+\varepsilon}$. So
the forward implication of Problem 113 fails, and the equivalence is false.
