[[problems/polynomials/E1132/claims/2026_09_05_gu|2026_09_05_gu]]: A write-up of September 2026 claims that the Lebesgue function satisfies
$\limsup_n L_n(x)/\log n\ge2/\pi$ almost everywhere. It also claims, with a
constant $C(x)$ depending on the point, a dense set where
$L_n(x)>(2/\pi)\log n-C(x)$ infinitely often.
