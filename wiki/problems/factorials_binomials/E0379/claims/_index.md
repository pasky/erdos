# problems/factorials_binomials/E0379/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0379/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0379/claims/2025_08_28_cambie_kovac_tao|2025_08_28_cambie_kovac_tao]]: For a suitable power of two $n$, every binomial coefficient in row $n$ is
divisible by the $r$-th power of $2$ or of a chosen prime, so $S(n)$ is
unbounded. Cambie, Kovač and Tao worked this out in the erdosproblems.com
discussion thread, and Tao formalized it in Lean.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0379/claims/2025_12_21_yuan|2025_12_21_yuan]]: Zheng Yuan posted on 21 December 2025 a Lean 4 proof, produced by Seed-Prover
1.5, that $S(n)$ is unbounded, by a construction different from Cambie, Kovač
and Tao's. A version of its Lean code was built here.
