# problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims/1979_01_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov|1979_01_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov]]: Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov's 1979 theorem, proved in full in Khadzhiivanov's
1988 account, says that more than $n^2/4$ edges force an edge on at least
$n/6$ triangles. So in Problem 80 the forced book is linear above density
$1/4$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0080/claims/2011_06_01_fox_loh|2011_06_01_fox_loh]]: Theorem 1.1 of Fox and Loh's refereed paper (Combinatorica 2012) shows that,
for every fixed density below $1/4$, the largest forced book is $n^{o(1)}$. So
the first closing question of Problem 80 has the answer no.
