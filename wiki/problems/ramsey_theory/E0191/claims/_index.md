# problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/claims/2003_04_23_rodl|2003_04_23_rodl]]: Rödl's 2003 paper proves that the least weight of a monochromatic clique
forced by every two-coloring of the pairs of $\{2,\ldots,n\}$ tends to
infinity with $n$, so the answer is yes. The paper is not held here.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0191/claims/2011_12_07_conlon_fox_sudakov|2011_12_07_conlon_fox_sudakov]]: Theorem 1.1 of Conlon, Fox and Sudakov (Duke Math. J. 2013) shows that for
large $n$, every two-coloring of the pairs of $\{2,\ldots,n\}$ has a
monochromatic clique of weight at least $2^{-8}\log\log\log n$. So the answer
is yes.
