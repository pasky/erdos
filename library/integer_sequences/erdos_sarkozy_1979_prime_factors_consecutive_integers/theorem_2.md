# Theorem 2 — a lower bound for the first rough block

***

For an integer $a>1$, let $P(a)$ be its largest prime factor. Define $n_k$
to be the least integer $n$ such that $P(n+i)>k$ for every integer
$1\le i\le k$. There is an absolute $k_0$ such that

$$
n_k>\frac{k^{5/2}}{16}\qquad(k>k_0).
$$

This is Theorem 2, p. 198, of
[[integer_sequences/erdos_sarkozy_1979_prime_factors_consecutive_integers/_index|Erdős and Sárközy (1979)]],
with $n_k$ defined on p. 197. Section 3, beginning on p. 212, supplies the
proof. **Read status: claims checked:** the definition, strict inequalities,
constant and sufficiently-large quantifier were checked in the printed
page images; the proof has not been verified here.
