# problems/divisors/E0164/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0164/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0164/claims/2022_02_04_lichtman|2022_02_04_lichtman]]: Lichtman proves that $\sum_{a\in A}1/(a\log a)$ over any primitive set $A$ is
at most its value over the primes, $1.6366\ldots$, settling the Erdős
primitive set conjecture. The proof was published in Forum of Mathematics, Pi
in 2023.

[[problems/divisors/E0164/claims/2026_05_01_alexeev_barreto_li_lichtman_price_shah_tang_tao|2026_05_01_alexeev_barreto_li_lichtman_price_shah_tang_tao]]: Alexeev and coauthors give a second proof that the primes maximize
$\sum_{a\in A}1/(a\log a)$ over primitive sets $A$, using a Markov chain on
the divisibility order with von Mangoldt weights. A Lean formalization of a
variant by the first author accompanies it.
