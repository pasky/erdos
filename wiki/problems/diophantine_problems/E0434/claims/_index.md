# problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims/2003_02_13_kiss|2003_02_13_kiss]]: Kiss proves that among coprime $k$-element subsets of the first $n$ integers,
with $k\ge2$, the set of the $k$ largest leaves the most positive integers
without a representation. This answers Problem 434 affirmatively.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0434/claims/2026_02_24_joshuab|2026_02_24_joshuab]]: An AI-assisted Lean proof, posted to the erdosproblems.com forum, that the $k$
largest of the first $n$ integers leave the most gaps, for $2\le k\le n$. It
argues interval by interval from Dixmier's Theorem 2 rather than through
Kiss's count.
