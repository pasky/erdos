# problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/claims/2018_04_08_de_grey|2018_04_08_de_grey]]: De Grey exhibits finite unit-distance graphs in the plane with no proper
$4$-coloring, the smallest on $1581$ vertices, so the chromatic number of the
plane is at least five. This refereed bound has since been superseded by the
bound six.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/claims/2026_05_13_reed|2026_05_13_reed]]: Reed's public manuscript announces $\chi(\mathbb R^2)=7$ through a density
bound on unit-independent subsets of a circle. It is rejected because OpenAI's
release preprint records that the bound fails for a half-open arc and that the
formal theorem assumes it.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: OpenAI proves that every coloring of the plane with five colors, with no
regularity assumed, has two points at distance one of the same color, and
seven colors suffice. This partial claim's kernel-checked Lean proofs were
built and axiom-audited here.
