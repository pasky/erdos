# problems/ramsey_theory/E0172/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0172/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0172/claims/1979_01_01_hindman|1979_01_01_hindman]]: Theorems 4.3 (Graham) and 4.4 of Hindman (Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 1979) show
that every two-coloring of the positive integers has distinct $x,y$ with $x$,
$y$, $x+y$ and $xy$ in one color. This is the case of two colors and $|A|=2$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0172/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: The OpenAI release's preprint of 23 September 2026 claims the full statement,
that every finite coloring of the positive integers has $m$ distinct integers
whose nonempty subset sums and products all lie in one color.
