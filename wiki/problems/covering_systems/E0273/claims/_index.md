# problems/covering_systems/E0273/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0273/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0273/claims/2026_07_11_ideal_ombrer|2026_07_11_ideal_ombrer]]: A pseudonymous research note of July 2026, posted on the erdosproblems.com
thread, claims a proof that every covering system with distinct moduli $p-1$,
$p\ge5$ prime, uses a modulus with $p>877$. It also claims a bound on the
reciprocal sum.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0273/claims/2026_07_26_zeraoulia|2026_07_26_zeraoulia]]: Rafik Zeraoulia claims, in a partial proof deposited 2026-07-26, that a
covering system with distinct moduli all of the form $p-1$, $p\ge5$ prime, has
moduli with least common multiple at least 393120. Whether such a system
exists stays open.
