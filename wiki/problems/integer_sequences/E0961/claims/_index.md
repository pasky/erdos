# problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1934_10_01_erdos|1934_10_01_erdos]]: Erdős gives a short proof of the theorem of Sylvester and Schur that among any
$k$ consecutive integers above $k$ there is one with a prime factor greater
than $k$. This is the upper bound $f(k)\le k$.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1955_01_01_erdos|1955_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős proves that for some constant $c>1$, every block of $ck/\log k$
consecutive integers above $k$ contains a multiple of a prime greater than
$k$.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0961/claims/1973_01_01_jutila_ramachandra_shorey|1973_01_01_jutila_ramachandra_shorey]]: Jutila, Ramachandra and Shorey bound $f(k)$ by
$c_1k\log\log\log k/(\log k\log\log k)$ for a constant $c_1$. This is the
record upper bound, as Erdős's 1976 survey attests.
