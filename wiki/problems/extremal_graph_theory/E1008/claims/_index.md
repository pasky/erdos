# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/claims/2014_01_27_conlon_fox_sudakov|2014_01_27_conlon_fox_sudakov]]: Theorem 2.1 of Conlon, Fox and Sudakov (arXiv 2014) gives every graph with $m$
edges a $C_4$-free subgraph with at least $\frac14m^{2/3}$ edges. It is also
Theorem 3.1 of their refereed paper (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2016), and the
claim is accepted.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/claims/2025_09_13_zach_hunter|2025_09_13_zach_hunter]]: Zach Hunter's forum thread post of 13 September 2025 proves that every graph
with $m$ edges has a $C_4$-free subgraph with at least $\frac12m^{2/3}$ edges.
It uses the deletion method with a sharper count of four-cycles and was
accepted on review.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/claims/2026_01_20_alexeev|2026_01_20_alexeev]]: On 20 January 2026 Boris Alexeev committed a Lean proof that every graph with
$m$ edges has a $C_4$-free subgraph with at least $\frac38m^{2/3}$ edges. The
automated prover Aristotle found the proof from the statement alone.
