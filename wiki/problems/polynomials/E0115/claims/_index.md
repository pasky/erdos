# problems/polynomials/E0115/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0115/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0115/claims/1994_09_01_eremenko_lempert|1994_09_01_eremenko_lempert]]: Eremenko and Lempert prove that if $p$ is monic of degree $n$ and
$\{\lvert p\rvert\le1\}$ is connected, then $\lvert p'\rvert\le2^{1/n-1}n^2$
on that set, with equality for a shifted Chebyshev polynomial. The result is
refereed and credited by the site.
