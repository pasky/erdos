# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/claims/1965_03_01_moon_moser|1965_03_01_moon_moser]]: Moon and Moser (Israel J. Math. 1965) bound $g(n)$ below by
$n-[\log n]-2[\log\log n]-4$ and above by $n-[\log n]$, with logarithms to
base 2. The paper is refereed, and its upper bound is the upper half of the
estimate $g(n)=n-\log_2n+O(1)$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/claims/1966_12_01_erdos|1966_12_01_erdos]]: Erdős (Israel J. Math. 1966) proves $g(n)\ge n-\log_2n-H(n)-O(1)$, where
$H(n)$ is the least $k$ for which the $k$-fold iterated logarithm of $n$ is
below 2. This refereed bound is the lower half of the conjectured formula.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/claims/1971_02_01_spencer|1971_02_01_spencer]]: Spencer (Israel J. Math. 1971) builds, for every $N>33000$, a graph on $N$
vertices with at least $N-\log_2N-4$ clique sizes, as the paper states, though
its own counts give one fewer in one case. This refutes the iterated-logarithm
term.
