# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/claims/1961_01_01_posa|1961_01_01_posa]]: Pósa proves that every graph on $n\ge4$ vertices with at least $2n-3$ edges
has a cycle with a chord, so $g_1(n)=2n-4$ for $n\ge4$, the case $k=1$ of
Problem 767. Pósa posed it as Problem 127 of Mat. Lapok 12 (1961).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/claims/2004_04_07_jiang|2004_04_07_jiang]]: Jiang proves that the largest number of edges on $n$ vertices with no cycle
carrying $k$ chords at one cycle vertex is $(k+1)n-(k+1)^2$ for all
$n\ge3k+3$. The result appears in a refereed note in J. Graph Theory 46 (2004)
and is known through its abstract.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/claims/2026_09_14_chen_ning|2026_09_14_chen_ning]]: Chen and Ning claim an exact formula for the largest number of edges on $n$
vertices with no cycle carrying $k$ chords at one cycle vertex, for every $k$
and $n\ge k+2$, with a sharp threshold near $5k/2$. The source is a 2026
preprint with the authors' own Lean development.
