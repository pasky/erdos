# problems/ramsey_theory/E0639/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0639/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0639/claims/2003_07_16_keevash_sudakov|2003_07_16_keevash_sudakov]]: Theorem 1.1 of Keevash and Sudakov (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2004) gives, for
every $n$, the maximum number of edges of a 2-colored $K_n$ that lie in no
monochromatic triangle. For every $n\ge7$ it is
$\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\le n^2/4$, which proves the bound for large $n$.
