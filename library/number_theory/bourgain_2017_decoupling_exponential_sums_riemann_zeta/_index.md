# Decoupling, exponential sums and the Riemann zeta function

[[number_theory/_index|..]]

[[number_theory/bourgain_2017_decoupling_exponential_sums_riemann_zeta/theorem_6|theorem_6]]: Theorem 6 of arXiv 1408.5794v2 gives the exponent pair with coordinates 13/84 plus epsilon and 55/84 plus epsilon, under the usual smooth-phase convention.

***

Jean Bourgain, *Decoupling, exponential sums and the Riemann zeta function*,
Journal of the American Mathematical Society **30** (2017), 205–224,
[DOI 10.1090/jams/860](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/860).
The edition read is [arXiv:1408.5794v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.5794v2),
revised 22 February 2016. The arXiv record specifies its non-exclusive
distribution license, which does not grant open redistribution. No file of
this source is held.

The paper uses decoupling to estimate a mean value arising in the
Bombieri–Iwaniec method. The relevant reusable conclusion is
[[number_theory/bourgain_2017_decoupling_exponential_sums_riemann_zeta/theorem_6|Theorem 6]],
in Section 5: for each $\varepsilon>0$,
$(13/84+\varepsilon,55/84+\varepsilon)$ is an exponent pair.
Its use requires the usual phase-class hypotheses, including control of
sufficiently many derivatives. Bounds on only a few derivatives do not by
themselves provide that interface.

**Read status: claims checked.** The introduction and all of Section 5 were
read in the arXiv v2 TeX source. Theorem 6 and its reduction to complete
intervals and the complementary parameter range were read for their
hypotheses and argument structure. The decoupling proof, the earlier
exponential-sum estimates, and the cited exponent-pair theory were not
independently verified. This is no proof or original-PDF fidelity warrant.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/integer_sequences/E0962/_index|Problem 962]]:
Theorem 6 is a source for stronger exponential-sum bounds in a uniform
smooth-integer interval argument. A deduction must check the complete
phase class and retain the uniformity of its constants; the theorem alone
does not give the conjectured subpower estimate.
