# problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/claims/1977_03_01_chvatal|1977_03_01_chvatal]]: Chvátal's refereed paper (J. Graph Theory 1977) proves $R(T,K_m)=(m-1)(n-1)+1$
for every tree $T$ on $n$ vertices. This is the case of Problem 550 in which
every class has one vertex.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/claims/1985_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1985_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: Theorem 1 of the refereed 1985 Combinatorica paper gives
$R(T,K(m_1,\ldots,m_k))=(k-1)(n-1)+m_1$ for every large tree of bounded
maximum degree. This yields the inequality of Problem 550 for those trees.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/claims/1989_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1989_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: The Theorem of the 1989 multipartite graph-tree paper (Ann. New York Acad.
Sci. 576) proves the inequality of Problem 550 for large trees when the
smallest class has size 1. It is a proceedings paper and is recorded as
claimed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0550/claims/2026_06_22_li|2026_06_22_li]]: Theorem 1.1 of Li's 2026 preprint claims the inequality of Problem 550 for
fixed class sizes and every large tree. The preprint is unrefereed, and its
Lean developments have not been built by this corpus.
