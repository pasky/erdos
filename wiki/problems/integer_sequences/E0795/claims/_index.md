[[problems/integer_sequences/E0795/claims/2025_01_06_raghavan|2025_01_06_raghavan]]: Raghavan proves that the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ with distinct
subset products has $\pi(n)+\pi(n^{1/2})+O(n^{5/12})$ members, which answers
the question yes. The paper is refereed in Acta Mathematica Hungarica and
credited by the erdosproblems.com curator.
