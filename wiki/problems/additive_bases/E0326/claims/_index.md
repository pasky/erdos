# problems/additive_bases/E0326/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0326/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0326/claims/2026_04_16_chojecki|2026_04_16_chojecki]]: Answers a variant (erdosproblems.com's earlier wording: whether every basis of
order $2$ has a sub-basis with no limit of $a_k/k^2$), not the Statement (a
minimal basis with $a_k/k^2\to c\ne0$), so it does not count toward the
problem's standing. Przemek Chojecki's note, credited to GPT-5.4, claims that
the integers with ternary digits $0$ and $1$ are a counterexample.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0326/claims/2026_05_20_bhalla|2026_05_20_bhalla]]: Aron Bhalla's manuscript of 2026-05-20, revised on 2026-06-14 with a Lean
formalization, constructs a minimal asymptotic basis of order $2$ with
$A(x)=C\sqrt x+O(1)$, so $a_k/k^2$ tends to a nonzero constant. The Lean is
not built in this corpus.
