# problems/set_theory/E0597/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0597/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0597/claims/1971_09_01_erdos_hajnal|1971_09_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Erdős and Hajnal (Period. Math. Hungar., 1971) proved in ZFC that
$\omega_1^2\to(\omega_1\omega,3)^2$. This answers the finite question for the
triangle, and so for every graph on at most three vertices.

[[problems/set_theory/E0597/claims/1989_01_01_baumgartner|1989_01_01_baumgartner]]: Baumgartner (Lecture Notes in Math. 1401, 1989) proved under Martin's axiom
for $\aleph_1$ dense sets that $\omega_1\omega\to(\omega_1\omega,n)^2$ for all
finite $n$. So the finite question holds in a model of ZFC for every finite
graph.

[[problems/set_theory/E0597/claims/2026_07_27_white|2026_07_27_white]]: White and Claude claim, in ZFC, the relation for every finite $K_4$-free block
graph, and a reduction of the finite question to the $2$-connected $K_4$-free
graphs. Among these, $C_4$ and $K_4-e$ are the first cases left open.

[[problems/set_theory/E0597/claims/2026_09_09_li|2026_09_09_li]]: Granted Baumgartner's relation, Li claims a negative answer to the first
question: a bipartite target on $\aleph_1$ vertices with no
$K_{\aleph_0,\aleph_0}$ subgraph for which the relation fails.
