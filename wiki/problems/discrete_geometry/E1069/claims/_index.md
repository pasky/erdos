# problems/discrete_geometry/E1069/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1069/claims/1983_09_01_szemeredi_trotter|1983_09_01_szemeredi_trotter]]: Theorem 2 of Szemerédi and Trotter shows that for $2\le k\le\sqrt n$, any $n$
points in the plane determine fewer than $cn^2/k^3$ lines containing at least
$k$ of them. It was refereed in Combinatorica and is credited by the site's
curator.
