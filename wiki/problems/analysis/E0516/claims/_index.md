# problems/analysis/E0516/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0516/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0516/claims/1954_12_01_erdos_macintyre|1954_12_01_erdos_macintyre]]: Erdős and Macintyre prove that an entire lacunary series with
$\sum_k1/(n_{k+1}-n_k)<\infty$ has $\limsup m(r)/M(r)=1$, which gives the
question's equality on that subclass. The paper is refereed in Proc. Edinburgh
Math. Soc.

[[problems/analysis/E0516/claims/1963_12_01_fuchs|1963_12_01_fuchs]]: Fuchs proves that an entire function of finite order whose exponents satisfy
$n_k/k\to\infty$ has $\log m(r)>(1-\epsilon)\log M(r)$ outside a set of
logarithmic density zero, for every $\epsilon>0$. The paper is refereed in
Illinois J. Math.

[[problems/analysis/E0516/claims/1965_06_01_kovari|1965_06_01_kovari]]: Kővári proves that an entire function of any order whose exponents satisfy
$n_k>k(\log k)^{2+c}$ for some $c>0$ has $\limsup\log m(r)/\log M(r)=1$. This
covers the finite-order functions among them, and the paper is refereed in
Michigan Math. J.
