# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0867/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0867/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0867/claims/1993_01_01_freud|1993_01_01_freud]]: Freud's 1993 note builds, for $N=144y-12$, a set of $76y-7$ integers up to $N$
in which no member is a sum of consecutive members, so the answer is no. It is
accepted on the site's credit and on the refereed paper that builds on it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0867/claims/1996_05_01_coppersmith_phillips|1996_05_01_coppersmith_phillips]]: Theorem 2.1 of Coppersmith and Phillips (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 1996) gives a
set of $13N/24-O(1)$ integers up to $N$ in which no member is a sum of
consecutive members, a second disproof. The paper also proves the upper bound
of its Theorem 3.7 and is accepted as refereed.
