# problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/1986_09_01_berger_felzenbaum_fraenkel|1986_09_01_berger_felzenbaum_fraenkel]]: Corollary IV of Berger, Felzenbaum and Fraenkel's 1986 paper in Canad. Math.
Bull. states that a coset partition of a finite nilpotent group into two or
more cosets has two cosets of the same order. It is accepted on the refereed
paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/2003_06_05_sun|2003_06_05_sun]]: Theorem 1.1 of Sun's 2004 paper in J. Algebra states that a nontrivial uniform
cover by cosets of subnormal subgroups repeats an index, so no abelian group
has a counterexample. It is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/2018_03_09_margolis_schnabel|2018_03_09_margolis_schnabel]]: Theorem A of Margolis and Schnabel's 2019 paper in Beitr. Algebra Geom. states
that every group of order below 1440 satisfies the Herzog-Schönheim
conjecture. It is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/2024_04_25_akman_sissokho|2024_04_25_akman_sissokho]]: Akman and Sissokho's 2025 paper in Beitr. Algebra Geom. shows that a coset
partition of any group into cosets of two to seven distinct subgroups repeats
an index, so a counterexample needs eight cells. It is accepted on the
refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/2026_08_17_itabe|2026_08_17_itabe]]: Rio Itabe claims an unreviewed computer-assisted proof, with a Lean 4 release,
that every exact partition of a group into two to seventeen cosets of
finite-index subgroups repeats an index. The general question stays open.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0274/claims/2026_10_02_menon|2026_10_02_menon]]: Murali Menon gives an AI-assisted Lean 4 proof, registered with Palomar, that
a partition of a solvable group into two or more cosets repeats an index. It
is unreviewed, has no informal write-up, and is pending.
