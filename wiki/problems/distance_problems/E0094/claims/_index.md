# problems/distance_problems/E0094/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0094/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0094/claims/1995_09_01_lefmann_thiele|1995_09_01_lefmann_thiele]]: Lefmann and Thiele prove that for $n$ plane points with no three on a line,
the squared distance multiplicities sum to $O(n^3)$. The vertices of a convex
polygon form such a set, so the bound Erdős conjectured holds.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0094/claims/2026_01_15_dingding|2026_01_15_dingding]]: The account Dingding, for the SpringSense Innovation Institute, posted a Lean
file generated with Seed-Prover on the site's thread on 15 January 2026. Its
proof gives the cubic bound, but its theorem states only a set-dependent
bound.
