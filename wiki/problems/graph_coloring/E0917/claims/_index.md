# problems/graph_coloring/E0917/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0917/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0917/claims/1970_01_01_toft|1970_01_01_toft]]: Toft's 1970 constructions, refereed in Studia Sci. Math. Hungar., give
$k$-critical graphs with $c_kn^2$ edges for every $k\ge4$, which answers the
first question yes. They also beat the proposed formula for $k\ge6$ not a
multiple of $3$.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0917/claims/2026_09_05_gu|2026_09_05_gu]]: Qiyuan Gu's AI-assisted September 2026 preprint claims $12$-critical graphs
with $(2/5+o(1))n^2$ edges, above the proposed $3n^2/8$ at $k=12$, which would
refute the asymptotic at a multiple of $3$. It is unreviewed and not refereed.
