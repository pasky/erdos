# problems/factorials_binomials/E0405/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0405/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0405/claims/1991_08_01_brindza_erdos|1991_08_01_brindza_erdos]]: In a refereed paper credited by the erdosproblems.com curator, Brindza and
Erdős prove that every solution of $(p-1)!+a^{p-1}=p^k$ has $p$, $a$ and $k$
below one effectively computable absolute constant. So there are finitely many
solutions in all.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0405/claims/1996_01_01_le|1996_01_01_le]]: In a refereed paper of 1996, independent of Yu and Liu's, Le determines every
solution of $(p-1)!+a^{p-1}=p^k$ for odd primes $p$: the three cases
$2!+1^2=3$, $2!+5^2=3^3$ and $4!+1^4=5^2$. erdosproblems.com does not credit
it.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0405/claims/1996_09_01_yu_liu|1996_09_01_yu_liu]]: In a refereed paper credited by the erdosproblems.com curator, Yu and Liu
determine every solution of $(p-1)!+a^{p-1}=p^k$ for odd primes $p$: the three
cases $2!+1^2=3$, $2!+5^2=3^3$ and $4!+1^4=5^2$. The list also has a
third-party Lean proof.
