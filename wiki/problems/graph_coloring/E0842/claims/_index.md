# problems/graph_coloring/E0842/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0842/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0842/claims/1992_05_01_fleischner_stiebitz|1992_05_01_fleischner_stiebitz]]: Theorem 1.1 of Fleischner and Stiebitz's 1992 paper shows that a $4$-regular
graph on $3n$ vertices that decomposes into a Hamiltonian circuit and $n$
disjoint triangles has chromatic number 3. It is refereed and credited by
erdosproblems.com as the solution.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0842/claims/1993_01_01_sachs|1993_01_01_sachs]]: Sachs's 1993 chapter in an edited volume gives an independent elementary proof
that a cycle-plus-triangles graph has an odd number of color-class partitions
into three independent sets. So such a graph is $3$-colorable.
