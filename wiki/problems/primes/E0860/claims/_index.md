# problems/primes/E0860/claims

[[problems/primes/E0860/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/1980_06_13_erdos_pomerance|1980_06_13_erdos_pomerance]]: Erdős and Pomerance (Indag. Math. 1980) prove
$h(n)\ll n^{3/2}/(\log n)^{1/2}$, the upper bound that the erdosproblems.com
commentary records. The result is refereed and partial.

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/1980_06_13_erdos_selfridge|1980_06_13_erdos_selfridge]]: Erdős and Selfridge's lower bound $h(n)>(3-o(1))n$, by Brun's method, is
recorded by Erdős and Pomerance (1980) and by Guy without a printed proof. The
claim is pending.

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/1995_01_01_ruzsa|1995_01_01_ruzsa]]: Ruzsa (Studia Sci. Math. Hungar. 1995) builds sets of $n$ primes with few
multiples in long intervals, which gives $h(n)/n\to\infty$. The result is
refereed and partial.

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/2026_07_26_korsky|2026_07_26_korsky]]: Samuel Korsky claims that
$h(n)\ge n\exp\bigl((\tfrac{\log2}{2}-o(1))\tfrac{\log n}{\log\log n}\bigr)$,
by adapting a quadratic-residue compression argument of Green and Ruzsa. The
bound later became Theorem 1.3 of a joint arXiv paper with Chen, and the claim
is pending.

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/2026_07_29_chen|2026_07_29_chen]]: Kaizhe Chen claims $h(n)\ge n\exp(c\log n/\log\log n)$ and $h(n)\ll n^{1.4}$,
with $F(n)\ll n^{1.4031}$ for the $F(n)$ of Problem 711. The second, joint
version of the arXiv paper with Korsky sharpens the upper bounds to $n^{4/3}$,
and the claim is pending.

[[problems/primes/E0860/claims/2026_08_13_chen_korsky|2026_08_13_chen_korsky]]: Theorem 1.2 of the second version of Chen and Korsky's arXiv paper states
$h(n)\ll n^{4/3}/(\log n)^{1/3}$, from a new estimate for unions of arithmetic
progressions. The work was made with ChatGPT-5.6 Sol, and the claim is
pending.
