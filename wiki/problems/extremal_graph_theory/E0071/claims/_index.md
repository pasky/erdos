# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0071/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0071/claims/1977_03_01_bollobas|1977_03_01_bollobas]]: Bollobás (Bull. London Math. Soc. 1977) proves that for odd $k$, linearly many
edges force a cycle of every length modulo $k$. The proof uses a fan lemma
that also gives every even residue modulo an even $k$, and the claim is
accepted on the refereed paper.
