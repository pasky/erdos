# problems/ramsey_theory/E0948/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0948/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0948/claims/1991_02_01_erdos_galvin|1991_02_01_erdos_galvin]]: Theorem 4.1 of Erdős and Galvin (Discrete Math. 1991, refereed) gives, for
every bound, a 2-coloring under which no sequence with monochromatic finite
sums meets the bound even once. It covers the case $k=2$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0948/claims/2026_06_21_price|2026_06_21_price]]: In June 2026 GPT Pro, prompted by Liam Price, produced a disproof: for every
$f$, some coloring of the integers gives every slowly growing sequence subset
sums of every color. erdosproblems.com accepted it after review.
