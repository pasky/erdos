# problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/1964_01_01_graham|1964_01_01_graham]]: In a refereed paper, Graham (1964) determines exactly which pairs $(t,\alpha)$
with $0<t<1$ and $1<\alpha<2$ make the sequence $\lfloor t\alpha^n\rfloor$
complete. These pairs form a region of area about $0.85$, which refutes
Erdős's conjecture.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/2025_09_08_van_doorn|2025_09_08_van_doorn]]: Van Doorn's 2026 preprint, with Graham's results, settles every base at or
above the golden ratio $\varphi$ and classifies entire completeness for bases
up to $5^{1/3}$. It also proves completeness regions below $\varphi$.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/2026_03_09_sothanaphan|2026_03_09_sothanaphan]]: Nat Sothanaphan's note of March 2026, written with GPT-5.2 Thinking, sharpens
van Doorn's infinite-area completeness region by a bounded amount. It also
certifies further rectangles of pairs $(\alpha,t)$ as complete.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/2026_06_10_cepadugato|2026_06_10_cepadugato]]: Lean proofs in a fork of formal-conjectures, contributed under the account
cepadugato in June 2026, show that no pair with base above $2$ or at most $1$
is complete. They also show that among positive integer pairs only $(1,2)$ is
complete.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/2026_09_05_kitamura|2026_09_05_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's unreviewed Lean theorem of September 2026 states that at the
base $\sqrt\varphi$, the square root of the golden ratio, the sequence
$\lfloor t\alpha^n\rfloor$ is complete for every $t>0$. This settles one base
of Problem 349 under every reading.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0349/claims/2026_09_06_geneson|2026_09_06_geneson]]: Jesse Geneson's 2026 note and preprint claim that at one Salem number
$\gamma\in(6/5,13/10)$, arbitrarily large $t$ make every term
$\lfloor t\gamma^n\rfloor$ even, refuting the conjectured completeness for all
$t>0$ below the golden ratio. It was reviewed internally and graded sound,
and its acceptance is pending.
