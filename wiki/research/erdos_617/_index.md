## Where things stand

**Unresolved.** The cases $r=3,4$ are established (Erdős and Gyárfás 1999;
$r=3$ first by Chung and Liu 1978). The cases $5\le r\le11$ are only
claimed, in unrefereed 2026 preprints and artifacts with no review that
counts as acceptance
([Problem 617](../../problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0617/_index.md)). The
results in this folder treat $r\ge12$ as the remaining range, so a proof
completed through them would still leave $5\le r\le11$ resting on those
pending claims. The latest attempt produced a parameter-uniform conditional
clique-completion theorem, but no universal proof, counterexample, or new
unrestricted infinite family of positive cases. The results below retain
their stated additional hypotheses; they are not a solution.

### Established core reductions

A counterexample yields a
[least-budget minimal-ratio core](minimal_ratio_potential.md) with

$$
|H|=ar+1,\quad \alpha(H)=a,\quad \delta(H)\ge r,\quad
\omega(H)\le r-1,\quad
2e(H)\le ar(r-1)+r(r+1),
$$

all hereditary density caps, and
$|W|\le r\alpha(H[W])$ for every proper induced set. Combining
[greedy block charges](greedy_block_charge.md),
[hereditary span](hereditary_span_bound.md), and
[core structure](minimal_core_structure.md) gives

$$
4\le a\le\min\{r-5,\lfloor3r/4\rfloor\},\qquad
r<64a^2,\qquad r<8(r-a)^2.
$$

This unbounded parameter range has not been excluded. Suffix expansion
loses the accumulated deficiency; the renewed concentration argument
did not justify suppressing chains of lighter parts.

The separate [critical-component reduction](critical_component_reduction.md)
has the budget, criticality, $K_r$-freeness, and two-support, but not a
general exception bound $b\le r$ or proper-induced minimality.
The [bounded-exception normalization](two_support_normalization.md)
has full private minimum degree but need not retain the budget.
[Potential-safe private deletion](private_deletion_potential.md) improves
row degrees only under its stated deficit conditions. These general
reductions still cannot be identified.

### New checked results and exact limitations

[Uniform clique completion](uniform_clique_completion.md) removes the
dependence on the total exception count from the weighted completion
argument. For a clique row of order $r-d$, all hereditary caps give
at most $4d-1$ outsiders of normalized row weight at least $1/3$ when
$24d<r+5$. A full family of $a$ light rows in an order-$ar+1$ graph
is therefore impossible under the stated robust transversal conditions,
uniformly in $a$. The needed family of all $a$ rows, or safe suppression
of the heavy remainder, is still missing. The
[topological-density transversal criterion](packing_tools.md) allows
larger cross degrees when pairwise edge counts are bounded, but does
not establish that missing extension hypothesis.

The [near-full-clique star count](cross_color_cores.md) guarantees two
rainbow stars over every monochromatic $K_{r-1}$, and $t(t+1)$ over
a $K_{r-t}$ in the specified quadratic span range. No useful mutual
edge pattern or augmentation of those outsiders has been obtained.

An [anisotropic-norm/Heisenberg template](heisenberg_frame_obstruction.md)
does admit a checked independence certificate on its low-incidence
row, but Hall robustness lets infinity augment it. For prime field
orders, every equivariant clique-component certificate on that row is
also excluded by an orbit count and Turan. No non-clique replacement
or whole-color independence certificate was constructed.

[Semiregular averaging](semiregular_parabola_template.md) now survives
equivariant critical pruning. Within that restricted symmetry class it
combines the budget, criticality, $K_r$-freeness, two-support, and
$b\le r-\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon$ records whether infinity lies
in a full component. It does not give minimum degree $r$ or
proper-induced minimality. None of the explicit square-field completions
has an independence certificate.

[Strong-product counting](construction_attempts.md) excludes every
nontrivial product template with seed palette sizes $s_i$, seed orders
$q_i\le s_i^2+1$, and monochromatic clique caps $s_i$, when
$r=\prod_i s_i\ge196$. Arbitrary puncturing and legal overlap choices
are allowed. This rules out that construction class, not all colorings.

[Unit-row folding](saturated_row_suppression.md) applies to a unit row
with exactly two nonadjacent incident exceptions. It preserves the exact
order/independence relation and one/two-support, and retains any available
budget when the row has private minimum degree $r$. Hereditary caps
can fail under weaker hypotheses, as an explicit example shows.
Their preservation under the full normalized-core hypotheses remains
unproved; the fold is not yet a valid induction.
The [parallel-fold analysis](parallel_unit_row_fold/_index.md) records the
two ordinary lifts, the double-lift cap and the normalized expansion constraints.
Its [near-clique bound](../../theory/combinatorics/L238_normalized_core_near_clique_bound/_index.md)
excludes the one-hole first-cap pattern for $r>2a$. Its finite local
counterexample has all hereditary caps but fails the full normalized hypotheses;
it does not settle fold preservation.

The [saturated-pencil lemma](full_clique_geometry.md), conditional on a
vertex lying in a full component of every color, isolates an uncolored
component and gives clique-valued neighbor fibers. Its neighbor-map
conditions nevertheless admit an auxiliary finite model.
The [rank-preserving cost attachment](light_independent_set_obstruction.md)
gives an exact stress test for low-energy rounding, not a proof or
counterexample to the full threshold. Bounded tests are not certificates.

### Remaining gaps and provisional directions

No argument excludes the surviving cores, supplies unrestricted safe
suppression, or constructs a coloring with every color independence at
most $r$. The [common balancing weights](cross_color_cores.md) still
lack full rank feasibility, and even rank feasibility leaves the
[low-energy rounding gap](light_independent_set_obstruction.md).
The [labeling approach](global_labeling_algebra.md) lacks cooperative
packing and augmentation; [near-full clustering](large_clique_clusters.md)
lacks a valid completion step.

**Provisional only:** a cap-preserving version of the fold, an extension
theorem for light-part dependency chains, or a justified rank-and-cost
repair could be investigated. None has been established. The
[parabola completion criteria](parabola_completion_criterion.md) and
other restricted constructions remain distinct from independence
certificates.
