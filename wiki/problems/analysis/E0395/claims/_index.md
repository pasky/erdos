# problems/analysis/E0395/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0395/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0395/claims/1983_03_01_beck|1983_03_01_beck]]: Beck proves that a random signed sum of $n$ unit vectors in $\mathbb R^d$ lies
in the ball of radius $\sqrt d$ with probability at least a constant times
$n^{-d/2}$. The planar case is the exact question.

[[problems/analysis/E0395/claims/2024_08_20_he_juskevicius_narayanan_spiro|2024_08_20_he_juskevicius_narayanan_spiro]]: He, Juškevičius, Narayanan and Spiro prove that a random signed sum of $n$
planar unit vectors has norm at most $\sqrt2$ with probability at least $c/n$,
the exact question. The result is an arXiv preprint, credited by the site's
curator and not refereed.
