# problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims/1966_01_01_folkman|1966_01_01_folkman]]: Theorem 1.3 of Folkman (1966) shows that a nondecreasing sequence of positive
integers with $a_n\le Mn^{\alpha}$ for some $M$ and some $\alpha<1$ is
subcomplete. This is the case of Problem 343 with counting function of order
above $N$, and the paper is refereed.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0343/claims/2005_07_26_szemeredi_vu|2005_07_26_szemeredi_vu]]: Theorem 6.3 of Szemerédi and Vu (2006) proves the corrected Statement of
Problem 343 in full. There is an absolute constant $C$ such that every
nondecreasing sequence of positive integers with at least $CN$ terms up to
$N$, for all large $N$, is subcomplete.
