# problems/analysis/E0973/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0973/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0973/claims/2026_07_15_luo_yang_zhu|2026_07_15_luo_yang_zhu]]: Luo, Yang and Zhu claim that for every fixed $\lambda>0$ and all large $n$,
complex numbers of modulus at least $1$ have a power sum of index between $2$
and $n+1$ above $e^{-\lambda n}$, so no constant $C>1$ exists. The claim is
unreviewed, with only partial Lean support.

[[problems/analysis/E0973/claims/2026_08_03_tan_wang_huang_chen|2026_08_03_tan_wang_huang_chen]]: Tan, Wang, Huang and Chen claim, from a residual bound for polynomials with
zeros in the closed unit disk, a lower bound $\exp(-(1+o(1))\sqrt n\log n)$
for the largest power sum of index $2$ to $n+1$ of $n$ complex numbers of
modulus at least one. The claim is unreviewed.
