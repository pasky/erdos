**Claim.** For every nondecreasing $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$ with
$f(n)\to\infty$ and $f(n)\le\log n$ eventually, the values

$$
F(f,n)=\frac{\tau((n+\lfloor f(n)\rfloor)!)}{\tau(n!)}
$$

with $n\ge N$ are dense in $(1,\infty)$ for every $N$. Taking
$f(n)=\log n$ answers the second question as well. Plasma AI's
[formalization](https://github.com/plasma-ai/erdos-420)
proves the two statements as `Erdos420.erdos_420.parts.iii` and
`Erdos420.erdos_420.parts.ii`.

**Covers.** The parts `logarithmic_density` and `monotone_density` of
[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0420/_index|Problem 420]]. The first question,
part `divergence`, asking whether $F((\log n)^C,n)\to\infty$ for large
$C$, is not covered.

**Formalization.** The version retained on
[[../library/arithmetic_functions/plasma_ai_2026_divisor_counts_shifted_factorials/_index|the source card]]
pins Lean and Mathlib `v4.35.0-rc4`. Its proof derives a uniform
power-saving prime-number theorem in arithmetic progressions from a vendored
Lean proof of the Dirichlet seven-eighths zero-free theorem, combines a
protected CRT covering with Maynard's sieve and a Selberg upper-sieve tail
bound, and concludes by sliding the factorial window. The zero-free theorem
is a proved dependency, not a hypothesis of the final statements.
`formalization.yaml` credits Jacob Parish as the formalization's author and
records assistance from Claude Code, model `claude-opus-5-5`.
