# problems/additive_bases/E0153/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0153/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0153/claims/2026_05_16_liu|2026_05_16_liu]]: A note of 2026-05-16 by Yu Leon Liu claimed a full proof that the mean squared
sumset gap of Sidon sets tends to infinity. Its one input, a
shifted-intersection bound, was retracted the same day, and the note was
withdrawn.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0153/claims/2026_05_19_liu|2026_05_19_liu]]: A note of May 2026 by Yu Leon Liu proves, through Pikhurko's uniformity lemma,
that the mean squared sumset gap tends to infinity along every sequence of
Sidon sets of size $n$ and diameter $(1+o(1))n^2$. The note is unexamined.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0153/claims/2026_08_14_kapoor|2026_08_14_kapoor]]: A write-up of August 2026 by Rajveer Kapoor bounds the mean squared sumset gap
of a Sidon set below by a constant times $\min\{\log n,\log(1/(\kappa-1))\}$,
where $\kappa$ is the diameter over $n^2$. For sets of nearly minimal diameter
the answer is yes.
