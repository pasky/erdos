# problems/number_theory/E0466/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0466/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0466/claims/1976_01_01_sarkozy|1976_01_01_sarkozy]]: Sárközy's Theorem 1 of 1976 gives $N(X,\delta)>X^{1/2-\delta^{1/7}}$ for small
$\delta$ and large $X$, and the paper also reports Graham's logarithmic
construction. So $N(X,\delta)$ grows without bound as the radius $X$ grows.
