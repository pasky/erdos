# problems/discrepancy/E0989/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0989/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0989/claims/1987_08_25_beck|1987_08_25_beck]]: Beck proved that every infinite plane set has a disc of radius in $[cr,r]$
with discrepancy $\gg r^{1/2}$, and that for each $r$ a periodic set keeps the
discrepancy of every disc of radius at most $r$ within $O((r\log r)^{1/2})$.
The paper is refereed, in Acta Math.
