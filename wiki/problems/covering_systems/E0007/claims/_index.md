# problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/1987_01_01_berger_felzenbaum_fraenkel|1987_01_01_berger_felzenbaum_fraenkel]]: The six-prime corollary of Berger, Felzenbaum and Fraenkel (Acta Arith. 1987)
shows that the least common multiple of the moduli of a distinct odd covering
has at least six distinct prime factors. It is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2017_03_06_hough_nielsen|2017_03_06_hough_nielsen]]: Theorem 1 of Hough and Nielsen's Duke paper (2019) shows that every distinct
covering system has a modulus divisible by 2 or by 3, so an odd covering must
have a modulus divisible by 3. It is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: Theorem 1.4 of Balister, Bollobás, Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Tiba (Inventiones
2022), accepted on the refereed paper, shows that the least common multiple of
the moduli of a distinct covering system is divisible by 2, 9 or 15. So an odd
covering's period is divisible by 9 or 15.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2019_01_31_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2019_01_31_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: Theorem 1.1 of Balister, Bollobás, Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Tiba (Algebra &
Number Theory 2021) shows that every finite covering system with distinct
square-free moduli greater than one has an even modulus. This settles the odd
square-free question and is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_01_11_gebyjaff|2026_01_11_gebyjaff]]: A Lean development posted by gebyjaff on 11 January 2026 and generated with
Archivara and Aristotle claims to derive that no odd distinct covering system
exists from the Hough–Nielsen theorem and an unproved good-fibre axiom.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_05_02_lee|2026_05_02_lee]]: Jinook Lee's five-page note and Lean development of 2 May 2026, audited by
Aristotle, claim that no covering system with distinct odd moduli exists. The
central axiom was shown false as encoded, the author conceded, and the claim
is rejected.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_07_28_mian_siddique|2026_07_28_mian_siddique]]: Mian and Siddique give a July 2026 proof, with a Lean 4 development, that
every covering system with distinct odd moduli greater than one has least
common multiple above 10000. The bound was already known, and the Lean
development has not been built here.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_08_18_egeozturk|2026_08_18_egeozturk]]: A forum announcement claims exact certificates excluding distinct odd coverings with period below 11,486,475 and some families with unbounded prime exponents.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_09_01_schroeder|2026_09_01_schroeder]]: A pending author claim excludes distinct odd covering systems whose periods have fewer than 7 distinct prime divisors, with arbitrary prime powers allowed.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_09_06_schroeder|2026_09_06_schroeder]]: A pending author claim excludes distinct odd covering systems whose periods have fewer than 8 distinct prime divisors, with arbitrary prime powers allowed.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_09_09_schroeder|2026_09_09_schroeder]]: A pending claim excludes distinct odd covering systems when every modulus has at most three prime divisors, without bounding the total prime support.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_09_13_schroeder|2026_09_13_schroeder]]: A pending author claim excludes distinct odd covering systems whose periods have fewer than 9 distinct prime divisors, with arbitrary prime powers allowed.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/claims/2026_10_19_plasma_ai|2026_10_19_plasma_ai]]: A distinct odd family whose classes with 73-smooth moduli leave at least a quarter of the reduced small-prime box uncovered cannot cover the integers, by an exact-loss large-prime tail; author-recorded at tier 0, catalog acceptance pending.
