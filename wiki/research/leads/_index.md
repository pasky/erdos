[[research/leads/aliquot_collision_density/_index|aliquot_collision_density/]]: Problem 955 is equivalent to linear collision bounds after arbitrarily
small input deletions; density-zero deletions cannot suffice. A small
cofactor and large primes give explicit class equations. The large-prime
sieve is proved, with an open large-divisor core. Full equations with
at least three primes have a proved local average but an open global
prime count. A census through 10^10 proves no asymptotic bound.

[[research/leads/balanced_auxiliary_prime_density/_index|balanced_auxiliary_prime_density/]]: Closed (resolved): A balanced auxiliary-prime schedule sharpens the explicit
loss at exponent root six minus two; exact review and grade scopes are
retained.

[[research/leads/binomial_common_prime_indices/_index|binomial_common_prime_indices/]]: Computer-assisted, author-recorded proofs that Problem 699 has no bad
triple at i = 7 or at any i >= 9, building on van Doorn and Rocca's
unreviewed manuscripts. At i = 3 a bad triple needs n > 10^19,
v_2(n) >= 49 and three distinct primes dividing (n-2)/2, and rational abc
gives finiteness. No effective row bound is known at i = 4, 5, 6 or 8.

[[research/leads/borsuk_dimension_63_public_claims/_index|borsuk_dimension_63_public_claims/]]: Closed (superseded): the accepted nine-dimensional counterexample, with the
paper's corollary for every higher dimension, covers the dimension-63
target; the dimension-63 claims and their replay map are kept as history.

[[research/leads/bounded_cost_prime_covers/_index|bounded_cost_prime_covers/]]: An exact gain identity reduces bounded-cost covers to globally coherent residue choices with enough net saving for reserved cleanup. Centered explicit covers have cost log log log N + log 4 + o(1), still unbounded. Incidence restrictions for the first window and a small-prime linear sieve leave both full-solution routes unresolved.

[[research/leads/complete_bipartite_size_ramsey/_index|complete_bipartite_size_ramsey/]]: Two full-solution routes for the size Ramsey number of K(n,n). The direct
route needs an integral overload bound (N0) for colourings of
K(kappa n^2, C 2^n), which would give order n^2 2^n; the conjectured
n^3 2^n needs balanced-host and arbitrary-host colourings. Both remain
open. Affine colourings, a reduction by deleting common neighbourhoods,
failed mechanisms and finite diagnostics are retained.

[[research/leads/concentric_polygon_census/_index|concentric_polygon_census/]]: Closed (refuted): No counterexample to Problem 982 occurs among half-step
concentric regular polygons for 3 <= m <= 32 or the specified diagonal
stretches of regular polygons for 8 <= n <= 24, by an author-recorded
exact critical-parameter census over each full positive parameter domain.

[[research/leads/consecutive_gap_bounds_coincident_points/_index|consecutive_gap_bounds_coincident_points/]]: Extends the 2026 lower bounds for the de Bruijn–Erdős consecutive-gap
constants from sequences of distinct points to the erdosproblems.com family
with coincident terms: a proved replacement for the short-interval list lemma,
the fixed-r ratio bound over all sequences, and the claimed growth bounds
conditionally on the claimed chain; independently reviewed on 2026-09-28.

[[research/leads/distinct_totient_blocks_from_collision_bounds/_index|distinct_totient_blocks_from_collision_bounds/]]: An author-recorded deduction from the uniform shifted-collision bounds of
Pollack, Pomerance and Treviño that almost every block of length below
(log x)^2/(log log x)^50 carries pairwise distinct totients, answering
Problem 1004 for every exponent below 2; the first-moment barrier at
exponent 2 and the every-exponent question remain.

[[research/leads/distinct_totient_preimage_ratio/_index|distinct_totient_preimage_ratio/]]: Conditional on the unrefereed totients manuscript, a continuous phase
quotient, finite-prime sums and a tail factorization reduce Problem 417 to
a cancellation in the cofactor fiber. The bounded tests leave it open: the
direct rectangle comparison misses the tail, and a proposed renewal
comparison needs fiber and error estimates not supplied by the premises.

[[research/leads/dyadic_root_floor_completeness/_index|dyadic_root_floor_completeness/]]: The accepted base-two theorem yields strong completeness at every root 2^(1/m) with m at least two, with the existential and universal base questions kept separate.

[[research/leads/exceptional_widths_shifted_omega/_index|exceptional_widths_shifted_omega/]]: An explicit exceptional-width construction answers the modified omega subquestion negatively while leaving uniform-width formulations and the full classification separate.

***

Each lead records a mathematical approach or unresolved task and the evidence
needed to assess it. A lead can connect several problems, and a problem can have
several leads. The records are intended to grow as sources are compiled and
investigations proceed.

Read each lead's target, evidence, obstacles, next investigation, and review
scope together. Its research state describes the work; it does not establish
the truth of a proposed conclusion or replace a problem's mathematical status.
Closed or unsuccessful routes remain useful when their limitations are recorded.
