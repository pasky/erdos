# problems/polynomials/E0522/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_04_20_chojecki|2026_04_20_chojecki]]: Chojecki, working with GPT-5.4 Pro and GPT-5.5 Pro, claims that almost surely
the number of roots in the closed unit disk is $n/2$ up to a power-saving
error. The claim rests on two notes on the erdosproblems.com thread, revised
after a reader found an error.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_04_28_kwon_zou|2026_04_28_kwon_zou]]: Kwon and Zou, working with ChatGPT 5.5 Pro, claim that almost surely the
number of roots in the closed unit disk is $n/2+O(n^{7/8+\delta})$. The note
is in a public repository, with no review.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_07_23_snyder|2026_07_23_snyder]]: A Lean 4 theorem credited to Colin Snyder of Star Fleet Math states that for
independent fair signs the number $R_n$ of roots in the closed unit disk has
$R_n/(n/2)\to1$ almost surely. It is in the lean-proofs repository, with no
write-up.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_09_25_kawada|2026_09_25_kawada]]: Kawada claims an almost-sure law for the number of roots in the disk of radius
$1+x/n$, whose case $x=0$ is the asked convergence, with a Lean 4
formalization. It rests on a Zenodo preprint and is registered on the
erdosproblems.com tab.

[[problems/polynomials/E0522/claims/2026_09_25_kitamura|2026_09_25_kitamura]]: Kitamura, with AI assistance, gives a Lean 4 proof that $R_n/(n/2)\to1$ almost
surely for the number $R_n$ of roots in the closed unit disk, for coefficients
in $\{-1,1\}$ and in $\{0,1\}$. Formal-conjectures links it, and it has not
been audited.
