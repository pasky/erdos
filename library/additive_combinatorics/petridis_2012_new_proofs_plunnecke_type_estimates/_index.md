## Scope

The introduction's Theorem 1.1 states the Plünnecke sumset bound for finite
sets in an abelian group. In particular, $|A+A|\leq\alpha|A|$ implies
$|hA|\leq\alpha^h|A|$ for positive integers $h$. Theorem 1.2 gives the
corresponding sum-and-difference bound. The paper also treats product sets in
general groups; their extra hypotheses must be retained.

**Read status: claims checked.** The abstract and introductory statements,
including the abelian hypotheses and the common-subset quantifier in Theorem
1.5, were read in the arXiv TeX source. Their proofs have not been checked here.

**Bears on.** [[../wiki/problems/set_systems/E0020/_index|Problem 20]]:
the abelian growth bounds are a prospective input for a sunflower argument on
families encoded by sets with small doubling. The doubling assumption is an
additional restriction; this source does not supply the unrestricted sunflower
bound.
