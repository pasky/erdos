# Routes

[[research/erdos_774/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_774/routes/adaptive_determinant_support|adaptive_determinant_support]]: Binary colorings split every determinant support from prescribed relation menus, even with same-color anchored factors; this does not exclude color-adaptive selection.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/anchor_gluing|anchor_gluing]]: Small-loss gluing improves three-color obstructions, but the Grow source has a checked no-anchor obstruction at every simplex arity, including the boundary.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/anchored_phase_gluing|anchored_phase_gluing]]: A phase correction gives an analytic gluing loss quadratic in the reciprocal number of blocks; diagonal padding shows that small loss does not ensure anchor propagation.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/angular_colorings|angular_colorings]]: Coloring the roots of odd squarefree order by arcs of argument bounds every prime grid by the least degree of a flat cyclotomic multiple, so unbounded coloring of the grids needs signed vanishing sums in arbitrarily short arcs.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/axis_line_coloring|axis_line_coloring]]: Three-direction connectivity and an entropy lower bound are proved, but unbounded dissociated coloring of L(3,4,...,t+2) remains open; full tensor products lose uniform extraction.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/axis_line_extraction|axis_line_extraction]]: Target-independent root-polygon rounding gives a direct uniform Sidon estimate for full axis-line indicators, including sides d_i=i+2; this does not inherit extraction from a full simplex tensor or prove unbounded coloring.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/axis_line_quotient|axis_line_quotient]]: Quotienting the point lattice by all axis lines produces full simplex tensors with an explicit unbounded coloring lower bound; lifting the forced flat point relations through actual line colors remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/axis_line_slice|axis_line_slice]]: Full axis-line indicators have an exact saturated integral presentation and give a smaller coloring target; direct extraction permits linearly growing sides.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/boolean_moments|boolean_moments]]: Critical Boolean moments have uniform hereditary full-dissociation extraction; entropy and restricted-color results do not yet prove unbounded actual coloring.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/bounded_height_flattening|bounded_height_flattening]]: A fixed palette forces uniformly bounded-height relations; Hall-tight determinant supports flatten coefficients safely, but forcing the needed monochromatic support is unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/coefficient_growth_flattening|coefficient_growth_flattening]]: Weighted-simplex subset counting gives a sufficient flattening criterion and a necessary double-exponential height escape; actual-color focusing still lacks the required growth control.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/difference_towers|difference_towers]]: Global seam deletion gives a near-lossless extraction theorem for cyclic difference families; all stages halve integer-relation height, but arbitrary-color growth remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/field_tower_lines|field_tower_lines]]: Field towers, linear flags, and a nested polynomial model retain uniform extraction; exact integer and Fourier formulas and an explicit mixed trade leave arbitrary-color growth unresolved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/fixed_height_focusing|fixed_height_focusing]]: Profile focusing works at one fixed coefficient height with unbounded chromatic number; the proved palette-dependent height theorem does not supply that quantifier.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/generic_voltage_coloring|generic_voltage_coloring]]: Independent incidence variables give flat cofactor relations at Hall deficiency and one finite voltage cover excludes every affine homomorphic q-coloring when the base has more than q times as many columns as rows; arbitrary coloring remains open.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/hadamard_gluing|hadamard_gluing]]: Saturated Hadamard feedback gives cheap chromatic jumps from a fixed anchored source, but sufficiently large order or diagonal padding loses every next-color anchor.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/incidence_cover_extraction|incidence_cover_extraction]]: Arbitrary finite covers of all common row duplications of linear-side axis-line matrices retain one uniform full-dissociation extraction constant; integer relations lift to ternary ones, but no color-unavoidable cover theorem is proved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/mobile_anchor_gluing|mobile_anchor_gluing]]: Triangular buffers give exact saturation and small analytic loss; for finite-threshold sources, buffers above an explicit source-dependent length prevent chromatic increase.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/moment_boundary_extraction|moment_boundary_extraction]]: Semistability and the weak stable-lattice Gaussian bound prove the fixed-scale theta estimate and one hereditary full-dissociation extraction constant at the rounded critical Boolean-moment boundary; coloring remains open.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/moment_gaussian_coloring|moment_gaussian_coloring]]: The logarithmic-scale reverse-Minkowski argument gives actual two-colorings away from the boundary; boundary extraction is proved by a separate argument, while its arbitrary-color growth remains open.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/moment_phase_entropy|moment_phase_entropy]]: A finite Gaussian-width extraction criterion, satisfied at the critical moment boundary by the weak stable-lattice Gaussian theorem.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/moment_phase_lattice|moment_phase_lattice]]: The exact phase-basis criterion remains valid; its basis premise is still open, but a separate weak stable-lattice theta argument proves critical moment extraction.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/parameter_word_limits|parameter_word_limits]]: A finite coloring makes every monochromatic parameter-word image rationally independent under any fixed-alphabet coordinatewise encoding into conductor-disjoint odd-root groups.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/partial_box_compression|partial_box_compression]]: Good monochromatic cells obey a quantitative compression bound; every fixed disjoint-box complexity has a uniform finite coloring, with 16 colors sufficient for two boxes.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/polynomial_fiber_flattening|polynomial_fiber_flattening]]: Prouhet identities flatten bounded coefficients on a profile-monochromatic progression, but that fiber already has its own ternary relation and full polynomial fibers lose uniform extraction; a sparse signed-span target is exactly height-three coloring.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/prime_reset_plateau|prime_reset_plateau]]: Root-span coverage resets maximally at S3 times S5 times S7, whose separation threshold is 3 or 4; ordinary coloring stays exactly three through prime 19.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/prime_tensor_extraction|prime_tensor_extraction]]: Explicit elementary prime estimates extend the tensor theorem to every squarefree prime grid and to all roots of unity additively; unbounded dissociated coloring remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/recurrent_high_height_roots|recurrent_high_height_roots]]: Dissociated root sets can have product-recurrent tails of increasing height independence and infinite rational coloring number; piecewise syndeticity gives an exact stronger reformulation of the unresolved premise.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/resolvable_matchings|resolvable_matchings]]: The phase induction works for arbitrary signed linear direction matchings of sizes i+2; resolvable supports have explicit common covers with boxes, but coloring growth remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/root_profile_compression|root_profile_compression]]: Finite prime-prefix colorings obey a uniform profile-deficit bound; global colorings have summable deficits, but arbitrary distinct profiles remain unexcluded.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/roots_coloring_structure|roots_coloring_structure]]: Finite dissociated coloring of all roots is equivalent to one prime-fiber transversal; equivariant partitions have an exact exponential obstruction that does not cover arbitrary colorings.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/shift_simplicial_gain_checks|shift_simplicial_gain_checks]]: Full shift supports and gain-graph stars have bounded colorings under stated hypotheses; simplicial boundaries at n/k asymptotic to C log n have uniform extraction but an open actual-coloring gap.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/simplicial_extraction|simplicial_extraction]]: The exact exterior-root relation lattice is semistable by an elementary irreducibility proof, giving uniform full-dissociation extraction for n/k asymptotic to C log n; actual coloring growth remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/tensor_coloring|tensor_coloring]]: Signed-span intersections characterize coloring after adding a simplex factor, isolating the missing ingredient in the uniform-extraction construction.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/tensor_extraction|tensor_extraction]]: A randomized lattice-rounding argument gives one uniform full dissociated extraction constant for an explicit tensor family; unbounded dissociated coloring is not proved.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/tensor_holes|tensor_holes]]: A proved root-coverage recurrence amplifies separation thresholds below a critical level, but an explicit arbitrarily large chromatic jump resets coverage maximally.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/tensor_schedule_constraints|tensor_schedule_constraints]]: A small-column entropy argument rules out uniform proportional dissociation when retained tensor sides satisfy p_i≤Ci for a fixed C; a lattice-volume bound explains the phase-lift cost.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/tensor_separation_lifts|tensor_separation_lifts]]: Exact constructions bound separation thresholds after large tensor factors, identifying coefficient doubling and a coarse fixed-base saturation bound.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/translation_cover_automata|translation_cover_automata]]: For a fixed one-dimensional voltage symbol, full ternary coloring is recognized by finite residual-state machines; infinite proper colorings periodicize, cyclic admissible degrees are ultimately periodic, and a sharp factor-two large-degree bridge holds.

[[research/erdos_774/routes/transversal_gadgets|transversal_gadgets]]: Arbitrary row twists preserve the phase mechanism; pure-transversal gluing has a two-witness reset under compatible next-step twists, while matching decorations retain uniform extraction and force three colors but no unbounded amplification.

***

The prime-tensor construction supplies the extraction half of the finite-block
target.
Its dissociated coloring number remains unresolved; the
[arc colorings](angular_colorings.md) bound it by the least degree of a flat
cyclotomic multiple, so unbounded coloring needs signed vanishing sums in
arbitrarily short arcs. The Grow anchor source
is excluded at every arity; different sources remain subject to the
primitive-width and mobile-buffer restrictions recorded here.
