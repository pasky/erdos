# An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets and the limit of the fixed-kernel energy method

[[additive_bases/_index|..]]

***

Chen, Haoyu, *An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets and the limit of the fixed-kernel energy method*, version 3, 10 October 2026. The Zenodo record shortens the title to *An explicit second-order bound for Sidon sets*.

Theorem 2.1 states that a Sidon subset of {1,...,N}, with diagonal pair sums included, has size at most sqrt(N) + (2 sqrt(2)/3) N^(1/4) + 1 for every integer N >= 4,600,000. The abstract also describes a limitation for a specified scalar fixed-kernel capacity method, subject to its stated hypotheses. Neither assertion improves the error exponent to the catalog conjecture. Claims of formal checking and referee reports are source-reported here.
