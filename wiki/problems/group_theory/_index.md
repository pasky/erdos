# Group Theory

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/group_theory/E0117/_index|E0117/]]: Asks for an estimate of $h(n)$,
the least number of Abelian subgroups that suffices to cover every group in
which any set of more than $n$ elements contains two distinct commuting
elements.

[[problems/group_theory/E0543/_index|E0543/]]: Asks whether $f(N)\leq\log_2
N+o(\log\log N)$, where $f(N)$ is the least $k$ such that a random $k$-subset of
an abelian group of order $N$ has subset sums covering the group with
probability at least $1/2$.

[[problems/group_theory/E1098/_index|E1098/]]: Asks whether, if the
non-commuting graph of a group has no infinite complete subgraph, its complete
subgraphs have bounded size. The graph has the group elements as vertices and
joins pairs that do not commute.

[[problems/group_theory/E1160/_index|E1160/]]: Asks whether the number $g(n)$ of
groups of order $n$ satisfies $g(n)\leq g(2^m)$ whenever $n\leq 2^m$.

[[problems/group_theory/E1161/_index|E1161/]]: Asks for which $k$ the number of
permutations in $S_n$ of order exactly $k$ is largest.

[[problems/group_theory/E1162/_index|E1162/]]: Asks for an asymptotic formula
for the number of subgroups of the symmetric group $S_n$, and for statistical
results on their orders.

[[problems/group_theory/E1163/_index|E1163/]]: Asks for a statistical
description of the arithmetic structure of the orders of subgroups of the
symmetric group $S_n$.

***

Combinatorial problems about groups, including non-commuting graphs of groups;
exact coverings of a group by cosets are filed with covering systems.
