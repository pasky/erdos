# problems/factorials_binomials/E0728/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0728/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0728/claims/2026_01_06_barreto|2026_01_06_barreto]]: For every $C$ and small $\varepsilon$, infinitely many triples with
$\varepsilon n\le a,b\le(1-\varepsilon)n$ have $a+b-n$ of order $C\log n$ and
$a!\,b!\mid n!\,(a+b-n)!$. The argument is GPT-5.2's and was formalized by
Aristotle.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0728/claims/2026_01_14_pomerance|2026_01_14_pomerance]]: Pomerance (Integers, 2026) proves that for almost all $m$, $\binom{m+k}{k}$
divides $\binom{2m}{m}$ for every $k\le\exp(0.8\sqrt{\log m})$. This gives the
triples the problem asks for, with a gap far beyond $C\log n$.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0728/claims/2026_07_13_pickhardt|2026_07_13_pickhardt]]: Two manuscripts by the Paratelligent Research Agent and Jeff Pickhardt, posted
to the erdosproblems.com thread after the problem was settled, each claim a
deterministic proof of the affirmative answer with any constant times
$\log n$.
