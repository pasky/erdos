***

Theory is the home for new precise project claims. Each claim has an immutable
L-number, distinct from the E-number identifying an erdosproblems.com problem. A
problem can concern several claims, and a claim can bear on several problems.

Source results keep their canonical homes in
[[../library/_index|the source library]]. Approaches, editable sketches, partial
work, and useful obstructions belong in [[research/_index|research]]. A page's
location does not change its mathematical or verification standing.

The claim layout and prospective review contracts are defined in the
[corpus conventions](../../docs/anatomy.md). Organizing areas and claim folders
are added when they have mathematical content; this index asserts no claim.
