# problems/divisors/E0673/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0673/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0673/claims/1982_01_01_erdos|1982_01_01_erdos]]: In a 1982 survey recalling the problem, Erdős states that the divergence of
$G(n)$ for almost all $n$ is trivial. The site records Tao's two-line proof
through the bound $G(n)\ge\tau(n/m)/m$ for a divisor $m>1$.

[[problems/divisors/E0673/claims/1983_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum|1983_01_01_erdos_tenenbaum]]: Erdős and Tenenbaum prove that $\sum_{n\le x}G(n)=(1+o(1))x\log x$. They also
show that $G(n)\ge\tau(n)/(2P(n))$, where $P(n)$ is the least prime factor of
$n$, which gives $G(n)\to\infty$ for almost all $n$.
