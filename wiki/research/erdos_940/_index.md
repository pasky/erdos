# Sums of powerful numbers

[[research/_index|..]]

[[research/erdos_940/affine_fiber_brauer|affine_fiber_brauer]]: For r at least five, the full nonzero affine diagonal fiber has only constant Brauer classes; no height-sensitive obstruction was obtained.

[[research/erdos_940/birational_diagonal_forms|birational_diagonal_forms]]: For degree at least four every birational similitude is monomial; direct multiplicative tensoring of proper powerful subsums is subcritical.

[[research/erdos_940/cartesian_extraction|cartesian_extraction]]: A proved output-defined restriction retains almost all inputs but has only small Cartesian rectangles; even vanishing relative entropy can erase an exponential sieve weight.

[[research/erdos_940/congruence_preserving_thinning|congruence_preserving_thinning]]: Congruence-preserving sampling retains the cubic profinite limit and every established major-arc estimate while losing a logarithmic amount of microscopic entropy.

[[research/erdos_940/critical_covering|critical_covering]]: Cassels bases defeat abstract sparsity arguments; nearby powerful-sum targets almost never admit sufficiently close root representations.

[[research/erdos_940/cubefree_cubic_values|cubefree_cubic_values]]: Positive input density of cube-free outputs, a bounded logarithmic cube-part moment, and primitive collisions that survive this restriction.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_carry_spectrum|cubic_carry_spectrum]]: Exact local uniformity, including prime-degree geometric forms, does not bound finite-height carries; primitive high-conductor cubic characters can retain rational resonances.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_collision_ranges|cubic_collision_ranges]]: Coordinate-sharing collisions and bounded secant, binary-norm, and binary-trace ratio strata are controlled; their unrestricted tails remain open.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_major_arcs|cubic_major_arcs]]: Growing major arcs have bounded cubic collision energy; truncated restriction still leaves the moderate-amplitude heavy-fiber correlation uncontrolled.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_matrix_composition|cubic_matrix_composition]]: Input-dependent conjugation and scalar repair of cubic matrix products produce only a density-zero set of products.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_rational_composition|cubic_rational_composition]]: No quadratic polynomial composition exists for a diagonal ternary cubic; any fixed rational composition has only O(N^(17/3)) positive integral input pairs.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_secant_content|cubic_secant_content]]: An exact valuation formula relates secant content to output divisibility and coordinate alignment; squarefree targets can still have integral third secants.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_split_prime_counts|cubic_split_prime_counts]]: A normal-order theorem prevents retaining positive input mass by restricting the special cubic to bounded split-prime complexity.

[[research/erdos_940/cubic_trace_counterexample|cubic_trace_counterexample]]: A sparse conic construction defeats unrestricted deterministic all-three-traces pigeonholing, but not a statistical multiplicity estimate.

[[research/erdos_940/eisenstein_collision_content|eisenstein_collision_content]]: An exact fixed-field parametrization turns cubic collision counting into a large-content problem; its critical gcd tail remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_940/energy_and_multiplicity|energy_and_multiplicity]]: Multiplicity criteria, exact prime scaling, and checked near-neighbor and square-class reductions; the critical cubic count remains open.

[[research/erdos_940/evidence/_index|evidence/]]: Preserved exact finite calculations supporting the topical proofs, run by one entry point; current research status is in the main index.

[[research/erdos_940/finite_core_reduction|finite_core_reduction]]: Quantitative canonical-core approximation, fixed-form equivalence, and checked homogeneity and polynomial-construction limitations.

[[research/erdos_940/generating_function_positivity|generating_function_positivity]]: Fixed finite singularity-positivity tests are exactly progression first-moment tests and cannot contradict quintic cofiniteness.

[[research/erdos_940/independent/_index|independent/]]: Archived unverified input; checked consequences and current status are recorded in the topical notes.

[[research/erdos_940/joint_shift_fibers|joint_shift_fibers]]: The complete intersections for arbitrarily many distinct target shifts are normal and have ample anticanonical bundle; increasing the number of shifts gives no general-type obstruction.

[[research/erdos_940/large_prime_roots|large_prime_roots]]: A positive-mass input restriction gives bilinear cubic sums, but elementary Type-II estimates lose a cofactor power and anchor congruences do not constrain competing roots.

[[research/erdos_940/linear_collision_families|linear_collision_families]]: Nontrivial positive linear identities have fewer than r parameters, with a stronger bound when nonproportional terms survive.

[[research/erdos_940/local_first_moments|local_first_moments]]: Residue surjectivity and a uniform positive progression first moment for exponents at least five.

[[research/erdos_940/metric_coefficients|metric_coefficients]]: Almost all real rounded forms have positive upper density, but a fixed polylogarithmic perturbation of powers can make every rational coefficient tuple exceptional.

[[research/erdos_940/moving_moduli|moving_moduli]]: Quintic first-moment bounds for moving moduli and intervals, and equidistribution under fixed continuous root-phase weights.

[[research/erdos_940/palm_void_strategy|palm_void_strategy]]: A single bounded Palm comparison would force gaps at any finite representation mean; the arithmetic coupling remains unproved.

[[research/erdos_940/powerful_subsum_counts|powerful_subsum_counts]]: A coefficient-uniform plane-curve estimate gives a power saving for tuples with powerful subsums, but not for arbitrary targets.

[[research/erdos_940/primitive_cubic_fibers|primitive_cubic_fibers]]: Common dilations have bounded entropy, low-height collision planes and three target cube classes are negligible, and the special cubic's output surfaces have no rational lines or conics.

[[research/erdos_940/profinite_cubic_density|profinite_cubic_density]]: Uniform congruence-level second moments and entropy, quantitative profinite convergence, and exact descent at three; the integer-fiber estimate remains open.

[[research/erdos_940/quadratic_compression|quadratic_compression]]: Higher-degree diagonal forms cannot be rationally represented by fixed positive quaternary quadratic forms; a stronger dimension bound holds for exponents twice odd.

[[research/erdos_940/quadratic_prime_harmonics|quadratic_prime_harmonics]]: An elementary two-sided logarithmic bound for real-character prime sums, used with a small coefficient in the quartic selection proofs.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_complement|quartic_complement]]: Author-recorded argument for positive lower density of the quartic complement; no whole-conclusion review of the prose is filed, and a separate Lean formalization supports an accepted partial claim.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_euler_majorant|quartic_euler_majorant]]: Coefficient-uniform quartic local Euler bounds from eleven nonprincipal character factors.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_finite_families|quartic_finite_families]]: The finite-family theorem, its resolved tail obstruction, and sharper odd-valuation support bounds for every principal-character exception.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_random_omission|quartic_random_omission]]: A checked alternative completion using a small infinite character bias, uniform quadratic bounds, and a growing exceptional-support cutoff.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_residue_obstruction|quartic_residue_obstruction]]: A finite certificate rules out one 32-prescription upper-bound objective, not the successful quartic proof.

[[research/erdos_940/quartic_universal_selector|quartic_universal_selector]]: A checked universal selector gives compatible growing progressions and another completion of the quartic theorem.

[[research/erdos_940/quintic_first_moment_barrier|quintic_first_moment_barrier]]: The uniform unordered quintic progression mean exceeds 2.468; a fifth-moment gap criterion is proved but its arithmetic hypotheses are not.

[[research/erdos_940/rational_transformations|rational_transformations]]: Fixed rational symmetries act on negligible positive integral input mass unless monomial; linear multiplier classes form a group of order dividing the number of variables.

[[research/erdos_940/remaining_questions|remaining_questions]]: Open density and higher-exponent questions, retained mechanisms, and rejected bounded tests.

[[research/erdos_940/representation_count_congruences|representation_count_congruences]]: A count-divisibility criterion would bypass a first-moment floor, but Frobenius, prime-adic layers, and fixed-prime valuation actions do not supply its arithmetic hypothesis.

## Where things stand

- **Target cases:** positive lower density of the complement is accepted
  for $r=3$, on
  [[problems/diophantine_problems/E0940/claims/2026_07_26_beyer_de_ryke|Beyer de Ryke's claim]],
  and for $r=4$, on
  [[problems/diophantine_problems/E0940/claims/2026_10_28_plasma_ai|Plasma AI's claim]].
  The acceptance evidence is the
  [[../library/diophantine_problems/plasma_ai_2026_powerful_sum_complements/_index|separate Lean formalization]].
  The [quartic prose argument](quartic_complement.md) remains author-recorded;
  its stronger progression statement is not covered by that acceptance.
  Neither complement result settles density zero.
- **Open targets:** density remains unresolved here for every $r\ge3$, and
  complement infinitude for $r\ge5$. The
  [finite-core equivalence](finite_core_reduction.md) reduces positive upper
  density to one fixed positive diagonal degree-$r$ form in $r$ variables; none
  is established.
- **Positive-density route:**
  [bounded typical integer multiplicity](energy_and_multiplicity.md) remains
  unproved. The [controlled ranges](cubic_collision_ranges.md),
  [major arcs](cubic_major_arcs.md), and
  [profinite bounds](profinite_cubic_density.md) do not close the
  [content endpoint](eisenstein_collision_content.md). Almost-unique inversion
  of a cube difference does not control the weighted incidence among three
  differences.
- **Higher-exponent gaps:** every quintic progression mean
  [exceeds $2.468$](quintic_first_moment_barrier.md); the
  [Palm comparison](palm_void_strategy.md) is unproved. The new
  [count-divisibility criterion](representation_count_congruences.md) could
  bypass a mean above one, but no required congruence was obtained. Frobenius
  and prime-adic layers retain the critical unknown, and the finite tests
  exclude only specified universal congruences.
- **Checked structural limitations:**
  [fixed rational cubic composition](cubic_rational_composition.md) gives only
  $O(N^{17/3})$ positive integral pairs in balanced boxes, while
  [joint shifted fibers](joint_shift_fibers.md) remain normal complete
  intersections with ample anticanonical bundle.
  [Fixed-prime valuation actions](representation_count_congruences.md#the-scope-of-prime-power-carry-actions)
  move only $o(X)$ source tuples. These are not bounds for arbitrary integer
  fibers.
- **Other retained limitations:**
  [metric coefficient extraction](metric_coefficients.md),
  [digit lifting](cubic_carry_spectrum.md), [ordinary Brauer--Manin on the full
  affine fibers](affine_fiber_brauer.md), and
  [coarse critical covering](critical_covering.md) supplied no missing
  arithmetic estimate. Detailed scope and earlier results remain in the linked
  topical notes.
- **Provisional directions:** a height-sensitive integer-fiber estimate, a
  genuine deletion comparison, or a count symmetry coupling different prime-free
  parts would require new input. None is an established route.
  [Detailed status](remaining_questions.md) records the gaps. The proof is
  incomplete.
