# problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2017_03_31_tsaturian|2017_03_31_tsaturian]]: Tsaturian proves that every red-blue coloring of the plane has a red unit pair
or a blue five-term progression with unit step. So the least avoiding length
is at least $6$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2017_05_05_conlon_fox|2017_05_05_conlon_fox]]: Conlon and Fox's line corollary gives a red-blue coloring of the plane with no
red unit pair and no blue unit progression of $10^{10}$ terms. So the least
avoiding length is at most $10^{10}$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/claims/2026_06_15_currier_mody_xie_zhang|2026_06_15_currier_mody_xie_zhang]]: Theorem 1.2 of Currier, Mody, Xie and Zhang's preprint claims there is a
red-blue coloring of the plane with no red unit pair and no blue unit
progression of $6330$ terms. This would show that the least avoiding length is
at most $6330$.
