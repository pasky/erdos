**Claim.** For every integer $k\ge3$,

$$
\chi_S\bigl(n,\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+1,C_{2k+1}\bigr)=\frac{n^2}{8}+o(n^2)
\qquad(n\to\infty),
$$

where $\chi_S(n,e,G)$ is the least $r$ for which some simple graph with $n$
vertices and exactly $e$ edges has an $r$-coloring of its edges under which
every copy of $G$ has pairwise distinct edge colors; this is the question of
[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0809/_index|Problem 809]] in the affirmative for every
$k$ it asks about. The result is the project's claim
[[theory/ramsey_theory/L17_rainbow_odd_cycle_threshold/_index|L17]], whose Lean
surface states the question in erdosproblems.com's exact-edge form. The
seven-cycle case is the project's own argument, a palette-savings proof followed
in the [[research/erdos_809/_index|research guide]]; the cases $k\ge4$ are the
theorem of Bucić, Chen and Ma, formalized natively, and a subgraph restriction
carries the result to exactly $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+1$ edges. The claim
determines $\chi_S$ at no fixed $n$ and says nothing about $C_3$ or $C_5$. The
development was posted to the site's proof-claims tab under the name Plasma AI,
from the account jacobparish, on 27 September 2026, as the site's proof claim
367, filed after Asad Shahab's independent proof claim 358 of the same day (see
Priority below), and registered on the Palomar registry on 30 September 2026 as
`PALOMAR-2026-09-30-000004` (theorem `Erdos809.main_result`, trust level high,
automated review outcome neutral), which pins the repository at the linked
commit. The posting also announces a second result outside this page and not
part of L17: for the seven-cycle at every fixed edge density $q=e/n^2$ with
$1/4<q<1/2$, the Bucić–Chen–Ma formula fails, with explicit lower and upper
bounds and the exact coefficient left open. The research notes record the
[[research/erdos_809/proofs/c7_density_lower_bound|lower bound]] and the
[[research/erdos_809/proofs/c7_five_clique_construction|construction]] as the
paper's results, not independently reviewed. The write-up is the paper linked
from the posting (`preprint` link, pinned to the same commit). Later commits to
the repository, up to the one that
[[research/erdos_809/evidence/_index|the research evidence]] compares, changed
only the paper's author line, one broken citation in it and the README, so the
paper's last change is `eb385a8` of 29 September 2026, the version the research
evidence holds.

**Submission note.** Posted to erdosproblems.com as a proof claim by Plasma AI
(account jacobparish) on 27 September 2026, giving "GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable
5.1" as the AI used:

> Great work Asad! The team at Plasma AI and I independently found a proof of
> the $k=3$ case and have formalized it in Lean, together with the $k\ge4$ proof
> of Bucić, Chen, and Ma. We also investigate the asymptotic number of colors as
> the density $q=e/n^2$ increases above the Turán threshold. For odd cycles of
> length at least nine, Bucić, Chen, and Ma determine the exact asymptotic
> coefficient. For 7-cycles, we show that their formula fails at every fixed
> density $1/4<q<1/2$, and give explicit lower and upper bounds. The exact
> coefficient in this interval remains open.

The Palomar registry's description of entry PALOMAR-2026-09-30-000004:

> A Lean proof resolving the Burr–Erdős–Graham–Sós conjecture (Erdős Problem
> 809): for every fixed odd cycle of length at least seven, the maximal
> anti-Ramsey threshold at floor(n²/4) + 1 edges is n²/8 + o(n²). The
> seven-cycle case is new to our knowledge. The development proves it and
> formalizes the stronger full-density result of Bucić, Chen, and Ma for odd
> cycles of length at least nine.

report and for independence, with tier 2 asserted. The review is bound to the
`lean/` tree that the non-author clean gate of 2026-09-29 checked, and a grade
decision applied the grade to the Lean v4.34.1 tree, which the non-author clean
gate of 2026-10-13 checked. This is the tier 2 warrant of `docs/anatomy.md`

No other evidence kind applies. Nobody outside the repository has examined the
proof, so nothing is listed as reviewed. There is no refereed write-up. The
paper linked as `preprint` is the write-up held in the repository, not a posting
on a preprint server. The site labeled the problem OPEN and credits only the
$k\ge4$ result. The posting has no comments. The registry's automated review
outcome is neutral, and its trust level concerns the build, not the mathematics.
Neither the fidelity review nor its grade read the Bucić–Chen–Ma paper. The
$k\ge4$ branch is a closed native proof whose truth does not rest on that
attribution.
