# problems/primes/E0240/claims

[[problems/primes/E0240/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0240/claims/1973_01_01_tijdeman|1973_01_01_tijdeman]]: Tijdeman's refereed 1973 paper constructs, for every $0<\epsilon<1$, an
infinite set of primes such that consecutive integers $n_i<n_{i+1}$ composed
of those primes satisfy $n_{i+1}-n_i>n_i^{1-\epsilon}$. So the gaps tend to
infinity.
