# problems/additive_bases/E0348/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0348/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0348/claims/2025_09_06_van_doorn|2025_09_06_van_doorn]]: Answers a variant (completeness in the strong sense, every positive integer a
subset sum), not the Statement (all sufficiently large integers), so it does
not count toward the problem's standing. Wouter van Doorn claims that in that
sense no sequence works for any $2\le m<n$.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0348/claims/2026_09_09_geneson|2026_09_09_geneson]]: Jesse Geneson's machine-assisted 2026 preprint claims that a complete sequence
that stays complete after every deletion of $m$ terms and fails after every
deletion of $n$ terms exists exactly when $m$ is $0$ or $1$.
