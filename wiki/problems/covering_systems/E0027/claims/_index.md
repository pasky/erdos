# problems/covering_systems/E0027/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0027/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0027/claims/2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu|2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu]]: Filaseta, Ford, Konyagin, Pomerance and Yu proved that distinct moduli in
$[N,CN]$, with $C$ below a slow power of $N$, leave nearly the trivial density
uncovered, so no constant $C$ works. The refereed paper appeared in J. Amer.
Math. Soc.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0027/claims/2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: Theorem 5.1 of the 2022 Inventiones paper of Balister, Bollobás, Morris,
Sahasrabudhe and Tiba is a second, refereed proof that no constant $C$ works.
Distinct moduli in $[n,Kn]$ with $n$ at least an absolute $M$ leave density at
least some $\delta(K)>0$ uncovered.
