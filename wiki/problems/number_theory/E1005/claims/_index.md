# problems/number_theory/E1005/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E1005/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E1005/claims/2025_08_28_van_doorn|2025_08_28_van_doorn]]: Van Doorn's 2025 preprint proves
$(\frac1{12}-o(1))n\le f(n)\le\lfloor n/4\rfloor+d$, with $d=1,2,2,4$ for
$n\equiv0,1,2,3\pmod4$, and conjectures that the upper bound is exact. It is
the upper half of the site's resolution.

[[problems/number_theory/E1005/claims/2026_07_04_cipollini|2026_07_04_cipollini]]: Cipollini's 2026 preprint, with declared AI assistance, proves the lower bound
$(\frac14-o(1))n$ and so the asymptotic with $c=1/4$. It is the site's
resolution and is unrefereed, and three outside Lean developments were not
built here.

[[problems/number_theory/E1005/claims/2026_07_28_wang_xie_zhao|2026_07_28_wang_xie_zhao]]: A 2026 preprint of Wang, Xie and Zhao, with declared AI assistance, claims
$f(n)=\lfloor n/4\rfloor+d$ for all sufficiently large $n$ and, with computer
checks, for every $n\ge4$ outside fifteen listed exceptions. It is a tab
claim, unreviewed.
