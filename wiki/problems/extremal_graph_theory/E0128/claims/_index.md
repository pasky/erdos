# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/1995_03_01_krivelevich|1995_03_01_krivelevich]]: Krivelevich's Theorem 3 (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 1995) shows that every
regular triangle-free graph of degree at least $2n/5$ has $n/2$ vertices
spanning at most $n^2/50$ edges. Only the blown-up $C_5$ meets the bound, and
the result is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2006_01_05_keevash_sudakov|2006_01_05_keevash_sudakov]]: Keevash and Sudakov (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2006) prove that a triangle-free
graph with at most $n^2/12$ edges, or with at least $n^2/5$ edges, has $n/2$
vertices spanning at most $n^2/50$ edges. The result is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2013_11_22_norin_yepremyan|2013_11_22_norin_yepremyan]]: Norin and Yepremyan (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2015) prove the sparse-halves
conjecture for triangle-free graphs with minimum degree at least $5n/14$, with
at least $(1/5-\gamma)n^2$ edges, or close to a blow-up of the Petersen graph.
The result is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/claims/2021_04_19_razborov|2021_04_19_razborov]]: Razborov (Mat. Sb. 2022) proves the sparse-halves conjecture for triangle-free
graphs with no induced $2K_2$, with girth at least $5$, with independence
number at least $2n/5$, with low density, or strongly regular. He also proves
the general bound $27n^2/1024$, and the paper is refereed.
