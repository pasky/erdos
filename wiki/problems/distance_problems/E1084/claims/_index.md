# problems/distance_problems/E1084/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E1084/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E1084/claims/1974_01_01_harborth|1974_01_01_harborth]]: Harborth proved that for every $n\ge2$ the largest number of unit distances
among $n$ points of the plane at mutual distance at least $1$ is
$\lfloor 3n-\sqrt{12n-3}\rfloor$. Pieces of the triangular lattice attain it.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1084/claims/2013_04_01_bezdek_reid|2013_04_01_bezdek_reid]]: Bezdek and Reid prove that among $n\ge2$ points of $\mathbb{R}^3$ at mutual
distance at least $1$, fewer than $6n-0.926\,n^{2/3}$ pairs are at distance
$1$. This is the upper half of Erdős's two-sided estimate for $d=3$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1084/claims/2026_06_11_sanexxxx777|2026_06_11_sanexxxx777]]: A Lean proof contributed to formal-conjectures shows that $n$ points of the
line at mutual distance at least $1$ have at most $n-1$ pairs at distance $1$,
with equality attained. This corpus has not built the proof.
