# problems/analysis/E1164/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1164/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1164/claims/1990_09_01_revesz|1990_09_01_revesz]]: In a 1990 monograph Révész proves two-sided exponential bounds on the
distribution of the time a planar simple random walk needs to cover a disc.
The bounds give the covered radius its typical scale, and the site credits the
result.

[[problems/analysis/E1164/claims/2001_07_26_dembo_peres_rosen_zeitouni|2001_07_26_dembo_peres_rosen_zeitouni]]: In a refereed paper, Dembo, Peres, Rosen and Zeitouni prove the Kesten–Révész
conjecture that the cover time $T_r$ of the planar disc of radius $r$
satisfies $\mathbb P(\log T_r\le t(\log r)^2)\to e^{-4/t}$. This inverts to
the limit law of the largest origin-centered disc covered by time $n$.

[[problems/analysis/E1164/claims/2026_08_26_alexeev|2026_08_26_alexeev]]: A Lean development in Boris Alexeev's repository proves that, for planar
simple random walk, the logarithm of the pathwise covered radius has two-sided
order $\sqrt{\log n}$ in probability. It has not been built or audited here.
