# problems/primes/E0237/claims

[[problems/primes/E0237/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0237/claims/1950_11_01_erdos|1950_11_01_erdos]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős's refereed 1950 paper shows that if $f(n)$ counts the
representations $n=2^k+p$, then $\limsup f(n)=\infty$, and $f(n)>c\log\log n$
for infinitely many $n$. This is the case $A=\{2^k\}$ of Problem 237.

[[problems/primes/E0237/claims/2022_01_26_chen_ding|2022_01_26_chen_ding]]: Chen and Ding prove, in a refereed paper credited by erdosproblems.com, that
for any infinite set of integers some integers have arbitrarily many
representations as a prime plus a member. For sets of more than $\log x$
elements up to $x$ they give a $\log\log x$ lower bound.
