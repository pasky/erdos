# problems/polynomials/E0228/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E0228/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E0228/claims/2019_07_22_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2019_07_22_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: Balister, Bollobás, Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Tiba prove that for every degree
$n\ge2$ some polynomial $P$ with coefficients $\pm1$ has
$\delta\sqrt n\le\lvert P(z)\rvert\le\Delta\sqrt n$ on the whole unit circle,
for fixed $\delta,\Delta>0$. It appeared in the Annals in 2020.

[[problems/polynomials/E0228/claims/2026_10_05_openai|2026_10_05_openai]]: The OpenAI release claims that every large length $N$ admits a polynomial with
coefficients $\pm1$ whose modulus lies between $(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt N$ and
$(1+\varepsilon)\sqrt N$ on the whole circle. The released Lean states the
upper bound only.
