**Claim.** Fix $\gamma=\sqrt2$. For every $\alpha,\beta>0$, the
interleaving of $\lfloor\alpha\gamma^n\rfloor$ and
$\lfloor\beta\gamma^n\rfloor$ is complete. In fact the first ray
alone remains complete after any finite deletion, even when sums use
distinct positive integer values. No irrationality condition on
$\alpha/\beta$ is needed.

**Covers.** The second question of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0354/_index|Problem 354]] under the reading
"for some $\gamma\in(1,2)$", with one base chosen before both
coefficients. It does not answer the reading "for every $\gamma$".
Those two unfixed readings have opposite pending answers, so this
partial result leaves the problem's derived standing open.

tail. No stronger deletion or distinct-value assertion is imported.
