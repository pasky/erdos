# problems/factorials_binomials/E0403/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0403/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0403/claims/1976_01_01_lin|1976_01_01_lin]]: Only finitely many powers of two are sums of distinct factorials, and the
largest is $2^7=2!+3!+5!$. Lin showed this in a Bell Laboratories memorandum
and Frankl showed it independently, as the problem's source reports.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0403/claims/2026_06_18_axiommath|2026_06_18_axiommath]]: A Lean 4 proof, produced by the AxiomProver system and published in the
AxiomMath repository, shows that $2^m$ is a sum of distinct positive
factorials exactly for $m\in\{0,1,3,5,7\}$. It lists the five solutions.
