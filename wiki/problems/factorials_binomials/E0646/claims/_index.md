[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0646/claims/1997_05_01_berend|1997_05_01_berend]]: In a refereed paper credited by the erdosproblems.com curator, Berend proves
that for every $k$ infinitely many $n$ have each of the first $k$ primes to an
even exponent in $n!$, with bounded gaps between them. The result has a
third-party Lean proof.
