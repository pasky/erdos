# problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims/2003_03_01_croot|2003_03_01_croot]]: Croot's 2003 coloring paper is credited, by the site and by Liu and Sawhney,
with showing that $A(N)<cN$ for some constant $c<1$ and all large $N$. This
refutes the expected $A(N)=(1+o(1))N$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0300/claims/2024_04_10_liu_sawhney|2024_04_10_liu_sawhney]]: Liu and Sawhney prove that every subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ of size at least
$(1-1/e+\varepsilon)N$ has a subset with reciprocal sum one. With the trivial
top-segment example, this gives $A(N)=(1-1/e+o(1))N$.
