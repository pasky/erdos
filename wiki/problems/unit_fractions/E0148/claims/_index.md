# problems/unit_fractions/E0148/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0148/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0148/claims/2014_01_01_konyagin|2014_01_01_konyagin]]: Konyagin's Theorem 1 bounds the number of $k$-term representations of $1$ by
distinct unit fractions below by $\exp(\exp(ck/\log k))$. It is accepted on
the refereed publication, though its printed proof contains a known false
identity.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0148/claims/2020_12_10_elsholtz_planitzer|2020_12_10_elsholtz_planitzer]]: Corollary 3(2) of Elsholtz and Planitzer bounds the number of $k$-term
representations of $1$ by a constant to the power $(2/5+\varepsilon)2^{k-1}$,
an upper bound of the form $c^{2^k}$. It is accepted on the refereed
publication.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0148/claims/2026_09_25_openai|2026_09_25_openai]]: Corollary 1.2 of the OpenAI mathematics release of 25 September 2026 states
that $ck\le\log\log F(k)\le Ck$ for all large $k$. It is accepted as a partial
answer on the Lean declarations that the corpus's verification built and
audited.
