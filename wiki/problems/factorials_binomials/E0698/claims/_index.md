# problems/factorials_binomials/E0698/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0698/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0698/claims/2008_06_03_bergman|2008_06_03_bergman]]: In a refereed paper credited by the site's curator, Bergman proves an explicit
lower bound for $\gcd(\binom ni,\binom nj)$ for all $2\le i<j\le n/2$. This
page deduces from it that the gcd is at least $\sqrt n/6$.
