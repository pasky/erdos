# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0876/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0876/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0876/claims/2026_07_18_price|2026_07_18_price]]: Liam Price's partial claim of 18 July 2026, credited to GPT 5.6 Sol Pro, that
every infinite sum-free sequence has $\limsup(a_{n+1}-a_n)/n=\infty$, a
negative answer to the second question. The argument rests on Fan's Corollary
1.2 for Problem 254.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0876/claims/2026_09_22_korsky|2026_09_22_korsky]]: Samuel Korsky's full claim of 22 September 2026, credited to GPT Astra: for
slowly varying $F\ge1$, gaps of order $nF(n)$ occur exactly when
$\int^\infty dx/(xF(x))$ is finite, and gaps $O(n^c)$ with infinitely many
bounded gaps need $c\ge(1+\sqrt{13})/2$.
