# problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/1959_10_01_birch|1959_10_01_birch]]: Birch proves that for coprime $a$ and $b$ every large integer is a sum of
distinct numbers of the form $a^kb^l$. The result, refereed in 1959 and
credited by the erdosproblems.com curator, was later formalized in Lean by
others.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/1960_01_01_cassels|1960_01_01_cassels]]: Cassels's 1960 Theorem I is a circle-method criterion under which every large
integer is a sum of distinct elements of a set. The completeness of the
numbers $a^kb^l$ is an immediate consequence.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2000_01_01_hegyvari|2000_01_01_hegyvari]]: Hegyvári (Acta Math. Hungar. 86 (2000)) proves in a refereed paper that the
numbers $p^aq^b$ with $b$ at most an explicit $K(p,q)$ already form a complete
set. This is the first effective form of Birch's theorem.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2015_07_08_bergelson_simmons|2015_07_08_bergelson_simmons]]: Bergelson and Simmons (Acta Arith. 177 (2017)) prove in a refereed paper that
the numbers $a^nb^k$ with at most $4a-4$ distinct exponents $k$, including 0,
form a complete set. Hence $K(a,b)\le4a-5$.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2017_05_10_fang_chen|2017_05_10_fang_chen]]: Fang and Chen (Acta Arith. 178 (2017)) give, in a refereed paper, explicit
towers $K$ and $B$ such that every integer at least $B$ is a sum of distinct
numbers $p^aq^b$ with $b\le K$.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2024_01_01_yu|2024_01_01_yu]]: Yu (Publ. Math. Debrecen 104 (2024)) proves in a refereed paper that every
large $n$ is a sum of distinct numbers $p^aq^b$, each greater than
$cn/(\log n)^{1+\varepsilon}$.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0246/claims/2026_09_03_song_yue|2026_09_03_song_yue]]: Song and Yue claim that for coprime $p,q>1$ the numbers $p^aq^b$ with
$b\le2p-3$ already form a complete set, a quantitative strengthening of the
Erdős–Birch theorem. The manuscript was posted in 2026 and is unreviewed.
