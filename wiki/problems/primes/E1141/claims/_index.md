[[problems/primes/E1141/claims/2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant|2026_04_08_alexeev_putterman_sawhney_sellke_valiant]]: Theorem 6.1 of Alexeev, Putterman, Sawhney, Sellke and Valiant states that,
for each fixed $a\ge1$, only finitely many $n$ have $n-ak^2$ prime for every
$k$ coprime to $n$ with $ak^2<n$. The case $a=1$ answers Problem 1141.
