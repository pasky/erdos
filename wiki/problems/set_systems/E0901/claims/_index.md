# problems/set_systems/E0901/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1963_01_01_erdos|1963_01_01_erdos]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős's 1963 paper shows that every family of at most $2^{n-1}$
sets of size $n$ has property B, so $m(n)>2^{n-1}$, and that
$m(n)>(1-\epsilon)2^n\log2$ for large $n$. This refereed result is the first
lower bound for Problem 901.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1964_09_01_erdos|1964_09_01_erdos]]: Theorem 1 of Erdős's 1964 paper chooses $n$-sets greedily in a ground set of
$2n^2$ points to get an $n$-uniform hypergraph that is not 2-colorable and has
at most $n^22^{n+1}$ edges. So $m(n)\le n^22^{n+1}$, which is still the best
upper bound.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1977_01_01_beck|1977_01_01_beck]]: Beck's 1977 paper proves $m(n)>c(\log n)2^n$, so $m(n)/2^n\to\infty$, as Erdős
and Lovász had conjectured. This refereed result is the first lower bound for
Problem 901 beyond the scale $2^n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/1978_01_01_beck|1978_01_01_beck]]: Beck's 1978 paper proves that, for some $g(n)\to0$, every $n$-uniform
hypergraph with at most $n^{1/3-g(n)}2^n$ edges is 2-colorable. So
$m(n)>n^{1/3-o(1)}2^n$, which remained the lower bound for Problem 901
until 2000.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/2000_01_01_radhakrishnan_srinivasan|2000_01_01_radhakrishnan_srinivasan]]: Radhakrishnan and Srinivasan prove in a refereed paper that for all large $n$
every $n$-uniform hypergraph with at most $0.7\sqrt{n/\ln n}\,2^n$ edges is
2-colorable. Hence $m(n)>0.7\sqrt{n/\ln n}\,2^n$, the best lower bound for
Problem 901.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/2009_02_10_pluhar|2009_02_10_pluhar]]: Corollary 1 of Pluhár's 2009 paper uses a greedy coloring along a random
vertex order to show $m(n)>0.5268\,n^{1/4}2^n$ for $n\ge3$. This is a two-page
refereed proof that $m(n)/2^n\to\infty$, though the bound is weaker than the
best one.

[[problems/set_systems/E0901/claims/2014_01_01_ostergard|2014_01_01_ostergard]]: Östergård's exhaustive computer search shows that the least number of edges of
a $4$-uniform hypergraph without property B is 23, closing the earlier range
$21\le m(4)\le23$. This refereed result gives $m(4)=23$, the value the site
lists for Problem 901.
