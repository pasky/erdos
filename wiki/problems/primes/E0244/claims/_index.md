# problems/primes/E0244/claims

[[problems/primes/E0244/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0244/claims/1934_12_01_romanoff|1934_12_01_romanoff]]: Satz II of Romanoff's refereed 1934 paper shows that, for each fixed integer
$a\ge2$, every interval $(0,x)$ holds more than a fixed positive proportion of
integers of the form $p+a^n$. So Problem 244 has the answer yes for every
integer $C$.

[[problems/primes/E0244/claims/2025_03_17_ding|2025_03_17_ding]]: Theorem 1.1 of Ding's arXiv preprint claims positive lower density for almost
every real $C>1$, and Theorem 1.2 of its fifth version claims it for the
golden ratio, a specified base. The preprint has no journal record.
