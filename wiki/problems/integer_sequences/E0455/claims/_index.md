# problems/integer_sequences/E0455/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0455/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0455/claims/1976_01_01_richter|1976_01_01_richter]]: Richter's 1976 Theorem proves $\liminf q_n/n^2\ge1/2.84010\ldots=0.3521\ldots$
for every sequence of primes with non-decreasing gaps. It appeared, refereed,
in Acta Arithmetica and leaves the limit question open.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0455/claims/2026_09_27_lin|2026_09_27_lin]]: A Lean 4 development by Yongxi Lin, published on 27 September 2026, claims
$\liminf q_n/n^2\ge255255/(295318+G)>0.864289$ for prime sequences with
non-decreasing gaps, by a max-plus certificate checked in Lean. The limit
question stays open.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0455/claims/2026_10_06_satorunet|2026_10_06_satorunet]]: A write-up posted to the erdosproblems.com proof-claims tab on 6 October 2026
claims $\liminf q_n/n^2\ge0.9200$ by a computer-certified residue argument,
with constraints on a counterexample. It leaves the limit question itself
open.
