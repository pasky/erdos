# problems/divisors/E0946/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0946/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1984_06_01_heath_brown|1984_06_01_heath_brown]]: Heath-Brown proves that $\tau(n)=\tau(n+1)$ for infinitely many $n$, indeed
for at least $cx/(\log x)^7$ integers $n\le x$, where $c$ is a constant. This
answers the question of Erdős and Mirsky affirmatively.

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1987_10_01_hildebrand|1987_10_01_hildebrand]]: Hildebrand proves that $\tau(n)=\tau(n+1)$ for at least $cx/(\log\log x)^3$
integers $n\le x$, where $c$ is a constant. This answers the question again
with a far larger count.

[[problems/divisors/E0946/claims/1997_12_01_pinner|1997_12_01_pinner]]: Pinner proves that for every positive integer $B$ there are infinitely many
$n$ with $\tau(n)=\tau(n+B)$. The case $B=1$ answers the question again.
