# problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2003_01_01_skalba|2003_01_01_skalba]]: Skałba, in a paper refereed in 2003, disproves the finiteness question as
worded, with the number of blocks free: disjoint blocks of four consecutive
positive integers have a square product infinitely often.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2005_01_01_ulas|2005_01_01_ulas]]: Ulas, in a paper refereed in 2005, disproves the finiteness question by
exhibiting infinitely many collections of $n$ disjoint blocks of four
consecutive integers whose product is a square, for $n=4$ and every $n\ge6$.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2007_01_01_bauer_bennett|2007_01_01_bauer_bennett]]: Bauer and Bennett, in a paper refereed in 2007, disprove the finiteness
question with infinitely many collections of $n$ disjoint blocks of four
consecutive integers with square product, for every $n\ge3$. This completes
the cases Ulas left.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0363/claims/2012_03_01_bennett_van_luijk|2012_03_01_bennett_van_luijk]]: Bennett and Van Luijk, in a paper refereed in 2012, disprove the finiteness
question with infinitely many collections of $r$ disjoint blocks of five
consecutive integers with square product, for every $r\ge5$.
