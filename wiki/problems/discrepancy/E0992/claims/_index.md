# problems/discrepancy/E0992/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0992/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0992/claims/1994_12_01_berkes_philipp|1994_12_01_berkes_philipp]]: Berkes and Philipp built an increasing integer sequence $(n_k)$ such that, for
almost every $\alpha$, the discrepancy of the multiples $n_k\alpha$ exceeds a
constant times $\sqrt{N\log N}$ infinitely often. The paper is refereed, in J.
London Math. Soc.
