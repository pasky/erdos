# problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/1984_03_01_simonovits_sos|1984_03_01_simonovits_sos]]: Theorem B of the 1984 Combinatorica paper of Simonovits and Sós: for $t\ge5$
and $n>ct^2$, the largest number of colors on $K_n$ with no rainbow path on
$2t+3+\epsilon_0$ vertices is $tn-\binom{t+1}{2}+1+\epsilon_0$. This is the
large-$n$ regime of the path half.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/2005_09_01_montellano_ballesteros_neumann_lara|2005_09_01_montellano_ballesteros_neumann_lara]]: Theorem 5 of Montellano-Ballesteros and Neumann-Lara (Graphs Combin. 2005):
for all $n\ge p\ge3$ the least number of colors forcing a rainbow $p$-cycle in
$K_n$ is the value $\mathbf E(n,p)$ conjectured in 1975. So
$\mathrm{AR}(n,C_k)=\mathbf E(n,k)-1$, which settles the cycle half.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E1105/claims/2021_02_01_yuan|2021_02_01_yuan]]: Theorem 1 of Yuan's 2021 arXiv preprint gives $\mathrm{AR}(n,P_k)$ for all
$n\ge k\ge5$ as the maximum of two terms, the 1975 formula of Erdős,
Simonovits and Sós. The site's curator credits the preprint as the proof of
the path half.
