# problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2015_04_25_cerda|2015_04_25_cerda]]: S. Cerdá's 2015 arXiv manuscript presents a method to find all solutions of
$n!+1=m^2$ by modular congruences. The author withdrew it in 2015 and again in
2016, the last time noting a mistake in the solution.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2017_02_14_sabihi|2017_02_14_sabihi]]: Ahmad Sabihi's ResearchGate manuscript claims a short solution of $n!=x^2-1$,
that $n=4,5,7$ are the only solutions, and a generalization. The claim was
rejected after a concrete error was shown in the erdosproblems.com discussion
thread.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2025_08_15_naciri|2025_08_15_naciri]]: Naciri proves that the only solutions of $n!+1=x^2$ with $x+1$ or $x-1$ 7-free
are $n=4,5,7$, and that $n=4$ is the only one with $x+1$ or $x-1$ a prime
power (Integers 25 (2025), #A71).

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0398/claims/2026_02_05_maiti|2026_02_05_maiti]]: Somnath Maiti's arXiv manuscript claims, as Theorem 2.15 of its second
version, that the Brocard-Ramanujan equation has no solution beyond $n=4,5,7$,
by a comparison of growth rates. It is unrefereed and unreviewed.
