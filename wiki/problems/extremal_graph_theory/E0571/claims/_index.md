# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2018_06_07_jiang_ma_yepremyan|2018_06_07_jiang_ma_yepremyan]]: Jiang, Ma and Yepremyan (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2022) show that for every
$s\ge2$ the rational $2-\frac2{2s+1}$, and also $\frac75$, is the Turán
exponent of a single bipartite graph. This gives infinitely many instances of
Problem 571.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2018_11_16_kang_kim_liu|2018_11_16_kang_kim_liu]]: Kang, Kim and Liu (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2021) show that $2-a/b$ is a Turán
exponent for all integers $b>a\ge1$ with $b\equiv\pm1\pmod a$. This gives
infinitely many instances of Problem 571.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2019_03_25_conlon_janzer_lee|2019_03_25_conlon_janzer_lee]]: Conlon, Janzer and Lee (Combinatorica 2021) show that the $1$-subdivision of
$K_{s,t}$ has Turán exponent $\frac32-\frac1{2s}$ for $t$ large in terms of
$s$, and that a graph with exponent $1+\frac{s}{sk+1}$ exists for all $s,k$.
This gives infinitely many instances.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2019_05_22_jiang_qiu|2019_05_22_jiang_qiu]]: Jiang and Qiu (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 2020) show that the $k$-subdivisions of
$K_{s,t}$ have Turán exponent $1+\frac1k-\frac1{sk}$ for $k=3$ and $k=4$ and
all $s\ge2$. This gives infinitely many instances of Problem 571.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2019_08_06_jiang_qiu|2019_08_06_jiang_qiu]]: Jiang and Qiu (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2023) show that $1+p/q$ is a Turán
exponent for all positive integers $q>p^2$, using unevenly subdivided complete
bipartite graphs. This gives infinitely many instances of Problem 571.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2020_07_06_jiang_jiang_ma|2020_07_06_jiang_jiang_ma]]: Jiang, Jiang and Ma (Ann. Appl. Math. 2022) show that $2-a/b$ is a Turán
exponent whenever
$\lfloor b/a\rfloor^3\le a\le\frac{b}{\lfloor b/a\rfloor+1}+1$. This gives
infinitely many instances of Problem 571.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2022_03_07_conlon_janzer|2022_03_07_conlon_janzer]]: Conlon and Janzer (Adv. Comb. 2022) show that every rational $2-a/b$ with
$b\ge\max(a,(a-1)^2)$ is a Turán exponent. The proof goes through the
Bukh–Conlon upper bound for the rooted trees of Jiang, Jiang and Ma.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2026_07_21_jiang_longbrake_yepremyan|2026_07_21_jiang_longbrake_yepremyan]]: A 2026 preprint of Jiang, Longbrake and Yepremyan shows that
$1+\frac{rt-1}{2rt+2r}$ is a Turán exponent for $t\ge2$ and $r\ge2t+3$, by
bounding rooted powers of the subdivided height-two tree. This gives
infinitely many instances.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/claims/2026_09_03_adamczewski|2026_09_03_adamczewski]]: A Lean proof, found by GPT-6 Astra and published in Adamczewski's repository,
shows that every rational $\alpha\in[1,2)$ is the Turán exponent of a finite
bipartite graph. It is accepted on Lean built here.
