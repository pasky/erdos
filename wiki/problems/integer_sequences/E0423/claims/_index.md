# problems/integer_sequences/E0423/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0423/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0423/claims/2026_01_15_bolan|2026_01_15_bolan]]: Bolan's note, posted in the erdosproblems.com thread, claims a proof that for
every $N$ some integer between $N$ and $2^{3N+2}$ is missing from the
Hofstadter consecutive-sum sequence, so that the sequence omits infinitely
many positive integers.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0423/claims/2026_01_15_tang|2026_01_15_tang]]: Tang claims a proof that $a_n-n$ is nondecreasing and unbounded, so the
sequence omits infinitely many integers. Tang also claims the bounds
$a_n\ge n+\log\log n/\log20-O(1)$ and $a_n\le n^{4175/2506+\varepsilon}$.
