# problems/set_theory/E0590/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/claims/1972_05_01_chang|1972_05_01_chang]]: Chang proves $\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,3)^2$, the statement of Problem
590, in a 57-page paper of 1972: every red-blue coloring of the pairs from the
ordinal $\omega^\omega$ has a red set of order type $\omega^\omega$ or a blue
triangle.

[[problems/set_theory/E0590/claims/1973_12_01_larson|1973_12_01_larson]]: Larson proves $\omega^\omega\to(\omega^\omega,m)^2$ for every finite $m$ by a
short argument, and the case $m=3$ is the statement of Problem 590. The Lean
formalization in Boris Alexeev's repository follows her proof.
