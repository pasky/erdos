# problems/diophantine_problems/E0445/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0445/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0445/claims/2012_04_28_browning_haynes|2012_04_28_browning_haynes]]: Theorem 1 of Browning and Haynes's refereed paper, taken with $J=1$, finds an
inverse pair in any two intervals whose lengths have product above
$Cp^{3/2}(\log p)^2$. This gives such a pair in every interval of length $p^c$
for every fixed $c>3/4$.
