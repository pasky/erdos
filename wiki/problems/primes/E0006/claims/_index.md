[[problems/primes/E0006/claims/2013_11_27_banks_freiberg_turnage_butterbaugh|2013_11_27_banks_freiberg_turnage_butterbaugh]]: Banks, Freiberg and Turnage-Butterbaugh prove that for every $m$ there are
infinitely many strings of $m+1$ consecutive primes whose gaps strictly
increase, and infinitely many whose gaps strictly decrease. The case $m=3$ is
the question, and the paper is refereed.
