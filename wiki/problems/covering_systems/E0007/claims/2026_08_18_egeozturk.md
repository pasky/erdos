***

**Claim.** The forum account egeozturk announces a preprint titled
*Closed-form nonexistence certificates for odd covering systems*. It claims
that a finite distinct odd covering system with moduli greater than one has
least common multiple at least $11{,}486{,}475$. It also claims noncoverage
for periods $3^a5^b7^c$ with arbitrary nonnegative exponents.

**Covers.** The negative answer to
[[problems/covering_systems/E0007/_index|Problem 7]] for the stated periods.
The announcement leaves $11{,}486{,}475$ uncertified by its method; it does
not claim a covering at that period. A three-prime formula tested on finite
examples is explicitly conjectural and is not included in this claim.

**Standing.** Claimed. The forum post was read on 2026-10-16. The linked
ResearchGate page returned HTTP 403, so the preprint was not read and no proof
or certificate was checked. The available announcement does not establish a
real author name, and `authors` is empty. No independent review or acceptance
is recorded.

**Depends on.** Nothing in this wiki. The announced exact certificates have
not been inspected.

2026-08-18.
