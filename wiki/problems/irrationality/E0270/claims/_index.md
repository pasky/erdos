irrational.

[[problems/irrationality/E0270/claims/2026_08_22_lambrinoudis|2026_08_22_lambrinoudis]]: Costa Lambrinoudis's manuscript of August 2026, written with generative AI,

claims transcendence, and hence irrationality, for every $b\ge1-a$.
