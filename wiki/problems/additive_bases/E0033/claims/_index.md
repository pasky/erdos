# problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims/1965_01_01_moser|1965_01_01_moser]]: Moser's 1965 proceedings paper proves that every additive complement of the
squares has counting function $A(N)$ with $\liminf A(N)/\sqrt N>1.06$, the
first yes to the liminf question. The proceedings volume is not shown to be
refereed, so the claim is pending.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims/1993_07_01_cilleruelo|1993_07_01_cilleruelo]]: Cilleruelo's 1993 theorem shows that a set completing the squares up to $N$
has at least $(4/\pi+o(1))\sqrt N$ elements. So every additive complement of
the squares has liminf at least $4/\pi$, which answers the liminf question
yes.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims/1995_03_01_habsieger|1995_03_01_habsieger]]: Habsieger's 1995 theorem gives an explicit lower bound for an additive
complement of a polynomial's values up to $N$, with constant $4/\pi$ for the
squares. So every additive complement of the squares has liminf at least
$4/\pi$.
