# problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/1973_05_01_erdos_graham_montgomery_rothschild_spencer_straus|1973_05_01_erdos_graham_montgomery_rothschild_spencer_straus]]: Theorems 13 and 20 of Euclidean Ramsey Theorems I (refereed, JCTA 1973) show
that every finite Ramsey set lies on a sphere and that the orthogonal product
of two Ramsey sets is Ramsey. So every rectangular box and each of its subsets
is Ramsey.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/1990_01_01_frankl_rodl|1990_01_01_frankl_rodl]]: Theorem 5.1 of Frankl and Rödl's 1990 paper shows that every finite affinely
independent set is super-Ramsey, with exponentially dense finite witnesses, so
every nondegenerate simplex is Ramsey. It is refereed, in J. Amer. Math. Soc.
3 (1990).

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/1991_07_01_kriz|1991_07_01_kriz]]: Theorem 4.3 of Kříž's refereed paper in PAMS 112 (1991) shows that a finite
set with a transitive soluble group of isometries is Ramsey, with a two-orbit
extension. So every regular polygon and each regular polyhedron is Ramsey.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/1992_10_01_kriz|1992_10_01_kriz]]: Kříž's refereed note in Discrete Math. 108 (1992) proves that every trapezoid
in its sense, a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle with two parallel sides,
is Ramsey.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/2026_09_20_palvolgyi|2026_09_20_palvolgyi]]: Pálvölgyi claims that seven points on a circle with a transcendental radius
form a set that is not Ramsey. This is the first claimed disproof of Graham's
conjecture that every spherical set is Ramsey, and the author attributes the
proof to ChatGPT.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/claims/2026_09_23_openai|2026_09_23_openai]]: OpenAI shows that a finite set is Ramsey exactly when an explicit linear
system over the tensor square of its coordinate field has a solution. The
result is kernel-checked in Lean and was built and axiom-audited here, but no
decision procedure is proved.
