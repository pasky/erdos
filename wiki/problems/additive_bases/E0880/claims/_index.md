[[problems/additive_bases/E0880/claims/2007_09_01_hegyvari_hennecart_plagne|2007_09_01_hegyvari_hennecart_plagne]]: Hegyvári, Hennecart and Plagne (2007) prove bounded gaps for a basis of order
two. For every order $k\ge3$ they construct a basis whose sums of distinct
elements have unbounded gaps, so the assertion fails except for $k=2$.
