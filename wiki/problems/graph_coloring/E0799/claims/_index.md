# problems/graph_coloring/E0799/claims

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0799/_index|..]]

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0799/claims/1992_06_01_alon|1992_06_01_alon]]: Alon proves that almost every graph on $n$ vertices has list chromatic number
at most a constant times $n\log\log n/\log n$, hence $o(n)$. The proof goes
through the choice number of complete multipartite graphs.

[[problems/graph_coloring/E0799/claims/1999_10_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov|1999_10_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov]]: Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov prove that the list chromatic number of the
random graph $G(n,p)$ is almost surely of order $np/\log(np)$ for
$2<np\le n/2$. At $p=1/2$, almost every graph has list chromatic number of
order $n/\log n$.
