# problems/discrepancy/E0067/claims

[[problems/discrepancy/E0067/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrepancy/E0067/claims/2015_09_17_tao|2015_09_17_tao]]: Tao proved in 2015 that for every sign sequence $f:\mathbb{N}\to\{-1,+1\}$ and
every $C>0$ there are $d,m\ge1$ with $\left|\sum_{k=1}^{m}f(kd)\right|>C$.
Refereed in Discrete Analysis in 2016, the result answers the question yes.
