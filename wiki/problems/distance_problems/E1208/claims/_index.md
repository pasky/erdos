# problems/distance_problems/E1208/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E1208/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E1208/claims/2026_06_14_lee|2026_06_14_lee]]: Sungchul Lee's manuscript of 14 June 2026 claims
$F_2(n)\ll n^{1/2-\varepsilon}$ for an absolute $\varepsilon>0$, a fixed
power below the square-grid bound $n^{1/2}/(\log n)^{1/4}$.

[[problems/distance_problems/E1208/claims/2026_07_06_lee_pohoata_zhu|2026_07_06_lee_pohoata_zhu]]: An arXiv preprint by Lee, Pohoata and Zhu claims a set of $n$ points in the
plane in which every subset of at least $n^{1/2-\delta}$ points repeats a
distance, so that $F_2(n)\lesssim n^{1/2-\delta}$ for an absolute $\delta>0$.
