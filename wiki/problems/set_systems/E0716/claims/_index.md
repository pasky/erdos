[[problems/set_systems/E0716/claims/1978_01_01_ruzsa_szemeredi|1978_01_01_ruzsa_szemeredi]]: Ruzsa and Szemerédi (1978) prove that a $3$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices
in which no six vertices span three edges has $o(n^2)$ edges. This answers the
Brown, Erdős and Sós question yes, and the bound is nearly sharp.
