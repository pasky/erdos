# problems/unit_fractions/E0305/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/claims/1976_05_01_bleicher_erdos|1976_05_01_bleicher_erdos]]: Bleicher and Erdős (J. Number Theory 1976) prove
$D(P)\ge P\lceil\log_2P\rceil$ for primes $P$ and $D(N)\le KN(\ln N)^3$. The
paper is refereed, and its prime lower bound shows that the exponent $1$ of
$\log b$ in Problem 305 cannot be lowered.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/claims/1976_12_01_bleicher_erdos|1976_12_01_bleicher_erdos]]: Bleicher and Erdős (Illinois J. Math. 1976) prove
$D(N)\le\lambda^3(N)N(\ln N)^2$ with $\lambda(N)\to1$ and sharpen their prime
lower bound. The paper is refereed, and its exponent $2$ does not reach the
$1+o(1)$ of Problem 305.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/claims/1988_10_01_yokota|1988_10_01_yokota]]: Yokota's refereed 1988 theorem states that $D(N)/N\le(\log N)^{1+\delta(N)}$
with $\delta(N)\to0$, where $D(N)$ is the least largest denominator. It proves
the Bleicher–Erdős conjecture, and the site credits it as the solution of
Problem 305.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0305/claims/2024_04_10_liu_sawhney|2024_04_10_liu_sawhney]]: Liu and Sawhney's Theorem 1.5 (2024; IMRN 2026) states that every fraction
$a/b$ is a sum of distinct unit fractions with denominators at most
$b(\log b)(\log\log b)^3(\log\log\log b)^{O(1)}$. This sharpens Yokota's
bound.
