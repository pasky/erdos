# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0053/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0053/claims/2003_05_01_chang|2003_05_01_chang]]: Theorem 2 of Chang (Ann. of Math. 2003) bounds the number of simple sums and
products of a $k$-element integer set below by $k$ to a power of order
$\log k/\log\log k$, which beats every fixed power of $k$. The site's curator
credits it, and it is refereed.
