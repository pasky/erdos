# theory/covering_systems

[[theory/_index|..]]

[[theory/covering_systems/L13_useful_supply_obstruction/_index|L13_useful_supply_obstruction/]]: Capping each prime-power cylinder's supply at its deficit gives an
explicit sufficient obstruction for all distinct nonunit-divisor profiles.

***

These claims state their own hypotheses on moduli, residues, and coverage.
A constraint proved under such hypotheses, such as distinct moduli dividing a
fixed integer, is not automatically a constraint on every covering system.
Source theorems retain their canonical homes in
[[../library/covering_systems/_index|the covering-systems library]].
