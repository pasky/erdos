# problems/integer_sequences/E0542/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0542/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0542/claims/1959_01_17_schinzel_szekeres|1959_01_17_schinzel_szekeres]]: Schinzel and Szekeres (1959) show that sets of integers up to $n$ with
pairwise least common multiples above $n$ have reciprocal sum at most $31/30$,
yet can leave only $o(n)$ integers divisible by no element, so the answers are
yes and no. The result is refereed and credited.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0542/claims/1996_01_01_chen|1996_01_01_chen]]: Chen (1996) shows that the largest reciprocal sum of a set of integers up to
$n$ with pairwise least common multiples above $n$ has limsup at most
$1.0170166$, so the $31/30$ bound holds for all large $n$. It is a refereed
partial proof.
