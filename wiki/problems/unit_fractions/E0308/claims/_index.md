# problems/unit_fractions/E0308/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0308/claims/1999_12_01_croot|1999_12_01_croot]]: Croot's refereed 1999 Main Theorem, credited by erdosproblems.com, places
between two floors of the harmonic sum the largest $n(N)$ such that every
integer up to it is a sum of distinct unit fractions with denominators at most
$N$. It answers both questions of the modified Statement for large $N$.
