# problems/set_theory/E1119/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/1964_03_01_erdos|1964_03_01_erdos]]: Erdős (Michigan Math. J., 1964) proves that when some cardinal lies strictly
between $\mathfrak m$ and the continuum, a family of analytic functions taking
at most $\mathfrak m$ values at each point has at most $\mathfrak m$ members.
Others have formalized the result.

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/2017_01_05_kumar_shelah|2017_01_05_kumar_shelah]]: Kumar and Shelah show that after adding $\aleph_1$ Cohen reals to a model with
$\mathfrak c=\aleph_2$, every family of $\mathfrak c$ entire functions takes
$\mathfrak c$ values at some point, so a yes answer is consistent. The paper
was refereed in 2017.

[[problems/set_theory/E1119/claims/2023_10_30_schilhan_weinert|2023_10_30_schilhan_weinert]]: Schilhan and Weinert force, over a model of GCH, $\aleph_2$ entire functions
taking at most $\aleph_1$ values at each point while $\mathfrak c=\aleph_2$,
so a no answer is consistent. With Kumar and Shelah's model, the problem is
independent of ZFC.
