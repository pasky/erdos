***

**Claim.** For real $\alpha$ and integer $u\ge1$ let $G_\alpha(u)$ be the
minimum, over all sets of $u$ distinct primes with largest member $P$ and
all blocks of $\lfloor\alpha P\rfloor$ consecutive positive integers, of
the number of integers in the block divisible by at least one of the
primes, each integer counted once. Equivalently, $G_\alpha(u)$ is the least
value of $F_{\lfloor\alpha p_u\rfloor}(p_1,\ldots,p_u)$ over all choices
of primes $p_1<\cdots<p_u$. For every real $\alpha$ with $2<\alpha<3$ and
every integer $u\ge1$,

$$
G_\alpha(u)=\lceil2\sqrt u\rceil.
$$

**Covers.** The estimate asked by
[[problems/primes/E1143/_index|Problem 1143]] in the range $k=\alpha p_u$,
for the least value over choices of the primes: that value exactly, for
$2<\alpha<3$ and every $u$, extending the perfect-square case of
[[problems/primes/E1143/claims/1978_01_01_erdos_selfridge|Erdős and Selfridge]].
It does not compute $F_k$ for a prescribed set of primes and says nothing
about $\alpha\ge3$.

**Credit.** The claim is a rectangular form of the construction in
Theorem 1 of Section 6 of
[[../library/extremal_graph_theory/erdos_1978_problems_results_combinatorial_analysis_combinatorial_number/_index|Erdős's 1978 paper]],
joint work with Selfridge, given in full with the prime number theorem and
the Chinese remainder theorem; the exposition and the extension to every
$u$ are author-recorded by Plasma AI, with no novelty claimed.
