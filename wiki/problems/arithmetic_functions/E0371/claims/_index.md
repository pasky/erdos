# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0371/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0371/claims/2021_01_20_wang|2021_01_20_wang]]: Wang proves that, under the Elliott–Halberstam conjecture for friable
integers, the $n$ with $P(n)<P(n+1)$ have asymptotic density $1/2$. The paper
is refereed, but the result is conditional, so it settles no standing.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0371/claims/2026_09_24_openai|2026_09_24_openai]]: The OpenAI release claims independent Dickman limit laws for the largest prime
factors of $n$ and $n+1$, and deduces that the $n$ with $P(n)<P(n+1)$ have
natural density $1/2$. This corpus built and audited its Lean proof of the
corollary, so the claim is accepted.
