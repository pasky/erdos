# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/1989_10_01_erdos_hajnal|1989_10_01_erdos_hajnal]]: Erdős and Hajnal (1989) prove their conjecture for every graph $H$ on at most
four vertices, alongside the general bound $\exp(c\sqrt{\log n})$ for every
$H$. The claim is accepted on the refereed Discrete Applied Mathematics paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2001_04_01_alon_pach_solymosi|2001_04_01_alon_pach_solymosi]]: Alon, Pach and Solymosi prove that the graphs with the Erdős-Hajnal property
are closed under vertex substitution, which with the four-vertex cases settles
every graph built from them. The claim is accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2008_06_28_chudnovsky_safra|2008_06_28_chudnovsky_safra]]: Chudnovsky and Safra prove that every bull-free graph on $n$ vertices has a
clique or a stable set of size at least $n^{1/4}$, which is the Erdős-Hajnal
conjecture for the bull. The claim is accepted on the refereed JCTB paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2021_02_09_chudnovsky_scott_seymour_spirkl|2021_02_09_chudnovsky_scott_seymour_spirkl]]: Chudnovsky, Scott, Seymour and Spirkl prove that for some $t>0$, every graph
$G$ with no induced five-cycle has a clique or a stable set of size at least
$|G|^t$. This is the Erdős-Hajnal conjecture for $C_5$, and the claim is
accepted on the refereed paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2023_07_12_nguyen_scott_seymour|2023_07_12_nguyen_scott_seymour]]: Nguyen, Scott and Seymour prove the Erdős-Hajnal conjecture for every graph
whose prime induced subgraphs $H'$ each have a vertex of degree one and a
vertex of degree $|H'|-2$. This family has infinitely many prime members, and
the paper is refereed (TAMS).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2023_12_23_nguyen_scott_seymour|2023_12_23_nguyen_scott_seymour]]: Nguyen, Scott and Seymour prove the Erdős-Hajnal conjecture for the
five-vertex path. With the earlier cases this completes every five-vertex
graph, and the claim is accepted on the refereed Proc. Lond. Math. Soc. paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/claims/2026_06_04_huang_ju_zhou|2026_06_04_huang_ju_zhou]]: Huang, Ju and Zhou (arXiv 2026) claim the Erdős-Hajnal conjecture for two
six-vertex graphs, the E-graph and the Bird graph. These are the first
six-vertex cases that do not follow from known operations.
