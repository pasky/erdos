# problems/diophantine_problems/E0433/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0433/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0433/claims/1990_02_01_dixmier|1990_02_01_dixmier]]: Dixmier proves two-sided bounds on $g(k,n)$, the largest Frobenius number of a
coprime $k$-element subset of the first $n$ integers. For fixed $k$ they give
$g(k,n)\sim n^2/(k-1)$, as Erdős and Graham conjectured.
