# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/claims/1980_11_01_ajtai_komlos_szemeredi|1980_11_01_ajtai_komlos_szemeredi]]: Theorem 2 of Ajtai, Komlós and Szemerédi (J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 1980) gives
every triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices with average degree $t$ an
independent set of at least $0.01(n/t)\ln t$ vertices. This is the case $r=3$
of the question.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/claims/2026_09_25_openai|2026_09_25_openai]]: Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI release manuscript of 25 September 2026 proves that
for every fixed $r\ge4$, a $K_r$-free graph on $n$ vertices with average
degree $d\ge2$ has an independent set of at least $c_rn\log d/d$ vertices.
