**Claim.** For a positive integer $M$ let $R_M$ be the set of positive
integers congruent to $0$, $1$ or $2$ modulo $M$, and put

$$
A=\bigcup_{k\ge0}\left(
\left(R_{2q}\cap\left[\frac{4q^2}{9},4q^2\right]\right)
\cup
\left(R_q\cap\left[\frac{5q^2}{9},q^2\right]\right)
\right),\qquad q=3^k.
$$

Every positive integer is $m^2+a$ with an integer $m\ge0$ and $a\in A$,
and, writing $A(X)=\lvert A\cap[1,X]\rvert$ for real $X$,

$$
\liminf_{X\to\infty}\frac{A(X)}{\sqrt X}=3\sqrt2,
\qquad
\limsup_{X\to\infty}\frac{A(X)}{\sqrt X}=\frac92.
$$

The upper limit is witnessed along $X=3^{2k}$. Hence the smallest possible
value of the limsup in the first question of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/_index|Problem 33]] is at most $9/2$.

**Covers.** An upper bound of $9/2$ for the smallest possible limsup, the
first question of [[problems/additive_bases/E0033/_index|Problem 33]]. The
value lies below the pending upper bounds of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims/2025_08_12_van_doorn|van Doorn]],
$2\varphi^{5/2}\approx6.66$, and of
[[problems/additive_bases/E0033/claims/2026_09_07_donteatllamas|the unreviewed write-up of 2026-09-07]],
$13\sqrt2/4\approx4.596$. The claim does not determine the smallest
limsup and asserts neither optimality of this set nor a bound
$A(X)\le(9/2)\sqrt X$ at every cutoff. The lower limit $3\sqrt2$ is that of
this one set and says nothing about the liminf question, which concerns every
complement.
