# problems/distance_problems/E0093/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0093/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0093/claims/1963_02_01_altman|1963_02_01_altman]]: The vertices of a convex $n$-gon in the plane determine at least
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ distinct distances, which is sharp for the regular
polygon. Altman proved this conjecture of Erdős in the Monthly in 1963, and
the site credits it and links a Lean file.
