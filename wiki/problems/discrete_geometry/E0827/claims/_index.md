# problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2014_02_25_martinez_roldan_pensado|2014_02_25_martinez_roldan_pensado]]: Martínez and Roldán-Pensado repair Erdős's 1978 argument and prove that $n_k$
exists with $n_k=O(k^9)$, and that $n_4\le9$ and $n_5\le37$. The paper is
refereed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2015_05_19_martinez_sandoval_raggi_roldan_pensado|2015_05_19_martinez_sandoval_raggi_roldan_pensado]]: From a sunflower anti-Ramsey theorem, Martínez-Sandoval, Raggi and
Roldán-Pensado derive $n_k=O(k^5/\log k)$ when no four points are concyclic.
The source is an arXiv manuscript.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2026_09_22_sallerk|2026_09_22_sallerk]]: A computer-assisted proof that $n_4=7$ under Erdős's general position uses SAT
search and ideal saturation in Singular, and gives lower bounds for $n_5$,
$n_6$ and $n_7$. It was posted on the site's thread with a dated write-up.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/claims/2026_09_24_kiichi|2026_09_24_kiichi]]: A proof by Kiichi, Shiori and Rin that $n_4=7$ under Erdős's general position
classifies the witness structures of six points and is checked end to end in
Lean 4. It was posted on the site's thread with a paper dated 2026-10-03.
