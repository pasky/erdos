***

**Claim.** T. Alexander Lystad's proof claim of 1 August 2026 on the
proof-claims tab of [[problems/irrationality/E0263/_index|Problem 263]] gives
a Lean 4 and Mathlib proof of the folklore growth criterion that the
erdosproblems.com remarks state: if a strictly increasing sequence of positive
integers satisfies $c\,a_n^{2+\epsilon}\le a_{n+1}$ for all large $n$, for some
$c,\epsilon>0$, then $\sum1/b_n$ is irrational for every sequence of
positive integers $b_n$ with $b_n/a_n\to1$. The development, generated with
Kimi K3 under Lystad's direction, is in the repository linked above, with the
write-up `PROOF.md`; the entry says the build completes with no `sorry`
outside the problem's first question, which it states as open.

**Submission note.** Posted to erdosproblems.com as a proof claim by T.
Alexander Lystad (account alexander_lystad) on 1 August 2026, giving "Kimi K3"
as the AI used:

> A Lean 4 + Mathlib proof of the folklore growth criterion: if a strictly
> increasing sequence of positive integers satisfies c·a_n^{2+ε} ≤ a_{n+1}
> eventually for some ε, c > 0, then for EVERY sequence b_n with b_n/a_n → 1 the
> sum ∑ 1/b_n is irrational. Proof: if ∑ 1/b_n = p/q, then q·∏_{n≤N} b_n ·
> ∑_{n>N} 1/b_n is a positive integer; forward iteration (a_{M+k} ≥ a_M^{2^k})
> bounds the tail by C/a_N^{2+ε}, while backward iteration (a_{N-k} ≤
> K·a_N^{θ^k}, θ = 1/(2+ε)) bounds the product by C^{N+1}·a_N^σ with σ =
> (2+ε)/(1+ε) < 2+ε — so the integer tends to 0, a contradiction. The
> criterion fails for a_n = 2^{2^n} by exactly an ε (there a_{n+1} = a_n²), so
> Q1 remains open. Notes: Verification: 'lake exe cache get && lake build' in
> the linked repository completes with zero 'sorry' outside the declared open
> obligation (Q1 is stated with an honest 'sorry'). Archived release: v1.0.0,
> DOI: \<DOI from Zenodo\>. The repository also contains a supporting
> zero-sorry package (including a formal proof that ∑ 1/2^{2^n} is irrational)
> and a fidelity-audited formal statement of the problem. Independently of this
> claim: the community formal statement in FormalConjectures
> (ErdosProblems/263.lean) omits the "increasing" hypothesis this site added on
> 2026-04-02, so its Q2 entry covers the pre-correction statement; reported
> upstream with fix PRs (google-deepmind/formal-conjectures issue #4677, PRs

**Why it is rejected.** It does not address the Statement.
[[problems/irrationality/E0263/_index|Problem 263]] asks whether
$a_n=2^{2^n}$ is an irrationality sequence and whether every irrationality
sequence has $a_n^{1/n}\to\infty$. The claim addresses instead the folklore
sufficient criterion, a known result the erdosproblems.com remarks state, which
$2^{2^n}$ misses by the $\epsilon$, as the entry says; it decides neither
question, so its mathematics does not settle this problem. The value `proved`
is its answer to the criterion.

**Standing.** Rejected, as not addressing the Statement. The entry carries four
comments on erdosproblems.com and no review; this corpus has neither read the
repository nor built the development, so it gives a formalization link and no
`formalized` evidence.
