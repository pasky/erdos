# problems/divisors/E0534/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0534/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0534/claims/1996_01_01_ahlswede_khachatrian|1996_01_01_ahlswede_khachatrian]]: Ahlswede and Khachatrian prove that the maximum is attained, for some $j$, by
the integers up to $N$ divisible by one of $2q_1,\ldots,2q_j,q_1\cdots q_j$,
where $q_1<\cdots<q_r$ are the prime factors of $N$. Erdős conjectured this
form after the original guess failed.
