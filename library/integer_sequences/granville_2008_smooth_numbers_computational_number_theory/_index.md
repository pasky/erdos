## Scope

The chapter surveys smooth-number counting estimates, applications to factoring
and other algorithms, and methods for intervals, arithmetic progressions and
polynomial values. Section 4.1, printed pp. 294–295, distinguishes uniform
short-interval results, almost-all results and conjectures, and points to
Friedlander–Lagarias, Harman and Croot.

The range summary for Harman on p. 295 is not used here as an unconditional
replacement for the original theorem. Harman's Theorem 2 requires its
exponent-pair condition and makes the simpler formula for $\theta$ conditional
on the exponent-pair conjecture; see
[[integer_sequences/harman_1999_smooth_integers_short_intervals_progressions/_index|the original statement and its qualifications]].
