# Non-reflexive curves

[[set_systems/_index|..]]

***

Abramo Hefez, *Non-reflexive curves*, *Compositio Mathematica* **69** (1)
(1989), 3–35. The edition read is the journal scan at the
[Numdam article record](https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1989__69_1_3_0/),
obtained from its
[PDF endpoint](https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1989__69_1_3_0.pdf).

Section 2 recalls the conormal projection, reflexivity and the
Segre–Wallace separability criterion. Its generic order of contact
statement (2.1) is cited to earlier work of Hefez and Kleiman. For a
nonlinear irreducible projective curve over an algebraically closed field
of characteristic $p>0$, the consequence at the top of printed p. 5
excludes non-reflexivity when its degree is less than $p$.

**Read status: claims checked; original proof unread.** The introduction
and the preliminary discussion on printed pp. 3–5 were read from the
scan's text. The degree consequence and its placement were checked on
the image of printed p. 5. The cited original proof of (2.1), the
separability criterion and the later sections were not verified.

**Bears on.** The degree-versus-characteristic condition is a source
interface in the curve obstruction relevant to
[[../wiki/problems/set_systems/E1159/_index|Problem 1159]], as used in
[[set_systems/asgarli_2025_blocking_sets_union_plane_curves/_index|Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip's curve argument]].
The componentwise and union hypotheses must also be matched to
[[set_systems/entin_2018_monodromy_hyperplane_sections_curves/_index|Entin's reflexivity interface]].
This source gives no approximation theorem for arbitrary blocking sets.
