# problems/distance_problems/E0958/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0958/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0958/claims/2025_05_07_clemen_dumitrescu_liu|2025_05_07_clemen_dumitrescu_liu]]: Clemen, Dumitrescu and Liu observe that equally spaced points on a short arc
of a unit circle, together with the center, determine $n-1$ distances with
multiplicities $n-1,\ldots,1$ for every $n$. So the characterization by lines
and circles fails.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0958/claims/2025_12_27_alexeev|2025_12_27_alexeev]]: In a claimed disproof by Boris Alexeev and Aristotle, the set $(0,0)$,
$(1,0)$, $(0,1)$, $(0,-1)$ determines three distances with multiplicities 3,
2, 1 over unordered pairs and lies on no line and no circle. This refutes the
characterization at $n=4$.
