# Discrete and Convex Geometry

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0101/_index|E0101/]]: Asks whether $n$ points in
the plane, no five on a line, determine only $o(n^2)$ lines containing exactly
four of them.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0102/_index|E0102/]]: Asks how many collinear
points are forced when $n$ points in the plane determine at least $cn^2$ lines
with more than three points each, and whether this number $h_c(n)$ tends to
infinity for fixed $c>0$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0104/_index|E0104/]]: Asks whether $n$ points in
the plane lie on only $o(n^2)$ distinct unit circles that each contain at least
three of them.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0105/_index|E0105/]]: Asks whether disjoint plane
sets $A$ and $B$ of $n$ and $n-3$ points, with $A$ not all collinear, always
admit a line through at least two points of $A$ and none of $B$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0106/_index|E0106/]]: Asks whether $f(k^2+1)=k$,
where $f(n)$ is the largest total side length of $n$ interior-disjoint squares
in the unit square.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0107/_index|E0107/]]: Asks whether $2^{n-2}+1$
points in the plane, no three on a line, always contain the vertices of a convex
$n$-gon.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/_index|E0173/]]: Asks whether every
two-coloring of the plane contains a monochromatic congruent copy of every
triangle, with at most one exception.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0174/_index|E0174/]]: Asks for a characterization
of the Ramsey sets: the finite point sets with a monochromatic copy in every
finite coloring of $\mathbb{R}^d$ for large enough $d$. OpenAI answered it in
2026 with an algebraic criterion that refutes Graham's conjecture.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0188/_index|E0188/]]: Asks for the least $k$ such
that the plane can be colored red and blue with no two red points at unit
distance and no blue $k$-term progression with step $1$. If the step is left
free, Erdős and Graham's question has no finite answer.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0189/_index|E0189/]]: Asks whether every finite
coloring of the plane has a color class containing the vertices of a rectangle
of every area.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0193/_index|E0193/]]: Asks whether every infinite
walk in $\mathbb{Z}^3$ with steps from a finite set must contain three collinear
points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0209/_index|E0209/]]: Asks whether $d\ge4$
non-parallel lines in the plane, no four through one point, must form a Gallai
triangle: three of the lines whose three intersection points each lie on only
two lines.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0210/_index|E0210/]]: Asks whether the least
number $f(n)$ of lines through exactly two of $n$ points in the plane, not all
collinear, tends to infinity, and how fast.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0211/_index|E0211/]]: Asks whether $n$ points in
the plane, at most $n-k$ on any line, always determine $\gg kn$ lines through at
least two of them.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0215/_index|E0215/]]: Asks whether some set
$S\subseteq\mathbb{R}^2$ has the property that every translated and rotated copy
of it contains exactly one point of $\mathbb{Z}^2$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0216/_index|E0216/]]: Asks whether, for each $k$,
enough points in general position in the plane always contain an empty convex
$k$-gon, and how many are needed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0224/_index|E0224/]]: Asks whether any $2^d+1$
points in $\mathbb{R}^d$ must include three that determine an obtuse angle.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0352/_index|E0352/]]: Asks whether some $c>0$
makes every measurable set $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}^2$ of measure at least $c$
contain the vertices of a triangle of area $1$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0353/_index|E0353/]]: Asks whether a measurable
set of infinite measure in the plane must contain the vertices of an isosceles
trapezoid of area $1$, or of other prescribed shapes.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0504/_index|E0504/]]: Asks for the largest angle
$\alpha_n$ such that every set of $n$ points in the plane has three points
determining an angle of at least $\alpha_n$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0505/_index|E0505/]]: Asks whether every set of
diameter $1$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ is the union of at most $n+1$ sets of smaller
diameter.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0506/_index|E0506/]]: Asks for the least number of
circles determined by $n$ points in the plane, not all on one circle or one line
(Elliott's reading). Purdy and Smith's correction settles every $n>393$, and a
2026 claim giving every value is pending.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0507/_index|E0507/]]: Asks to estimate the
smallest area $\alpha(n)$ such that every set of $n$ points in the unit disk
contains three points forming a triangle of area at most $\alpha(n)$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0508/_index|E0508/]]: Asks for the fewest colors
needed to color the plane so that no two points at distance $1$ share a color.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0526/_index|E0526/]]: Asks for a necessary and
sufficient condition on arc lengths $a_n\to0$ with $\sum a_n=\infty$ under which
independent random arcs of these lengths cover the unit circle with probability
$1$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0528/_index|E0528/]]: Asks for the limiting growth
rate per step, $C_k=\lim_{n\to\infty}f(n,k)^{1/n}$, of the number $f(n,k)$ of
self-avoiding walks of $n$ steps from the origin in $\mathbb{Z}^k$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0529/_index|E0529/]]: Asks whether the expected
end-to-end distance $d_k(n)$ of an $n$-step self-avoiding walk in $\mathbb{Z}^k$
satisfies $d_2(n)/n^{1/2}\to\infty$, and $d_k(n)\ll n^{1/2}$ for every $k\ge3$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0588/_index|E0588/]]: Asks whether, for $k\ge4$,
$n$ points in the plane with no $k+1$ on a line determine only $o(n^2)$ lines
through $k$ points.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/_index|E0589/]]: Asks to estimate $g(n)$, the
size of the largest subset with no three points on a line that can always be
found among $n$ points in the plane with no four on a line.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0606/_index|E0606/]]: Asks which values can occur
as the number of distinct lines determined by $n$ distinct points in the plane.
The values are determined for all sufficiently large $n$, while the small cases
are open.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0607/_index|E0607/]]: Asks whether the number of
distinct sets of line sizes determined by $n$ points in the plane is at most
$\exp(O(\sqrt{n}))$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0633/_index|E0633/]]: Asks which triangles can
only be cut into a square number of congruent triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0634/_index|E0634/]]: Asks for all $n$ such that
some triangle can be cut into $n$ congruent triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0651/_index|E0651/]]: Asks whether the number
$f_k(n)$ of points in general position in $\mathbb{R}^k$ needed to guarantee $n$
in convex position grows exponentially in $n$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0669/_index|E0669/]]: Asks for bounds on how many
lines can pass through at least $k$, or through exactly $k$, of $n$ points in
the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/_index|E0704/]]: Asks to estimate the
chromatic number of the unit distance graph in $\mathbb{R}^n$, whose edges join
points at distance exactly $1$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0705/_index|E0705/]]: Asks whether some girth
bound forces every finite unit distance graph in the plane to be 3-colorable.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0733/_index|E0733/]]: Asks to prove that at most
$\exp(O(n^{1/2}))$ nondecreasing sequences can be the point counts of lines,
each through at least two of $n$ points in the plane.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0735/_index|E0735/]]: Asks when $n$ points in the
plane can be given positive weights so that every line through at least two of
them has the same total weight.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0755/_index|E0755/]]: Asks whether $n$ points in
$\mathbb{R}^6$ span at most $(\frac{1}{27}+o(1))n^3$ unit equilateral triangles.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0769/_index|E0769/]]: Asks for bounds on the least
$c(n)$ such that the $n$-dimensional unit cube splits into $k$ homothetic cubes
for every $k\ge c(n)$, and in particular whether $c(n)\gg n^n$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0798/_index|E0798/]]: Asks to estimate $t(n)$, the
fewest points in the grid $\{1,\ldots,n\}^2$ whose pairwise connecting lines
cover every point of the grid, and in particular whether $t(n)=o(n)$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0827/_index|E0827/]]: Asks how many points in
general position in the plane force $k$ of them whose triples all determine
circles of distinct radii.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0831/_index|E0831/]]: Asks to estimate the least
number of distinct radii among circles through three of $n$ points in the plane,
with no three collinear and no four concyclic.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0838/_index|E0838/]]: Asks to estimate the least
number $f(n)$ of distinct convex subsets determined by $n$ points in the plane
with no three collinear, and in particular whether $\log f(n)/(\log n)^2$ tends
to a constant.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0846/_index|E0846/]]: Asks whether an infinite
plane set in which, for some fixed $\epsilon>0$, every $n$ of its points include
at least $\epsilon n$ with no three collinear must be a finite union of sets
with no three collinear.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0898/_index|E0898/]]: Asks whether the sum of
distances from an interior point to a triangle's vertices is at least twice the
sum of its distances to the three sides.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0960/_index|E0960/]]: Asks how many ordinary lines
$n$ points in the plane with no $k$ on a line must have to force $r$ of the
points to span only ordinary lines, and whether $o(n^2)$ suffice.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1069/_index|E1069/]]: Bounds the number of lines
containing at least $k$ of $n$ points in the plane by $n^2/k^3$ for $k\le
n^{1/2}$. The bound fails as worded at $k=1$, and Szemerédi and Trotter proved
it for $2\le k\le n^{1/2}$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1070/_index|E1070/]]: Asks for estimates of the
largest $f(n)$ such that any $n$ points in the plane include $f(n)$ points with
no two at distance $1$, and whether $f(n)\ge n/4$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1071/_index|E1071/]]: Asks whether a finite family
of pairwise disjoint unit segments in the unit square can be maximal, so that no
further unit segment can be added.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1088/_index|E1088/]]: Asks for estimates of the
least $f_d(n)$ such that any $f_d(n)$ points in $\mathbb{R}^d$ contain $n$
points with all pairwise distances distinct, and whether $f_d(n)=2^{o(d)}$ for
fixed $n\ge3$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1090/_index|E1090/]]: Asks whether, for each
$k\ge3$, some finite set in the plane has the property that every $2$-coloring
of it gives a line meeting it in at least $k$ points, all of the same color.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1121/_index|E1121/]]: Asks whether circles in the
plane can always be covered by one circle whose radius is the sum of their
radii, provided no line missing all of them splits them into two nonempty
groups.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E1124/_index|E1124/]]: Asks whether a square and a
circle of the same area can be cut into finitely many congruent pieces.
