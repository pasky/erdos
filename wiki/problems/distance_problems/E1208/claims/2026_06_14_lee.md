***

**Claim.** Sungchul Lee, *Planar Point Sets with Small Distinct-Distance
Subsets*, a manuscript of 14 June 2026 in the author's repository Erdos1208
(the `preprint` link, pinned to the repository's last revision of that day),
states as Theorem 1.1 that there is an absolute constant $\varepsilon>0$ with

$$
F_2(n)\ll n^{1/2-\varepsilon},
$$

where $F_2(n)$ is the function of
[[problems/distance_problems/E1208/_index|Problem 1208]] for $d=2$: every set
of $n$ points in the plane contains $F_2(n)$ points with all pairwise
distances distinct. This is a fixed power below the square-grid bound
$F_2(n)\ll n^{1/2}/(\log n)^{1/4}$. The manuscript builds its point sets from
boxes in the rings of integers of a tower of totally real number fields of
growing degree and bounded root discriminant, the arithmetic input of the
OpenAI unit-distance construction, in which fixed primes $q\equiv1\pmod4$
split completely. It lifts to them a split-prime sieve of Pohoata and Sheffer
that bounds the distinct-distance subsets of the integer grid by
$N\exp(-c\log N/\log\log N)$; each split prime contributes one sign choice per
prime ideal, and that saving, exponential in the degree, becomes a fixed power
of $n$. Its section "Use of AI" says that the author used OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Pro
to explore proof strategies and obtain ideas, and then verified the details
himself. The statement is read from the TeX source at the pinned revision;
the proof was not checked here.

**Submission note.** Posted to the site's forum by Sungchul Lee on 14 June
2026:

> With the help of GPT-5.5 Pro, I have obtained a polynomial improvement to the
> upper bound for this problem:
>
> $$
> F_2(n) \ll n^{1/2-\varepsilon}
> $$
>
> for some absolute constant $\varepsilon>0$. This improves the previous
> square-grid upper bound $F_2(n)\ll n^{1/2-o(1)}$.
>
> The manuscript is available here: https://github.com/lsngchl/Erdos1208
>
> The main idea is to combine the number-field towers of bounded root
> discriminant used in the recent OpenAI unit-distance construction with a
> split-prime argument of Pohoata and Sheffer.
>
> I have checked the mathematical details of the proof myself, but I am not an
> expert in this area, so I would be very grateful for any comments,
> corrections, or pointers to relevant prior work.

**Covers.** An upper bound in the case $d=2$: $F_2(n)\ll n^{1/2-\varepsilon}$.
It does not determine the order of $F_2(n)$, and nothing is claimed for
$d\ge3$.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki. The manuscript takes its number-field
tower from the OpenAI unit-distance construction and from Alon, Bloom, Gowers,
Litt, Sawin, Shankar, Tsimerman, Wang and Wood (arXiv:2605.20695, 2026), and its
grid sieve from a blog post of Pohoata and Sheffer.

**Standing.** Claimed. The manuscript is self-published, with no journal
publication and no Lean proof. In the site's thread, Nat Sothanaphan reported
on 14 June 2026 a screening check that found no error, which is not a review of
the proof. The site's remarks credit the bound to the later joint paper of Lee,
Pohoata and Zhu, recorded on
[[problems/distance_problems/E1208/claims/2026_07_06_lee_pohoata_zhu|its claim page]],
whose acknowledgments say that this manuscript first established it.
