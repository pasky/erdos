# Bloking sets in finite planes and spaces

[[set_systems/_index|..]]

***

Tamás Szőnyi, *Bloking sets in finite planes and spaces*, Ratio Mathematica
**5** (1992), 93–106. The title retains the spelling printed in the paper and
in the [journal record](https://eiris.it/ojs/index.php/ratiomathematica/article/view/196).

The [fourteen-page scanned article](szonyi_1992_blocking_sets_finite_planes_spaces.pdf)
was obtained from the journal's
[PDF endpoint](https://eiris.it/ojs/index.php/ratiomathematica/article/download/196/227).
The abstract and first-page introduction discuss blocking sets in higher
finite dimensions, sets with few collinear points, and inversive planes.
They contrast geometric constructions with probabilistic covering methods.

**Bears on.** The few-collinear-points part is a prospective literature source
for
[[../wiki/problems/set_systems/E1159/_index|Problem 1159's bounded line-intersection question]].
Only the abstract and first-page introduction have been read for this card;
no theorem or proof is consumed here as a premise.
