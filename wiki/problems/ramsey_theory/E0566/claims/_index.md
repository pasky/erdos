# problems/ramsey_theory/E0566/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0566/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0566/claims/1993_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp|1993_12_01_erdos_faudree_rousseau_schelp]]: The 1993 paper of Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp that posed the question
proves Ramsey size linearity for three families, each inside the corrected
hypothesis: connected $p$-vertex graphs with at most $p+1$ edges, the graphs
$K_1+T_{p-1}$, and the graphs with Turán number $O(n^{3/2})$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0566/claims/2022_02_21_bradac_gishboliner_sudakov|2022_02_21_bradac_gishboliner_sudakov]]: Bradač, Gishboliner and Sudakov prove that every subdivision of $K_4$ on at
least six vertices is Ramsey size linear (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 2024, Theorem
4). Each such graph meets the corrected hypothesis, so the corrected question
has answer yes for them.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0566/claims/2026_09_28_barria|2026_09_28_barria]]: A September 2026 preprint by Barría claims that every graph with no $K_4$
minor is Ramsey size linear, with $R(G,H)\le624\,v(G)\,e(H)$, which covers
every $2$-tree. This is an unreviewed partial result on the corrected
question.
