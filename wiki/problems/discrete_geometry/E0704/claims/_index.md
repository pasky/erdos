# problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/claims/1981_12_01_frankl_wilson|1981_12_01_frankl_wilson]]: Frankl and Wilson's refereed 1981 intersection theorem gives an exponential
lower bound on the chromatic number of the unit distance graph of
$n$-dimensional space. This answers the exponential-growth question yes.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0704/claims/2000_04_30_raigorodskii|2000_04_30_raigorodskii]]: Raigorodskii's refereed 2000 note proves that the chromatic number of the unit
distance graph of $n$-dimensional space is at least $(1.239\ldots+o(1))^n$.
This raises Frankl and Wilson's base and answers the exponential-growth
question yes.
