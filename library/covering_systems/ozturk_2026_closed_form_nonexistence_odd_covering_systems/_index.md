# Closed-form nonexistence certificates for odd covering systems

[[covering_systems/_index|..]]

***

Ege Öztürk, *Closed-form nonexistence certificates for odd covering systems*,
preprint, version 2 (related work updated), August 2026,
[DOI 10.13140/RG.2.2.13216.55049](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.13216.55049).

The edition read is the eight-page version 2 PDF, obtained from the preprint's
ResearchGate record on 2026-10-16. It is not peer reviewed.

For odd $M$, let $u(M)$ be the least number of residues modulo $M$ left
uncovered when one residue class is chosen for every divisor $d > 1$ of $M$; a
covering system with distinct odd moduli dividing $M$ exists exactly when
$u(M) = 0$. The preprint builds closed-form certificates that $u(M) > 0$ (a peel
threshold, a first-order grid volume certificate from the Chinese remainder
structure of the survivors of the prime-power classes, and a second-order
refinement through a matching of coprime-support box pairs), each evaluable in
exact rational arithmetic without search. Its main claimed application is that
no covering system with distinct odd moduli greater than one has least common
multiple below $11{,}486{,}475$. It also claims infinite families of
covering-free moduli, the exact value $u(qr) = qr - q - r$ for distinct odd
primes $q$ and $r$, and an account of where certificates of this type must stop
short of the full question.
