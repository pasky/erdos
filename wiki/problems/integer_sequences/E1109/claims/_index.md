# problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/1987_03_01_erdos_sarkozy|1987_03_01_erdos_sarkozy]]: Erdős and Sárközy (Acta Math. Hungar. 1987) prove that the largest subset of
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with all pairwise sums squarefree has size between
$(\log N)/248$ and $3N^{3/4}\log N$ for large $N$. The result is refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/2002_08_01_gyarmati|2002_08_01_gyarmati]]: Gyarmati (Period. Math. Hungar. 2001) gives a set of size $\gg\log N$ in
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with all pairwise sums squarefree. This is a second proof, by
graph theory, of Erdős and Sárközy's lower bound, and it is refereed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1109/claims/2004_06_30_konyagin|2004_06_30_konyagin]]: Konyagin (Izv. Math. 2004) proves that the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$
with all pairwise sums squarefree has size at least $c\log^2N\log\log N$ and
at most $N^{11/15}$ times a subexponential factor. The result is refereed.
