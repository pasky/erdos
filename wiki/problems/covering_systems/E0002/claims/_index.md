# problems/covering_systems/E0002/claims

[[problems/covering_systems/E0002/_index|..]]

[[problems/covering_systems/E0002/claims/2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu|2005_07_18_filaseta_ford_konyagin_pomerance_yu]]: Theorem A of Filaseta, Ford, Konyagin, Pomerance and Yu (J. Amer. Math. Soc.
2007) shows that large distinct moduli with a slowly growing reciprocal sum
cannot cover the integers, so covering systems with bounded reciprocal sum
have bounded minimum modulus. The paper is refereed.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0002/claims/2013_07_02_hough|2013_07_02_hough]]: Hough proves in the Annals of Mathematics (2015) that every finite covering
system with distinct moduli greater than one has least modulus at most
$10^{16}$. The result is accepted on the refereed paper and the
erdosproblems.com credit.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0002/claims/2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba|2018_11_08_balister_bollobas_morris_sahasrabudhe_tiba]]: Theorem 8.1 of Balister, Bollobás, Morris, Sahasrabudhe and Tiba (Inventiones
2022) shows that distinct moduli all at least 616000 cannot cover the
integers. This second proof that the minimum modulus is bounded is accepted on
the refereed paper and the erdosproblems.com credit.

[[problems/covering_systems/E0002/claims/2022_11_15_cummings_filaseta_trifonov|2022_11_15_cummings_filaseta_trifonov]]: Theorem 1.1 of Cummings, Filaseta and Trifonov (Acta Math. Hungar. 2025) shows
that a covering system with distinct squarefree moduli has smallest modulus at
most 118. This is a sharper bound for that restricted class, and the paper is
refereed.
