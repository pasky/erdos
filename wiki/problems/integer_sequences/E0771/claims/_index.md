# problems/integer_sequences/E0771/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0771/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0771/claims/1988_12_01_alon_freiman|1988_12_01_alon_freiman]]: Alon and Freiman find an $m$ for which the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$
with no subset summing to $m$ has $(\frac12+o(1))n/\log n$ elements, matching
the Erdős-Graham lower bound. The paper appeared in Combinatorica (1988) and
the site credits it.
