**Claim.** Junichiro Fukuyama, *The Sunflower Conjecture Proven*,
[arXiv:2212.13609v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13609v3), claims an absolute
constant $C>0$ such that every family of sets of size at most $m$ with
more than $(Ck^4)^m$ members contains $k$ distinct sets with a common
pairwise intersection. For positive $n$ and fixed $k$, specializing to
$n$-uniform families and enlarging the base gives the strict exponential
bound asked in [[problems/set_systems/E0020/_index|Problem 20]].

**Covers.** The full displayed sunflower conjecture.
