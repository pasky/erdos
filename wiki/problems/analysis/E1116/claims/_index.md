# problems/analysis/E1116/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1116/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1116/claims/1976_01_01_toppila|1976_01_01_toppila]]: Toppila's 1976 Theorems 2 and 3 give a meromorphic function, and an entire
function, with $\limsup n(r,a)/n(r,b)=\infty$ for every pair of distinct
values $a,b$ (finite values in the entire case). This answers the question
yes, and the paper is refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E1116/claims/1978_01_01_goldberg|1978_01_01_goldberg]]: Gol'dberg's 1978 theorem gives an entire function with
$\limsup n(r,a)/n(r,b)=\infty$ and $\liminf n(r,a)/n(r,b)=0$ for every pair of
distinct complex values $a,b$. This answers the question yes in the
entire-function reading, and the paper is refereed.
