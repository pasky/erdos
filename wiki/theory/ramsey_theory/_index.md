# theory/ramsey_theory

[[theory/_index|..]]

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L17_rainbow_odd_cycle_threshold/_index|L17_rainbow_odd_cycle_threshold/]]: For every fixed k at least 3, the fewest colors on some n-vertex graph with
exactly one edge more than the Turán number that make every cycle of
length 2k+1 rainbow is n squared over eight plus little o of n squared,
answering Problem 809.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L260_dilated_prefix_color_omission/_index|L260_dilated_prefix_color_omission/]]: Refuted: no exponential threshold below the growth rate of the Schur
numbers forces every coloring above it to have a dilated prefix of
length delta N omitting a color; the constant-loss recursion this would
give is unavailable in that form.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L261_difference_domain_thinning/_index|L261_difference_domain_thinning/]]: Refuted: no fixed proportion of an arbitrary finite set keeps its
positive differences free of one color under every Schur-free coloring
of those differences; powers of four encode triangle-free colorings,
whose Ramsey ratios are unbounded.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L262_sum_free_arc_colorings/_index|L262_sum_free_arc_colorings/]]: If every class of a coloring of 1 to N by k colors lies in a set of x
with theta x modulo 1 in a sum-free arc, then N+1 is at most 8^k; Schur's
tripling coloring is of this kind.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L263_signed_cube_sufficiency/_index|L263_signed_cube_sufficiency/]]: If the coloring number of the signed cube of dimension r, under vector
addition, is at least c r for every r, then the Schur forcing threshold
f(k) is at most 2^(k/c+1).

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L264_signed_cube_linear_growth/_index|L264_signed_cube_linear_growth/]]: Open: coloring the vectors of {-1,0,1}^r with last nonzero coordinate 1
so that no class contains v, w and v+w needs at least c r colors; by L263
this would make the Schur forcing threshold exponential.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L265_bohr_cell_avoidance_consequences/_index|L265_bohr_cell_avoidance_consequences/]]: If every Schur-free coloring has, on every Bohr cell of radius rho, a
color of the cell's differences avoiding some rank-one neighborhood of
radius rho, then f(k) is at most ceil(1/rho)^k, and the signed-cube
coloring number grows at least linearly.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L266_bohr_cell_uniform_avoidance/_index|L266_bohr_cell_uniform_avoidance/]]: Refuted: no fixed radius and exponential threshold in the number of
colors on a cell's differences force one of those colors to avoid a
rank-one Bohr neighborhood. Sparse powers are Bohr cells and encode
the accepted superexponential triangle Ramsey construction.

[[theory/ramsey_theory/L67_containment_pattern_levels/_index|L67_containment_pattern_levels/]]: A containment-based cell level gives cliques of every finite size for
the displayed height-three pattern, even at distinct root lengths.
On good partitions it instead makes every interior cell have level one.
