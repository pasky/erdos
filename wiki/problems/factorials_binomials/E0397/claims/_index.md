# problems/factorials_binomials/E0397/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0397/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0397/claims/2026_01_11_somani|2026_01_11_somani]]: Neel Somani's family: for every $a\ge2$, the central binomial coefficients
with indices $a$, $2a+2$ and $8a^2+8a+1$ have the same product as those with
indices $a+1$, $2a$ and $8a^2+8a+2$. So the equation has infinitely many
solutions with distinct indices.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0397/claims/2026_01_29_feng|2026_01_29_feng]]: Theorem 4 of the Gemini case study by Tony Feng and coauthors: for every
$k\ge3$, the central binomial coefficients with indices $k$, $2k-2$ and
$8k^2-8k+2$ have the same product as those with indices $k-1$, $2k$ and
$8k^2-8k+1$. This is Somani's family again.
