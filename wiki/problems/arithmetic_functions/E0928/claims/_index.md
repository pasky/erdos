# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0928/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0928/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0928/claims/2021_01_20_wang|2021_01_20_wang]]: Wang proves that, under the Elliott–Halberstam conjecture for friable
integers, the $n\le x$ with largest prime factor $P(n)\le x^s$ and
$P(n+1)\le x^t$ have density $\rho(1/s)\rho(1/t)$. The result is refereed but
conditional, so it settles no standing.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0928/claims/2026_09_24_openai|2026_09_24_openai]]: The OpenAI release claims that the integers $n$ with largest prime factor
$P(n)\le n^\alpha$ and $P(n+1)\le n^\beta$ have natural density
$\rho(1/\alpha)\rho(1/\beta)$, the independence Erdős asked for. Its Lean
proof was built and audited by this corpus, so the claim is accepted.
