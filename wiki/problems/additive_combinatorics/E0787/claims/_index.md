# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/1971_12_01_choi|1971_12_01_choi]]: Choi (Proc. London Math. Soc. 1971) proves $g(n)\ll n^{2/5+o(1)}$, the first
power-saving upper bound for the Erdős–Moser function, and reduces the problem
to integer sets. The paper is refereed and is known here through the papers
that cite it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2005_03_01_ruzsa|2005_03_01_ruzsa]]: The Theorem of Ruzsa (Ramanujan J. 2005, refereed) shows that for every
$c>\sqrt{8\log2}$, some $n$ positive integers have no sum-avoiding subset
larger than a constant times $e^{c\sqrt{\log n}}$. This is the best upper
bound for $g(n)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2018_04_10_sanders|2018_04_10_sanders]]: Theorem 1.2 of Sanders (Canad. J. Math. 2021, refereed) shows that every
finite set $A$ of integers has a subset of size at least $(\log|A|)^{1+c}$,
with $c>0$ absolute, in which no sum of two distinct elements lies in $A$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2025_01_17_beker|2025_01_17_beker]]: Theorem 1.2 of Beker's preprint, accepted by Int. Math. Res. Not., states that
for every $c<1/68$ every large finite set $A$ of integers has a subset of size
at least $(\log|A|)^{1+c}$ with no sum of two distinct elements in $A$. There
was no journal record on 2026-10-07.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/claims/2026_09_25_zhi|2026_09_25_zhi]]: Theorem 1.3 of Zhi's unrefereed preprint, a refinement of Beker's argument,
states that for every $c<1/36$ every large finite set $A$ of integers has a
subset of size at least $(\log|A|)^{1+c}$ with no sum of two distinct
elements in $A$.
