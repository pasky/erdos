# theory/set_systems

[[theory/_index|..]]

[[theory/set_systems/L214_spread_intersecting_families/_index|L214_spread_intersecting_families/]]: For every integer rho >= 2 an explicit intersecting family of n-sets is
rho-spread under its uniform measure, with log2 n about rho (log2 rho)^2,
and has absolutely r-spread subfamilies for every r >= 16. This explicit
substitution construction gives an obstruction to constant-spread matching.

[[theory/set_systems/L215_spread_large_core_sunflowers/_index|L215_spread_large_core_sunflowers/]]: For every real R >= 3 and integer g >= 0 an explicit intersecting family is
R-spread under its uniform measure while every k-sunflower in it, for every
k >= 3, has a core of at least 2^g points; no bounded-core sunflower lemma
follows from spread.

[[theory/set_systems/L268_linear_word_sunflowers/_index|L268_linear_word_sunflowers/]]: A linear word space of dimension at least sn contains an s-dimensional
subspace whose coordinate maps are zero or injective. Consequently an
affine linear word family without a k-sunflower has fewer than k^(2n)
members, uniformly over finite fields and coordinate alphabet dimensions.

***

Each claim states its conventions, hypotheses and conclusion, with its proof
and evidence standing on the card. The two spread claims concern the
sunflower problem through the spread-family method: the first shows that
spread does not force two disjoint members, and the second that it does not
force a sunflower with a bounded core. Their research context is the
sunflower exponential-bound folder.
