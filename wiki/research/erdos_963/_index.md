## Where things stand

**Incomplete.** Neither the requested lower bound nor a finite
counterexample has been proved. The equivalent positive-set target is
$|P|\le2^{d(P)}-1$. It is established through $d=4$ (the real-set
assertion through $n=63$), at intrinsic rank defect at most three,
and under the recorded restricted pair-sum hypothesis.

**Machine-checked bound.** The binary residue-tree argument of
[[research/erdos_963/proof_sketch|the proof sketch]] is proved in this
repository's Lean as
[[theory/number_theory/L22_near_logarithmic_dissociated_subset/_index|L22]]
(tier 2 for the Lean sources and statement as they stood on 2026-09-28, the
default branch at 13:50:48Z, with its fidelity audit graded pass and the
warrant bound to the Lean v4.34.1 tree that the non-author clean gate of
2026-10-13 checked): every nonempty finite real set $A$ contains a dissociated
subset of size at least $\log_2|A|-\log_2(\log_2|A|+1)$, over the seven modules
under `lean/Erdos/Problem963/`, which the claim's
[[theory/number_theory/L22_near_logarithmic_dissociated_subset/_proof|proof page]]
maps. The prose argument keeps its own author-recorded standing, and the exact
$\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor$ target stays open.

**Author-recorded bounds.** The
[[research/sharp_dissociated_subset/proof_sketch|bounded-ratio and dyadic argument]]
gives L213's triple-logarithmic loss, at tier 0, with the exact statement and
source-free proof there. Its recentered-certificate obstruction L270 does not
disprove the catalog target. No new Lean or independent warrant is supplied.

The complementary small-dimension recursion
in [[research/erdos_963/proof_sketch|this folder's proof sketch]] uses
[[research/erdos_963/positive_seven|positive seven-point extraction]],
[[research/erdos_963/affine_fourteen|affine extraction]], and
[[research/erdos_963/congruence_grid|the congruence grid theorem]]. It gives
$|A|\le2U_{d(A)}+1$, with $(U_3,U_4,U_5,U_6)=(6,14,42,104)$
and the recorded recursion thereafter. The six- and seven-element
extraction thresholds are 86 and 210, not the required 64 and 128.
The stronger odd counts remain $F(5)\le28$, $F(6)\le62$, versus
targets 16 and 32.

**Latest proved work.** The new
[[research/erdos_963/collision_trade_method|collision-code approach]]
characterizes ordered positive realizability of a relation space $L$ by
$\tau(L)\cap\mathbb R_{\ge0}^n=\{0\}$, where $\tau(u)_j=\sum_{i\ge j}u_i$. Its
circuit-covering inequality forces every counterexample to have a minimal
circuit of size $R>\theta(d+1)$, where $H_2(\theta)+\theta=1$ and
$\theta\approx0.2271$. The established high-girth result also forces a circuit
of size at most six when $d\ge9$. The target is proved directly when circuits
have pairwise intersections of size at most one, or when every circuit has a
singleton side. These special cases do not improve the universal estimates.

**Exact gap and next step.** Circuit counting treats supports separately;
the realization criterion constrains arbitrary linear combinations.
No argument combines them to exclude $n\ge2^d$. The
[[research/erdos_963/pairwise_circuit_models|pairwise-model note]] shows that
pairs of circuits with free orientation cannot: their only restriction is the
unique completion already used by the counting inequality, and the ternary
projective model satisfies all such data with $(3^d-1)/2$ points and circuits
of every size, including mixed overlaps whose coefficient-two cancellations are
relations modulo three. One common positive orientation already excludes that
model. The note gives an exact two-profile interval test in a fixed order for
every pair of circuit sizes, but no bound combines these constraints with
covering every $(d+1)$-set. The ordered greedy estimate gives the target for
$d\le3$ and $n\le18$ for $d=4$, against the needed $15$; this failure does
not refute the ordered assumptions. Intersecting circuits of sizes four and
seventeen occur in the retained $K_{4,4}$ example, below the counterexample
threshold. Reopening the all-$d$ route needs a coupled covering estimate or a
model meeting the common-order conditions at $n\ge2^d$; another separate
support count does not supply either.

**Other routes remain conditional.** The
[[research/erdos_963/common_subsum|common-subsum lemma]] handles one almost-full
representation family over all affine bases; unrestricted mixed-sign families
remain outside its hypothesis. The
[[research/erdos_963/antipodal_lifts|antipodal]] and
[[research/erdos_963/global_lift_gap|cube-lift]] existence assertions require
global replacement, not greedy projection. The cube two-relation subproblem
still has odd uniform cores 7–759 unresolved; solving those would not itself
prove global existence. The earlier matching and joint-fiber gaps remain as
recorded in the proof sketch.

**Remaining gap.** The bounded unbounded-height SMT attempt supplied
neither a counterexample nor an impossibility certificate. Exact new
obstructions to smallest-pair merging and threshold Euler extraction
are in [[research/erdos_963/continuation_routes|the continuation note]].
The target is unresolved.
