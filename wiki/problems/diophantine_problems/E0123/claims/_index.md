# problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/1996_04_01_erdos_lewin|1996_04_01_erdos_lewin]]: Erdős and Lewin prove in a refereed paper (Math. Comp. 1996) that the products
of powers of 2, 5 and $p$ are $d$-complete for each prime $p$ from 7 to 19, as
are those of 3, 5 and 7. These are the problem's first settled cases.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/2016_02_03_ma_chen|2016_02_03_ma_chen]]: Ma and Chen's refereed theorem (J. Number Theory 2016) shows that the products
of powers of 2, 5 and $c$ are $d$-complete for $c\in\{9,21,23,27,29,31\}$. It
also gives a finite-interval criterion for other $c>6$ coprime to 10.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/2023_03_20_chen_yu|2023_03_20_chen_yu]]: Chen and Yu's refereed theorem (Acta Arith. 2023) shows that the products of
powers of 2, 5 and $r$ for $r\le87$, of 2, 7 and $r$ for $r\le33$, and of 3, 5
and $r$ for $r\le14$ are $d$-complete, with $r$ coprime to the other two
bases.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/2026_07_15_snyder|2026_07_15_snyder]]: Colin Snyder claims, with a Lean proof, that for pairwise coprime $a,b,c>1$
every large integer is a sum of distinct numbers $a^kb^lc^m$, none dividing
another. The erdosproblems.com curator accepted it, and nothing was built in
the corpus.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0123/claims/2026_07_20_principia_math|2026_07_20_principia_math]]: Claims a second proof that the products of powers of three pairwise coprime
bases are $d$-complete, with the summands confined to a short multiplicative
window. The proof is written up and formalized in Lean, and erdosproblems.com
shows no verdict.
