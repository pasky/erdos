# Conditional smooth-successor implication

This subject is the complete implication from the explicit premises
P and E through the signed Type II estimate, prime extraction and
Hypothesis A+ to the coprime squarefree-pair bound for every fixed
positive epsilon and all sufficiently large real x, with strict
1 <= a < b < x. P and E are hypotheses of this examination. The
implication does not establish either antecedent.

The weighted-dilation method is OpenAI's. The successor-prime
application and final counting reduction are Shisheng Li and Zhong
Zhuang's; that reduction follows Pollack and Pomerance after Erdos.
The project exposition and parameter checks are credited to Plasma AI.
read_set.json supplies the source identities and names each permitted
mathematical file. The source excerpts are retained transcription
text, with no PDF-image comparison or original classical-theorem
proofs supplied.

The five mathematical files, this README and read_set.json form the
mathematical read set. Links are citations, not permission to read
additional material. The uniform omission marker is [cut]. No
mathematical program accompanies this subject.
