# problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims/1975_01_01_parsons|1975_01_01_parsons]]: Theorems 1 and 2 of Parsons (Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 1975) give
$R(C_4,K_{1,q^2+1})=q^2+q+2$ and $R(C_4,K_{1,q^2})=q^2+q+1$ for every prime
power $q$, from polarity graphs of projective planes. They are refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims/2015_01_24_wu_sun_zhang_radziszowski|2015_01_24_wu_sun_zhang_radziszowski]]: Theorem 3 of Wu, Sun, Zhang and Radziszowski (Graphs Combin. 2015), a refereed
result, gives $R(C_4,K_{1,q^2-2})=q^2+q-1$ for every prime power $q\ge3$. For
even $q$ it also gives the values at $n=q^2-k-1$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims/2016_12_23_zhang_chen_cheng|2016_12_23_zhang_chen_cheng]]: Theorems 6 and 7 of Zhang, Chen and Cheng (Finite Fields Appl. 2017) give
$R(C_4,K_{1,n})$ at $n=(q-1)^2+t$ for even prime powers $q$ and at
$n=q(q-1)-t$ for odd prime powers $q$. The results are refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims/2017_01_03_zhang_chen_cheng|2017_01_03_zhang_chen_cheng]]: Theorem 4 of Zhang, Chen and Cheng (Discrete Math. 2017), a refereed result,
gives $R(C_4,K_{1,q^2-t})=q^2+q-(t-1)$ for every odd prime power $q$ and every
$t$ with $1\le t\le2\lceil q/4\rceil$ and $t\ne2\lceil q/4\rceil-1$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0552/claims/2024_09_19_boza|2024_09_19_boza]]: Theorem 10 of Boza's preprint determines $R(C_4,K_{1,n})$ for $n=27,\dots,33$,
$37$ and $67$, which with the known values gives every $n\le38$. The result is
from a preprint and is listed as claimed.
