# problems/set_systems/E0719/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0719/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0719/claims/1966_01_01_erdos_goodman_posa|1966_01_01_erdos_goodman_posa]]: Erdős, Goodman and Pósa (1966, refereed) prove that every graph on $n$
vertices is the union of at most $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges and triangles,
no two sharing an edge. This is the case $r=2$ of the Erdős–Sauer question,
for every $n$.

[[problems/set_systems/E0719/claims/2026_09_07_zeraoulia|2026_09_07_zeraoulia]]: Rafik Zeraoulia's September 2026 preprint states that the Erdős–Sauer
decomposition bound holds for every $3$-uniform hypergraph on at most nine
vertices, and gives local packing lemmas. It is AI-assisted, not refereed and
unreviewed.
