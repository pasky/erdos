**Claim.** The note *A Linear Intersection Bound*, an Overleaf document that
Liam Price linked in the discussion thread of
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0836/_index|Problem 836]] on 27 April 2026 as a
solution of the second question claimed by GPT-5.5 Pro, states as its Theorem
that for $r\ge2$ every $r$-uniform intersecting hypergraph $G$ with
$\chi(G)=3$ has two distinct edges $E,F$ with

$$
|E\cap F|\ge\left\lfloor\frac r2\right\rfloor,
$$

and that the same holds for every $r$-uniform intersecting hypergraph that is
not properly $2$-colorable. So some two edges meet in $\gg r$ vertices, and
the second question has the answer yes. The proof passes by compactness to a
finite subhypergraph $H$ that is not $2$-colorable and is minimal under edge
deletion, fixes an edge $A$, and takes a proper red–blue coloring of
$H\setminus\{A\}$ in which $A$ is red and the blue vertices are fewest.
Recoloring a vertex $x\in A$ blue then gives an edge $F_x$ that meets $A$ only
in $x$ and is otherwise blue, and recoloring a blue vertex $b$ red gives an
edge $G_b$ whose only blue vertex is $b$. With $t$ the largest intersection
of two edges of $H$, the intersecting property puts each such $b$ in at least
$r-t$ of the sets $F_x\setminus\{x\}$, and counting
$\sum_{y\in A\setminus\{x\}}|F_x\cap F_y|$ in two ways gives
$(r-1)t\ge(r-1)(r-t-1)$, so $2t\ge r-1$. The note also observes that a star
whose edges meet only at its center refutes the statement when the
parenthetical condition is read as $3$-colorability alone, the literal
reading that the problem page's Notes set aside. The note prints no author
line.

**Submission note.** Posted to the erdosproblems.com forum by Liam Price on 27
April 2026:

> GPT-5.5 Pro claims a solution to the 2nd question here.

**Covers.** The second question of the modified Statement, with chromatic
number exactly $3$: some two edges meet in at least $\lfloor r/2\rfloor$
vertices, a linear bound in place of the $r/\log r$ of Erdős, Lovász and
Shelah. The first question, on the number of vertices, is not addressed;
[[problems/graph_coloring/E0836/claims/1975_01_01_erdos_lovasz|Erdős and Lovász's claim page]]
records its negative answer.

**Depends on.** No page of this wiki; the note proves the compactness lemma
it uses and is otherwise self-contained.

**Standing.** Claimed. The note is an editable Overleaf document with no
fixed version, no author line, and no arXiv or journal record. On 28 April
2026 a commenter in the thread reported that a standard check had found no
issues. The same comment said that the proof seemed very short, that the
second question is mentioned in the literature (the introduction of
Bucić, Glock and Sudakov's paper), and asked for further scrutiny; the thread
records no review. The concluding remarks of
[[../library/graph_coloring/bucic_2020_intersection_spectrum_3_chromatic_intersecting_hypergraphs/_index|Bucić, Glock and Sudakov]]
still treat the $r/\log r$ bound as the best known. The erdosproblems.com
proof-claim tab has no entry, its commentary does not mention the note, and
Bloom's catalog labeled the problem OPEN. This corpus has read the note but has
not reviewed its proof, so no evidence kind is listed.
