# problems/number_theory/E0482/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0482/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0482/claims/1991_06_01_rabinowitz_gilbert|1991_06_01_rabinowitz_gilbert]]: Rabinowitz and Gilbert's 1991 paper gives, for every positive real $w$, a
Graham–Pollak floor recurrence with multipliers $a$ and $2/a$ whose
differences $u_{2n+1}-2u_{2n-1}$ are the binary digits of $w$. It was refereed
and published in Math. Mag.

[[problems/number_theory/E0482/claims/2005_05_24_stoll|2005_05_24_stoll]]: Stoll's theorems of 2005 and 2006 give, for every positive real $w$ and every
base $g\ge2$, infinitely many Graham–Pollak floor recurrences whose
differences $u_{2n+1}-gu_{2n-1}$ are the base-$g$ digits of $w$.
