The archived body is retained as supplied. Its claims are not accepted
solely because they are called proved in that record. The review checked
the promising arguments and compared them with the existing wiki:

- [Layers and obstructions](../layers_and_obstructions.md) retains the
  angular rank improvement, quantitative layer and separation bounds,
  abstract coloring obstruction, and source corrections. A literal
  minimum-distance identification in the cited two-ring construction
  fails; using its actual radial minimum repairs the needed asymptotic
  obstruction to choosing the shortest and second-largest classes.
  The depth lemma and qualitative sparse-layer result repeat existing work;
  the input's exponential sampling proof is weaker than the old wiki bound.
- [Affine deformations](../affine_deformations.md) proves the coordinate
  criterion and improves the input's bound to $\lceil n/4\rceil$ by a
  midpoint-fiber argument. Generic deformation remains invalid.
- [Small orders](../small_orders.md) proves the first assertion through
  ten points using checked source classifications and exact model counts.
  It records why the eleven- through thirteen-point restrictions do not
  complete the next orders. The input's eleven-point example is congruent
  to the existing [motif](../eisenstein_motif.md); its extension obstruction has
  a direct certificate independent of the grid enumeration.
- [Circular configurations](../circular_configurations.md) strengthens the
  input's warning about the two-polygon rich-distance constructions:
  the existing positive-circle theorem already forces linearly many
  rare distances in those families.

These additions do not resolve either unrestricted assertion. The
macroscopic-layer obstruction remains.

## Review of the second independent input

The body of
[second independent input](large_distance_graph_and_convex_extension.md) is
archived unchanged, apart from added metadata. It remains unverified input as a
whole; the checked arguments are recorded in canonical topical notes. Its
references to companion files are not additional available evidence in this
research folder.

- [Layers and obstructions](../layers_and_obstructions.md) incorporates
  the balanced-circle 2-core proof of $m_A(r_k)\le(2k-1)|B_k|$ and the
  sharpened floor bounds. The depth lemma, sparse-layer divergence,
  aspect-ratio criterion, and source corrections repeat established work.
- [Circular configurations](../circular_configurations.md) proves the
  two-nonvertex/cocircular-hull claim with a shorter argument using the
  unique-diameter theorem. It also allows the last point anywhere when
  the other added point is noncentral and in the circular subset's hull.
  [Nearly convex](../nearly_convex.md) explains why the existing
  all-but-one convex theorem is stronger than the input's hull version.
- [Small orders](../small_orders.md) records the rechecked spectra and
  equivalent nine-point models. The input's twelve-point six-distance
  gap is already excluded by the unique-diameter theorem; arbitrary
  eleven-point five-distance sets remain unclassified here.
- [Unrestricted frontier](../unrestricted_frontier.md) distinguishes the
  input's unresolved convex asymptotic branch from the established
  short-edge inverse proof. Its subset-restoration warning and the
  unrestricted interior-support gap remain valid.

The new geometric proofs were checked by the author and are
author-recorded; the finite classifications were checked in the supplied
full sources after their digests. No unrestricted solution results.

## Review of the third independent input

The body of [third independent input](layer_bound_and_circular_majority.md) is
retained unchanged apart from metadata. Its assertions were compared
with the indexed topical notes rather than accepted wholesale. None
of its linked standalone companion files is present in this research folder.

- [Regular cores](../regular_cores.md) incorporates a checked elementary
  small-difference lemma, including the finite-group exterior argument.
  It replaces Kneser's theorem in that note's majority proof and gives
  the explicit bound $R(A)\ge h-n/2-10n^{6/7}$. The shifted-grid
  incidence estimate and strict $t+1$ new-pair budget were reconstructed
  and checked. This is an elementary alternative with an explicit
  constant, not an improvement of the established positive-fraction
  circle scope, its asymptotic bound, or the $q^{7/8}$ threshold.
- [Layers and obstructions](../layers_and_obstructions.md) retains the
  valid disk-core argument and its local exterior-neighbor bound.
  Its $(6k-5)|B_k|$ estimate is weaker than the existing
  $(2k-1)|B_k|$ bound; the existing $O(n)$ aspect-ratio criterion is
  stronger too. The unused Ramsey constant remains unverified, with
  the geometric-versus-vertex-disjoint distinction recorded explicitly.
- [Small orders](../small_orders.md) records exact isometries identifying
  the report's two lattice models with the existing nine-point types.
  Its finite spectra were rechecked, but its undisplayed coordinate
  reference was not certified. The present twelve-point theorem does
  not require classifying eleven-point deletions.
  [Nearly convex](../nearly_convex.md) records that both hull arguments
  are valid but weaker than the current one- and two-exception results.
- [Unrestricted frontier](../unrestricted_frontier.md) distinguishes the
  report's historical convex-asymptotic gap from the established
  short-edge inverse theorem. It also preserves the subset-restoration
  warning and limits the perturbation warning to what it actually
  proves; equality-preserving methods remain available.

The author checked the new elementary proofs and their constants; they
are author-recorded. The source classifications were checked after their
digests, and finite computation was limited to the explicit models.
Both unrestricted assertions remain unresolved.
