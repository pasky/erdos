# problems/factorials_binomials/E0419/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0419/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0419/claims/1996_01_01_erdos_graham_ivic_pomerance|1996_01_01_erdos_graham_ivic_pomerance]]: Erdős, Graham, Ivić and Pomerance, credited by the site, prove that
$\tau(n!)/\tau((n-1)!)=1+P(n)/n+O(n^{-1/2})$, with $P(n)$ the largest prime
factor of $n$. So the limit points of the ratio are exactly $1$ and the
numbers $1+1/m$.
