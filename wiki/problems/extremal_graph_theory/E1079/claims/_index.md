# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/claims/1981_08_01_bollobas_thomason|1981_08_01_bollobas_thomason]]: Bollobás and Thomason (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 1981) prove that a graph with
at least the Turán number of edges either is the Turán graph or has a vertex
of linear degree with a dense neighborhood. The paper is refereed, named by
the site and cited by Bondy.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/claims/1983_02_01_bondy|1983_02_01_bondy]]: Bondy (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 1983) proves that in a graph with more than
the Turán number of edges every vertex of maximum degree has a dense
neighborhood. The refereed result is partial, since graphs with exactly the
Turán number of edges are outside its hypothesis.
