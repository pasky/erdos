# problems/integer_sequences/E0711/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0711/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0711/claims/2026_01_05_van_doorn|2026_01_05_van_doorn]]: Van Doorn's refereed Theorem 1 (Integers 26 (2026), #A7) gives
$\max_mf(n,m)-f(n,n)>0.36\,n\log n/\log\log n$ for all large $n$. This answers
Problem 711's second question yes.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0711/claims/2026_07_11_kominers|2026_07_11_kominers]]: Kominers's note of 11 July 2026, an unreviewed arXiv preprint, claims
$\liminf(F(n)-f(n,n))/(n\log n)\ge1/e$ for $F(n)=\max_mf(n,m)$, a stronger yes
to Problem 711's second question than van Doorn's.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0711/claims/2026_07_29_chen_korsky|2026_07_29_chen_korsky]]: Chen and Korsky's unreviewed preprint (v1 by Chen, 29 July 2026) bounds
$F(n)=\max_mf(n,m)$ between
$n\exp((\frac{\log2}2-o(1))\frac{\log n}{\log\log n})$ and $n^{4/3+o(1)}$.
With Erdős–Pomerance, this gives Problem 711's comparison.
