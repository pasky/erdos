# Graham–Leeb–Rothschild: Ramsey theorem for classes of partitions

***

No copy of this paper is held and no library card exists for it. The
intended paper is Graham, Leeb and Rothschild's Ramsey theorem for a class
of categories (likely *Advances in Mathematics* 8 (1972), 417--433), but
that identification has not been checked against a held copy, and nothing
in the research consumes it.

This paper develops a Ramsey theorem for categories/classes of partitions,
giving a broad categorical formulation of structured monochromatic
subobjects.  It can support an E0774 construction whose finite gadgets are more
naturally partitions or quotients than words.

Its relevance is conditional: first define a functorial encoding of the
relation gadget and show that a monochromatic subobject yields the desired
signed relation.  The theorem does not control the independence ratio, so that
must come from a separate sparse-intersection, partial-Steiner, or rank
argument.
