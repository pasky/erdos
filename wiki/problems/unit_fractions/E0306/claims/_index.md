# problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/_index|..]]

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/1977_08_01_barbeau|1977_08_01_barbeau]]: Barbeau's 1977 list gives 101 distinct products of two distinct primes whose
reciprocals sum to 1, the first representation of 1 with such denominators. It
appeared in a problem-solving journal and covers only the instance $a/b=1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/1978_08_01_johnson|1978_08_01_johnson]]: Johnson's 1978 letter lists 48 distinct products of two distinct primes whose
reciprocals sum to 1. It answers Barbeau's question about the fewest terms
with the record that stood until 2020, and covers only the instance $a/b=1$.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/2020_09_01_watanabe|2020_09_01_watanabe]]: Watanabe's 2020 preprint prints seventeen representations of 1 as a sum of 47
reciprocals of distinct products of two distinct primes, the shortest known.
It covers only the instance $a/b=1$, and one representation has an outside
Lean certificate.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/2026_06_13_li|2026_06_13_li]]: Li's unrefereed preprint of June 2026 proves that every natural number, and
every $a/b$ with $b$ squarefree and $a/b$ above an explicit threshold of at
most $1/5$, is a sum of distinct unit fractions with semiprime denominators.
The claim is partial.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/2026_06_19_tang|2026_06_19_tang]]: Tang's Lean 4 development of June 2026, released on Zenodo, asserts the whole
statement with two Rosser–Schoenfeld bounds taken as axioms. The manuscript
was frozen in August 2026 and published in October 2026.

[[problems/unit_fractions/E0306/claims/2026_09_26_li|2026_09_26_li]]: Li's preprint of September 2026, with a Lean 4 formalization, asserts that
every positive rational with squarefree denominator is a sum of distinct unit
fractions with semiprime denominators. It is erdosproblems.com's one full
proof claim.
