# problems/set_systems/E0447/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0447/claims/1971_01_01_kleitman|1971_01_01_kleitman]]: Kleitman proves that a family of subsets of $[n]$ with no $A\cup B=C$ among
distinct members has at most $(1+o(1))\binom{n}{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ sets,
which answers both of the problem's questions. The site's curator credits the
result.
