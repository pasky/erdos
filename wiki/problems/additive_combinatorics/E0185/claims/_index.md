# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims/1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson|1991_12_01_furstenberg_katznelson]]: Furstenberg and Katznelson's 1991 density Hales–Jewett theorem is refereed and
credited by erdosproblems.com. Its three-letter case shows that a subset of
the ternary $n$-cube with no three collinear points has size $o(3^n)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/claims/2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros|2012_09_22_dodos_kanellopoulos_tyros]]: Dodos, Kanellopoulos and Tyros's refereed 2012 combinatorial proof of the
density Hales–Jewett theorem gives $f_3(n)=o(3^n)$ in its ternary case,
because a combinatorial line is collinear. A Lean formalization exists but was
not built here.
