# problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/1985_06_01_monier|1985_06_01_monier]]: Jean-Marie Monier's published 1985 solution of Monthly Problem 6447 is
credited with $n_k\le k!$ for $k\ge3$ and with a proof of the Erdős–Selfridge
exception. It is refereed in The American Mathematical Monthly.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_07_25_white|2026_07_25_white]]: A repository of 25 July 2026 by Patrick White, with a construction by GPT-5.6
Sol, claims $n_k\le\exp(Ck\log\log k/\log k)$ for all large $k$. It was not
posted to the site's forum and is unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_07_27_cipollini|2026_07_27_cipollini]]: Ricky Cipollini's proof claim, posted to the site's proof-claim tab on 27 July
2026, asserts that
$\log n_k\le\frac{k}{\log k}(\log\log k+\log\log\log k+\log2+o(1))$. The
write-up requires a sign-in, and the claim is unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_08_04_cipollini|2026_08_04_cipollini]]: Ricky Cipollini's proof claim, posted to the site's proof-claim tab on 4
August 2026, asserts that $\log n_k\ge c(\log k)^2$, a superpolynomial lower
bound on $n_k$. The write-up requires a sign-in, and the claim is unreviewed.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E1063/claims/2026_10_01_leap|2026_10_01_leap]]: A Lean 4 proof of 1 October 2026 by the LEAP prover agent shows that
$n_k=O\bigl(\exp\bigl(\frac{k}{\log k}(\log\log k+\log\log\log k+\log2)\bigr)\bigr)$,
which is little-$o$ of Cambie's bound. It is linked by formal-conjectures as a
variant's formal proof.
