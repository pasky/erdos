# problems/analysis/E1126/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1126/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1126/claims/1965_08_01_jurkat|1965_08_01_jurkat]]: Jurkat proves, by a conull-sumset construction, that a real function additive
for almost every pair of reals agrees almost everywhere with a unique
everywhere-additive function. The paper is refereed and credited by the site.

[[problems/analysis/E1126/claims/1966_01_01_debruijn|1966_01_01_debruijn]]: De Bruijn proves, by translation differences, that a real function additive
for almost every pair of reals agrees almost everywhere with an
everywhere-additive function. The paper is refereed and credited by the site,
and others have formalized the result in Lean.
