# problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2020_06_04_schoen|2020_06_04_schoen]]: Schoen's Theorem 1 (Electron. J. Combin. 2021, refereed) gives
$W(3,k)\le\exp(Ck^{1-c})$ for absolute constants $C,c>0$. This is the first
bound of the shape Erdős asked for, and the erdosproblems.com curator credits
it.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2021_02_02_green|2021_02_02_green]]: Green's Theorem 1.1 (Forum of Mathematics, Pi 2022, refereed) gives
$W(3,k)\ge k^{c(\log k/\log\log k)^{1/3}}$, the first superpolynomial lower
bound. It refutes the quadratic conjecture of Ahmed, Kullmann and Snevily.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0721/claims/2021_11_01_hunter|2021_11_01_hunter]]: Hunter's Theorem 1 (Combinatorica 2022, refereed, credited by the
erdosproblems.com curator) gives $W(3,k)\ge k^{c\log k/\log\log k}$, improving
Green's bound. The sources expect $W(3,k)\le k^{O(\log k)}$.
