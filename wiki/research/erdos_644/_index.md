## Where things stand

**Neither assertion of [Problem 644](../../problems/set_systems/E0644/_index.md) has
been resolved.** In the asymptotic range the bounds are

$$
3\lfloor k/4\rfloor+1\le f(k,7)\le\lceil7k/8\rceil\qquad(k\ge40),
$$

so $f(k,7)\ge(3/4-o(1))k$. The bounds are due to
[Fon-der-Flaass, Kostochka, and Woodall](source_notes/fon_der_flaass_et_al_1999_transversals_uniform_hypergraphs_property_7_2.md):
their Theorem 2 gives the upper bound for every $k\ge8$, and their Theorem 1
gives the lower bound only at multiples of four, $f(4q,7)\ge3q+1$ for
$q\ge10$. It reaches every $k\ge40$ because $f(k,7)$ is nondecreasing in $k$:
adding to each set a new point of its own keeps the seven-set hypothesis and
does not lower the transversal number. At $k\equiv2,3\pmod4$ this gives less
than $\lceil3k/4\rceil$, which is not known to be a lower bound for every $k$.
No fixed-positive-excess counterexample or unrestricted convergence theorem
is known.

The [review of the third independent
record](independent/_index.md#review-of-the-third-record) does not change this
status. It verifies a
[six-spectrum certificate](block_profile_families.md#a-checked-six-spectrum-certificate-from-the-independent-input),
extends the
[shifting failure to every uniformity](compression_obstructions.md#the-same-failure-at-every-uniformity),
and corrects a [rejected candidate's transversal number to
$95$](certificates_and_obstructions.md#endpoint-two-profile-candidate-a-finite-correction).
The
[sharp universal cloning criterion](convex_profile_amplification.md#exact-universal-condition-for-complete-clone-classes)
still loses a factor tending to five for $r=7$; none of these gives an
unrestricted scaling mechanism.

The [individual-seed cloning obstruction](convex_profile_amplification.md#each-fixed-seed-and-the-parity-construction)
also rules out complete clone-class amplification of the known parity
families. A seven-edge tuple with maximum vertex incidence four shows that
preservation requires $N<4s/3$ when every vertex is replaced by all
$s$-subsets of an $N$-point class. For $s\ge2$ the resulting
transversal-to-uniformity ratio is at most $31/80<3/4$; $s=N=1$ is
unchanged. This author-recorded obstruction leaves restricted inner families
and other amplification operations open.

### Established results and their scope

- **Arbitrary-seed coupling is proved.** The conceptual
  [separation argument](arbitrary_seed_coupling.md) establishes
  $Q_N\subseteq K_N$ for every finite seed. The
  [Hall formula and integral rounding](constant_composition_families.md)
  therefore give

  $$
  \tau\le3k/4+C_N,\qquad C_N=N+49\cdot2^N(2N(N+1)+1)
  $$

  for every $P_7$ constant-composition family with an $N$-point seed. This
  proves the three-quarter upper coefficient when $N$ is fixed, or
  $N^2 2^N=o(k)$. No reduction from arbitrary families to this class is known
  here. Earlier restricted bounds remain in the
  [block-profile](block_profile_families.md),
  [join-layer](join_layer_families.md), and
  [matroid-base](matroid_base_families.md) notes.

- **A stronger guard projection is proved.**
  [Nonuniform guard fibers](nonuniform_guard_fibres.md) may be projected away
  preserving $P_7$ when each fiber separately has rank at most $s$ and
  transversal number greater than $2s$. The fibers need not have any common
  allowed edges. The missing step is controlling the aggregate cost of
  exceptional fibers. Conversely,
  [guarded near-three-quarter examples](quotient_compression_obstruction.md)
  have transversal number $o(k)$ after **every** quotient onto
  $O(k^{2-\varepsilon})$ points. This defeats vertex identification alone, even
  after exact conjunction-primality, but not prior guard removal.
- **Criticality gives exact trace information, not locality.** The
  [critical-cover trace cube](incidence_minimal_families.md#exact-transversal-numbers-on-the-critical-cover-trace-cube)
  satisfies $\tau(\mathcal H[S])=|S|+1$, and deleting a point of the
  distinguished edge leaves transversal number $|S|$. Tracing onto that edge
  together with its critical cover preserves global transversal number, but a
  checked 4-uniform, simultaneously edge-critical and incidence-minimal $P_8$
  example loses even $P_3$ under this projection. Seven-row mandatory-point
  witnesses still lack the extra test slot needed for an ambient rank estimate.
- **The bounded-witness reduction remains incomplete.**
  [Degree-bounded selections](degree_bounded_selections.md#a-nine-edge-witness-at-the-three-quarter-threshold)
  force a bad subfamily of at most nine edges above $\lceil3k/4\rceil+1$. A
  fixed-excess $P_7$ counterexample therefore has minimum bad-subfamily size
  eight or nine. The [six-row aggregate inequality](six_row_exchanges.md) is
  unproved. One explicit eight-row obstruction now forces its ambient family to
  satisfy $\tau\le37k/49+O(1)$, still above three quarters by $k/196+O(1)$; this
  is not a general upper bound.
- **Two coding restrictions are settled more broadly.**
  [Füredi's fractional matching theorem](fano_classification_extension.md)
  forces the degree-four spectra of every bad seven-tuple on $n<9k/5$ points to
  be exactly the seven Fano caps. This extends the sufficient threefold-XOR
  criterion, not the separate entropy range of the narrow-ground upper bound.
  [Distinct-seven XOR](distinct_seven_xor.md) proves that a family with
  $\tau>(3/4+\varepsilon)k$ and $7k/4\le n<2k$ necessarily has seven distinct
  members whose total XOR has size at most $4n-7k$. It rules out avoiding all
  such low-XOR tuples as a construction strategy, not arbitrary coding
  constructions or arbitrary $P_7$ families.
- **No unrestricted scaling mechanism was obtained.**
  [Profile amplification](block_profile_convergence.md) proves convergence under
  its stated block/support/slack assumptions only.
  [Qualitative Boolean-template results](boolean_reductions.md#why-qualitative-promise-template-classification-is-insufficient),
  [low-influence normalization](boolean_factor_reduction.md), and the new
  projection lemma still supply no uniform rank-sensitive transfer.
  [Incidence moments](incidence_moment_rounding.md) rule out sublinear VC
  dimension for a fixed-excess counterexample;
  [bounded Helly number](helly_and_chromatic_reduction.md#a-shortest-edge-bound-for-bounded-helly-number)
  rules out Helly number at most three. These do not control the remaining
  regime.

### Open questions and provisional directions

The first assertion still requires an unrestricted $3k/4+o(k)$ upper bound or a
family with a fixed positive asymptotic excess satisfying the **full** local
condition. The second still requires proving equality of the normalized liminf
and limsup for every fixed $r$; no restricted-class result above supplies that
conclusion.

**Provisional ideas, not established reductions:** combine the exact
critical-cover trace constraints with the mandatory-point witnesses; remove
low-rank guards with controlled loss before compressing the ground set; or
connect arbitrary families to the proved coupling theorem without assuming
block-permutation closure. The notes identify the specific failures of the
unnormalized versions of these ideas. The arguments written up here are checked
auxiliary results, not a completed solution of Problem 644.

## Source caution

The supplied 1997 transcription's floor roundings for both $r=4$ and $r=5$
conflict with nonvacuous examples: all triples on seven points have $P_4$ and
transversal number five, while all triples on six points have $P_5$ and
transversal number four. [Complete families](complete_families.md) gives the
checks. These bounded discrepancies do not challenge the reported asymptotic
coefficients; no argument here relies on the disputed exact formulas.
