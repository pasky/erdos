# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0897/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0897/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0897/claims/1981_01_01_wirsing|1981_01_01_wirsing]]: Wirsing's 1981 paper, crediting Erdős, gives an additive $f(q)=g(q)\log q$
with slowly growing $g$ whose normalized jumps tend to zero and whose ratios
stay bounded. So both answers are no, and the site has accepted the result.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0897/claims/2025_12_26_archivara|2025_12_26_archivara]]: Archivara's 2025 manuscript, written by its research agent, constructs an
explicit additive function that answers both questions no. The site's thread
reads it as a rediscovery of Wirsing's construction, and it was later
formalized in Lean.
