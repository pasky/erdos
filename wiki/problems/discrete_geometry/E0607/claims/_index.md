# problems/discrete_geometry/E0607/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0607/claims/1983_09_01_szemeredi_trotter|1983_09_01_szemeredi_trotter]]: Szemerédi and Trotter bound the number of distinct sequences of line sizes
realizable by $n$ points in the plane by $\exp(O(\sqrt n))$, which gives the
bound Erdős asked for. The paper is refereed, and erdosproblems.com credits it
as the proof.
