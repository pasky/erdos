# problems/ramsey_theory/E0645/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0645/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0645/claims/1999_08_01_brown_landman|1999_08_01_brown_landman]]: Theorem 7 of Brown and Landman (Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 1999) states that,
for any $f$, every 2-coloring of the positive integers has a monochromatic
three-term progression $a,a+d,a+2d$ with $d\ge f(a)$. This page applies it
with $f(a)=a+1$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0645/claims/2025_10_20_alweiss|2025_10_20_alweiss]]: The erdosproblems.com curator records this case analysis as Ryan Alweiss's
proof that every 2-coloring of the positive integers has a monochromatic
three-term progression $x,x+d,x+2d$ with $d>x$. It is accepted on the basis of
a Lean proof built here.
