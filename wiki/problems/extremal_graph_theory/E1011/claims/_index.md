# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims/1941_01_01_turan|1941_01_01_turan]]: In its triangle case, Turán's refereed paper (Mat. Fiz. Lapok 1941) shows that
a graph on $n$ vertices with more than $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges contains a
triangle. With the complete bipartite graph, this gives
$f_2(n)=\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+1$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims/1962_03_01_erdos|1962_03_01_erdos]]: Lemma 1 of Erdős's refereed 1962 paper in Illinois J. Math., with its
sharpness example, gives $f_3(n)=\lfloor(n-1)^2/4\rfloor+2$ for every $n\ge5$.
Erdős found the lemma jointly with Gallai, and Andrásfai found it
independently.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims/2024_04_11_ren_wang_wang_yang|2024_04_11_ren_wang_wang_yang]]: Ren, Wang, Wang and Yang's preprint of April 2024 (v2 October 2025) proves
that $f_4(n)=\lfloor(n-3)^2/4\rfloor+6$ for every $n\ge90$, attained by
blow-ups of the Grötzsch graph. The preprint has no journal record, so its
status is claimed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims/2026_09_10_kentakitamura|2026_09_10_kentakitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's forum comment of 10 September 2026 announces a Lean 4
development said to determine $f_4(n)$ for every $n$, extending the preprint's
range. It was read as text only, is unreviewed and is not accepted by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/claims/2026_09_19_kentakitamura|2026_09_19_kentakitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's forum comment of 19 September 2026 announces a kernel-checked
Lean 4 proof that $f_5(n)=\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor-3n+15$ for every $n\ge80$. It
was read as text only, is unreviewed and is not accepted by erdosproblems.com.
