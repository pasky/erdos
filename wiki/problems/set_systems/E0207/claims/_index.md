[[problems/set_systems/E0207/claims/2022_01_12_kwan_sah_sawhney_simkin|2022_01_12_kwan_sah_sawhney_simkin]]: Kwan, Sah, Sawhney and Simkin prove Erdős's 1973 conjecture that for every
$g$, every large admissible order admits a Steiner triple system in which no
$j$ triples span at most $j+2$ vertices for $2\le j\le g$. The refereed paper
appeared in Ann. of Math.
