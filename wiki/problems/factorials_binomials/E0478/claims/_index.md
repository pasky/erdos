# problems/factorials_binomials/E0478/claims

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0478/_index|..]]

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0478/claims/2022_04_03_grebennikov_sagdeev_semchankau_vasilevskii|2022_04_03_grebennikov_sagdeev_semchankau_vasilevskii]]: In a refereed paper (Rev. Mat. Iberoam. 2024), Grebennikov, Sagdeev,
Semchankau and Vasilevskii prove that the factorials modulo $p$ take at least
$(\sqrt2+o(1))\sqrt p$ distinct values.

[[problems/factorials_binomials/E0478/claims/2026_07_23_hu|2026_07_23_hu]]: Xiyu Hu's manuscript, with a partial Lean formalization, proves that at least
about $p^{8/15}$ residue classes modulo a prime $p$ are factorials, beyond the
square-root bound. It leaves positive density and the asymptotic the problem
asks for open.
