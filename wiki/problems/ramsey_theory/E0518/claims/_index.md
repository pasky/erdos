# problems/ramsey_theory/E0518/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0518/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0518/claims/2024_09_05_pokrovskiy_versteegen_williams|2024_09_05_pokrovskiy_versteegen_williams]]: Theorem 1.3 of Pokrovskiy, Versteegen and Williams (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B
176 (2026)) shows that for all $n>20^{40}$, every two-edge-colored complete
graph on $n$ vertices is covered by $\sqrt n$ monochromatic paths of one
color. It is refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0518/claims/2026_07_24_chen_chen|2026_07_24_chen_chen]]: Theorem 1.5 of Chen and Chen's unrefereed 2026 arXiv preprint claims that for
every $n$, every red-blue edge-colored complete graph on $n$ vertices is
covered by at most $\sqrt n$ monochromatic paths of one color. The site has
not adopted it.
