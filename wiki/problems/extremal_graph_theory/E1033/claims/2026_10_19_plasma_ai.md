**Claim.** For every integer $n\ge10^{13}$ there is a finite simple graph
on exactly $n$ vertices with more than $n^2/4$ edges and every triangle
degree sum strictly below $183n/125$. Thus $h(n)<183n/125$ throughout
that range, contradicting the proposed asymptotic lower bound with
constant $2(\sqrt3-1)>183/125$.

**Covers.** The definite question `conjectured_bound` on
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1033/_index|Problem 1033]]. The optimum
of $h(n)/n$ and a complete asymptotic evaluation are not supplied.
