# problems/polynomials/E1041/claims

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/_index|..]]

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_03_17_ani|2026_03_17_ani]]: A forum user, working with GPT-5.4, claimed a proof that two roots are always
joined by a path of length less than $2$, cut from a tree of gradient flow
lines. Tao found the tree structure false, and the author agreed.

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_04_22_kasko37|2026_04_22_kasko37]]: A forum user posted a write-up by GPT-5.4 Thinking announcing a proof that two
roots are always joined by a path of length less than $2$. Two readers found
the same serious gap in a subharmonicity step, which was judged not easily
repairable.

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_06_23_pendyala|2026_06_23_pendyala]]: Pendyala claims that for a monic quartic with all zeros in the open unit disk,
two zeros are joined inside the lemniscate by a polygonal path of length less
than $2$. It is an arXiv preprint whose Lean formalization was not shared, and
it has had no review.

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_09_07_ani|2026_09_07_ani]]: A forum user, working with GPT 6, claims a family of monic degree-seven
polynomials with all roots in the open unit disk for which no path of length
less than $2$ inside the lemniscate joins two roots. Two readers checked the
claim.

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_09_11_cook|2026_09_11_cook]]: Cook, with a write-up by Astra and other AI tools, claims that for a monic
trinomial with all zeros in the open unit disk, every root is joined to the
origin inside the lemniscate, so two roots are joined by a short path.

[[problems/polynomials/E1041/claims/2026_09_18_borisov|2026_09_18_borisov]]: Borisov claims that for a monic cubic with all roots in the open unit disk,
two roots are joined inside the lemniscate by a two-segment path of length
less than $2$ through a critical point. It is a Zenodo preprint, listed as a
partial claim on the erdosproblems.com tab.
