# problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/1995_05_15_alon_erdos|1995_05_15_alon_erdos]]: Alon and Erdős's 1996 Theorem 1.1 places $f(n)$ between
$n^{1/3}/(\log n)^{4/3}$ and $n^{1/3}(\log\log n)^{1/3}/(\log n)^{1/3}$ up to
constants, so $f(n)=n^{1/3+o(1)}$. It is refereed, and erdosproblems.com
records it.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/2006_11_16_vu|2006_11_16_vu]]: Vu's 2007 paper improves the Alon–Erdős lower bound to
$f(n)\gg n^{1/3}/\log n$. It is refereed and recorded by erdosproblems.com,
but the paper is not held here.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0360/claims/2021_04_30_conlon_fox_pham|2021_04_30_conlon_fox_pham]]: Theorem 1.5 of the 2021 preprint of Conlon, Fox and Pham determines $f(n)$ up
to a constant factor as
$n^{1/3}(n/\phi(n))/((\log n)^{1/3}(\log\log n)^{2/3})$. erdosproblems.com
accepts it as the solution, and it is to appear in J. Eur. Math. Soc.
