# problems/diophantine_problems/E0841/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0841/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0841/claims/2001_01_15_granville_selfridge|2001_01_15_granville_selfridge]]: In a refereed paper, Granville and Selfridge prove that $t_n$ equals the
largest prime factor $P(n)$ whenever $P(n)>\sqrt{2n}+1$, and that
$t_n\le3\sqrt{n/2}+1$ otherwise.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E0841/claims/2022_11_22_bui_pratt_zaharescu|2022_11_22_bui_pratt_zaharescu]]: Bui, Pratt and Zaharescu show that $t_n\le n^c$ holds for the same proportion
of integers as the bound $P(n)\le n^c$ on the largest prime factor, and they
give upper and lower bounds on $t_n$. The paper is refereed, and the site's
curator labels the problem solved.
