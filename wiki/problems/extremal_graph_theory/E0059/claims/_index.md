# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims/1986_12_01_erdos_frankl_rodl|1986_12_01_erdos_frankl_rodl]]: Theorem 1.6 of Erdős, Frankl and Rödl (Graphs Combin. 1986) shows that the
number of $G$-free graphs on $n$ vertices is $2^{(1+o(1))\mathrm{ex}(n;G)}$
whenever $G$ has chromatic number at least 3. The claim is accepted on the
refereed publication.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/claims/2013_09_11_morris_saxton|2013_09_11_morris_saxton]]: Proposition 1.4 of Morris and Saxton (Adv. Math. 2016) gives a constant $c>0$
such that for infinitely many $n$ there are at least
$2^{(1+c)\mathrm{ex}(n;C_6)}$ graphs on $n$ vertices with no $C_6$. So the
question fails for $G=C_6$, and the claim is accepted.
