# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/claims/2003_11_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov|2003_11_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov]]: Corollary 2.3 of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2003)
proves $\mathrm{ex}(n,H)=O(n^{2-1/r})$ when every vertex on one side of the
bipartite graph $H$ has degree at most $r$. This refereed result is the case
of the problem that holds.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/claims/2026_08_01_openai|2026_08_01_openai]]: Theorem 1.2 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI's 2026 report gives a connected bipartite
2-degenerate graph $H$ with $\mathrm{ex}(n,H)\ge c\,n^{3/2+\varepsilon}$, so
the conjectured $O(n^{2-1/r})$ fails at $r=2$. The site's curator credits it,
and it is not refereed.
