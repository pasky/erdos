# problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/claims/1991_01_01_erdos_freud|1991_01_01_erdos_freud]]: Proposition 1 of Erdős and Freud (J. Number Theory 1991) gives a set of about
$\sqrt N$ integers up to $N$ with $(3/8-o(1))N$ distinct sums up to $N$, the
lower bound $f(N)\ge(3/8-o(1))N$. The paper is refereed and the site credits
it.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/claims/2026_05_15_liu|2026_05_15_liu]]: Yu Leon Liu's note of 2026-05-15, posted to the site's discussion thread,
claims $\liminf f(N)/N\ge(16\sqrt2-17)/12$ with a proof found using OpenAI's
GPT-5.5. It is not on the tab and the site has not examined it, so its status
is claimed.
