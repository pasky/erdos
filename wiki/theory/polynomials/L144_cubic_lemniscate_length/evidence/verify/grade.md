## Exact restatement and clause coverage

$\{z\in\mathbb C:|p(z)|=1\}$. For every real
$0<a\le 2^{-1/3}$, with the nonnegative real square root
$c=\sqrt{1-4a^6}$, the statement is

$$
\ell(z^3+3a^2z-c)<L_0,
\qquad
L_0=\ell(z^3-1)=\frac{2\pi\Gamma(1/3)}{\Gamma(2/3)^2}.
$$

For every monic complex polynomial of degree exactly three,
$\ell(p)\le L_0$, and equality holds exactly for

$$
p(z)=(z-z_0)^3-w,

The report preserves these quantifiers, strictness, monicity, complex domain,
and geometric length convention. It includes the endpoint $c=0$, excludes

### Strongest attack: the actual deformation

The report correctly refuses the invalid inference that existence of one
maximizer with critical values on the unit circle classifies all maximizers.
Its first derivation examines the stronger local fact used in the frozen
proof. For a maximizing monic cubic $p$, take an off-circle critical value $v$
and a disc separated from the other critical value and the unit circle.
The perturbation $\phi_\lambda(z)=z+\lambda\Phi(z)$ gives the coefficient

$$
\mu_{\phi_\lambda\circ p}(z)
=\frac{\overline{p'(z)}}{p'(z)}
 \frac{\lambda\Phi_{\bar z}(p(z))}{1+\lambda\Phi_z(p(z))}
$$

almost everywhere. Properness gives compact support, and small complex
parameters keep its norm below one. Through the explicitly identified
normalized Beltrami theorem, the straightening has
$\psi_\lambda(z)=z+o(1)$ at infinity. Consequently
$\phi_\lambda\circ p\circ\psi_\lambda^{-1}$ is an entire **monic cubic**.
The normalization is essential: arbitrary scaling would change the optimization
problem. Local topological degree preserves the two simple critical points and
moves only $v$.

The support stays away from the original lemniscate. The report checks both
$E(p_\lambda)=\psi_\lambda(E(p))$ and conformality on a common neighborhood.
Analytic parameter dependence and local uniform boundedness justify integrating
$|\psi_\lambda'|$ as a subharmonic function of the parameter. Global maximality
then forces this length to be constant near zero. These are the essential
deductions from the source construction, not an appeal to Lemma 5's statement
alone.

The continuation objection is also answered in the retained report. Along an
arc avoiding the other value $w$, depressed coefficients satisfy
$b=(t+w)/2$ and $|u|^3=|w-t|/4$. Their boundedness and source coefficient
continuity make the realizable-maximizer set relatively closed as well as
locally open. They also supply the terminal limit. A second move, if needed,
ends with two distinct unit critical values, contradicting the proved strict
family bound. This is sufficient without assuming a global continuous choice
of cubic roots or completion of infinitely many local moves. The grader finds
this attack answered in the settled report; no new proof repair is supplied
by the grade.
