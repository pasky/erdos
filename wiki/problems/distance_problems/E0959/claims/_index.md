# problems/distance_problems/E0959/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0959/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0959/claims/2025_05_07_clemen_dumitrescu_liu|2025_05_07_clemen_dumitrescu_liu]]: Clemen, Dumitrescu and Liu prove that for all large $n$, some $n$-point planar
set has its most frequent distance occurring $\Omega(n\log n)$ more times than
the next one (Corollary 1.10, from Theorem 1.9). The paper is refereed and
appeared in Acta Math. Hungar.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0959/claims/2026_07_15_snyder|2026_07_15_snyder]]: Colin Snyder claims that for all large $n$, some $n$-point planar set has its
most frequent distance occurring at least $n^{1+c/\log\log n}$ more times than
the next one, with $c=1/50000$. A Lean 4 proof is supplied but not built here.

[[problems/distance_problems/E0959/claims/2026_07_21_xeff|2026_07_21_xeff]]: Theofil Xeff claims that for all large $n$, some $n$-point planar set has its
most frequent distance occurring at least $n^{1+c}$ more times than the next
one, for an absolute $c>0$. The proof tunes the construction behind the
unit-distance disproof.
