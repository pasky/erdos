# problems/divisors/E0884/claims

[[problems/divisors/E0884/_index|..]]

[[problems/divisors/E0884/claims/2025_09_10_tao|2025_09_10_tao]]: Tao's note argues that, assuming the qualitative Hardy–Littlewood prime tuples
conjecture, the ratio of the all-pairs divisor-gap reciprocal sum to one plus
the consecutive-gap sum is unbounded. The construction uses products of primes
in a tight admissible tuple.

[[problems/divisors/E0884/claims/2026_03_27_larsen|2026_03_27_larsen]]: Larsen proves that the ratio of the all-pairs divisor-gap reciprocal sum to
one plus the consecutive-gap sum is unbounded, by a multiscale version of
Tao's prime construction. The result is credited by the erdosproblems.com
curator and was formalized in Lean by others.
