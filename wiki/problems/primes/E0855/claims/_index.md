# problems/primes/E0855/claims

[[problems/primes/E0855/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/1973_01_01_hensley_richards|1973_01_01_hensley_richards]]: Hensley and Richards (Acta Arith. 25) prove that the prime $k$-tuples
conjecture implies $\pi(x+y)>\pi(x)+\pi(y)$ for infinitely many $y$ at every
large $x$. The paper is refereed, and the result is conditional, so it settles
no standing.

[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2002_01_01_dusart|2002_01_01_dusart]]: Theorem 1 of Dusart's refereed 2002 paper proves
$\pi(x+y)\le\pi(x)+\pi(y)$ for all real $2\le x\le y\le\frac75x\log x\log\log
x$. It settles the pairs of comparable size and leaves the very unbalanced
pairs open.

[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2019_09_27_axler|2019_09_27_axler]]: Theorem 1.3 of Axler's arXiv preprint claims $\pi(m+n)\le\pi(m)+\pi(n)$ for
all integers $m\ge n\ge2$ with $n\ge c_0m/\log^2m$, $c_0=0.7088\ldots$. The
preprint has no journal record.

[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2022_06_03_alkan|2022_06_03_alkan]]: Theorem 1 of Alkan's refereed 2022 paper, applied to the set of all primes,
gives $\pi(x+y)<\pi(x)+\pi(y)$ for $x,y\ge x_0$ and $Kx/\log x\le y\le x$,
with effective constants. For large pairs Dusart's range already contains
this region.

[[problems/primes/E0855/claims/2025_03_04_chahal_elma_fellini_vatwani_vo|2025_03_04_chahal_elma_fellini_vatwani_vo]]: Theorem 1.1 of the paper, refereed in Acta Arithmetica, proves
$\pi(x+y)\le\pi(x)+\pi(y)$ for large $x$ and
$3CR(2x)\log^2x/\log\log x\le y\le x$, where $CR(x)$ bounds the error in the
prime number theorem. Smaller $y$ stay open.
