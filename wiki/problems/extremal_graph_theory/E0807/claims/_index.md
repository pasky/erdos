# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/claims/2014_02_26_alon|2014_02_26_alon]]: Alon's Theorem 1.1 (J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 2015, refereed) shows that for
most $n$, with high probability, the random graph $G=G(n,1/2)$ has bipartition
number $\tau(G)\le n-\alpha(G)-1$, where $\alpha(G)$ is its independence
number.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/claims/2014_09_22_alon_bohman_huang|2014_09_22_alon_bohman_huang]]: Alon, Bohman and Huang (J. Graph Theory 2017, refereed) show that for every
$n$, with high probability, the random graph $G=G(n,1/2)$ has bipartition
number $\tau(G)\le n-(1+c)\alpha(G)$, where $\alpha(G)$ is its independence
number.
