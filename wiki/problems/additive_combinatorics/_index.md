# Sumsets and Arithmetic Progressions

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0001/_index|E0001/]]: Asks whether a set of
$n$ integers in $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ whose subset sums are all distinct forces
$N\gg2^n$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0003/_index|E0003/]]: Asks whether every set
$A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ with $\sum_{n\in A}1/n=\infty$ must contain arbitrarily
long arithmetic progressions.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0036/_index|E0036/]]: Asks for the largest
constant $c$ such that, for large $N$, every partition of $\{1,\ldots,2N\}$ into
sets $A$ and $B$ of size $N$ has some $x$ with at least $cN$ solutions to
$a-b=x$ with $a\in A$ and $b\in B$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0037/_index|E0037/]]: Asks whether a lacunary
set $A$ can be an essential component, meaning that $d_s(A+B)>d_s(B)$ for every
$B$ with $0<d_s(B)<1$, where $d_s$ is Schnirelmann density.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0052/_index|E0052/]]: Asks whether every
finite set $A$ of integers has $\max(\lvert A+A\rvert,\lvert
AA\rvert)\gg_\epsilon\lvert A\rvert^{2-\epsilon}$ for every $\epsilon>0$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0053/_index|E0053/]]: Asks whether, for every
$k$, every large enough finite set $A$ of integers has at least $\lvert
A\rvert^k$ integers that are sums or products of distinct elements of $A$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0109/_index|E0109/]]: Asks whether every
$A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ of positive upper density contains a sumset $B+C$ with
$B$ and $C$ both infinite.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0138/_index|E0138/]]: Asks for better bounds
on the van der Waerden number $W(k)$, the least $N$ such that every two-coloring
of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a monochromatic $k$-term arithmetic progression, for
example a proof that $W(k)^{1/k}\to\infty$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0139/_index|E0139/]]: Asks whether
$r_k(N)=o(N)$, where $r_k(N)$ is the size of the largest subset of
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no non-trivial $k$-term arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0140/_index|E0140/]]: Asks whether $r_3(N)\ll
N/(\log N)^C$ for every $C>0$, where $r_3(N)$ is the size of the largest subset
of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no non-trivial $3$-term arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0141/_index|E0141/]]: Asks whether, for every
$k\ge3$, there are $k$ consecutive primes in arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0142/_index|E0142/]]: Asks for an asymptotic
formula for $r_k(N)$, the size of the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with no
non-trivial $k$-term arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0160/_index|E0160/]]: Asks for estimates of
$h(N)$, the least number of colors for $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that every
four-term arithmetic progression gets at least three distinct colors.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0168/_index|E0168/]]: Asks for
$\lim_{N\to\infty}F(N)/N$, where $F(N)$ is the size of the largest subset of
$\{1,\ldots,N\}$ containing no set $\{n,2n,3n\}$, and whether this limit is
irrational.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0169/_index|E0169/]]: Asks for estimates of
$f(k)$, the supremum of $\sum_{n\in A}1/n$ over sets $A$ of positive integers
with no $k$-term arithmetic progression, and whether $f(k)/\log W(k)\to\infty$,
where $W(k)$ is the van der Waerden number.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0170/_index|E0170/]]: Asks for the value of
$\lim_{N\to\infty}F(N)/N^{1/2}$, where $F(N)$ is the smallest size of a set
$A\subseteq\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$ with $\{0,1,\ldots,N\}\subseteq A-A$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0171/_index|E0171/]]: Asks whether, for every
$\epsilon>0$ and integer $t\ge1$, every subset of $[t]^N$ of size at least
$\epsilon t^N$ contains a combinatorial line once $N$ is large enough.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0179/_index|E0179/]]: Asks for upper bounds
on $F_k(N,\ell)$, the least number of $k$-term arithmetic progressions that
forces a set of $N$ integers to contain an $\ell$-term one, for $k<\ell$. Also
asks whether $F_3(N,4)=o(N^2)$ and $\log F_3(N,\ell)/\log N\to2$ for every
$\ell>3$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0185/_index|E0185/]]: Asks whether
$f_3(n)=o(3^n)$, where $f_3(n)$ is the largest size of a subset of $\{0,1,2\}^n$
with no three points on a line.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0186/_index|E0186/]]: Asks for the order of
growth of $F(N)$, the largest size of a subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ in which no
element is the average of two or more other elements.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0190/_index|E0190/]]: Asks whether
$H(k)^{1/k}/k\to\infty$, where $H(k)$ is the least $N$ such that every coloring
of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a monochromatic or a rainbow $k$-term arithmetic
progression; proved by Bae and by Fox and Hunter in 2026 preprints.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0192/_index|E0192/]]: Asks in which
dimensions $d$ an infinite walk in $\mathbb{R}^d$ with positive unit-vector
steps must contain a three-term arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0194/_index|E0194/]]: Asks whether, for $k\ge
3$, every ordering of the real numbers contains an increasing or decreasing
$k$-term arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0195/_index|E0195/]]: Asks for the largest
$k$ such that every permutation of the integers contains a monotone $k$-term
arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0196/_index|E0196/]]: Asks whether every
permutation of the positive integers contains a monotone four-term arithmetic
progression. A Lean-checked construction, certified by the bounty site
Conjectures.io in September 2026, gives a permutation with none.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0197/_index|E0197/]]: Asks whether the
positive integers can be split into two sets, each of which can be permuted to
avoid monotone three-term arithmetic progressions.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0198/_index|E0198/]]: Asks whether the
complement of a set of integers with all pairwise sums distinct must contain an
infinite arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0199/_index|E0199/]]: Asks whether the
complement of a set of reals with no three-term arithmetic progression must
contain an infinite arithmetic progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0200/_index|E0200/]]: Asks whether the
longest arithmetic progression of primes in $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has length $o(\log
N)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0201/_index|E0201/]]: Asks how large a subset
free of $k$-term arithmetic progressions can be guaranteed in any set of $N$
integers, and how that size compares with the case of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0219/_index|E0219/]]: Asks whether the primes
contain arithmetic progressions of every finite length.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0245/_index|E0245/]]: Asks whether every
infinite set $A$ of naturals with $|A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|=o(N)$ satisfies
$\limsup_{N\to\infty}|(A+A)\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|/|A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|\ge 3$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0271/_index|E0271/]]: Asks for an explicit
description and the growth rate of the greedy sequence that starts with $0$ and
$n$ and avoids three-term arithmetic progressions.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0272/_index|E0272/]]: Asks for the largest
number of subsets of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ whose pairwise intersections are all
non-empty arithmetic progressions. The exact value is open, while Szabó's
linear-error question, whether it is $N^2/2+O(N)$, has a Lean proof that
Conjectures.io accepted.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0328/_index|E0328/]]: Asks whether a set in
which each $n$ is a sum of two distinct elements at most $C$ times splits into
boundedly many parts in which each $n$ is such a sum fewer than $C$ times.
Nešetřil and Rödl answer no, and the site's count of ordered pairs makes $C=2$
trivial.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0331/_index|E0331/]]: Asks whether two sets
of integers whose counting functions are both $\gg N^{1/2}$ must share
infinitely many equal nonzero differences.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0335/_index|E0335/]]: Asks for a
characterization of the pairs of sets $A,B$ of integers with positive density
such that $d(A+B)=d(A)+d(B)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0350/_index|E0350/]]: Asks whether a finite
set of integers with all subset sums distinct must have reciprocals summing to
less than $2$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0475/_index|E0475/]]: Asks whether every
finite set of nonzero residues modulo a prime can be ordered with all partial
sums distinct (Graham's rearrangement conjecture); four range results prove it
for all large primes with no explicit threshold.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0476/_index|E0476/]]: Asks whether, for
$A\subseteq\mathbb{F}_p$, the set of sums $a+b$ with $a\neq b$ in $A$ has size
at least $\min(2|A|-3,p)$ (the Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture); proved in 1994.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0494/_index|E0494/]]: Asks whether, for
$k>2$, the multiset of sums of $k$ distinct elements of a finite set
$A\subset\mathbb{C}$, together with $|A|$, determines $A$ when $|A|$ is large
enough in terms of $k$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0656/_index|E0656/]]: Asks whether every set
$A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ of positive upper density contains, after some shift, all
sums of two distinct members of an infinite subset of $A$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0658/_index|E0658/]]: Asks whether every
subset of the $N\times N$ grid with at least $\delta N^2$ points contains the
four vertices of a square once $N$ is large in terms of $\delta$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0741/_index|E0741/]]: Asks whether every
$A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ with $A+A$ of positive upper density splits into two
parts whose sumsets both have positive upper density, and whether some basis of
order $2$ has no split in which both self-sumsets have bounded gaps.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0749/_index|E0749/]]: Asks whether, for each
$\epsilon>0$, some $A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ has $A+A$ of lower density at least
$1-\epsilon$ while every integer has $O_\epsilon(1)$ representations as a sum of
two elements of $A$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0763/_index|E0763/]]: Asks whether, for some
set $A$ of naturals and some $c>0$, the number of representations of $n$ as a
sum of two elements of $A$, summed over $n\le N$, equals $cN+O(1)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0764/_index|E0764/]]: Asks whether, for some
set $A$ of naturals and some $c>0$, the number of representations of $n$ as a
sum of three elements of $A$, summed over $n\le N$, equals $cN+O(1)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0781/_index|E0781/]]: Asks for an estimate of
the least $n$ such that every two-coloring of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ has a
monochromatic $k$-term descending wave, and whether it equals $k^2-k+1$ for all
$k$; it does not.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0785/_index|E0785/]]: Asks whether infinite
sets $A,B$ of naturals with $A+B$ containing all large integers and
$A(x)B(x)\sim x$ must have $A(x)B(x)-x\to\infty$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0787/_index|E0787/]]: Asks how large a subset
of any $n$ real numbers can be chosen with no two distinct elements summing to
one of the $n$ numbers (the Erdős–Moser function $g(n)$). Known bounds are
$(\log n)^{1+c}\ll g(n)\ll\exp(O(\sqrt{\log n}))$ for some $c>0$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0788/_index|E0788/]]: Asks for the least
value, over $B\subset(2n,4n)$, of $|B|$ plus the size of a largest
$C\subset(n,2n)$ whose sums of distinct pairs avoid $B$. Choi's interval
function lies between $n^{1/2}$ and $n^{3/5+o(1)}$, and an unreviewed 2026 claim
asserts the conjectured $n^{1/2+o(1)}$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0789/_index|E0789/]]: Estimates $h(n)$, the
size of the largest subset guaranteed inside any $n$ integers in which equal
sums of elements can occur only between equally many terms.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0790/_index|E0790/]]: Estimates $l(n)$, the
size of the largest subset guaranteed inside any $n$ integers in which no
element equals the sum of two or more other distinct elements. Known bounds are
$(n\log n/\log\log n)^{1/2}\ll l(n)\ll n/\log n$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0791/_index|E0791/]]: Estimates $g(n)$, the
fewest elements of $\{0,\ldots,n\}$ whose pairwise sums cover $\{0,\ldots,n\}$
(the smallest finite additive $2$-basis); open. Its square lies between $2.181n$
and $3.458n$, and the guess $g(n)\sim2\sqrt n$ is refuted (Hämmerer and
Hofmeister, Mrose).

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0792/_index|E0792/]]: Estimates $f(n)$, the
size of the largest sum-free subset (no solution to $a+b=c$) guaranteed inside
any set of $n$ integers. The main term $n/3$ is settled, and the second-order
term lies between $c\log\log n$ and $o(n)$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0806/_index|E0806/]]: Asks whether every set
of at most $\sqrt n$ integers in $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ lies in $B+B$ for some $B$ of
size $o(\sqrt n)$. Alon, Bukh and Sudakov proved it with a basis of order $\sqrt
n\log\log n/\log n$, which is the sharp order.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0808/_index|E0808/]]: Asks whether, for any
graph with many edges on a set of $n$ integers, the sums or the products along
its edges must number nearly as many as the edges.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0817/_index|E0817/]]: Estimates $g_k(n)$, the
least $N$ for which some $n$-element subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a set of
subset sums with no $k$-term progression, and asks whether $g_3(n)\gg3^n$. It is
open, and a 2026 preprint claims a negative answer to the $k=3$ question.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0818/_index|E0818/]]: Asks whether a finite
set $A$ of integers with $|A+A|\ll|A|$ must satisfy $|AA|\gg|A|^2/(\log|A|)^C$
for some constant $C>0$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0819/_index|E0819/]]: Estimates $f(N)$, the
largest size of $(A+A)\cap[1,N]$ for $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with $\lfloor
N^{1/2}\rfloor$ elements; open. Erdős and Freud (1991) bounded $f(N)/N$
asymptotically by $3/8$ and $1/2$, and an unreviewed 2026 note claims the lower
constant $0.469$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0847/_index|E0847/]]: Asks whether an
infinite set of naturals must be a finite union of sets with no three-term
arithmetic progression if, for a fixed $\epsilon>0$, any $n$ of its members
include at least $\epsilon n$ forming no such progression.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0865/_index|E0865/]]: Asks whether every
subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ of size at least $\frac58N+C$, for some constant $C$,
contains three distinct members whose pairwise sums also lie in the set. It was
proved in a 2026 preprint, developed with GPT-5.5 Pro, which the site accepted.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0866/_index|E0866/]]: Estimates, for $k\ge3$,
how far above $N$ the size of a subset of $\{1,\ldots,2N\}$ must be to force $k$
integers whose pairwise sums all lie in the set. It is open; the threshold is
$1$ for $k=3$, $3$ for $k=4$, and bounded for $k=5$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0867/_index|E0867/]]: Asks whether a subset
of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ in which no sum of consecutive members lies in the set has
size at most $N/2+O(1)$; false, by Freud's 1993 construction of density $19/36$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0874/_index|E0874/]]: Estimates $k(N)$, the
size of the largest subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ whose sets of sums of $r$
distinct members are disjoint for distinct $r$, and asks whether
$k(N)\sim2N^{1/2}$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0875/_index|E0875/]]: Asks how slowly an
infinite set of naturals can grow while its sets of sums of $r$ distinct members
stay disjoint for different $r$, and for which $c$ its gaps can be at most
$n^c$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0876/_index|E0876/]]: Asks how small the gaps
of an infinite sum-free set of naturals can be, and whether the $n$th gap can
stay below $n$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0877/_index|E0877/]]: Estimates the number
$f_m(n)$ of maximal sum-free subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and asks whether
$f_m(n)=o(2^{n/2})$. The answer is yes, and $f_m(n)=(C_i+o(1))2^{n/4}$ with a
constant $C_i$ that depends on $n$ modulo $4$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0895/_index|E0895/]]: Asks whether, for all
large $n$, every triangle-free graph on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ has three pairwise
nonadjacent vertices of the form $a$, $b$ and $a+b$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0899/_index|E0899/]]: Asks whether every
infinite set $A$ of naturals with density zero has
$|(A-A)\cap[1,N]|/|A\cap[1,N]|$ unbounded as $N\to\infty$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E0984/_index|E0984/]]: Asks whether
$\mathbb{N}$ can be $2$-colored so that every monochromatic $k$-term arithmetic
progression starting at $a$ has $k\ll_\epsilon a^\epsilon$ for all $\epsilon>0$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1097/_index|E1097/]]: Asks how many distinct
values can occur as the common difference of a three-term arithmetic progression
inside a set of $n$ integers.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1112/_index|E1112/]]: Asks whether, for gap
bounds $d_1<d_2$ and $k\ge3$, every sufficiently lacunary sequence misses the
$k$-fold sumset of some sequence with gaps between $d_1$ and $d_2$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1179/_index|E1179/]]: Estimates the least
size $g_\epsilon(N)$ of a random subset of an abelian group of order $N$ whose
subset sums hit every element nearly equally often, and asks whether
$g_\epsilon(N)=(1+o_\epsilon(1))\log_2N$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1185/_index|E1185/]]: Asks whether every
dense subset of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ has a $k$-term arithmetic progression whose
common difference lies in $B-B$, for every large enough set $B$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1186/_index|E1186/]]: Estimates $\delta_k$,
where every $2$-coloring of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ has at least $(\delta_k+o(1))n^2$
monochromatic $k$-term arithmetic progressions.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/_index|E1187/]]: Asks whether every
finite coloring of the integers has, for each $k\ge3$, a monochromatic $k$-term
progression of primes, and one whose common difference is prime.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1193/_index|E1193/]]: Asks whether, for
$A\subset\mathbb{N}$ and a positive non-decreasing $g$, the set of $n$ with
exactly $g(n)$ representations as a sum of two elements of $A$ always has lower
density $0$ and upper density below some $c<1$.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1213/_index|E1213/]]: Asks whether, for every
starting value $a$ and gap bound $K$, any long enough integer sequence starting
at $a$ with gaps at most $K$ has two intervals with equal sums. The answer is
yes, by Hegyvári's 1986 Theorem 3, with an explicit bound.
