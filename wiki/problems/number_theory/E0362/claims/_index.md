# problems/number_theory/E0362/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0362/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0362/claims/1965_01_01_sarkozy_szemeredi|1965_01_01_sarkozy_szemeredi]]: The Satz of Sárközy and Szemerédi: for $n$ distinct positive reals and large
$n$, no value is a subset sum in more than
$(1+\varepsilon)\frac8{\sqrt\pi}\cdot\frac{2^n}{n^{3/2}}$ ways. This is the
affirmative answer to the first question of Problem 362.

[[problems/number_theory/E0362/claims/1977_09_01_halasz|1977_09_01_halasz]]: Halász's Theorem 2: if $n$ 1-separated vectors $a_k$ in $d$-dimensional space
are such that for every unit vector $e$ at least $\delta n$ of them have
$|(a_k,e)|\ge1$, at most $c\,2^nn^{-1-d/2}$ signed sums lie in a unit ball.
The case $d=2$ answers the second question.
