[[problems/graph_coloring/E0736/claims/2002_12_04_komjath_shelah|2002_12_04_komjath_shelah]]: Komjáth and Shelah (J. Graph Theory 2005) prove it consistent with ZFC that
some graph of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ has no graph of chromatic number
above $\aleph_2$ whose finite subgraphs all occur in it. So a yes answer is
unprovable.
