[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0584/claims/2007_06_13_fox_sudakov|2007_06_13_fox_sudakov]]: Fox and Sudakov prove the second clause at density $n^{-\beta}$ for

count toward the problem's standing. Przemek Chojecki's note, credited to

fails for every $4/5<\beta<1$.

of it. The preprint is unrefereed.
