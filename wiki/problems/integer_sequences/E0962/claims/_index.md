# problems/integer_sequences/E0962/claims

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0962/_index|..]]

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0962/claims/1976_01_01_erdos|1976_01_01_erdos]]: Display (6) of Erdős's 1976 paper in Publ. Math. Debrecen, proved by a count
of smooth numbers, gives the refereed lower bound
$\log k(n)\ge(1/\sqrt2-o(1))\sqrt{\log n\log\log n}$. Both questions of the
problem stay open.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0962/claims/1976_01_01_erdos_upper|1976_01_01_erdos_upper]]: In 1976 Erdős stated, without proof, that he can show
$n_k>k^2\exp((\log k)^c)$ for some $c>0$, which gives
$k(n)\le n^{1/2}\exp(-(\log n)^{c'})$. It is recorded as claimed, since no
proof is printed or referenced.

[[problems/integer_sequences/E0962/claims/2025_12_28_tang|2025_12_28_tang]]: A two-page note by Quanyu Tang, posted to the site's thread on 28 December
2025, proves $\log k(n)\ge(1/\sqrt2-o(1))\sqrt{\log n\log\log n}$ by a
pigeonhole count of smooth integers. It is not refereed.
