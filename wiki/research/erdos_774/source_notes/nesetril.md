# On Pisier Type Theorems

[Library card and held copy](../../../../library/integer_sequences/nesetril_2024_pisier_type_theorems/_index.md).

***

Jaroslav Nešetřil, Vojtěch Rödl, and Marcelo Sales, "On Pisier type
theorems," *Combinatorica* **44** (2024), 1211--1232.

## What the research consumes

The card carries the digest: Theorems 1.1--1.3, the partial-Steiner
construction of Section 3, the carry-free integer encoding of Theorem 4.1,
and Conjecture 6.1. The research uses three things.

- **Theorem 1.1 as the closest negative result and as a scope marker.**
  For each fixed $h$ there is a locally proportionally $h$-free set that
  is not a finite union of $h$-free sets, with extraction constant
  $k(h)^{-k(h)}$ depending on $h$. Fixed-order $h$-freeness is not
  dissociation, and the [bounded-support lemma](../lemmas/bounded_support.md)
  shows that bounded-support, bounded-entry representations force a
  uniform rational-independent partition, so the fixed-width mechanism
  cannot give E0774.
- **The carry-free encoding of Theorem 4.1 and the separated blocks in
  the proof of Theorem 1.1**, which the research reproves in its own form
  as the [integer realization](../lemmas/rational_realization.md) and the
  [finite-block theorem](../lemmas/finite_blocks.md).
- **Theorem 3.2, the ordered partial-Steiner edge-Ramsey theorem, and
  Theorem 5.1, the weighted shift hypergraphs**, as inputs whose scope was
  checked: the Ramsey host does not preserve cover degrees
  ([cover extraction, Section 8](../routes/incidence_cover_extraction.md)),
  and the full shift supports of the high-fractional regime admit a
  six-coloring ([shift checks](../routes/shift_simplicial_gain_checks.md)).

Reading depth is claims checked for these statements in the held copy.
