# problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims/1973_01_01_erdos|1973_01_01_erdos]]: Erdős observed in 1973 that coloring $n$ by the fractional part of $n\alpha$,
for a quadratic irrational $\alpha$, bounds the length of every monochromatic
progression of difference $d$ by $cd$ for a constant $c$. So the best $f$ is
at most linear in $d$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0187/claims/1980_11_01_beck|1980_11_01_beck]]: Beck's 1980 theorem gives, for every $\varepsilon>0$, a two-coloring of the
integers in which no monochromatic progression of difference $d$ has more than
$(1+\varepsilon)\log_2d$ terms once $d$ is large. So the best $f$ is at most
$(1+o(1))\log_2d$.
