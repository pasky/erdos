# problems/set_theory/E1172/claims

[[problems/set_theory/E1172/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_theory/E1172/claims/1956_09_01_erdos_rado|1956_09_01_erdos_rado]]: Erdős and Rado (Bull. Amer. Math. Soc., 1956) prove
$(2^\kappa)^+\to(\kappa^+)^2_\kappa$. Under GCH at $\kappa=\aleph_1$ this
gives the first relation of Problem 1172 as printed,
$\omega_3\to(\omega_2,\omega_1+2)^2$.
