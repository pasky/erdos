# problems/distance_problems/E0754/claims

[[problems/distance_problems/E0754/_index|..]]

[[problems/distance_problems/E0754/claims/2011_08_24_swanepoel|2011_08_24_swanepoel]]: Swanepoel proves that $n$ points in $\mathbb R^4$, each with a chosen
distance, have at most $n^2/2+O(n)$ pairs at the chosen distances. So if every
point of a set has $f(n)$ equidistant points, then $f(n)\le n/2+O(1)$.
