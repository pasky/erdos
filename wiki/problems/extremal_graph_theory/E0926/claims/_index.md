# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/claims/1991_03_01_furedi|1991_03_01_furedi]]: Theorem 1.4 of Füredi (Combinatorica 1991) bounds the extremal number of the
problem's graph $H_k$ by a multiple of $n^{3/2}$ for every fixed $k$,
answering Problem 926 in the affirmative. It is refereed and accepted by
erdosproblems.com.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/claims/2003_11_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov|2003_11_01_alon_krivelevich_sudakov]]: Theorem 6.1 of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov (Combin. Probab. Comput. 2003)
bounds the extremal number of the three-layer Boolean-cube graph $H_k$ on $2n$
vertices by $4kn^{3/2}$. This refereed result is a second proof that the
answer to Problem 926 is yes.
