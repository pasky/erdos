# problems/analysis/E1118/claims

[[problems/analysis/E1118/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E1118/claims/1977_01_01_camera|1977_01_01_camera]]: Camera's 1977 London thesis is credited with establishing Hayman's conjectured
bound, $\int^\infty r\,dr/\log\log M(r)<\infty$, and its sharpness, for entire
functions bounded outside a set of finite area.

[[problems/analysis/E1118/claims/1977_12_01_hansen|1977_12_01_hansen]]: Hayman and Lingham's survey credits Hansen's 1977 Canadian Journal of
Mathematics paper with an independent proof of Hayman's conjectured bound for
entire functions bounded outside a set of finite area. The paper is refereed.

[[problems/analysis/E1118/claims/1979_01_01_goldberg|1979_01_01_goldberg]]: Gol'dberg's 1979 paper proves Hayman's conjectured bound
$\int^\infty r\,dr/\log\log M(r)<\infty$, shows that it is best possible, and
answers the second question no. The paper is refereed.
