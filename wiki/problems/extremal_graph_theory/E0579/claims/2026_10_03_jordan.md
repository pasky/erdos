**Claim.** The statement of
[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0579/_index|Problem 579]] is false. For every
$c>0$ and every threshold $N$ there is a $K_{2,2,2}$-free graph on some
$n\ge N$ vertices with at least $(3/2048)n^2$ edges and independence number
below $cn$. So no constant $c(\delta)$ exists for any $\delta\le3/2048$, and in
the sources' language $\mathrm{RT}(n;K_{2,2,2};o(n))$ is not $o(n^2)$: the
question of Balogh and Lenz whether $\theta(K_{2,2,2})=0$, and Problem C of
Liu, Reiher, Sharifzadeh and Staden, are answered in the negative. Together
with Theorem 1 of Erdős, Hajnal, Sós and Szemerédi (1983), which gives the
statement for $\delta>1/8$, the Ramsey--Turán density $\theta(K_{2,2,2})$ lies
in $[3/2048,1/8]$.

**The result.** The claim is a Lean 4 file submitted to the bounty site
Conjectures.io against its task for this problem (task type disprove). The
formal target is the formal-conjectures statement `Erdos579.erdos_579`, quoted
under Formalization on the problem page, with its open answer fixed to true;
the problem page's Formulation paragraph reads that statement as the problem's
Statement clause for clause. The file proves the exact negation of the target
from a lemma whose statement restates the universal assertion, by an explicit
finite construction: Boolean-cube stages, masked compatibility graphs and
random perfect-matching realizations assemble, for every $c>0$ and every $N$,
an octahedron-free graph on $n\ge N$ vertices at the fixed unordered edge
density $3/2048$ with independence number below $cn$. The record credits the
proof to the solver Jordan; the file's copyright headers credit one author
writing with OpenAI Codex, and its preamble says that selected portions are
modified from the TCSlib and FABL Lean libraries. The file (810,011 bytes,
18,336 lines, its dependencies bundled as source) has a target, a
pinned-statement definition and final theorems consistent with the site's
statement, and contains no `sorry`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `unsafe` or
`set_option`, as the problem page records.

**Acceptance.** Formalized. This corpus's verification rebuilt the record's
solution file on the repository's pinned toolchain, `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1`
with Mathlib at the revision `lean/lake-manifest.json` pins, after two lemma
renames that Mathlib made since the site's toolchain; the target's statement is
unchanged. The kernel replayed, from an empty environment, every declaration the
target `Bounty.target` depends on, Lean core, Mathlib and formal-conjectures
included, and accepted them all. The target's axioms are exactly `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Its type, and every definition its
statement reaches, are identical to the formal-conjectures statement of the
site's task, at the catalog commit the task pins, which the site builds from a
public catalog commit and an audit patch and the replay reproduced exactly. In
that statement `octahedron` is $K_{2,2,2}$, `Free` means containing no copy as a
subgraph, edges are counted unordered and `indepNum` is the independence number,
so its negation is the disproof of the problem's Statement. Acceptance rests on
this replay and this check.
