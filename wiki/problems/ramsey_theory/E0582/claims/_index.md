# problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/_index|..]]

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1970_01_01_folkman|1970_01_01_folkman]]: Folkman's Theorem 1 (SIAM J. Appl. Math. 18, 1970) gives a graph of clique
number $\max(k_1,k_2)$ in which every two-coloring of the edges has a red
$K_{k_1}$ or a blue $K_{k_2}$. With $k_1=k_2=3$ it is the $K_4$-free graph
asked for.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1986_12_01_frankl_rodl|1986_12_01_frankl_rodl]]: Frankl and Rödl (Graphs Combin. 2, 1986) give the first concrete bound,
showing that some $K_4$-free graph on fewer than $7.02\cdot10^{11}$ vertices
forces a monochromatic triangle in every two-coloring of its edges. The result
is refereed.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/1988_11_01_spencer|1988_11_01_spencer]]: Spencer (J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 49, 1988, corrected by an erratum in vol.
50) proves by a probabilistic argument that some $K_4$-free graph on at most
$3\cdot10^9$ vertices forces a monochromatic triangle in every two-coloring.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2008_01_01_dudek_rodl|2008_01_01_dudek_rodl]]: In a refereed paper (Experiment. Math. 17, 2008), Dudek and Rödl use a MAX-CUT
criterion to show that the circulant graph $G(941,5)$ is $K_4$-free and has a
monochromatic triangle in every 2-coloring of its edges, so $f(2,3,4)\le941$.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2008_01_22_lu|2008_01_22_lu]]: Lu's Theorem 1 (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 21, 2008, refereed) gives
$f(2,3,4)\le9697$ through the explicit circulant graph $L(9697,4)$, which is
$K_4$-free and has a monochromatic triangle in every 2-coloring of its edges.

[[problems/ramsey_theory/E0582/claims/2012_07_16_lange_radziszowski_xu|2012_07_16_lange_radziszowski_xu]]: Theorem 3 of Lange, Radziszowski and Xu (arXiv 2012; J. Combin. Math. Combin.
Comput. 88, 2014, refereed) gives $F_e(3,3;4)\le786$. The witness is a
$K_4$-free graph whose arrowing is certified by a semidefinite MAX-CUT bound.
