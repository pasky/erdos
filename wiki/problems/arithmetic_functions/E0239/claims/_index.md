# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0239/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0239/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0239/claims/1967_09_01_wirsing|1967_09_01_wirsing]]: Wirsing proves that every multiplicative function with values $\pm1$ has a
mean value, which answers the question yes. The result appeared in a refereed
paper in Acta Mathematica Academiae Scientiarum Hungaricae, September 1967.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0239/claims/1968_09_01_halasz|1968_09_01_halasz]]: Halász determines the asymptotic behavior of the partial sums of every
multiplicative function of modulus at most one, which gives the mean value for
real-valued functions. The result appeared in a refereed paper in Acta Math.
Acad. Sci. Hungar., 1968.
