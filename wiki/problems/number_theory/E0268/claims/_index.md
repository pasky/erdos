# problems/number_theory/E0268/claims

[[problems/number_theory/E0268/_index|..]]

[[problems/number_theory/E0268/claims/2024_05_13_kovac|2024_05_13_kovac]]: Kovač's Theorem 1: the triples of the sums of $1/n$, $1/(n+1)$ and $1/(n+2)$
over infinite sets of positive integers with convergent reciprocal sum form a
set with nonempty interior, containing a ball of radius $10^{-24}$. This
answers Problem 268 yes.

[[problems/number_theory/E0268/claims/2024_11_27_kovac_tao|2024_11_27_kovac_tao]]: Corollary 2.10 of Kovač and Tao: for every $d$, the $d$-tuples of the sums of
$1/(n+j)$, $j<d$, over infinite sets of positive integers with convergent
reciprocal sum form a set with nonempty interior. The case $d=3$ is
Problem 268.
