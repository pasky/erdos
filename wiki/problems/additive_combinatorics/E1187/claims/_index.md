# problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/claims

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/claims/2004_04_08_green_tao|2004_04_08_green_tao]]: Theorem 1.2 of Green and Tao (Ann. of Math. 2008, refereed) shows that in any
finite coloring some color class contains $k$ primes in arithmetic
progression. This answers yes to the first question only.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/claims/2026_04_08_bloom|2026_04_08_bloom]]: The erdosproblems.com curator's own counterexample to the second question
colors the integers by residue modulo $4$, which leaves no monochromatic pair
with prime difference. No publication or independent review exists, so the
claim is pending.

[[problems/additive_combinatorics/E1187/claims/2026_05_12_kitamura|2026_05_12_kitamura]]: Kenta Kitamura's Lean 4 development of 12 May 2026, prepared with Codex and
GPT-5.5 xhigh, proves that coloring the natural numbers modulo $4$ leaves no
monochromatic progression with prime difference. The claim is pending and
covers the second question only.
