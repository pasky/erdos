# On a theorem of Hildebrand

[[integer_sequences/_index|..]]

***

Carsten Dietzel, *On a theorem of Hildebrand*, Moscow Journal of
Combinatorics and Number Theory **8** (2019), 189–191,
[DOI 10.2140/moscow.2019.8.189](https://doi.org/10.2140/moscow.2019.8.189).
The edition read is [arXiv:1810.01518v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.01518v1),
2 October 2018, three pages; the journal edition has not been compared.

Theorem 4 (p. 2) concerns any multiplicative subgroup
$A\le\mathbb Q_{>0}$ of finite index. It supplies positive integers
$b_1,b_2,\ldots$ such that, for every nonempty finite set $F$ of indices,

$$
s=\sum_{i\in F}b_i\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s,s+1\in A.
$$

Thus the integer pair starts contain an IP-set. The proof uses Hindman's
finite-unions theorem and a sequence with divisibility between ordered
block sums. Dividing by the first selected block makes that block equal
to $1$. Finite sums use distinct blocks; this construction does not
supply a second copy of the normalized first block to obtain $s+2$.

**Read status: claims checked.** The full TeX was read, and Theorem 4,
its proof and Comment 1 were compared with the page images on pp. 2–3.
The proof was read for structure; the external Hindman theorem was not
checked. Definition 1 prints a decreasing sequence, whereas Lemma 1
constructs an increasing one. The source's printed wording is retained.

**Bears on.** The pair theorem strengthens the known pair case of
[[../wiki/problems/integer_sequences/E0436/_index|Problem 436]]. Comment 1
explicitly leaves the odd-index triple question open beyond index $3$.
