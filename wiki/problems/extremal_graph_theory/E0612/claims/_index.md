# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/1989_08_01_erdos_pach_pollack_tuza|1989_08_01_erdos_pach_pollack_tuza]]: Theorem 2 of Erdős, Pach, Pollack and Tuza (JCTB 1989) bounds the diameter of
a connected triangle-free graph by $2n/\delta+O(1)$. This is the instance
$r=1$ of part (ii) in the erdosproblems.com wording, and it is refereed, so it
is accepted as a partial claim.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2020_09_05_czabarka_singgih_szekely|2020_09_05_czabarka_singgih_szekely]]: Czabarka, Singgih and Székely construct connected $K_{2r}$-free graphs whose
diameter exceeds the conjectured bound for every $r\ge2$ and every large
admissible minimum degree. This refutes part (i), and the result is refereed.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2025_02_12_cambie_jooken|2025_02_12_cambie_jooken]]: Cambie and Jooken exhibit $3$-colorable graphs of minimum degree $16$ whose
diameter exceeds the bound of part (i) at $r=2$, inside the degree window that
the first counterexamples left open. The source is a preprint backed by a
computer search.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2026_09_03_chen_chen|2026_09_03_chen_chen]]: Chen and Chen announce connected $K_{2r+1}$-free graphs whose diameter exceeds
the bound of part (ii) for every $r\ge4$, which would refute part (ii) and
with it the problem. The source is an unrefereed preprint (arXiv v1).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/claims/2026_09_08_kitamura|2026_09_08_kitamura]]: Kitamura announces AI-assisted Lean proofs that part (ii) fails at $r=3$, by a
$K_7$-free family that beats the bound by an unbounded margin, and holds at
$r=2$. Kitamura also announces Lean proofs that the amended conjecture holds
for $k=3,4$ and fails for $k=5,6$.
