# problems/primes/E0049/claims

[[problems/primes/E0049/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0049/claims/2023_09_05_tao|2023_09_05_tao]]: Tao proves that a set of integers up to $x$ on which Euler's totient is
nondecreasing has at most $(1+O((\log\log x)^5/\log x))\pi(x)$ elements. So
strict examples have at most $(1+o(1))\pi(N)$ elements, hence $o(N)$, and the
paper is refereed.
