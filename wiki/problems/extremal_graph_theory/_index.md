# Extremal and Structural Graph Theory

[[problems/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0022/_index|E0022/]]: Asks whether, for each
$\epsilon>0$ and large $n$, some $K_4$-free graph on $n$ vertices with at least
$n^2/8$ edges has independence number at most $\epsilon n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0023/_index|E0023/]]: Asks whether every
triangle-free graph on $5n$ vertices can be made bipartite by deleting at most
$n^2$ edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0024/_index|E0024/]]: Asks whether every
triangle-free graph on $5n$ vertices contains at most $n^5$ copies of $C_5$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0059/_index|E0059/]]: Asks whether the number
of graphs on $n$ vertices containing no copy of a fixed graph $G$ is at most
$2^{(1+o(1))\mathrm{ex}(n;G)}$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0060/_index|E0060/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with more than $\mathrm{ex}(n;C_4)$ edges contains $\gg n^{1/2}$
copies of $C_4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0061/_index|E0061/]]: Asks whether, for each
graph $H$, there is $c=c(H)>0$ such that every graph on $n$ vertices with no
induced copy of $H$ has a clique or an independent set of size at least $n^c$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0062/_index|E0062/]]: Asks whether any two
graphs of chromatic number $\aleph_1$ must share a common subgraph of chromatic
number $4$, or even $\aleph_0$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0064/_index|E0064/]]: Asks whether every
finite graph with minimum degree at least $3$ contains a cycle of length $2^k$
for some $k\ge2$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0065/_index|E0065/]]: Asks whether the
reciprocals of the distinct cycle lengths of a graph with $n$ vertices and $kn$
edges sum to $\gg\log k$, and whether a complete bipartite graph minimizes that
sum.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0071/_index|E0071/]]: Asks whether, for every
infinite arithmetic progression $P$ containing even numbers, there is a constant
$c(P)$ such that every graph of average degree at least $c(P)$ has a cycle whose
length lies in $P$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0072/_index|E0072/]]: Asks whether there are a
set $A\subset\mathbb{N}$ of density $0$ and a constant $c>0$ such that every
large graph of average degree at least $c$ has a cycle whose length lies in $A$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0073/_index|E0073/]]: Asks whether a graph in
which every subgraph on $n$ vertices has an independent set of size at least
$(n-k)/2$ becomes bipartite after deleting $O_k(1)$ vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0081/_index|E0081/]]: Asks whether the edges
of every chordal graph on $n$ vertices can be partitioned into $n^2/6+O(n)$
cliques.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0082/_index|E0082/]]: Asks whether $F(n)/\log
n\to\infty$, where $F(n)$ is the size of the largest regular induced subgraph
guaranteed in every graph on $n$ vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0084/_index|E0084/]]: Asks whether the number
$f(n)$ of cycle sets of graphs on $n$ vertices is $o(2^n)$, and whether
$f(n)/2^{n/2}\to\infty$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0086/_index|E0086/]]: Asks whether every
subgraph of the $n$-dimensional hypercube with at least $(1/2+o(1))n2^{n-1}$
edges, slightly more than half of all its edges, must contain a $C_4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0113/_index|E0113/]]: Asks whether a bipartite
graph $G$ has Turán number $\mathrm{ex}(n;G)=O(n^{3/2})$ exactly when it is
$2$-degenerate, that is, has no induced subgraph of minimum degree at least $3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0127/_index|E0127/]]: Asks whether, for graphs
with $m$ edges, the guaranteed excess $f(m)$ of a largest bipartite subgraph
over $m/2+(\sqrt{8m+1}-1)/8$ edges is unbounded along some sequence of $m$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0128/_index|E0128/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices must contain a triangle if every induced subgraph on at least
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ vertices has more than $n^2/50$ edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0133/_index|E0133/]]: Asks for the order of
growth of the largest $f(n)$ such that every triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices
with diameter $2$ has a vertex of degree at least $f(n)$, and whether
$f(n)/\sqrt{n}\to\infty$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0134/_index|E0134/]]: Asks whether a
triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices with maximum degree below $n^{1/2-\epsilon}$
can be made into a triangle-free graph of diameter $2$ by adding at most $\delta
n^2$ edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0136/_index|E0136/]]: Asks for the least
number $f(n)$ of colors for the edges of $K_n$ such that every $K_4$ contains at
least $5$ colors.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0146/_index|E0146/]]: Asks whether every
bipartite $r$-degenerate graph $H$ has $\mathrm{ex}(n;H)\ll n^{2-1/r}$;
disproved at $r=2$ by Theorem 1.2 of Chapter 10 of OpenAI's 2026 report, which
the site's curator credits.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0147/_index|E0147/]]: Asks whether every
bipartite graph $H$ of minimum degree $r$ has $\mathrm{ex}(n;H)\gg
n^{2-\frac{1}{r-1}+\epsilon}$ for some $\epsilon=\epsilon(H)>0$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0149/_index|E0149/]]: Asks whether the strong
chromatic index of every graph, the least number of induced matchings that
partition its edges, is at most $\frac54\Delta^2$, where $\Delta$ is the maximum
degree. It is open, and the best refereed bound is $1.772\Delta^2$ for large
$\Delta$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0150/_index|E0150/]]: Asks whether
$c(n)^{1/n}\to\alpha$ for some $\alpha<2$, where $c(n)$ is the maximum number of
minimal cuts (minimal disconnecting vertex sets) of a graph on $n$ vertices. It
is proved, and refereed papers place $\alpha$ between $1.4457$ and the golden
ratio.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0151/_index|E0151/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices has a clique transversal of at most $n-H(n)$ vertices
(Erdős–Gallai), where $H(n)$ is the independence bound for triangle-free graphs.
It is open; the best bounds are $n-\sqrt{2n}+\sqrt2$ explicitly and
$n-c\sqrt{n\log n}$ asymptotically.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0167/_index|E0167/]]: Asks whether a graph
with at most $k$ edge-disjoint triangles can be made triangle-free by deleting
at most $2k$ edges (Tuza's conjecture). It is open, the site's label is
falsifiable, and Haxell's $66/23$ is the best refereed constant.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0180/_index|E0180/]]: Asks whether every
finite family $\mathcal{F}$ of forbidden graphs has a member $G$ whose extremal
number is comparable to the family's,
$\mathrm{ex}(n;G)\ll_{\mathcal{F}}\mathrm{ex}(n;\mathcal{F})$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0182/_index|E0182/]]: Asks, for each $k\ge3$,
for the maximum number of edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no $k$-regular
subgraph, and whether it is $\ll n^{1+o(1)}$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0184/_index|E0184/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices decomposes into $O(n)$ edge-disjoint cycles and single edges
(the Erdős–Gallai cycle decomposition conjecture). The answer is yes, by the
OpenAI release's 2026 theorem.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0426/_index|E0426/]]: Asks whether some graph
on $n$ vertices has $\gg 2^{\binom{n}{2}}/n!$ distinct unique subgraphs, that
is, subgraphs that occur in exactly one way.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0500/_index|E0500/]]: Asks for
$\mathrm{ex}_3(n,K_4^3)$, the largest number of triples on $n$ vertices with no
four vertices carrying all four of their triples (Turán's tetrahedron problem).
The density lies between Turán's $5/9$ and a flag-algebra bound of $0.5615$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0533/_index|E0533/]]: Asks whether, for fixed
$\delta>0$ and large $n$, every $K_5$-free graph on $n$ vertices with at least
$\delta n^2$ edges has a set of $\gg_\delta n$ vertices spanning no triangle.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0548/_index|E0548/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n\ge k+1$ vertices with at least $\frac{k-1}{2}n+1$ edges contains every
tree on $k+1$ vertices. The Erdős–Sós tree edge bound is proved, and the page
records the precise relation between its sharp threshold and the site's
statement.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0571/_index|E0571/]]: Asks for a bipartite
graph $G$ with $\mathrm{ex}(n;G)\asymp n^{\alpha}$ for each rational
$\alpha\in[1,2)$. The 2026 proof is accepted on a Lean development built here,
and the page records the qualifications on its exposition.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0572/_index|E0572/]]: Asks whether
$\mathrm{ex}(n;C_{2k})\gg n^{1+1/k}$ for every $k\ge3$, that is, whether some
$C_{2k}$-free graph on $n$ vertices has that many edges. It is known for $k=3$
and $k=5$ and open for every other $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0573/_index|E0573/]]: Asks whether
$\mathrm{ex}(n;\{C_3,C_4\})$, the most edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no
triangle and no four-cycle, is asymptotic to $(n/2)^{3/2}$. The ratio is known
only to lie between $1$ and $\sqrt2$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0574/_index|E0574/]]: Asks whether, for every
$k\ge2$, the most edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no cycle of length
$2k-1$ or $2k$ is $(1+o(1))(n/2)^{1+1/k}$; disproved at $k=3$ and $k=5$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0575/_index|E0575/]]: Asks whether every
finite family $\mathcal{F}$ with a bipartite member has a bipartite member $G$
with $\mathrm{ex}(n;G)\ll_{\mathcal{F}}\mathrm{ex}(n;\mathcal{F})$. It is false
as written for two forests, and false for families of bipartite graphs with
cycles by OpenAI's 2026 report.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0576/_index|E0576/]]: Asks for the behaviour
of $\mathrm{ex}(n;Q_k)$, the most edges of a graph on $n$ vertices with no
$k$-dimensional hypercube $Q_k$. For the cube the order lies between $n^{3/2}$
and $n^{8/5}$, which refutes Erdős's first guess.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0577/_index|E0577/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $4k$ vertices with minimum degree at least $2k$ contains $k$ vertex-disjoint
$4$-cycles (the Erdős–Faudree conjecture). Wang proved it in 2010 as Theorem B
of a refereed paper.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0578/_index|E0578/]]: Asks whether a random
graph on $2^d$ vertices, with each edge included with probability $1/2$, almost
surely contains the $d$-dimensional hypercube $Q_d$. Riordan proved it for every
fixed edge probability above $1/4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0579/_index|E0579/]]: Asks whether, for fixed
$\delta>0$ and large $n$, every $K_{2,2,2}$-free graph on $n$ vertices with at
least $\delta n^2$ edges has an independent set of size $\gg_\delta n$. It is
false at $\delta=3/2048$ by a Lean construction that Conjectures.io certified.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0580/_index|E0580/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices in which at least $n/2$ vertices have degree at least $n/2$
contains every tree on at most $n/2$ vertices. Zhao proved it for large $n$, and
finitely many orders remain unchecked.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0581/_index|E0581/]]: Asks for $f(m)$, the
largest number of edges of a bipartite subgraph that every triangle-free graph
with $m$ edges must contain. Alon determined its order,
$f(m)=m/2+\Theta(m^{4/5})$, with the exponent sharp; the exact value is open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0583/_index|E0583/]]: Asks whether every
connected graph on $n$ vertices can be partitioned into at most $\lceil
n/2\rceil$ edge-disjoint paths (Gallai's path decomposition conjecture). It is
proved for several classes and open in general.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0584/_index|E0584/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with $\delta n^2$ edges, where $\delta$ may shrink as a power of
$n$, contains dense subgraphs in which any two edges lie together on a short
cycle.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0585/_index|E0585/]]: Asks for the maximum
number of edges in a graph on $n$ vertices with no two edge-disjoint cycles on
the same vertex set. Open; the maximum is known to be $\Omega(n\log\log n)$ and
$O(n(\log n)^t)$ for some constant $t$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0600/_index|E0600/]]: Let $e(n,r)$ be the
least number of edges that forces an edge in at least $r$ triangles in an
$n$-vertex graph where every edge lies in a triangle. Asks whether
$e(n,r+1)-e(n,r)\to\infty$ and $e(n,r+1)/e(n,r)\to1$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0608/_index|E0608/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with more than $n^2/4$ edges has at least $2n^2/9$ edges that
lie in a $C_5$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0610/_index|E0610/]]: Asks for estimates of
the clique transversal number of an $n$-vertex graph. Proved under the site's
PROVED (LEAN) label, with the answer $n-\Theta(\sqrt{n\log n})$: the
Joret–Micek–Reed–Smid clique-coloring bound gives the upper bound and Kim's
triangle-free graphs the lower bound.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0611/_index|E0611/]]: Asks whether cliques of
at least $cn$ vertices force a sublinear clique transversal $\tau$, and which
clique size $k_c(n)$ forces $\tau<(1-c)n$. Open; by 1992, $k_c(n)\ge
n^{c'/\log\log n}$ for infinitely many $n$, and $\tau\le n-\sqrt{kn}$ with small
exceptions if cliques exceed $k$ vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0612/_index|E0612/]]: Asks whether the two
Erdős–Pach–Pollack–Tuza diameter bounds hold for connected graphs with no
$K_{2r}$ (part (i)) or no $K_{2r+1}$ (part (ii)). Part (i) is refuted in a
refereed paper; part (ii) is proved at $r=1$, and pending claims refute it for
some larger $r$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0614/_index|E0614/]]: Asks for the fewest
edges in a graph on $n$ vertices in which every set of $k+2$ vertices induces a
subgraph of maximum degree at least $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0616/_index|E0616/]]: Asks for the best bound
$t$ on the covering number of an $r$-uniform hypergraph, $r\ge3$, in which every
subhypergraph on at most $3r-3$ vertices has covering number at most $1$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0617/_index|E0617/]]: Asks whether every
$r$-coloring of the edges of $K_{r^2+1}$, for $r\ge3$, has $r+1$ vertices whose
induced $K_{r+1}$ misses a color.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0618/_index|E0618/]]: Asks whether every
triangle-free graph with maximum degree $o(n^{1/2})$ can be given diameter $2$,
still triangle-free, by adding $o(n^2)$ edges; proved by Alon. The page also
records the catalog's notation and diameter qualifications and the earlier
bounded-degree results.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0619/_index|E0619/]]: Asks whether there is a
constant $c>0$ such that every connected triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices can
be given diameter at most $4$, still triangle-free, by adding fewer than
$(1-c)n$ edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0620/_index|E0620/]]: Asks how large a
triangle-free induced subgraph every $K_4$-free graph on $n$ vertices must
contain (the Erdős–Rogers problem). The refereed record gives the answer up to a
logarithmic factor, and a 2026 preprint claims it is of order $\sqrt{n\log n}$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0621/_index|E0621/]]: Asks whether, in every
graph on $n$ vertices, the largest edge set containing at most one edge of each
triangle and the smallest edge set meeting every triangle have sizes summing to
at most $n^2/4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0622/_index|E0622/]]: Asks whether every
regular graph of degree $n+1$ on $2n$ vertices has $\gg 2^{2n}$ vertex subsets
spanned by a cycle; proved, and the limiting constant is $1/2$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0641/_index|E0641/]]: Asks whether there is a
function $f$ such that every finite graph with chromatic number at least $f(k)$
has $k$ edge-disjoint cycles on the same vertex set, for every $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0642/_index|E0642/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices in which every cycle has more vertices than chords must have at
most a constant times $n$ edges.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0666/_index|E0666/]]: Asks whether, for every
$\epsilon>0$ and all large $n$, every subgraph of the hypercube $Q_n$ with at
least an $\epsilon$ fraction of its edges contains a $C_6$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0712/_index|E0712/]]: Asks for the Turán
density of the complete $r$-uniform hypergraph on $k$ vertices, for any $k>r>2$;
open. Turán's 1941 theorem settles $r=2$, no pair with $r>2$ is known, and
Erdős's two prize offers stand.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0713/_index|E0713/]]: Asks whether every
bipartite graph $G$ with at least two edges has $\mathrm{ex}(n;G)\sim cn^\alpha$
for some $c>0$ and $\alpha\in[1,2)$, and whether $\alpha$ must be rational;
open. The exponent forms Erdős proposed in 1967 were disproved in 1970.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0714/_index|E0714/]]: Asks whether the maximum
number of edges in a $K_{r,r}$-free graph on $n$ vertices is $\gg n^{2-1/r}$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0715/_index|E0715/]]: Asks whether every
$4$-regular graph contains a $3$-regular subgraph, and whether some $r$ forces
one in every $r$-regular graph. Tashkinov answered both yes in 1982, for $r=4$
and for every $r\ge3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0717/_index|E0717/]]: Asks whether every
$n$-vertex graph $G$ has $\chi(G)\ll n^{1/2}\sigma(G)/\log n$, where $\sigma(G)$
is the order of the largest clique subdivision in $G$. This Erdős–Fajtlowicz
conjecture was proved by Fox, Lee and Sudakov in 2013.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0718/_index|E0718/]]: Asks whether there is a
constant $C$ such that every graph on $n$ vertices with at least $Cr^2n$ edges
contains a subdivision of $K_r$. This conjecture of Erdős, Hajnal and Mader was
proved by Bollobás and Thomason and by Komlós and Szemerédi.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0742/_index|E0742/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices with diameter $2$, in which deleting any edge raises the diameter,
has at most $n^2/4$ edges. Füredi proved this for all large $n$, and one
unreviewed proof claim covers every $n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0743/_index|E0743/]]: Asks whether $K_n$ can
always be written as the edge-disjoint union of given trees $T_2,\ldots,T_n$,
where $T_k$ has $k$ vertices. This tree packing conjecture of Gyárfás is open,
and one arXiv proof claim was withdrawn.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0745/_index|E0745/]]: Asks for the size of the
second largest component of the random graph on $n$ vertices with edge
probability $1/n$. Aldous (1997) gives order $n^{2/3}$, while the site credits
Komlós, Sulyok and Szemerédi's supercritical $\log n$ bound.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0746/_index|E0746/]]: Asks whether a uniform
random graph on $n$ vertices with at least $(\tfrac12+\epsilon)n\log n$ edges is
almost surely Hamiltonian. This Erdős–Rényi conjecture was settled by Korshunov
and by Komlós and Szemerédi.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0752/_index|E0752/]]: Asks whether a graph
with minimum degree $k$ and girth greater than $2s$ must have $\gg k^s$ distinct
cycle lengths.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0765/_index|E0765/]]: Asks for an asymptotic
formula for $\mathrm{ex}(n;C_4)$, the largest number of edges in a graph on $n$
vertices with no $C_4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0766/_index|E0766/]]: Asks for estimates of
$f(n;k,l)$, the least Turán number over graphs with $k$ vertices and $l$ edges,
for $k<l\le k^2/4$, and whether it is strictly monotone in $l$. Open;
asymptotics are known only at the pairs $(5,6)$ and $(6,9)$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0767/_index|E0767/]]: Asks whether, for large
$n$, the most edges in an $n$-vertex graph with no cycle carrying $k$ chords at
one cycle vertex is $(k+1)n-(k+1)^2$. Proved for $n\ge3k+3$ by Jiang (2004); a
2026 preprint claims the threshold.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0777/_index|E0777/]]: Asks how many comparable
pairs a family of $m$ subsets of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ can have for $m$ near
$2^{n/2}$. The three questions are answered yes, no and yes: the first by Alon,
Das, Glebov and Sudakov, the other two by Alon and Frankl.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0778/_index|E0778/]]: Two players alternately
color the edges of a complete graph, and the first wins if her largest
monochromatic clique beats her opponent's. Asks whether the first player can
win.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0794/_index|E0794/]]: Asks whether every
$3$-uniform hypergraph on $3n$ vertices with more than $n^3$ edges has four
vertices spanning three edges. False as written, by a $28$-edge example; the
Turán density of $K_4$ minus an edge stays open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0802/_index|E0802/]]: Asks whether every
$K_r$-free graph on $n$ vertices with average degree $t$ has an independent set
of size $\gg_r n\log t/t$. True for $r=3$ since 1980, and for every $r\ge4$ by a
Lean-checked theorem of the 2026 OpenAI release.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0803/_index|E0803/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with $n\log n$ edges has an almost-regular subgraph on $m$
vertices with $\gg m\log m$ edges. False by Alon's 2008 random bipartite
construction; Janzer and Sudakov's bound is the best positive one.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0804/_index|E0804/]]: Estimates the largest
independent set forced in an $n$-vertex graph in which every induced subgraph on
$m$ vertices has an independent set of size $\log n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0805/_index|E0805/]]: Asks for which functions
$g(n)$ there is a graph on $n$ vertices in which every induced subgraph on
$g(n)$ vertices has a clique and an independent set of size $\log n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0807/_index|E0807/]]: Asks whether the random
graph $G(n,1/2)$ almost surely needs exactly $n-\alpha(G)$ complete bipartite
graphs to partition its edges. Disproved by Alon, then by Alon, Bohman and
Huang.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0813/_index|E0813/]]: Estimates the largest
clique forced in an $n$-vertex graph in which every seven vertices span a
triangle. The exponent lies between $5/12$ (Bucić and Sudakov) and $1/2$ (Erdős
and Hajnal), and whether either bound can be improved is open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0814/_index|E0814/]]: Asks whether a graph
with one edge more than the count forcing a subgraph of minimum degree $k$ has
such an induced subgraph on at most $(1-c_k)n$ vertices. Proved by Sauermann for
$k\ge3$; the case $k=2$ is elementary.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0815/_index|E0815/]]: Asks whether, for large
$n$, a graph with $n$ vertices, $2n-2$ edges and no proper induced subgraph of
minimum degree $3$ contains $C_k$ for each fixed $k$. False at $k=23$ (Narins,
Pokrovskiy and Szabó), true for $k\le6$, open for even $k$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0816/_index|E0816/]]: Asks whether a graph
with $2n+1$ vertices and $n^2+n+1$ edges has two vertices of equal degree joined
by a path of length $3$, corrected to $n\ge2$ since $n=1$ (the triangle) fails.
Proved for $n\ge600$ by Chen and Ma; claimed for every $n\ge2$ by Liu and Zeng.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0883/_index|E0883/]]: Asks whether a set
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ above the triangle threshold of the coprime graph
forces all odd cycles up to length $n/3+1$ and, for large $n$, complete
$(1,\ell,\ell)$ tripartite subgraphs. Sárközy settled the second in 1999;
pending claims answer the first.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0900/_index|E0900/]]: Asks whether the uniform
random graph with $n$ vertices and $cn$ edges, $c>1/2$, almost surely has a path
of length $f(c)n$, where $f(c)\to0$ as $c\to1/2$ and $f(c)\to1$ as $c\to\infty$.
Proved by Ajtai, Komlós and Szemerédi in 1981.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0902/_index|E0902/]]: Estimates the least
order of a tournament in which every $n$ vertices have a common dominator. Open:
Erdős's 1963 upper bound and the 1965 lower bound of Szekeres and Szekeres
differ by a factor of order $n$, and exact values are known only up to $n=3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0904/_index|E0904/]]: Asks whether, for $n\ge
r$, every graph with $n$ vertices and $m\ge t_r(n)$ edges, $t_r(n)$ the Turán
number for $K_{r+1}$, has an $r$-clique with degree sum at least $2rm/n$. Proved
by Bollobás and Nikiforov in 2005; the site's wording, with no range, fails
below $r$ vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/_index|E0905/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with more than $n^2/4$ edges has an edge in at least $n/6$
triangles. Proved by Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov in 1979, by Edwards
(unpublished), and by Bollobás and Nikiforov in 2005.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0914/_index|E0914/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $rm$ vertices with minimum degree at least $m(r-1)$ has $m$ vertex-disjoint
copies of $K_r$. This is Erdős's conjecture, proved by Hajnal and Szemerédi in
1970 and reproved in refereed papers of 2008 and 2010.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0915/_index|E0915/]]: Asks whether a graph
with $1+n(m-1)$ vertices and $1+n\binom{m}{2}$ edges has two vertices joined by
$m$ disjoint paths. False for $m\ge5$ if the paths are vertex-disjoint, true for
every $m$ if they are edge-disjoint; the site labels it solved.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0916/_index|E0916/]]: Asks whether, for
$n\ge4$, every graph with $n$ vertices and $2n-2$ edges has a cycle and another
vertex adjacent to three of its vertices. Proved by Thomassen in 1974; the
site's wording, with no range, fails at $n=1$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0926/_index|E0926/]]: Asks whether an
$n$-vertex graph avoiding $H_k$ has $\ll_k n^{3/2}$ edges, where $H_k$ is a
vertex joined to $k$ others and each pair of these $k$ has its own further
common neighbor.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0927/_index|E0927/]]: Asks whether the largest
number of distinct clique sizes in a graph on $n$ vertices is
$n-\log_2n-\log_*(n)+O(1)$. Disproved by Spencer, whose construction removes the
$\log_*(n)$ term.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0934/_index|E0934/]]: Estimates $h_t(d)$, the
least number of edges forcing a graph of maximum degree at most $d$ to have two
edges at distance at least $t$. Open: it is known exactly for $t=1$ and $t=2$
and at $h_3(3)=23$, lies between $0.629^td^t$ and $\frac32d^t+1$ in general, and
2026 preprints treat $t=3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0993/_index|E0993/]]: Asks whether the
sequence counting the independent sets of each size in a tree or forest is
unimodal; proofs are claimed, but none is accepted.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1006/_index|E1006/]]: Asks whether every graph
of girth at least five has an acyclic orientation that stays acyclic when any
one edge is reversed. False, by Nešetřil and Rödl's graphs of large girth with a
monotone cycle under every ordering of the vertices.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1007/_index|E1007/]]: Asks for the fewest
edges in a graph of dimension $4$, where a graph's dimension is the least $n$
such that it embeds in $\mathbb{R}^n$ with every edge a unit segment. The answer
is nine, attained only by $K_{3,3}$ up to isolated vertices (House; Chaffee and
Noble).

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1008/_index|E1008/]]: Asks whether every graph
with $m$ edges has a $C_4$-free subgraph with $\gg m^{2/3}$ edges. True (Conlon,
Fox and Sudakov); Bollobás and Erdős's first form, with $m^{3/4}$, fails by
Folkman's example.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1009/_index|E1009/]]: Asks whether, for each
$c>0$, every graph on $n$ vertices with $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+k$ edges, $k<cn$,
has at least $k-f(c)$ edge-disjoint triangles. Proved by Győri (1988), a paper
known only through attestation.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1010/_index|E1010/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+t$ edges, $t<\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, has
at least $t\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ triangles. This is the Erdős-Rademacher
conjecture, proved in full by Lovász and Simonovits.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1011/_index|E1011/]]: Asks for $f_r(n)$, the
least number of edges that forces a triangle in a graph on $n$ vertices with
chromatic number at least $r$. It is known exactly for $r\le3$ and for $r=4$
with $n$ large, and open in general.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1012/_index|E1012/]]: Asks to determine or
estimate how large $n$ must be, in terms of $k$, for
$\binom{n-k-1}{2}+\binom{k+2}{2}+1$ edges to force a cycle on $n-k$ of the $n$
vertices. Settled by Woodall's 1972 Corollary 11.1, which covers every
$n\ge2k+3$ and the smaller $n$ too.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1016/_index|E1016/]]: Asks to estimate $h(n)$,
the fewest edges beyond $n$ that an $n$-vertex graph needs to have cycles of
every length from $3$ to $n$. It is known that $h(n)\ge\log_2(n-1)-1$, Bondy
claimed an upper bound of $\log_2n$ plus an iterated logarithm, and a 2026 proof
claim is pending.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1017/_index|E1017/]]: Asks to estimate the
number of edge-disjoint complete graphs needed to partition the edges of a graph
on $n$ vertices with more than $n^2/4$ edges. It is open, though Győri and
Keszegh settle the $K_4$-free case up to about $n^2/16$ edges beyond $n^2/4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1018/_index|E1018/]]: Asks whether, for large
$n$, every graph on $n$ vertices with at least $n^{1+\epsilon}$ edges has a
non-planar subgraph of bounded size. Answered yes by Kostochka and Pyber in
1988, through a subdivided $K_5$ of bounded size.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1019/_index|E1019/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+\lfloor\frac{n+1}{2}\rfloor$ edges
contains a saturated planar subgraph on more than $3$ vertices. Proved by
Simonovits in his thesis, as attested by Erdős and the site.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1021/_index|E1021/]]: Asks whether, for every
$k\ge3$, $\mathrm{ex}(n,G_k)\ll n^{3/2-c_k}$ for some $c_k>0$, where $G_k$ is
the bipartite graph joining each pair among $k$ vertices to its own vertex.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1031/_index|E1031/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices with no empty or complete subgraph on $10\log n$ vertices has an
induced regular subgraph on $\gg\log n$ vertices that is neither empty nor
complete.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1033/_index|E1033/]]: Asks to estimate $h(n)$,
the largest degree sum forced on some triangle in a graph on $n$ vertices with
more than $n^2/4$ edges, and whether $h(n)\ge(2(\sqrt3-1)-o(1))n$, about
$1.464n$. It is open, between Fan's $21n/16$ and a construction's
$2(\sqrt3-1)n+O(1)$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1034/_index|E1034/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with more than $n^2/4$ edges has a triangle with more than
$(\frac12-o(1))n$ vertices each joined to at least two of its vertices. This is
the Erdős–Faudree conjecture, disproved by Ma and Tang's construction with
constant $2-\sqrt{5/2}$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1035/_index|E1035/]]: Asks whether there is a
constant $c>0$ such that every graph on $2^n$ vertices with minimum degree
greater than $(1-c)2^n$ contains the $n$-dimensional hypercube $Q_n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1036/_index|E1036/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices with no empty or complete subgraph on more than $c\log n$ vertices
has $2^{\Omega_c(n)}$ pairwise non-isomorphic induced subgraphs.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1037/_index|E1037/]]: Asks whether a graph on
$n$ vertices with each degree occurring at most twice and more than
$(\frac12+\epsilon)n$ distinct degrees has an empty or complete subgraph much
larger than $\log n$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1066/_index|E1066/]]: Asks to estimate $g(n)$,
the largest $g$ such that every graph on $n$ points in the plane at pairwise
distance at least $1$, with edges joining the pairs at distance exactly $1$, has
an independent set of size $g$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1077/_index|E1077/]]: Asks whether every graph
on $n$ vertices with $n^{1+\alpha}$ edges has an almost-regular subgraph on
$m>n^{1-\alpha}$ vertices with at least $\epsilon m^{1+\alpha}$ edges. It is
false as written; on the site's account of a 2025 preprint, $n^\alpha$ is the
right size.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1078/_index|E1078/]]: Asks whether an
$r$-partite graph with $n$ vertices in each part and minimum degree at least
$(r-\frac32-o(1))n$ must contain a $K_r$. Proved by Haxell, with the exact
threshold following from Haxell and Szabó by complementation.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1079/_index|E1079/]]: Asks whether, for
$r\ge4$, a graph on $n$ vertices with at least $\mathrm{ex}(n;K_r)$ edges has a
vertex of degree $d\gg_r n$ whose neighborhood has at least
$\mathrm{ex}(d;K_{r-1})$ edges. Proved by Bollobás and Thomason in 1981 and
strengthened by Bondy in 1983.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1080/_index|E1080/]]: Asks whether some $c>0$
makes every bipartite graph on $n$ vertices with a part of $\lfloor
n^{2/3}\rfloor$ vertices and at least $cn$ edges contain a $C_6$. Disproved by
the superlinear $C_6$-free constructions the site credits, whose papers are not
held.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1111/_index|E1111/]]: Asks whether bounded
clique number and large chromatic number force two anticomplete vertex sets,
both of large chromatic number. This is the El-Zahar–Erdős problem, open beyond
the case of chromatic number $3$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1155/_index|E1155/]]: Asks for the typical
structure of the graph left by removing uniformly random triangles from $K_n$
until none remain, and whether its edge count has order $n^{3/2}$. The sharp
constant is formally verified, but the typical structure is open.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1157/_index|E1157/]]: Asks for the most edges
an $r$-graph on $n$ vertices can have with no $k$ vertices spanning $s$ edges,
the Brown–Erdős–Sós problem. The associated conjecture is proved for $s=3$ and,
in its linear form, for large uniformity, but open for $3$-graphs with $k=s+3$
and $s\ge4$.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1158/_index|E1158/]]: Asks whether Erdős's
1964 upper exponent for the Turán number of $K_t(r)$, the complete $t$-partite
$t$-uniform hypergraph with $r$ vertices per class, is attained up to $o(1)$. It
is known only for $t=2$ and $r\le3$.

edge counts, cycles and girth, degree and subgraph-counting conditions,
planarity, and structural questions such as the Erdos-Hajnal conjecture.
