# problems/set_systems/E0702/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/claims/1977_08_01_frankl|1977_08_01_frankl]]: Frankl (1977) proves that for $k\ge4$ and $n$ large in terms of $k$, any
family of more than $\binom{n-2}{k-2}$ sets of size $k$ in an $n$-set has two
members meeting in exactly one point. The result is refereed, credited by the
site's curator and formalized.

[[problems/set_systems/E0702/claims/2026_08_18_alexeev|2026_08_18_alexeev]]: Answers the site's wording (every $n$), not the modified Statement
($n>n_0(k)$, Erdős's own range), so it does not count toward the problem's
standing. A Lean theorem in Alexeev's repository refutes that wording at the
five $4$-subsets of a $5$-set.
