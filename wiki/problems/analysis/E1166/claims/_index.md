[[problems/analysis/E1166/claims/2024_09_02_hao_li_okada_zheng|2024_09_02_hao_li_okada_zheng]]: The eventual bound of three on planar favorite sites by Hao, Li, Okada and
Zheng, combined with the Erdős–Taylor estimate for the maximum local time,
gives almost surely $O((\log n)^2)$ sites ever favorite by time $n$. The
inputs are refereed, but the deduction itself is unpublished.

the repository's formalization of Problem 1165. It has not been built or
reviewed here.
