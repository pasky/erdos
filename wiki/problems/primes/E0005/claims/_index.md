# problems/primes/E0005/claims

[[problems/primes/E0005/_index|..]]

[[problems/primes/E0005/claims/2005_08_10_goldston_pintz_yildirim|2005_08_10_goldston_pintz_yildirim]]: Theorem 2 of Goldston, Pintz and Yıldırım (Ann. of Math. 2009) states that
$\liminf_n(p_{n+1}-p_n)/\log p_n=0$, so $0$ is a limit point, the case $C=0$
of the question. It is accepted on the basis of the refereed publication.

[[problems/primes/E0005/claims/2013_05_27_pintz|2013_05_27_pintz]]: Theorem 3 of Pintz's chapter in From Arithmetic to Zeta-Functions (2016)
states that every $C$ in $[0,c]$ is a limit point of the normalized prime
gaps, for an ineffective $c>0$. It is recorded as claimed, with no evidence of
refereeing.
