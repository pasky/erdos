# problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/_index|..]]

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims/1981_01_01_odoni|1981_01_01_odoni]]: Odoni shows that the number of integers up to $x$ that are sums of two
squarefull numbers exceeds any constant times $x/\sqrt{\log x}$, so the
asymptotic in Problem 1081 fails. The paper is refereed, and the site credits
it.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims/2004_01_30_blomer|2004_01_30_blomer]]: In a refereed paper (J. Reine Angew. Math., 2004), Blomer bounds the number of
sums of two squarefull numbers up to $x$ below by $x(\log x)^{-0.253}$. This
lower bound exceeds any constant times $x/\sqrt{\log x}$.

[[problems/diophantine_problems/E1081/claims/2006_11_01_blomer_granville|2006_11_01_blomer_granville]]: In a refereed paper (Duke Math. J., 2006), Blomer and Granville bound the
number of sums of two powerful numbers up to $x$ within powers of $\log\log x$
of $x/(\log x)^{1-2^{-1/3}}$. So the asymptotic $c\,x/\sqrt{\log x}$ fails.
