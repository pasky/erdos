# problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/claims/1975_01_01_erdos_graham_montgomery_rothschild_spencer_straus|1975_01_01_erdos_graham_montgomery_rothschild_spencer_straus]]: Theorem 1 of the 1975 colloquium paper reduces a triangle to the three
equilateral triangles with the same side lengths. Its ladder, roulette and
five-point methods prove several families of triangles Ramsey in the
two-colored plane, which the site lists as claimed.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/claims/1976_05_01_shader|1976_05_01_shader]]: Shader's 1976 theorem says that every two-coloring of the plane contains a
monochromatic congruent copy of every right triangle, and of two further
one-parameter families of triangles. It is refereed, in J. Combin. Theory Ser.
A.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0173/claims/2024_02_22_currier_moore_yip|2024_02_22_currier_moore_yip]]: Currier, Moore and Yip prove that every two-coloring of the plane contains a
monochromatic congruent copy of three equally spaced collinear points, and
hence of every $(a,2a,xa)$ triangle with $x\in[1,3]$. The paper is refereed,
in Combinatorica 44 (2024).
