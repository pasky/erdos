# problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims/2001_07_01_grytczuk_luca_wojtowicz|2001_07_01_grytczuk_luca_wojtowicz]]: Grytczuk, Luca and Wójtowicz prove that $\phi(n-\phi(n))>\phi(n)$ for
infinitely many $n$, with a growing gap, and that the reverse holds on a set
of lower density at least 0.54. The paper is refereed, and the site credits
the result.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E1064/claims/2002_01_01_luca_pomerance|2002_01_01_luca_pomerance]]: Luca and Pomerance prove that $\phi(n)>\phi(n-\phi(n))$ for almost all $n$, by
a margin of any order below $n$. They remark without proof that the reverse
holds infinitely often by any factor.
