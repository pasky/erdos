[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E1158/claims/2026_09_18_menezes|2026_09_18_menezes]]: An arXiv preprint of Menezes claims, for every $q=2^m$, an algebraic box-free
tripartite $3$-graph with $q^4$ vertices in each part and $q^{11}$ edges, so
that $\mathrm{ex}_3(n,K_3(2))=\Theta(n^{11/4})$. This answers the case $t=3$,
$r=2$ yes; no other case with $t\ge3$ is touched.
