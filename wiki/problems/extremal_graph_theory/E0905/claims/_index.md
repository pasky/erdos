# problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/_index|..]]

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/1977_01_01_edwards|1977_01_01_edwards]]: Edwards's unpublished 1977 manuscript proves the $n/6$ book bound and is
accepted on the site's curator's credit, though the proof was never published.
The site and Bollobás and Nikiforov credit it as an independent proof, and
Erdős credits it as a proof of the conjecture.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/1979_10_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov|1979_10_01_khadzhiivanov_nikiforov]]: Khadzhiivanov and Nikiforov (C. R. Acad. Bulgare Sci. 1979) prove that every
graph with $n$ vertices and more than $n^2/4$ edges has an edge on at least
$n/6$ triangles. The claim is accepted on the 1988 reproof, Fox and Loh, and
the site's credit.

[[problems/extremal_graph_theory/E0905/claims/2004_05_05_bollobas_nikiforov|2004_05_05_bollobas_nikiforov]]: Corollary 3 of Bollobás and Nikiforov, Books in graphs (European J. Combin.
2005), gives every graph with $n$ vertices and more than $n^2/4$ edges a book
of size greater than $n/6$. The claim is accepted on the refereed publication.
