# problems/arithmetic_functions/E0367/claims

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0367/_index|..]]

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0367/claims/2025_11_20_van_doorn|2025_11_20_van_doorn]]: Van Doorn's Pell construction, completed by Tao with Gemini Deepthink, makes
the product of the powerful parts of three consecutive integers exceed
$c\,n^2\log n$ infinitely often, so the bound $\ll_k n^2$ fails for $k\ge3$.
The claim is pending.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0367/claims/2026_06_10_hughes|2026_06_10_hughes]]: Scott Hughes claims, in a Lean development, that the product of the powerful
parts of three consecutive integers exceeds any multiple of $n^2\log n$, by
running the Pell construction over many primes at once. There is no public
paper, and the claim is pending.

[[problems/arithmetic_functions/E0367/claims/2026_06_10_hughes_conditional|2026_06_10_hughes_conditional]]: Scott Hughes's conditional theorem says that, under a lower bound on the
radical implied by the abc conjecture, the product of the powerful parts of
$k$ consecutive integers is at most a constant times $n^{2+\varepsilon}$.
There is no public paper, and the claim is pending.
