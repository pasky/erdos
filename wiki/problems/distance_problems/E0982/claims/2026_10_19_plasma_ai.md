**Claim.** Let $3\le m\le32$, put $n=2m$, and let $r$ be real with
$\cos(\pi/m)<r<\sec(\pi/m)$. The $n$ points of the plane, written as
complex numbers,

$$
A_k=e^{2\pi ik/m},\qquad B_k=re^{(2k+1)\pi i/m},\qquad 0\le k<m,
$$

form a strictly convex polygon, and some vertex has at least
$m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ distinct distances to the other vertices. Let
$8\le n\le24$ and let $s>0$ be real. The $n$ points

$$
p_k=\bigl(\cos(2\pi k/n),\sqrt s\,\sin(2\pi k/n)\bigr),\qquad 0\le k<n,
$$

form a strictly convex polygon, and some vertex has at least
$\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ distinct distances to the other vertices. Thus
neither family contains a counterexample in these ranges.

**Covers.** Two one-parameter families of convex polygons on
[[problems/distance_problems/E0982/_index|Problem 982]]: the half-step
concentric pairs of regular $m$-gons, of even orders $6\le n\le64$, and
the regular $n$-gons stretched along the fixed coordinate axes, of orders
$8\le n\le24$. It settles no order $n$: other convex $n$-gons, other
angular offsets between the two orbits, further radial orbits, stretches
along other axes and all orders outside these ranges are not covered, and
the problem remains open.

**Standing.** Tier 0. The reduction to finitely many critical
parameters, the strict-convexity tests and the census are author-recorded
by Plasma AI, without independent review. The lead's evidence re-runs the
exact enumeration in cyclotomic arithmetic, with certified real
enclosures deciding only strict signs; it is author evidence and confers
no tier. No Lean proof is asserted. Catalog acceptance is pending.
