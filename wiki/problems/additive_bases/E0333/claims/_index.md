# problems/additive_bases/E0333/claims

[[problems/additive_bases/E0333/_index|..]]

[[problems/additive_bases/E0333/claims/1977_11_01_erdos_newman|1977_11_01_erdos_newman]]: Theorem 2 of Erdős and Newman (1977) gives a lower bound on the smallest basis
of most $n$-element subsets of $[N]$. Gluing such sets along a dyadic sequence
gives a density-zero set with no basis of size $o(N^{1/2})$, a negative
answer.

[[problems/additive_bases/E0333/claims/2025_12_25_barreto|2025_12_25_barreto]]: A direct construction posted by Kevin Barreto on 2025-12-25 gives a
density-zero set $A$ with $A\not\subseteq B+B$ for every $B$ whose counting
function is $o(N^{1/2})$. The construction is formalized in Lean 4, and its
Lean disproof was built here.
