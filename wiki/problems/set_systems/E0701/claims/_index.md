# problems/set_systems/E0701/claims

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/_index|..]]

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2011_01_01_borg|2011_01_01_borg]]: Borg (European J. Combin. 2011, refereed) proposes a weighted form of
Chvátal's conjecture and proves it for weighted families with a dominant
element. With unit weights this covers such families, a partial result.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2018_09_05_eifler_gleixner_pulaj|2018_09_05_eifler_gleixner_pulaj]]: Eifler, Gleixner and Pulaj (ACM Trans. Math. Softw. 2022, refereed) verify
Chvátal's conjecture for every downset on at most seven elements, a partial
result. They use exact integer programming with checked certificates.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2022_01_11_frankl_kupavskii|2022_01_11_frankl_kupavskii]]: Frankl and Kupavskii (Discrete Math. 2023, refereed) prove Chvátal's
conjecture for intersecting subfamilies of covering number at most $2$, a
partial result. The proof uses a matching between disjoint members of two
down-sets.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_16_chang_liu_liu|2026_09_16_chang_liu_liu]]: Chang, Liu and Liu's September 2026 preprint claims a proof of Chvátal's
conjecture for subsets of a finite set, through a sharp correlation inequality
for increasing Boolean functions. It is not refereed, and its third-party Lean
development has not been built.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_20_keevash|2026_09_20_keevash]]: Keevash's September 2026 preprint claims a proof of Kahn's flow conjecture in
full generality, which implies Kleitman's and Chvátal's conjectures,
sharpening the analysis of Chang, Liu and Liu. It was found with GPT-6 Astra
and is not refereed.

[[problems/set_systems/E0701/claims/2026_09_23_ellis_filmus_friedgut|2026_09_23_ellis_filmus_friedgut]]: Ellis, Filmus and Friedgut's September 2026 note claims a two-page spectral
proof of Chvátal's conjecture, a spectral proof of Kleitman's conjecture and a
projection-packing strengthening. It was found with ChatGPT-6 Astra and is not
refereed.
