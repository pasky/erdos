deductions against the held source and took the page's input pages at their

of the fourteen reports, adjudicates every finding against the sources
and the pages, and lists the accepted corrections C1--C10 with their exact
replacements. Those reconstruction records assign no tier: the pages remain
author-recorded reconstructions.

connections page and its two leads on 2026-09-28, are graded by
[[research/erdos_354/evidence/verify/connections_grade|the connections grade]],
which records pass for each, accepts the corrections C1--C12 and assigns no
tier.
