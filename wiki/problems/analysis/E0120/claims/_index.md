# problems/analysis/E0120/claims

[[problems/analysis/E0120/_index|..]]

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2020_01_08_cruz_lai_pramanik|2020_01_08_cruz_lai_pramanik]]: An arXiv preprint of Cruz, Lai and Pramanik claimed that every infinite set of
reals is avoided by a compact set of positive measure, the full Erdős
similarity conjecture. The authors withdrew it three days later over a gap in
Proposition 3.3.

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_07_03_mora_cuellar_iosevich_kulkarni_rojas_aravena_yavicoli|2026_07_03_mora_cuellar_iosevich_kulkarni_rojas_aravena_yavicoli]]: An arXiv preprint of Mora Cuellar, Iosevich, Kulkarni, Rojas Aravena and
Yavicoli asserts that $G+A$ and $G-A$ are never measure universal when $A$ is
an arbitrary infinite set and $G$ is a geometric sequence, or any set
containing a lacunary sequence of at most exponential decay.

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_09_03_iosevich_kulkarni_mora_cuellar_rojas_aravena_yavicoli|2026_09_03_iosevich_kulkarni_mora_cuellar_rojas_aravena_yavicoli]]: An arXiv preprint of Iosevich, Kulkarni, Mora Cuéllar, Rojas Aravena and
Yavicoli asserts that every set supporting a probability measure whose
Fourier–Stieltjes transform vanishes at infinity is avoided by a closed
periodic set of relative measure at least $1-\varepsilon$.

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_09_25_openai|2026_09_25_openai]]: An OpenAI release manuscript proves that for every $0<\eta<1$ a compact set in
$[0,1]$ of measure above $1-\eta$ contains no affine copy of
$\{2^{-n}:n\ge1\}$ with nonzero dilation of either sign. The corpus accepts it
as a partial answer on the Lean declaration its verification built and
audited.

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_10_05_openai|2026_10_05_openai]]: An OpenAI release manuscript claims that for each fixed ratio $q\in(0,1)$ and
each $\eta\in(0,1)$ some compact subset of $[0,1]$ of measure above $1-\eta$
contains no affine copy of $\{q^n:n\ge1\}$ with nonzero dilation. The claim is
pending, and only the ratio $q=1/2$ is formally verified.

[[problems/analysis/E0120/claims/2026_10_08_jiang_xi|2026_10_08_jiang_xi]]: An arXiv preprint of Jiang and Xi claims that every sequence of positive
reals tending to $0$ with $\liminf a_{n+1}/a_n>0$ is avoided by a compact
subset of $[0,1]$ of measure arbitrarily close to $1$. This contains the
release's geometric and dyadic cases; null sequences whose terms, in
decreasing order, have $\liminf a_{n+1}/a_n=0$ are not covered.
