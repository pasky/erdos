# problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/claims

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/_index|..]]

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/claims/1991_05_01_furedi|1991_05_01_furedi]]: Füredi's refereed 1991 theorem shows that the largest no-three-collinear
subset guaranteed among $n$ points with no four collinear has size above
$c\sqrt{n\log n}$ and is $o(n)$. This refutes Erdős's expectation that
$g(n)\gg n$.

[[problems/discrete_geometry/E0589/claims/2017_04_17_balogh_solymosi|2017_04_17_balogh_solymosi]]: Balogh and Solymosi's Theorem 2.1, refereed in Discrete Analysis, constructs
$n$-point planar sets with no four points collinear in which every subset of
$n^{5/6+o(1)}$ points has a collinear triple. So $g(n)\le n^{5/6+o(1)}$.
