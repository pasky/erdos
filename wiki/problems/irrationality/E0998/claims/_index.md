[[problems/irrationality/E0998/claims/1966_01_01_kesten|1966_01_01_kesten]]: Kesten's 1966 Theorem 4 shows that an interval has bounded discrepancy along
the multiples of an irrational $\alpha$ exactly when its length is a
fractional multiple of $\alpha$. This proves the corrected Statement.

[[problems/irrationality/E0998/claims/2026_08_17_alexeev|2026_08_17_alexeev]]: A Lean theorem in Alexeev's repository refutes the site's wording (both
endpoints on the orbit of $\alpha$) with $\alpha=\sqrt2/10$ and the interval
$[1/4,1/4+\alpha)$. It does not answer the corrected Statement (the length on
the orbit), so it does not count toward the problem's standing.
