***

**Claim.** For every sequence $z_1,z_2,\dots$ on the unit circle there is a
$c>0$ such that

$$
M_n=\max_{\lvert z\rvert=1}\Bigl\lvert\prod_{i\le n}(z-z_i)\Bigr\rvert>(\log n)^c
$$

for infinitely many $n$; in particular $\limsup M_n=\infty$. This is
Wagner, *On a problem of Erdős in Diophantine approximation*, Bull. London
Math. Soc. 12 (1980), no. 2, 81–88, cited as [Wa80] on the problem page. It
answers the first question of [[problems/polynomials/E0119/_index|Problem
119]], which erdosproblems.com records as Problem 4.1 of Hayman's 1974
collection [Ha74], attributed there to Erdős.

**Covers.** The first question only, that $\limsup M_n=\infty$. It does not
reach a power of $n$, which is the second question, answered by
[[problems/polynomials/E0119/claims/1991_11_01_beck|Beck 1991]], nor the sum
of the third question, answered by
[[problems/polynomials/E0119/claims/2026_07_14_korsky|Korsky 2026]].

**Acceptance.** Refereed: the paper appeared in the Bulletin of the London
Mathematical Society in March 1980. Reviewed: erdosproblems.com states that the
weaker conjecture $\limsup M_n=\infty$ was proved by Wagner, with the bound
$(\log n)^c$ infinitely often (page last edited 2026-09-01). The corpus counts
this as documented independent acceptance by the erdosproblems.com curator, T.
F. Bloom (erdosproblems.com). Proof coverage: none. The paper is not held in the
